Q. 
Why is it important to have an appropriate balance of PRAs and NRAs? 
1 
A. 
An appropriate level of PRAs coupled with reasonable “reach” targets 
2 
motivate improved performance.  Fundamentally, incremental PRAs will 
3 
drive increased levels of performance because attainment requires 
4 
increased creativity, effort, and resources.  Examples where PRAs have 
5 
increased the level of the Company’s performance include 1) the Damage 
6 
Prevention Program where the Company’s performance includes 
7 
significantly better than the state utility average: 2) Emergency Response 
8 
where the percent of gas odor and carbon monoxide response time within 
9 
30 minutes has risen from 79.2% in 2017 to 83.3% in 2019; and 3) LPP 
10 
elimination where the Company has replaced 6 additional miles in each of 
11 
the past two years.  
12 
Q.  
What does an appropriate balance of PRAs and NRAs look like? 
13 
A.  
Except for the 75 bps associated with compliance violations which 
14 
represent minimum requirements under 16 NYCRR Part 255 and Part 
15 
261, total PRA and NRA levels should be equivalent. Allowing the 
16 
Company an opportunity to earn an equal level of PRAs to NRAs will spur 
17 
improved performance instead of just preventing a degradation in 
18 
performance.  Moreover, both PRAs and NRAs should have reasonable 
19 
targets.
