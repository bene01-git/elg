Petition to Establish Calendar Year 2024 Earnings Adjustment Mechanism Targets  
 
Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
In accordance with the terms of the Joint Proposal adopted by the Public Service 
Commission (“Commission”) in these proceedings,1 Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a 
National Grid (“National Grid” or “the Company”) hereby petitions to establish earning 
adjustment mechanism (“EAM”) targets applicable to the Company’s performance during 
calendar year 2024.   
 
The Joint Proposal established a base three-year rate plan period (July 1, 2021 – June 30, 
2024), and adopted several electric and gas EAMs that are typically measured on a calendar year 
(“CY”) basis for each rate year.2  In addition to the base three-year rate plan period, the Joint 
Proposal includes a “stayout” period beginning July 1, 2024, and ending the earlier of the 
effective date of revised rates for the Company or March 31, 2025.3  The Joint Proposal also 
provides that “[f]or periods beyond RY3, the Company may annually file a petition for 
Commission consideration proposing EAMs for the upcoming year until it files to establish new 
base delivery rates.”4  
 
By this petition, the Company proposes to implement four EAMs that were included in 
the Joint Proposal for the period beyond RY3 to be based on CY2024 performance and to
