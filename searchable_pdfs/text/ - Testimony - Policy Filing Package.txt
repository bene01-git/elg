Please describe the differences between the capital investments proposed by 
1 
KEDNY and KEDLI. 
2 
A. 
As noted above, there is a significant difference between the revenue deficiencies 
3 
of KEDNY and KEDLI, and that difference is largely attributable to several capital 
4 
cost drivers that arise in KEDNY’s service territory.  New York City is undertaking 
5

6 
of KEDNY’s city/state construction work (“CSC”), which involves the relocation 
7 
and/or protection of gas facilities that conflict with municipal public works projects, 
8 
continues to grow and now represents approximately one-third of KEDNY’s total 
9 
capital investment plan.  The timing and scope of CSC projects are outside of 
10 
KEDNY’s control, and these projects are expensive and prone to a high degree of 
11 
cost variability.  At the same time, KEDNY is experiencing higher unit costs 
12 
resulting from new work requirements, including recent changes to paving and 
13 
restoration requirements in New York City, as well as increased costs for 
14 
contractors due to demand.  These increased costs impact many of the mandated 
15 
programs in KEDNY’s capital investment plan, including CSC and main 
16 
replacement projects.  These factors, which are further compounded by the 
17 
increased volume and scope of construction projects in New York City, have 
18 
contributed to the differences in capital requirements and costs between the 
19 
Companies as these cost drivers are not currently experienced to the same degree 
20 
in KEDLI’s service territory.
