ChargePoint is a world leading electric vehicle charging network, providing scalable 
8 
solutions for every charging scenario from home and multifamily to workplace, parking, 
9 
hospitality, retail, and transport fleets of all types. ChargePoint’s cloud subscription platform 
10 
and software-defined charging hardware is designed to enable businesses to support drivers, 
11 
add the latest software features and expand fleet needs with minimal disruption to overall 
12 
business.   
13 
 
14 
ChargePoint’s hardware offerings include Level 2 (L2) and DC fast charging (DCFC) 
15 
products, and ChargePoint provides a range of options across those charging levels for 
16 
specific use cases including light duty, medium duty, and transit fleets, multi-unit dwellings, 
17 
residential (multi-family and single family), destination, workplace, and more. 
18

19 
charging onsite with features like Waitlist, access control, charging analytics, and real-time 
20 
availability. With modular design to help minimize downtime and make maintenance and 
21 
repair more seamless, all products are also UL-listed and CE (EU) certified, and Level 2 
22 
solutions are ENERGY STAR® certified. 
23 
 
24

25 
to businesses and organizations while offering tools that empower station owners to deploy 
26 
EV charging designed for their individual application and use case. ChargePoint provides 
27 
charging network services and data-driven, cloud-enabled capabilities that enable site hosts 
28 
to better manage their charging assets and optimize services. For example, with those 
29 
network capabilities, site hosts can view data on charging station utilization, frequency and 
30 
duration of charging sessions, set access controls to the stations, and set pricing for charging 
31 
services. These features are designed to maximize utilization and align the EV driver

II. 
Program Summary 
17 
Q: 
Please describe the Companies’ proposed Beneficial Electrification programs.  
18 
A: 
The Companies’ Beneficial Electrification programs consist of the following three 
19 
components: 
20 
• MHD Make-Ready Program: With a budget of $52.9 million over three years, the 
21 
MHD Make-Ready Program will provide make-ready infrastructure incentives to support 
22 
the installation of 1,128 L2 EV ports and 842 DCFC ports. This program will cover up 
23 
to 100% of make-ready costs for public sector fleets, including school buses and other 
24 
transit and municipal vehicles. Private fleet operators, including but not limited to 
25 
delivery vehicles, will be eligible for incentives covering up to 50% of their make-ready 
26 
costs. The program is similarly structured to the existing Light-Duty Make-Ready 
27 
Program with customer incentives of up to $6,000 per plug towards L2-related 
28 
infrastructure and up to $55,000 per plug for DCFC-related infrastructure.1 
29

10

11

12 
needs for light-duty, medium-duty, and heavy-duty vehicles within the NYSEG service 
13 
area. The project will include one or more third parties that will own and operate the 
14 
chargers, and the operator will also be responsible for all driver amenities. The hub will 
15 
be near a NYSEG substation, include a dedicated feeder to serve an expected load of 20 
16 
MW. The Companies propose to apply existing make-ready program incentives. NYSEG 
17 
and the third-party owner/operator will seek federal funding to contribute to the cost of 
18 
building the charging hub, which will also reduce the capital requirement being 
19 
recovered in rates.
