COST OF EQUITY

Q.

2

No.  Based on the quantitative and qualitative analyses presented in the
3 
rebuttal testimony of Company Witness Nowak, Mr. Hale’s
4

7

8 
capital market impacts on investors’ perceptions including the interest rate

10 
inflationary pressure, and continues to apply the same Discounted Cash
11 
Flow (“DCF”) and Capital Asset Pricing Model (“CAPM”) formulas

established nearly 30 years ago; and (iii) includes methodological
13 
shortcomings that result in lower ROE results than Company Witness
14 
Nowak’s estimates.  In addition, as Mr. Nowak discusses in his rebuttal
15 
testimony, Mr. Hale fails to take into account the increased regulatory and
16 
business risk of other Staff panel testimony recommendations for higher
17 
Negative Revenue Adjustments (“NRAs”).
