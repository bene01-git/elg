INTRODUCTION

(Corning or the Company) filed tariff amendments to its tariff

2022 Rate Order.1  The Company’s proposed tariff amendments

is the twelve-month period ending June 30, 2025.  The tariff 
amendments became effective on a temporary basis on July 1,  
2024.  By this Order, the Commission authorizes the tariff 
amendments listed in the Appendix, which effectuate the rates

BACKGROUND 
 
 
The 2022 Rate Order adopted the terms of the Joint 
Proposal and approved a three-year rate plan for service 
commencing on July 1, 2022, and concluding June 30, 2025.2  For 
RY 3, the 2022 Rate Order authorized a levelized total revenue 
increase of $1,740,870, or 5.1 percent.  Under the 2022 Rate 
Order, the tariff delivery rates were calculated using the 
unlevelized revenue requirement and a surcharge credit/debit 
mechanism was used to achieve the levelized amounts of revenue 
requirement for RY 3.   
The 2022 Rate Order directed Corning to file, on not 
less than 30 days’ notice, to take effect on a temporary basis 
on July 1, 2024, tariff amendments for RY 3 as necessary to 
effectuate the provisions of the 2022 Rate Order.  The 
amendments set forth in the compliance filing would not become 
effective on a permanent basis until approved by the Commission.    
 
TARIFF FILING 
On May 16, 2024, Corning filed tariff amendments to 
P.S.C. No. 7 - Gas to effectuate the rates and provisions for 
RY 3 pursuant to the Commission’s 2022 Rate Order.  The 
amendments became effective, on a temporary basis, on July 1, 
2024. 
The Company complied with the requirements of 
newspaper publication pursuant to Public Service Law (PSL) 
§66(12)(b) and Title 16 of the New York Codes, Rules and

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), 66(1), and 66(12), the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the proposed tariff 
leaves, as well as modify, reject or approve such filed tariffs 
to ensure that the rates and services governed by those tariffs 
are just and reasonable and otherwise consistent with law.  As 
such, the Commission has the legal authority to review and 
approve tariff amendments on a permanent basis, as prescribed in 
this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
Corning’s proposed tariff amendments to schedule 
P.S.C. No. 7 - Gas properly reflect the directives of the 2022 
Rate Order and include the rates, charges, rules and regulations 
necessary to effectuate the rates and provisions of RY 3.  Also, 
no comments were received regarding the filing.  Therefore, by 
this Order, the Commission authorizes the tariff amendments 
listed in the Appendix to become effective on a permanent basis 
as of the date of issuance of this Order. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by Corning Natural Gas 
Corporation, and listed in the Appendix to this Order, are 
authorized to become effective on a permanent basis upon the 
date of issuance of this Order.
