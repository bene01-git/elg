II. 
Purpose of Testimony 
14 
Q. 
What is the purpose of your supplemental direct testimony? 
15 
A. 
The purpose of my supplemental direct testimony is to discuss the impact 
16 
to the sales forecasts in the Companies’ April 2019 rate filings if they are 
17 
unable to access incremental natural gas supplies from Transcontinental 
18 
Gas Pipe Line, LLC’s Northeast Supply Enhancement (“NESE”) project, 
19 
which would help to relieve current gas supply constraints in the 
20 
Companies’ service territories during the Rate Year and Data Years.  In my 
21 


--- Page 4 ---
Page 2 of 6 
 
direct testimony, I identified certain impacts to the Companies’ filings if the 
1 
NESE project was not placed in service by the winter of 2020/2021.  As 
2 
discussed more fully in the supplemental testimony of Company Witness 
3 
Elizabeth D. Arangio, recent decisions by the New York State Department 
4 
of Environmental Conservation (“DEC”) and the New Jersey Department 
5 
of Environmental Protection regarding required water permits create some 
6 
uncertainty as to whether and when the NESE project may proceed with 
7 
permitting and construction.  While the Companies remain cautiously 
8 
optimistic the NESE project will go forward, and therefore are not 
9 
proposing any changes to their April 2019 filings at this time, I address the 
10 
impact that continued gas supply constraints would have on the Companies’ 
11 
gas sales forecasts in the Rate Year and Data Years.

As a result of the recent denials (without prejudice) of the water permits for 
1 
the NESE project, National Grid stopped processing all applications for new 
2 
or expanded firm gas service in Brooklyn, Queens and on Long Island as of 
3 
May 15, 2019, thereby curtailing customer load growth in the Companies’ 
4 
service territories.  The “no-growth” scenario presented in Exhibit ___ 
5 
(TEP-5S) assumes that, over the period of May 2019 through April 2020, 
6 
the Companies will fulfill the residential and commercial gas service 
7 
requests that were approved (919 customers in KEDNY and 553 in KEDLI) 
8 
prior to May 15th.  No additional customers will be added to the system in 
9 
the sales forecast.  Also, because existing customers will not be able to 
10 
increase their gas loads, this “no-growth” scenario assumes there will be no 
11 
changes to the existing uses-by-customer (i.e. the normalized March 2019 
12 
– February 2020 use-per-customer values will continue into the future).   
13 
 
 
14 
 
While the Companies will continue processing applications for non-firm 
15 
service from qualifying customers to the extent the Companies are able to 
16 
provide service, the Companies did not adjust their forecasts to reflect 
17 
additional non-firm customers because there is no conclusive information 
18 
on the number of customers who might desire such a service in the current 
19 
environment.
