III. 
Updates to the Revenue Requirement 
14 
Q. 
Please explain the impact of the NE:NY Order on the Company’s 
15 
revenue requirement. 
16 
A. 
As supported by the Future of Heat Panel, the Company used the energy 
17 
efficiency targets and budgets set forth in the Commission’s December 13, 
18 
2018 “Order Adopting Accelerated Energy Efficiency Targets” in Case 18-
19 
M-0084 (“Accelerated Efficiency Order”) to set the proposed Rate Year 
20 
energy efficiency rate allowance of $9.478 million.  This amount was 
21 
exclusive of $0.171 million of pass through Clean Energy Fund expenses 
22 
and $0.474 million of evaluation, measurement, and verification 
23 


--- Page 5 ---
Page 3 of 7 
 
(“EM&V”) costs that the Company originally proposed to move to base 
1 
rates and track outside of the energy efficiency portfolio.  Additionally, the 
2 
Company proposed to include the costs of its low-income energy efficiency 
3 
HEAT Program ($2.5 million) in base rates.   
4 
 
5 
 
The NE:NY Order increased KEDLI’s energy efficiency budget by an 
6 
incremental $1.232 million in the Rate Year, as compared to the budget 
7 
established in the Accelerated Efficiency Order.  In addition, the NE:NY 
8 
Order provided that EM&V costs are included in the authorized energy 
9 
efficiency budgets established by the Commission.  Based on the foregoing, 
10 
KEDLI is adjusting its revenue requirement to incorporate the incremental 
11 
energy efficiency budget increase, remove EM&V costs from base rates, 
12 
and remove the costs of the HEAT Program, as the HEAT program costs 
13 
will be funded through the increase to the energy efficiency budget.  Table 
14 
1 on page 5 summarizes the changes to the revenue requirement.   
15 
 
16 
Q. 
Is the Company proposing any additional updates to the revenue 
17 
requirement to address the impacts of the NE:NY Order? 
18 
A. 
Yes.  As explained by the Future of Heat Panel, to meet the new targets set 
19 
forth in the NE:NY Order, address the compliance and reporting 
20 
requirements, and to help optimize the energy efficiency portfolio to 
21 


--- Page 6 ---
Page 4 of 7 
 
achieve maximum customer benefits, the Company updated the revenue 
1 
requirement to reflect KEDLI’s allocable share of eight incremental FTEs.  
2 
KEDLI proposes to phase in the FTEs throughout the Rate Year and Data 
3 
Year 1 as it implements the provisions of the EE Order.  The request 
4 
increases the revenue requirement by $0.258 million in the Rate Year. 
5 
 
6 
Q. 
Does the Panel have any additional comments about the NE:NY Order? 
7 
A. 
Yes.  The Company recognizes the Commission’s expectation in the 
8 
NE:NY Order that any uncommitted and unspent energy efficiency funds 
9 
be applied to mitigate the cost of expanded energy efficiency programs.  
10 
While KEDLI generally supports the use of regulatory liabilities to mitigate 
11 
customer bills, the Company has concern for using significant regulatory 
12 
liabilities to offset the revenue requirement in this case without considering 
13 
the cash flow implications, the Company’s regulatory asset position, and 
14 
the long-term ratemaking implication.  Indeed, KEDLI has a significant 
15 
regulatory asset balance on its books that the Company is not proposing to 
16 
recover from customers in this rate filing - but must be addressed eventually. 
17 
Therefore, the Company believes there must be a balance between the 
18 
appropriate level of regulatory liabilities used now and those reserved for 
19 
future rate mitigation. 
20 
 
21
