Dear Secretary Phillips: 
The New York State Public Service Commission ("Commission") approved Joint Proposal ("JP"} in Corning 
Natural Gas Corporation's ("Corning" or "Company") Case 21-G-0394 at page 21 states "For years 
subsequent to CY 2024 Corning LDC shall replace or remove not less than 7 .0 miles per year until LPP 
replacement or removal is completed or until that target is changed by the Commission. The Signatory 
Parties agree that Corning LDC shall have the right to file a plan with the Commission to request 
modification of the 7.0-mile target applicable to RY 4 and subsequent Rate Years. If the Company files such 
a plan no later than the mid-point of RY 3, Staff will review the plan and provide its recommendations to 
the Commission pursuant to statutory deadlines incorporating the need for all necessary stakeholder 
input." 
Pursuant to the above referenced order, the Company requests that the CY 2025 LPP target be changed 
from 7.0 miles to 5.0 miles. In support of its request the Company provides the following·information. 
The Company's current rate plan requires that a total of 28 miles of LPP pipe be replaced over the period 
CY 2022, 2023 and 2024. The Company will meet the Commission established targets through 2024. At the 
end of 2024 the Company, will have only 19 miles of LPP pipe remaining, having replaced approximately 
150 miles of LPP pipe since 2005. The Company will file a rate case in 2024 to become effective on July 1, 
20251. The LPP replacement targets are set on a calendar year basis and not on a rate year basis. 
Therefore, the LPP replacement targets in the rate case filing will cover the CY 2026 to 2028. The CY 2025 
LPP target will be 7 miles per the current rate agreement unless modified. 
Since Corning's last rate case New York State enacted a major piece of legislation that materially impacts 
the cost of LPP replacement. On September 15, 2023, New York enacted the Roadway Excavation Quality 
Assurance Act ("Prevailing Wage") legislation. The Company estimates that Prevailing Wage legislation will 
nearly double the Company's project cost for its LPP replacement projects.2 In order to minimize the cost 
impact of the new Prevailing Wage legislation on the remaining 19 miles of LPP replacement, the Company 
has developed a plan to complete two miles annually of the remaining LPP replacement program with 
lower cost in-house personnel. The Company believes it does not have sufficient in-house personnel to

Conclusion 
The lower mileage target will permit more time to plan an orderly completion of the LPP pipe replacement 
program. The Company's proposed modification would add only one additional year to the expected 
completion date of the LPP replacement. If the Company's modification is granted the removal of the LPP 
pipe from the Corning system will take 23 years4 allowing for the completion of LPP replacement in 2028. 
For the reason stated above the Company asks that the LPP pipe replacement target of 7.0 miles currently 
set for CY 2025 be reduced to 5.0 miles and the JP approved by the Commission in Case21-G-0394 be 
modified accordingly.
