Q.    
Does the Panel agree with the use of a two-year average for setting the 
13 
leak backlog target? 
14 
A. 
No.  Calculating the leak backlog of 55 leaks by utilizing only two years of 
15

16 
which can be driven by several factors such as weather, material defects, 
17 
manufacture recalls, excavation damages or the number of existing leaks 
18 
removed on distribution improvement replacement projects in any given 
19 
year.  Utilizing only two years of data is not sufficient to accurately reflect 
20 
the impacts these factors may have on future leaks.

Q.    
Please describe how weather can impact the number of leaks found each 
1 
year. 
2 
A. 
Prolonged periods of colder temperatures with less snow cover drive the 
3 
frost line deeper into the ground, which in turn can cause more leaks.  The 
4 
Company generally finds more leaks in years experiencing such 
5 
conditions than in a year with a mild winter. 
6 
Q.    
Please describe the volatility in the leak backlog for the past three years. 
7 
A. 
Over the past three years, the Company’s leak backlog varied between a 
8 
maximum of 81 leaks during 2020, a minimum of 47 leaks in 2021, and 
9 
ended with 62 leaks in 2022.  The average leak backlog for this period is 
10 
63, while the standard deviation was approximately 14 leaks. 
11 
Q.    
What NRAs is the Company proposing for leak backlog? 
12 
A. 
The Company continues to propose the NRA targets and levels set forth in 
13 
the Panel’s direct testimony.  
14 
Q.    
Why is a target of 86 leaks appropriate?  
15 
A. 
A reasonable approach to setting the leak backlog target would be to use 
16 
the three-year average adjusted for volatility.  Specifically, due to the 
17 
volatility inherent in the historic year-end leak backlog, adding two 
18 
standard deviations to the three-year average would be appropriate.  
19 
Utilizing this approach would produce a leak backlog target for the 
20 
Company that is slightly greater than, but consistent with the Company’s 
21 
direct testimony target of 86.
