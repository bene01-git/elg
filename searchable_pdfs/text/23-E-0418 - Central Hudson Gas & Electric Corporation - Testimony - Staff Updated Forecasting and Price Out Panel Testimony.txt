Q. 
What are post-model adjustments? 
13 
A. 
Post-model adjustments are intended to adjust a 
14 
forecasting model for incremental changes in 
15 
trends that are not reflected in the historic 
16 
data upon which the econometric forecasting 
17 
model was estimated, such as changes in policy

that are expected to increase or decrease the 
19 
rate of adoption going forward relative to the

existing trend of adoption.  For example, the 
21 
existing rate of adoption reflected in the 
22 
recent trends of EVs do not take into account 
23 
policies and programs geared toward greatly

Q. 
What effect do post-model adjustments have on 
3 
the sales forecast? 
4 
A. 
The direct effect of each type of post-model 
5 
adjustment varies by the nature of the 
6 
adjustment.  For example, since EVs and HPs tend 
7

8

11 
cause electric sales to decrease due to self-
12 
supply and energy export, omitting the PV post-
13 
model adjustment would cause the forecast of 
14 
electric sales to be too high.
