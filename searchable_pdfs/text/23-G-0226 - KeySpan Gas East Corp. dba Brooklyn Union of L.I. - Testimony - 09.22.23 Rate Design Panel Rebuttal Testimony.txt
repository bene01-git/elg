6

The Companies’ revenue allocation proposals move the returns produced by 
7

13 
increases than average, and classes with returns below the tolerance were

V. 
Rate Design 
14 
Q. 
Please briefly describe the Companies’ approach to rate design. 
15 
A. 
The purposes of rate design are: (i) to collect the revenue target established for 
16 
each service class in the revenue allocation process; (ii) to avoid severely 
17 
disproportionate impacts on customer subgroups within a class; and (iii) to 
18 
provide signals to customers to reflect the effects of their usage on the overall 
19 
system, including supporting conservation. 
20 
 
21

1

3 
compared to the present rate design.  A lower slope supports conservation by

5 
a service class were increased by the same percentage.
