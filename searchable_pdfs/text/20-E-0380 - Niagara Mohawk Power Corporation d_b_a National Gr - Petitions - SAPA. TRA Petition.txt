NYS DEPARTMENT OF STATE 
NOTICE OF PROPOSED RULEMAKING 
Proposed Limited Waiver of the Transmission Revenue Adjustment 
PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION 
Pursuant to the provisions of the State Administrative Procedures Act, NOTICE is hereby 
given to the following proposed rule: 
1.
Proposed Action: 
The Public Service Commission (“Commission”) is considering a 
petition filed by Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“Niagara Mohawk” or 
the “Company”) regarding a limited waiver of the Transmission Revenue Adjustment
(“TRA”) provisions included in Rule 43.5.1 of the P.S.C. 220 – Electric Service Tariff (“Electric 
Tariff”).
2.
Statutory Authority Under Which the Rule is Proposed:  Public Service Law Sections 
5, 65, and 66.
3.
Subject of the Proposed Rule:  Proposed revisions to Niagara Mohawk’s TRA 
provisions included in Rule 43.5.1 of its Electric Tariff.
4.
Purpose of the Proposed Rule:  To determine whether to authorize Niagara Mohawk’s 
proposed revisions to the TRA provisions included in Rule 43.5.1 of its Electric Tariff.
5.
Substance of the Proposed Rule:  The Commission is considering a petition (“Petition”) 
filed by Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“Niagara Mohawk” or the
“Company”) on December 22, 2023, in Case 20-E-0380. The Petition requests approval for a 
limited waiver of the Transmission Revenue Adjustment (“TRA”) provisions included in Rule 
43.5.1 of the P.S.C. 220 – Electric Service Tariff (“Electric Tariff”). Niagara Mohawk seeks to 
temporarily increase the $8 million monthly cap on the amount that can be collected from or 
credited to customers to $16 million through the end of the Company’s current rate plan, which is 
March 31, 2025. The current TRA deferral balance as of December 2023 is $386,193,354 and 
increasing the cap would enable the Company to credit customers and reduce the current TRA 
deferral balance more expeditiously. Additionally, the Company requests waiver of the newspaper 
publication requirements under Public Service Law Section 66(12) and 16 NYRR Section 
720-8.1. The full text of the Petition and the full record of the proceedings may be reviewed 
online at the Department of Public Service web page: www.dps.ny.gov. The Commission may 
adopt, reject, or modify, in whole or in part, the action proposed and may resolve related matters.
