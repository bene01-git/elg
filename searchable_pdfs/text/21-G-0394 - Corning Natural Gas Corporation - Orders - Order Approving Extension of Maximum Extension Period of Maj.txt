CASE 21-G-0394 - Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to 
  
the Rates, Charges, Rules and Regulations of 
  
Corning Natural Gas Corporation for Gas

ORDER APPROVING EXTENSION OF MAXIMUM SUSPENSION PERIOD  
OF MAJOR RATE FILING 
 
(Issued and Effective May 13, 2022) 
 
 
BY THE COMMISISON: 
 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On July 16, 2021, Corning Natural Gas Corporation 
(Corning or the Company) filed amendments to its tariff schedule, 
P.S.C. No. 7 - Gas, requesting to increase the annual gas 
delivery revenues for the rate year ending June 30, 2023.  The 
Company is requesting an increase in annual gas delivery 
revenues of approximately $5.8 million (or 20.43 percent on an 
overall bill basis).  The requested increase in gas delivery 
revenues will result in a total monthly bill increase of about 
$36.72 (41.0 percent on a total bill) for a typical residential 
heating customer using 77 hundred cubic feet per month.

The Company asserts that the major cost drivers of the 
gas revenue request include mandated programs cost recovery, 
increased operating costs, recovery of regulatory amortization, 
increase in depreciation expense, reduced sales, and a change in 
return on equity compared to what is approved currently in 
rates.  By Notices issued July 23, 2021, and September 30, 2021, 
the Secretary to the Commission, pursuant to authority delegated 
to her1, extended the suspension period applicable to the 
effective date of the proposed tariff amendments through     
June 12, 2022, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.   
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
To accommodate the settlement process, by letter dated 
April 13, 2022, Corning agreed to extend the maximum suspension 
period of this proceeding through June 30, 2022.   The request 
would provide additional time to facilitate the settlement 
process and is subject to a make whole provision that would keep 
the Company in the same financial position it would have been in 
had there been no extension.  The Company’s request for an 
extension will allow the settlement process to occur in an 
orderly fashion.  
To facilitate the orderly conduct of this proceeding, 
the effective date of the tariff leaves filed by the Company is 
further extended through June 30, 2022, unless otherwise ordered 
by the Commission.

The Commission orders: 
1. The operation of the matter suspended by the 
Notices issued July 23, 2021, and September 30, 2021, in this 
proceeding, is further suspended and the use of the rates, 
charges, rules and regulations stated in said Notices be further 
extended through June 30, 2022, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission. 
2. Corning Natural Gas Corporation is  
directed to file with the Commission, on not less than one day’s 
notice on or before June 7, 2022, a consecutively numbered 
suspension supplement announcing the extension of the suspension 
period of suspended amendments.  
3. Corning Natural Gas Corporation shall be made whole 
for the period between the start of Rate Year One of the new 
rate plan and the date the Commission sets new rates, charges, 
rules and regulations for the new rate plan in the final 
decision in this proceeding. 
4.  This proceeding is continued.
