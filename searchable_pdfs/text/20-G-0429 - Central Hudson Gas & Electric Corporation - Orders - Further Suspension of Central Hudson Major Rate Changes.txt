RECOMMENDATION:  Further Suspension of the Effective Date of the 
Major Rate Changes. 
 
 
On August 27, 2020, Central Hudson Gas & Electric 
Corporation (Central Hudson or the Company) filed amendments to 
its tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 15 – Electricity and P.S.C. 
No. 12 - Gas, to increase the annual electric and gas delivery 
rates for the rate year ending June 30, 2022. 
 
Central Hudson is requesting an increase in annual 
electric delivery revenues of approximately $32.8 million (8.4% 
increase in base delivery revenues, or 5.1% increase in total

For its gas business, Central Hudson is requesting an 
increase in annual gas delivery revenues of approximately 
$14.4 million (12.1% increase in base delivery revenues, or 7.7% 
increase in total system revenues) compared to the revenues 
approved in Rate Year Three of the Company’s current rate plan.

monthly bill increase of about $9.45 (10.11% on the delivery 
bill, or 7.96% increase on the total bill) for an average 
residential heating customer using 70 Ccf per month.  The 
Company has proposed using $8 million of moderation in the form 
of bill credits, which would reduce the impact on customers from 
$14.4 million to $6.4 million (5.4% increase in base delivery 
revenues, or 3.4% increase in total system revenues).  The 
resulting impact with the use of moderators on an average

By Notice issued September 8, 2020, the proposed 
amendments were suspended through January 23, 2021.  The 
investigation of these proceedings is continuing. 
 
 
 
Pending the proceedings and a decision on the proposed 
changes, the operation of these amendments is further suspended 
and the use of the rates, charges, rules, and regulations are 
deferred through July 23, 2021, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission.  The rates, charges, rules, and regulations altered 
by the suspended amendments shall continue in effect until these 
proceedings are concluded or until the period of further 
suspension and any extended suspension expires, unless otherwise 
ordered by the Commission. 
 
 
 
Central Hudson is directed to file with the Commission 
not later than January 19, 2021, consecutively numbered 
supplements announcing suspension of the amendments.
