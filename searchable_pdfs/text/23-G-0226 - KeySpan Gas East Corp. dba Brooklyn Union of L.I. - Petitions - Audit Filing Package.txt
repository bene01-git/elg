III. 
Management Audit Update 
1 
Q. 
Please provide a brief summary of the Management Audit process. 
2 
A. 
On May 17, 2018, the New York State Public Service Commission 
3 
(“Commission”) issued an “Order Instituting Proceeding and Authorizing 
4 
Issuance of a Request for Proposals” (“Audit Order”) for an independent 
5 
consultant to conduct a Management Audit of National Grid’s New York 
6 
electric and gas businesses in accordance with Section 66(19) of the Public 
7 
Service Law (“PSL”).  In September 2018, the Commission selected SCG 
8 
to conduct the Management Audit under the supervision of DPS Staff.  
9 
From November 2018 through October 2019, SCG audited National Grid’s 
10 
New York electric and gas businesses, focusing on 12 traditional audit 
11 
areas:   
12 
• Corporate Governance 
13 
• Information Systems 
14 
• Electric Planning and Grid Modernization 
15 
• Electric Load Forecasting and Supply Procurement 
16 
• Gas Planning 
17 
• Gas Safety 
18 
• Budgeting and Finance 
19 
• Project Management 
20 
• Program Management 
21

• Work Management  
1 
• Performance Management  
2 
• Customer Operations  
3 
 
4 
In October 2019, DPS Staff informed the Company they were terminating 
5 
the contract with SCG, due to no fault of National Grid.  In December 2019, 
6 
DPS Staff advised they would complete the Management Audit based 
7 
primarily on the interviews and materials already obtained from National 
8 
Grid, with additional interviews and information requests, as necessary.

its full capabilities and receiving meaningful feedback that would drive 
14 
useful recommendations to improve the Company’s electric and gas 
15 
operations for the benefit of its customers.  Over the course of almost two 
16

Q. 
Please describe the results of the Management Audit. 
5 
A. 
In September 2020, DPS Staff completed its final audit report 
6 
(“Management Audit Report”) for Commission consideration.  The report 
7 
described generally strong performance by National Grid’s New York 
8 
business in a number of key functional areas, including electric and gas 
9 
operations, performance and project management, capital delivery, 
10 
budgeting and finance, and customer service.  The Management Audit 
11 
Report also acknowledged the efforts undertaken by National Grid to 
12 
manage customer bill impacts through the Company’s aggressive efficiency 
13 
programs.  The Management Audit Report offered 24 recommendations 
14 
aimed at improving National Grid’s performance.  The Commission 
15 
released the Management Audit Report on November 19, 2020.   
16 
 
17 
While the Company did not agree with all of DPS Staff’s findings and 
18 
conclusions, National Grid acknowledged that, overall, DPS Staff’s 
19 
recommendations provided a set of constructive proposals to enhance the 
20 
day-to-day performance of the New York electric and gas businesses.  
21

16

17 
each recommendation, implementation action steps (major activities), and 
18 
the deliverables identified in the Implementation Plan.  To date, the 
19 
Company has submitted ten updates to the Implementation Plan.  These

IV. 
Management Audit Costs 
11 
Q. 
Does the revenue requirement include costs associated with the 
12 
Management Audit or future management audits? 
13 
A. 
No.  There are no Management Audit costs in the Historic Test Year.  
14 
Similarly, KEDNY and KEDLI did not reflect any future audit costs in the 
15 
Rate Year or Data Years because the timing of the next management and 
16 
operational audit is unknown.  If the Commission were to undertake a future 
17 
management or operational audit, the Company’s expectation is that it 
18 
would be able to defer those costs for future recovery.
