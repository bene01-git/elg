DPS Staff Recommended Equity Ratio is Too High 
3 
Q.   Do you believe that the DPS staff recommended equity ratio 
4 
of 48% is too high? 
5 
A:   Yes. 
6 
Q:   What is the basis for this? 
7 
A:   The 48% common equity ratio is higher than necessary to 
8 
maintain the company’s creditworthiness and financial 
9 
stability. As discussed hereinabove, the company has 
10 
significant and robust access to capital, very strong 
11 
investor confidence, strong earnings growth, and a strong 
12 
fiscal position. The company’s actual equity ratio has 
13 
historically hovered around 46% to 48%. As such, the 
14 
recommendations would not strike a reasonable balance 
15 
between a fair and equitable return for investors, on the 
16 
one hand, and affordability, fairness and ratepayer equity 
17 
and the public interest, on the other hand. 
18 
Q:   What do you believe is an appropriate equity ratio? 
19 
A:   A 47% equity ratio would strike a fairer balance between 
20 
the needs of the company for fiscal stability and financial 
21 
strength and ratepayer impact, avoiding unnecessary cost 
22 
burdens on ratepayers. This would avoid the top end of the 
23 
historical equity range.
