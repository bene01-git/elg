National Grid: Halt Construction on the non-essential infrastructure:
North Brooklyn “MRI” Pipeline during Governor Andrew M. Cuomo’s
Executive Order “New York State on PAUSE"
March 23, 2020
Delivered via EMAIL
To: John Bruckner,  President of National Grid New York john.bruckner@nationalgrid.com
cc: Honorable Andrew Cuomo, Governor of New York State via online form
cc: Commissioner John Rhodes, Chair of New York State Public Service Commission
john.rhodes@dps.ny.gov
cc: Secretary Michelle Phillips of the New York State Public Service Commission
secretary@dps.ny.gov
Dear Mr. Bruckner,
We, the undersigned, are calling on National Grid to immediately halt construction of the
Metropolitan Reliability Infrastructure (MRI) project for the health and safety of construction
workers and the North Brooklyn community.
As of March 19, 2020, workers were seen on the construction site in close proximity to one another,
putting themselves and our community at risk by being in clear violation of suggested CDC social
distancing practices. And in the construction update emailed out by National Grid on March 23, the
company says they intend to continue construction along at least 5 North Brooklyn streets this week.
In light of ABC-7 reporting that a Consolidated Edison construction worker has tested positive for
COVID-19 , it is only appropriate for New York utilities to take the necessary precautions to
1
protect their workers and the communities in which they work and halt construction.
The North Brooklyn community has never consented to this pipeline being built through our
neighborhoods, using our rate payer dollars and for National Grid to continue to potentially expose our
community to novel coronavirus shows a further lack of disregard for our safety.
Furthermore, once construction is halted in response to the current public health crisis, we
encourage you to read the hundreds of public comments submitted by our community and
ratepayers across New York in Case 19-G-0309, requesting that no more ratepayer dollars be
approved for the MRI pipeline and that construction be halted permanently to address the
climate crisis and safety of our community.
Sincerely,
1 https://abc7ny.com/6022486/
