NY-GEO notes that compromise is necessary to achieving a workable outcome in rate cases and that the 
COVID driven economic/health crisis, CLCPA influence and climate change are important issues in this case. A 
major compromise we are encouraged by is the agreement to reduce billed gas sales by 2.5% over the next 4 
years. With this backdrop, we have indicated support for the Joint Proposal but would also like to acknowledge 
the following concerns: 
 
NY-GEO is appreciative of the Company’s willingness to seek clarification and maintain several basic elements 
of the Geothermal Rate Impact Credit (GRIC), but are disappointed that funding is not from the return of 
ratepayers over-payments via the Revenue Decoupling mechanism, and instead is derived from incentive based 
2020 Energy Efficiency Order funds.  In the future, we look forward to the establishment of fair and reasonable 
rates that reflect the true value of geothermal systems to ratepayers and the entire energy system. 
 
Modification of the Company website to end the marketing of gas is a positive aspect of the JP.  We hope this 
spirit of candidness and accuracy will carry over to all printed and verbal marketing following the example of 
other public utilities such as item 7case 19-E-0378 appendices. 
 
To NY-GEO and its members, this rate case negotiation is not just about the short term economics of our 
industry growth, or individual members profits. While important, we see this as a stepping stone/building block to 
a future that truly provides safe, reliable, affordable emissions free energy to our customers within Central 
Hudson’s Gas and Electric service territory, as well as maintaining a viable, vibrant and innovative Company. For 
the reasons mentioned above, NY-GEO supports this Joint proposal.
