INTRODUCTION 
 
This Statement In Reply To Opposition (Reply Statement) is filed by Department of 
Public Service Staff (Staff) in response to the following general issues raised by the opposing 
parties:1 1) compliance with the Commission’s settlement guidelines,2 2) the general 
affordability of the proposed rate increases, 3) the treatment of the Commission’s energy 
assistance program (EAP)3 and low income customers, the proposed earnings sharing 
mechanism, the proposed costs of capital, compliance with the Climate Leadership and 
Community Protection Act (CLCPA), 4) disadvantaged communities, gas issues, 5) electric 
issues, and 6) energy efficiency (EE) and electrification, and 7) distribution vegetation 
management.  For the reasons stated in Staff’s Statement in Support of the Joint Proposal and the 
reasons stated herein, the Commission has ample grounds to find that the Joint Proposal filed on 
June 14, 2023, in these proceedings is in the public interest.  Accordingly, Staff recommends that 
the Commission adopt the terms of the Joint Proposal and establish rate plans for New York 
Electric and Gas Corporation (NYSEG) and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation (RG&E) as 
set forth therein. 
 
REPLY TO OPPOSITION

Procedures and Guidelines, establishing its criteria for evaluating proposed rate case 
settlements.4  The Settlement Guidelines states that proposed settlements, like all Commission 
decisions must be and appear to be “just, reasonable, and in the public interest,” and in

valid and should be used by the Commission to evaluate the Joint Proposal.  Therefore, AARP’s 
criticism of the settlement guidelines should be disregarded.

favored in evaluating a proposed settlement, there is no minimal threshold that must be met.  The 
signatories to the Joint Proposal represent a spectrum of ratepayers, government agencies and 
other parties that demonstrate support across interest groups.  Accordingly, AARP’s subjective 
argument concerning the shallow support for the Joint Proposal should be rejected.

efficiency gains when rates are reset because efficiencies lead to lower costs, which will be 
reflected in future rates.
