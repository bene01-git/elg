Preliminary Notice of Non-Utility Controlled Outage Events – September 2025 
Director Frymire: 
Pursuant to the terms of Appendix K of the Joint Proposal (“JP”) adopted by the State of New 
York Public Service Commission (the “Commission”) in its October 12, 2023 Order Adopting 
Joint Proposal, in the above-captioned proceedings, New York State Electric & Gas Corporation 
(“NYSEG”) and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation (“RG&E” and jointly, the 
“Companies”)  are required to provide preliminary notice and supporting documentation for 
annual report exclusions, other than major storms, to the Director of the Office of Resilience and 
Emergency Preparedness for review within 45 days of the event.  In keeping with these 
requirements, the Companies hereby provide notice and attached supporting documentation for 
SAIFI and CAIDI non-utility controlled outage exclusions occurring during the month of 
September 2025.  
If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.
