What is the purpose of the Panel’s corrections and updates testimony?  
12 
A. 
The purpose of our testimony is to provide updates and corrections to certain 
13 
exhibits based on: 
14 
• An updated gas sales forecast provided by the Gas Load Forecasting Panel; 
15 
• An updated forecast cost of gas based on updated NYMEX pricing as of May

their initial filing; and  
20 
• Updated non-migration and off-system sales (“OSS”) revenues to include all 
21 
data for FY 2023 (through March 2023).

Q. 
Please describe the impact of the updated gas sales forecast to the exhibits. 
17 
A. 
Overall, the impact of the updated gas sales forecast was minimal as customer  
18 
requirements decreased less than one percent. 
19 
 
20 
Q. 
Please describe the impact of the updated NYMEX pricing to the exhibits. 
21 
A. 
The updated NYMEX pricing as of May 26, 2023 reflects a decrease in forecast 
22 
commodity costs.  Specifically, the projected storage dollars and forecast variable 
23

gas expense both decreased by approximately ten percent.  Additionally, marginal 
1

3 
Q. 
Please describe the updates and corrections made to Exhibit__ (GSP-7). 
4 
A. 
In addition to being updated to reflect the May 26, 2023 NYMEX pricing, this 
5 
exhibit was also updated to reflect the correct amount of Total Fixed Costs.   In the 
6 
Company’s April 2023 filing, only KEDLI fixed costs were included for each of 
7 
the twelve month periods in Exhibit__ (GSP-7), Forecast of Purchased Gas 
8 
Expense. The exhibit was corrected to include both KEDLI and KEDNY fixed costs 
9 
in each of the twelve-month periods.  The omission only impacted this exhibit.    
10 
 
11 
Q. 
Please describe the impact of the updates to non-migration and OSS revenues 
12 
to the exhibits. 
13 
A. 
The total non-migration capacity release and OSS revenues increased by 
14 
approximately two percent and ten percent, respectively.
