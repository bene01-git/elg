Case 20-G-0101 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to 
the Rates, Charges, Rules and Regulations of 
Corning Natural Gas Corporation for Gas Service.    
 
 
ORDER ON EXTENSION OF MAXIMUM SUSPENSION PERIOD 
OF A MAJOR RATE FILING 
 
(Issued and Effective January 22, 2021) 
 
 
BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
On February 27, 2020, Corning Natural Gas Corporation  
(Corning or the Company) filed amendments to its tariff 
schedule, P.S.C. No. 7 – Gas, requesting to increase the 
annual gas delivery revenues for the rate year ending 
January 31, 2022. 
 
 
Corning is requesting an increase in annual gas 
delivery revenues of approximately $6.3 million (32.1% increase 
in delivery revenues or 23.4% increase in total revenues), for 
the rate year ending January 31, 2022.  The requested increase 
in gas delivery revenues will result in a total monthly bill 
increase of about $28.76 (28.6% on the total bill) for a typical 
residential heating customer using 82 therms per month.

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
On November 20, 2020, the Company filed its consent to 
extend the maximum suspension period of this proceeding through 
and including May 31, 2021.  The Company requested the extension 
to provide additional time to facilitate the proceeding’s 
settlement process.  The request is subject to a make whole 
provision that would keep Corning and its customers in the same 
economic position as they would have been absent the extension. 
To facilitate the orderly conduct of this proceeding, 
the effective date of the tariff leaves filed by the Company is 
further extended through and including May 31, 2021, unless 
otherwise ordered by the Commission. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. 
The operation of the matter suspended by Notices  
issued March 6, 2020, and June 16, 2020, in this proceeding, is 
further suspended and the use of the rates, charges, rules and 
regulations stated in said Notices are further extended through 
May 31, 2021, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.  
2. 
Corning Natural Gas Corporation is directed to file 
with the Commission, on not less than two days’ notice on or 
before January 27, 2021, consecutively numbered supplements
