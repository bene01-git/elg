Good afternoon.  We're here today for 
a public statement hearing that was noticed on 
February 28th, 2022.  Consolidated Edison Company of 
New York Inc., which I -- I will refer to as Con 
Edison from now on, filed amendments to its Electric 
and Gas tariff schedules on January 28th, 2022 
proposing to increase its annual electric and gas 
delivery revenues effective January 1st, 2023. 
Con Edison proposes to increase its 
electric delivery revenues by approximately $1.2 
billion, a 17.6 percent increase in base delivery 
revenues or an 11.2 percent increase in total 
revenues.  And it proposed to increase its natural 
gas delivery revenues by approximately five hundred 
million dollars, which would be a 28.1 percent 
increase in base delivery revenues or an 18.2 percent 
increase in total revenues.
