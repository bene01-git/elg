Q. 
Is the catch-up adjustment optional? 
1 
A. 
No.  Ruling 202141001 makes clear the IRS’ position that gross salvage as a protected 
2 
item must be included in depreciation assumed for EADIT amortization purposes.  As 
3 
this ruling was released in October 2021, these rate cases represent the first opportunity 
4 
for the Companies to modify their calculations and avail themselves of the normalization 
5 
protections provided by Rev. Proc. 2020-39.  Section 4.01(6) of the Revenue Procedure 
6 
includes a transition rule that provides the following:  
7 
“Utilities may correct any method of reversing ETR {excess tax reserve} that is 
8 
not in accord with this revenue procedure at the next available opportunity.  The 
9 
methods adopted prior to the publication of this revenue procedure that are not in 
10 
accord with this revenue procedure are not considered to be a violation of the 
11 
normalization rules if so corrected.  This corrective action will require the utility 
12 
to consult with its regulator and obtain its regulator’s consent.  Utilities are not in 
13 
conflict with section 13001(d) of the TCJA if the utilities follow such a path to 
14 
correct potential normalization violations prospectively.  These rules extend to 
15 
companies that may not have started the amortization of ETRs or may be re-
16 
deferring the amortization as they evaluate their records.”

16 
Q. 
What is Municipal Gross Receipts Tax? 
17 
A. 
Cities and villages in New York State are authorized, via General City Law § 20-b and 
18 
Village Law § 5-530, to impose local taxes on utility services.  The tax is imposed on the 
19 
“gross income” of utilities doing business in New York State.  Utilities are authorized to 
20 
pass this tax, via Tax Surcharge Factors, along to customers within the jurisdictions 
21 
imposing the tax.  These local and municipal laws are based on Tax Law § 186-a, which 
22 
provides for the State Gross Receipts Tax imposed by the State of New York.
