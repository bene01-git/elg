Dear Secretary Burgess: 
 
The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (“KEDNY”) and KeySpan 
Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“KEDLI”) (collectively, the “Companies”) submit for 
filing corrections and updates to their April 30, 2019 gas base rate filings in the above matters.  
The attached filings include the corrections and updates testimony and revised exhibits of the 
witnesses identified in Attachment A. 
 
The net impact of the corrections and updates is a decrease of $41 million to KEDNY’s 
revenue requirement and an increase of $11.8 million to KEDLI’s revenue requirement in the Rate 
Year. The primary drivers of the changes to the revenue requirements are updates to the 
Companies’ revenue forecast (the primary driver), capital investment plans, and O&M expenses.  
As a result of the corrections and updates, a typical residential heating customer (using 83 Therms 
per month) will see the following increases as compared to the Companies’ April 30th filing:
