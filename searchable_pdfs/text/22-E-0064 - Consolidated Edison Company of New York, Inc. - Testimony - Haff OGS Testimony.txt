Q. 
WHAT WAS THE ORIGIN OF THE PASNY RATE? 
13 
A. 
Con Edison’s delivery service to NYPA originated with Con Edison’s sale to 
14 
NYPA in the 1970s of two partially constructed electric generating facilities.  As 
15 
part of that transaction, Con Edison agreed to provide delivery service to NYPA 
16 
for power that NYPA sold to certain customers in Con Edison’s service territory.  
17 
The rates charged to governmental customers, specifically, were first established 
18 
in 1976 by the Commission by deriving individual rates from existing Con Edison 
19 
service classes.  Over time, Con Edison further refined the PASNY rates by, for 
20 
example, adding time-of-day classes for large customers.  Importantly, the 
21 
Commission held that “by establishing rates for the new service based on [existing 
22 
equivalent full service class] costs . . . , we can keep similar customers on an equal
