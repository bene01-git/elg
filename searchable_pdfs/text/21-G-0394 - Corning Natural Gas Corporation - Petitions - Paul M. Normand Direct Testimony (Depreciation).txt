Q. 
Is a proper Net Salvage estimate an important aspect to establishing reasonable and 
1 
equitable depreciation accrual rates? 
2 
A. 
Yes it is.  Net salvage is an important cost that must be recovered in an equitable manner 
3 
over the useful life of an asset from those customers who benefit from the use and service 
4 
of an asset.  To defer the proper recovery of these costs over a realistic useful life until 
5 
much later at retirement, or underestimating these levels, will introduce a subsidy to 
6 
existing customers by the recovery of these costs from only future customers who may in 
7 
fact only use an asset for a very small portion of its life.
