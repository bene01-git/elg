Dear Customer Service Commission: 
The included petitions are signed by NYSEG residential customers from Pawling and Holmes (a 
hamlet of Pawling), NY 12564 and 12531 who protest the extreme rate and bill increases for 
power energy and delivery for 2022. While we understand costs increase, the manner and size 
are grossly unreasonable and unsustainable, OFTEN AS HIGH, OR HIGHER THAN OUR 
MORTGAGES! Something is horrifically wrong when customers' typical bills are under $200-
$300/month increase to $700-$1,000/month. Individual NYSEG customers have been told the 
rates went up 50%. But our rates have increased by 100-300%! This is untenable. 
So far NYSEG's explanations are unsatisfactory. We understand the differences between "on 
and off-peak hours." This seems to have little bearing on the total cost of overall increases 
since other than laundry and lower temperature while sleeping, it is unreasonable to expect 
energy use for daily living to be done during off peak hours - Unless off peak hours are 
expanded to something like Bp.m. to 7 a.m. Also, none of the petition signers- indeed most 
NYSEG customers - are NOT eligible for HEAP since they do not live at or near the poverty 
level. 
We insist on reopening an investigation into NYSEGS's application for rate increases; knowing 
how the new rates were determined and justified; a WELL ADVERTIZED public hearing; actual 
(not estimated) monthly meter readings and a reduction of current rates, to more reasonable, 
consumer tolerable levels, while still allowing NYSEG to make a reasonable profit. 
We appreciate your attention to these crucial matters and look forward to your timely response. 
Sincerefy,

Sandro Ore, Board President 
Cedar Valley Townhomes Association 
Cc: Tom Bundarin
