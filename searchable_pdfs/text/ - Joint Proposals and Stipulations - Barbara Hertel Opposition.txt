Re: Opposition to  
Case 19-G-0309 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, Charges,

19-G-0310 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, Charges, Rules 
and Regulations of KeySpan Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid for Gas Service  
 
Dear Secretary Phillips:  
 
Please find my statement of opposition to The Company’s Joint Proposal for filing in the above-
mentioned cases. 
 
I am an unemployed person who receives public assistance. I manage a building and make art. 
We do not need these pipelines in the first place. To raise our rates even a bit is unacceptable. I 
oppose the Joint Proposal and ask the Public Service Commission to take responsibility for our 
future, and figure out ways to go forward that don’t harm people who are already facing so many 
challenges. Making us pay for the pipeline and the LNG, when we don’t even need it, is 
protecting National Grid, not the people of New York City. And further, we are facing a climate 
disaster, and the Public Service Commission is deciding to listen to National Grid, a for profit 
corporation, instead of listening to the science. Floods and fires and the extinction of so many 
species, and diseases like COVID are the huge evidence of what is happening on the planet. 
No one, anywhere, should be increasing fossil fuel. 
 
Please do not allow this project to go forward and raise the people’s natural gas costs. 
 
 
Respectfully submitted,  
Barbara Hertel 
 
 
 
cc: Active Parties in Cases 19-G-0309 and 19-G-0310
