II. OVERVIEW OF TESTIMONY  
10 
Q. 
Please provide an overview of the Panel’s testimony. 
11 
A. 
The Companies are committed to offering Energy Efficiency and Demand Response 
12 
programs that prioritize carbon reduction, provide clean heating alternatives, support 
13 
low-to-moderate income (“LMI”) customers and communities, and help customers 
14 
manage their energy usage.  For over a decade, NYSEG and RG&E have offered 
15 
cost-effective Energy Efficiency programs to our residential, commercial, and municipal 
16 
customers.   
17 
For us, reducing energy usage in the State is an integral component of reducing 
18 
greenhouse gas emissions and satisfying the ambitious goals of the Climate Leadership 
19 
and Community Protection Act (“CLCPA”).  We remain committed to working toward 
20 
the 2025 Energy Efficiency targets laid out by the Commission in its Order Authorizing 
21 
Utility Energy Efficiency and Building Electrification Portfolios Through 2025, issued 
22 
January 16, 2020 in Case 18-M-0084 (“NENY Order”). 
23

4 
During our current rate plan, our electric Energy Efficiency and Demand 
1 
Response programs reduced the equivalent of 141,000 metric tons of CO2 from the air.  
2 
This reduction equates to the removal of 29,000 gasoline-powered passenger 
3 
vehicles driven for one year, or 26,000 homes’ electric use for one year, or over 311,000 
4 
barrels of oil not consumed, or carbon sequestered from over 2 million tree seedlings 
5 
grown for 10 years.   
6 
Similarly, our gas Energy Efficiency and Demand Response programs reduced 
7 
the equivalent of 18,500 metric tons of CO2 from the air.  This reduction equates to the 
8 
removal of 4,000 gasoline-powered passenger vehicles driven for one year, or 3,600 
9 
homes’ electric use for one year, or 42,000 barrels of oil not consumed, or carbon 
10 
sequestered from over 300,000 tree seedlings for one year. 
11 
And we are just getting started.  In this testimony, we lay out our plans for the 
12 
future, including the Companies’ proposal to launch four new Energy Efficiency 
13 
programs in the Rate Year.

17 
energy conscious customers in the future.

3 
Q. 
Please describe the Companies’ energy efficiency group. 
4 
A. 
The energy efficiency group supports various initiatives related to energy efficiency, such 
5 
as: 1) overseeing delivery of the Companies’ Energy Efficiency and Demand Response 
6 
programs; 2) developing and monitoring program budgets; 3) overseeing all aspects of 
7 
third-party services (e.g., procurement, vendor activities, program 
8 
performance); 4) participating in working groups with the other New York electric and 
9 
gas utilities, NYSERDA and New York State Department of Public Service Staff; and 5) 
10 
tracking and reporting regulatory requirements.   
11
