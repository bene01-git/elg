RE: Case 21-G-0394 - Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, Charges, 
Rules and Regulations of Corning Natural Gas Corporation for Gas Service
Dear Secretary Phillips: 
On September 24, 2021, pursuant to the Ruling Establishing Case Schedule issued in this 
proceeding on September 7, 2021, we filed on behalf of Corning Natural Gas Corporation 
(“Corning” or the “Company”) certain updates and corrections to the Company’s initial filing.  
Specifically, the update/correction filing consisted of the Additional Direct Testimony of Charles 
Lenns, Additional Direct Testimony of Matt J. Cook, and Exhibit CNG-15.  Subsequent to that 
filing, the Company became aware that certain data included in the model used to determine the 
Corning revenue requirement were based on a scenario for the replacement of leak-prone pipe 
(“LPP”) that the Company had presented as an alternative to its proposal, not as the proposal 
itself.  The incorrect information has an impact on the costs associated with the LPP replacement 
program and carries through to the revenue requirement in this proceeding.  We are making this 
filing to correct that information. 
In his prepared direct testimony in this proceeding, Corning witness Cook had proposed that the 
Company set its LPP replacement program target at 9.0 miles per year for 2022, 2023 and 2024 
(MJC-10).  He also stated that, as a means of employing additional rate mitigation, the LPP 
program targets could be reduced to 7.5 miles per year (ibid.).  The 9.0 mile per year target, 
however, is the Company’s primary proposal in this proceeding.  The Corning rate model, 
however, used the alternative 7.5 mile per year target, rather than the 9.0 mile per year target, as 
the basis for its calculations, which yielded a revenue requirement that is understated compared 
with what it would have been if the correct target had been used.  Upon discovery of the error, 
the model, which is accessible on the SharePoint site for this proceeding, was corrected.
