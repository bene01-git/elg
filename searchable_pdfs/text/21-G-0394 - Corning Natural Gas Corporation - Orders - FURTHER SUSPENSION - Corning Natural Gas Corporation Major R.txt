RECOMMENDATION:  Further Suspension of the Effective Date of the 
 
 
 
  Major Rate Changes. 
 
 
 
On July 16, 2021, Corning Natural Gas Corporation 
(Corning or the Company) filed amendments to its tariff schedule, 
P.S.C. No. 7 – Gas, requesting to increase the annual gas 
delivery revenues for the rate year ending June 30, 2023.  
 
     Corning states the Company is requesting an increase 
in annual gas delivery revenues of approximately $5.8 million 
(31.7% increase in delivery revenues or 20.4% increase in total 
revenues) for the rate year ending June 30, 2023.  In addition, 
Corning states the requested increase in gas delivery revenues 
will result in a total monthly bill increase of about $36.72 
(41.0% on a total bill) for the average residential heating 
customer using 77 hundred cubic feet per month.

By Notice issued July 23, 2021, the proposed 
amendments were suspended through December 12, 2021.  The 
investigation of this proceeding is continuing. 
 
 
     Pending the proceeding and decision on the proposed 
changes, the operation of these amendments is further suspended, 
and the use of the rates, charges, rules, and regulations are 
deferred through June 12, 2022, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission.  The rates, charges, rules, and regulations altered 
by the suspended amendments shall continue in effect until this 
proceeding is concluded or until the period of further 
suspension and any extended suspension expires, unless otherwise 
ordered by the Commission. 
 
 
     Corning Natural Gas Corporation is directed to file 
with the Commission not later than December 3, 2021, a 
consecutively numbered supplement announcing further suspension 
of the amendments.
