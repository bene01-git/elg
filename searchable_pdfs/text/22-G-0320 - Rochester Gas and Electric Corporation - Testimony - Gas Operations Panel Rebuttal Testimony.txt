Q. 
What is the overall purpose of the Panel’s Rebuttal Testimony? 
1 
A. 
We respond to Staff’s recommendations and adjustments as described in the testimony of 
2 
the Staff Gas Safety Panel (“SGSP”) and Staff Gas Infrastructure and Operations Panel 
3 
(“SGIOP”).  We disagree with the SGSP’s adjustments to our Incremental Full-Time 
4 
Equivalent (“FTE”) positions because each is fully justified, necessary and warranted for 
5 
the continued provision of safe, adequate, and reliable natural gas service, to meet 
6 
incremental workload demands, and to provide for adequate supervision of the field 
7 
workforce.  Indeed, as we will describe further, not adding these FTEs would put 
8 
customers at risk.  We also disagree with the SGIOP’s reductions to the Companies’ 
9 
Incremental Maintenance Program and Reconcilable Gas Program proposals because 
10 
these programs and proposals are critical to providing safe, adequate and reliable service 
11 
to customers.  In addition, we disagree with the SGSP’s proposed adjustments to our leak 
12 
management targets related to Negative Revenue Adjustments (“NRAs”) because the 
13 
SGSP’s proposal does not take into account the myriad of factors that cause leaks (many 
14 
of which are outside the Companies’ control).  We request Positive Revenue Adjustments 
15 
(“PRAs”) for the Companies’ Damage Prevention metric (which exist at other New York 
16 
utilities) because it will incentivize improved performance and increase public safety.  
17 
We likewise disagree with Staff’s removal of the Leak Prone Pipe (“LPP”) PRAs because 
18 
utilities should be incentivized to remove LPP more quickly for environmental reasons.  
19 
Finally, we suggest a slight modification to the SGSP’s proposed addition to the 
20 
exclusion request language related to emergency response metric. 
21

Historic Test Year expenditure levels without inflationary adjustments is also arbitrary 
14 
because it denies the Companies the equivalent costs in the Rate Year.  Moreover, the

SGIOP has not explained why it was appropriate to eliminate general escalation from 
16 
some programs but not others.  Our inability to hire the personnel we need to run our 
17 
businesses and to make the investments needed for gas safety and operational programs 
18 
will significantly hinder our ability to meet customer expectations and continue to 
19 
provide safe, adequate and reliable natural gas service to our customers.  Our testimony

addresses these concerns in more detail and provides further justification for why our

proposals should be adopted.
