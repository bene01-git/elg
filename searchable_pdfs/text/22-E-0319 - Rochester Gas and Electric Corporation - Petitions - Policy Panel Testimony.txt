programs and various approaches to further mitigate rates.   
6

10 
significant upward pressure on the revenue requirements and proposed delivery rates in

these cases for costs that cannot be avoided.

noted at page eight that “total impacts overall, including considering all underlying

changes to surcharges and sur credits, should be kept at or below 2% per year.”

19 
Examples of such moderators included the full utilization/amortization of many

regulatory liability balances, including those associated with the 2017 Tax Cut Act, some

of which have now been extinguished, the Companies making capital investments

resulting in rate base additions at levels needed to ensure reliability for customers but
