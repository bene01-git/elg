Q.  
Please explain why you disagree with Staff’s opposition to acting on clean energy 
10 
proposals outside of generic proceedings? 
11 
A. 
Opposition to acting on clean energy proposals outside of generic proceedings combined 
12 
with the infrequent review periods for those generic proceedings will likely serve to temper 
13 
progress in New York’s clean energy transition.  The framework for many of the state’s 
14 
clean energy programs involves setting a six-year target with a single mid-term review.  
15 
Such infrequent reviews and updates cause utilities to pursue overly conservative targets 
16 
that are based on stale data, rather than optimizing efficient, cost-effective progress on 
17 
attaining the state’s climate and clean energy objectives.  Rate cases provide an opportunity 
18 
for utility-specific review and adjustments that can compensate for the infrequent reviews 
19 
and updates that occur in the state’s generic proceedings.   
20
