This post-bid estimate was prepared by the Gas Project Estimating Department to show expected 
construction costs for the above project. The estimate is based on issued for construction drawings dated 
5/18/18 and discussions with the project team. Members of Gas Project Estimating, Complex 
Construction, Project Engineering & Design and Project Management conducted a review of the 
project risk register on 7/6/18, and a risk contingency allowance was calculated using a Monte Carlo 
simulation. 
 
Assumptions 
 
Gas main installation, including trenching, welding (dead pipe), testing, in-line inspection and 
restoration will be performed by contractor/s selected via National Grid's competitive bid 
process. Tie-in work will be performed by National Grid construction crews. 
 
Shutdown/tie-in support labor estimated based on similar projects as SOP not yet complete. 
 
No well-point dewatering or treatment systems will be required to manage groundwater during 
excavation. 
 
Cathodic Protection- Pipe wrapping and jeeping will be performed by contractor and test 
stations installation and periodic inspection will be performed by NGrid corrosion dept. 
 
Includes Full Time Certified Welding Inspectors.  
 
Assumes 100% Radiographic Examination of welds. 
 
Environmental monitoring during excavation is not required. 
 
 
Work restrictions between the hours of 5:00 A.M. to 8:00 P.M. (and 8:00 P.M. to 5:00 A.M. for 
night work days) are included in this estimate.   
 
Major downtime due to weather, construction embargoes, moratoriums, construction related 
false starts and construction rework or unforeseen circumstances are not included.  The 
assumption is made that construction schedule will be adjusted accordingly and no idle 
crew/equipment charges need to be paid to contractor due to local community restrictions. 
 
Major downtime due to weather or unforeseen circumstances is not included. 
 
AFUDC was estimated based on the current project schedule and assumes the asset will be 
placed in service immediately upon project completion. 
 
Material quantities and pricing are based on BOM/Invoices. 
 
This estimate DOES incorporate labor, material and equipment costs associated with attending 
community outreach meetings, producing/implementing community outreach programs and for 
attending meetings with local State/County/City/Town/Village officials for project approvals.

3.1
Background
The pipelines within the KEDLI system were built over various decades beginning in the
early 1950’s and continuing to the current year. This project addresses the lack of
documentation required to validate the maximum allowable operating pressure (MAOP),
lack of documentation of materials used to construct these pipelines and the lack of
non-destructive examination (NDE) records for the welded joints for Gas Main 1, 8 and
16 (GM-i) in northwest Nassau. Replacing this pipeline with pipe manufactured under
modern processes and controls, constructed under modern construction standards,
NDE of welded joints and a pressure test proving the integrity of the pipeline will
mitigate the risk of operating the pipeline system in northwest Nassau.
Additionally, this project will bring increased supply to the NY Facilities System to help
address capacity and system pressure in northwest Nassau.
3.2
Drivers
There are three major drivers to this project: Supply; Integrity; and Reliability. The new
interconnect is necessary to maintain system pressures in northwest Nassau during
peak demand periods. Additionally, the Pipeline Safety Improvement Act of 2002; the
Pipeline Safety, Regulatory Certainty, and Job Creation Act of 2011; and the Notice of
Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) for Safety of Gas Transmission and Gathering Pipelines
- require down-rating or replacement of pipelines where operators do not have records
that are traceable, verifiable, and complete to establish MACP..
This project is also necessary to maintain minimum system pressures in northwest
Nassau during periods of peak demand.
