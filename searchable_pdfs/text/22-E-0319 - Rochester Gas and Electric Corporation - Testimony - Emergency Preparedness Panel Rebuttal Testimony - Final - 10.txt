 Automated Rapid Infrastructure Evaluation System (“ARIES”) 
11 
Q. 
Did Staff make a recommendation related to funding for the ARIES project? 
12 
 
Yes.  Staff rejected the proposed funding for two reasons: 1) the ARIES trailers have 
13 
been deployed infrequently over the past two years; and 2) the Companies did not explain 
14 
the required data capture and necessary data processing improvements. 
15 
Q. 
Do the Companies agree with Staff’s recommendation to reject the funding? 
16 
 
No.  The improvements that will be made through this proposed funding will allow the 
17 
Companies to deploy the ARIES trailer on a more frequent basis, which should alleviate 
18 
Staff’s concerns. 
19 
Q. 
Please explain. 
20 
 
The majority of storms causing power outages in New York last less than three days.  
21 
Currently, the processing time for ARIES data is greater than three days, which makes 
22 
ARIES unusable for most restoration events at this time.  The proposed funding will 
23

update ARIES to reduce the processing time so it can be used for assessing shorter 
1 
duration outage events.  This will provide value to customers as most events tend to be 
2 
less than three days and more rapid, thorough damage assessment collection will foster 
3 
more effective restoration.   
4 
Q. 
What data capture and proceeding improvements will be made to ARIES? 
5 
 
The improvements to ARIES will shorten the processing and converting time of LiDAR 
6 
damage data.  The collection of LiDAR information and picture imaging consumes data 
7 
in the order of terabytes (“TBs”).  Converting TBs of data is time consuming with an 
8 
approximate 1:1 ratio of flying time to processing time.  The proposed updates will 
9 
reduce the time necessary to process and convert damage assessment data allowing 
10 
ARIES to be used more often (e.g., in minor outage events).  In addition, ARIES has 
11 
benefits over other LiDAR systems as it uses change detection capabilities to provide 
12 
damage assessment details.  Change detection using LiDAR alone requires a before and 
13 
after flight, is more costly, and often must be repeated.  ARIES also adds a level of safety 
14 
by reducing the need for damage assessors to enter hazardous and hard to reach areas 
15 
(e.g., blocked and/or washed-out roadways).  The proposed updates will allow ARIES 
16 
broken pole and low/down conductor damage to be integrated into Spectrum OMS, using 
17 
automation.

18

19
