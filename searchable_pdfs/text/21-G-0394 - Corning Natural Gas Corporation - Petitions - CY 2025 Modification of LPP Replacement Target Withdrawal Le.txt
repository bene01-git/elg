RE: CASE 21-G-0394 - Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, Charges, 
Rules, and Regulations of Corning Natural Gas Corporation for Gas Service. ORDER 
ADOPTING TERMS OF JOINT PROPOSAL. 
Dear Secretary Phillips: 
On February 27, 2024, Corning Natural Gas Corporation ("Corning" or the "Company") filed a request to 
change the Leak-Prone Pipe ("LPP") replacement target of 7.0 miles currently set for Calendar Year ("CV") 
2025 be reduced to 5.0 miles and that the Joint Proposal ("JP") approved by the Commission in Case 21-G-
0394 be modified accordingly. 
In conducting a final inventory of bare steel pipe replacement in conjunction with a data request from Staff 
in Case 24-G-0145 (Local Distribution Company Performance Related to Pipeline Safety Measures), Corning 
discovered that 8.0 miles of pipe was misclassified and must be replaced. The Company is targeting the 
replacement of the final 27.0 miles of bare steel pipe (including the reclassified eight miles of pipe) over a 
five-year period. The Company's plan is to replace 7.0 miles in 2025 and 5.0 miles each year during the 
period 2026 to 2029. 
Based on Corning's revised LPP replacement plan, the Company hereby withdraws its February 27, 2024, 
request to reduce the JP's LPP replacement target of 7.0 miles currently set for CV 2025 to 5.0 miles. 
Please contact me if you have any questions regarding this matter.
