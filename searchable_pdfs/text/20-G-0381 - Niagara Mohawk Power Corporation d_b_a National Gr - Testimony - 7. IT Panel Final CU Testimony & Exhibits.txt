A. 
COVID-19/Plan Forward 
1 
Q. 
Please describe the updates associated with the Company’s response to the 
2 
COVID-19 pandemic.  
3 
 A. 
National Grid, like most companies, has had to undertake several IT investments to 
4 
support its workforce and customers during the COVID-19 pandemic.  These 
5 
investments implement technologies that promote new ways of working for what 
6 
has largely become a remote workforce.  In addition, National Grid identified 
7 
several high priority infrastructure investments that were integral to its COVID-19 
8 
pandemic response.  Collectively, this effort is referred to as “Plan Forward.”  
9 
National Grid authorized a $37.5 million increase to its IT-related capital 
10 
expenditures in June 2020 for the Plan Forward efforts.  The investments were in 
11 
the very preliminary planning stages at the time of the original filing but have 
12 
progressed since then.  These investments are included in the Technology 
13 
Modernization and Business Technology programs.  The “Business Technology” 
14 
program is a new program specifically targeted to supporting National Grid’s 
15 
workforce under remote operating conditions and includes a combination of new 
16 
investments and investments previously categorized as part of Technology 
17 
Modernization.  The new investments in the two programs include:

B. 
Customer Experience Program - CXP 
6 
Q. 
Please describe the update to the Customer Experience Program.   
7 
A. 
The Customer Experience Program, which is described in the Company’s initial 
8 
testimony and consists of INVP 4750 J, K, M, N, on Exhibit _ (ITP-4) and Exhibit 
9 
__ (ITP-5), was in the midst of a re-planning exercise at the time of the initial filing.  
10 
The project team had completed the delivery of new functionality to support 
11 
customers’ web experience and was re-assessing the technology platform on which 
12 
the new functionality operates to ensure it would meet the Company’s long-term 
13 
objectives. The outcome of the planning process was a decision to migrate to one 
14 
web platform for CSS and CRIS; specifically, to the SalesForce.com platform, 
15 
which was recently designated as the Company’s standard Customer Relationship 
16 
Management (“CRM”) platform. The new platform will provide significant 
17 
benefits in supportability and speed to market for new functionality for 
18 
customers.  The change to a Salesforce.com platform also provides a foundation for 
19 
future customer engagement enhancements in a more agile, and cost-effective 
20 
manner by leveraging Salesforce.com’s capabilities to deliver an improved 
21

1 
representatives.  The decision to migrate to the Salesforce.com platform increases

the cost of the project by approximately $14 million dollars in Fiscal Year 2021.

INVP 6013
Azure Cloud Foundation
This project will establish Microsoft Azure as the Cloud Platform into 
which National Grid applications, data, and infrastructure will be 
deployed.   The initiative will create a Cloud 2.0 version to enable agile 
methodologies and increase application release frequency while 
increasing the stability, scalability, and reliability of the Cloud Platform.  
The project will provide:
1. scalable cloud platform supporting business needs;
2. faster provisioning of infrastructure services;
3. repeatable designs that can be used across the enterprise; and 
4. increased reliability and resiliency resulting in less downtime
INVP 6018
Security Operations Services (SOS)
The SOS project will implement threat protection and security 
management technologies /capabilities for National Grid's Cloud 
environments.  The following products will be installed:  Azure Security 
Center, Azure Sentinel, Azure Security Alerts Integration; and Azure 
Advanced Threat Protection. 
The following benefits will be achieved:
1. Enables National Grid to gain visibility and control over security resources, 
workflows, and policies; 
2. Increased security posture in National Grid datacenters; and
3. Ability to integrate Azure security alerts into one Security Information Event 
Management solution.
INVP 6043
Business Technology Plan Forward Program - 
COVID Phase 1
In March of 2020, in response to the COVID-19 Global Pandemic, 
National Grid made tactical decisions allowing its employees to work 
remotely while still delivering excellent service to its customers. As a 
result of this action, a number of capabilities have been identified that 
challenge employee efficiency or create risk to ongoing operations. Due 
to lessons learned during the initial phases of the pandemic, the 
company has embraced its “Plan Forward” initiative that will drive us 
toward true “Work from anywhere” capability, removing obstacles that 
would otherwise hinder optimal employee performance when working 
remotely. General areas for investment include: 
* Maturing and stabilizing application remote access capabilities in a 
secure environment;
* Provide application upgrades required to support remote access 
technologies in a Windows 10 environment;
* Deploy portable/ mobile technologies for office, call center, and field 
workers as needed to support efficient operations and foster company 
communication; 
* Expand licensing of technologies supporting remote access;
* Deliver medical screening solutions for employee safety in alignment 
with regulatory direction.
The program will deliver the following benefits:
* Improve safety of National Grid Employees and communities they serve; 
* Increase effectiveness of employees working remotely; and 
* Provide alignment to local, state, and federal directives for social distancing.

INVP 6026
Mainframe Capacity Upgrade
This project will upgrade the MIPS (million instructions per second), 
memory, and capacity on two Production Mainframes.  The current 
capacity is unable to support future demands and the current processes 
are taking extended periods of time to complete.
The upgrade will provide sufficient capacity for the next two years and will 
improve the processing speed of several critical business processes.
INVP 5974
IBM P9 Refresh
This project will upgrade the existing IBM Power 7 servers that are 
located in the DXC Datacenter.  The servers host several key National 
Grid business applications and are reaching the end of their service life.  
Failure to upgrade will result in a reduced level of support and the risk 
of a service interruption.      
The project will provide the following benefits: 
1. Increased platform stability and reliability;
2. Removing the risk of an interruption to service 
3. Improved performance and efficiency 
4. Provides sufficient UNIX AIX capacity to meet the current five-year forecast; 
and 
5. Reduced security risk
INVP 5874
Cloud Center of Excellence
This project will build a Cloud Center of Excellence that brings Cloud and 
DevOps capabilities in-house.   Working with Microsoft, the project will 
implement the Azure DevOps process and tooling capabilities and 
design an IT Operational Platform that ensure alignment between the 
Microsoft Azure Cloud platform  and National Grid's operational 
procedures.  
The implementation of Cloud and DevOps capabilities will lead to: delivering 
applications in a faster time, increased options to support new services, 
improved reliability due to the close collaboration with Microsoft on the 
design of the operational platform; and reduced application development and 
implementation costs. 
INVP 5886B
Device Refresh
This project is provisioning laptops for new hires and provides break/fix 
replacements.  
This is a like for like replacement of end of life user devices, inline with a three 
year refresh policy which ensures the devices are able to run new applications 
and services and support the latest security rules.  
INVP 6049
Hosting Refresh to HCI
This project will replace the Virtual Computing Environment (VCE) that 
hosts several National Grid applications at the DXC Data Center.  The 
proposed solution is to procure new Hyper Converged Infrastructure 
(HCI) as a replacement for the VCE environment. 
The new HCI will modernize the National Grid infrastructure landscape by 
providing tighter security controls,  improved data protection, improved 
performance and represents an environment that is supported under our 
current maintenance and support contract.

INVP 6052
Migration of Current Storage To Strategic 
Deployment
National Grid has engaged Dell Technologies Services to enable full 
utilization of the recently installed storage infrastructure. They will 
configure the hardware and existing data to meet National Grid’s needs 
for supported and high performance storage across block and file 
storage in DXC data centers in Norwich CT and Newark DE
The project will deliver the following benefits:
1. Improved access to data on new storage infrastructure 
2. Mitigates security exposure from running on end of life infrastructure 
3. Increased resiliency 
4. Utilizes supported capacity and allows for addition of future capacity
INVP 6046
SD WAN Expansion
National Grid is partnering with Verizon to redesign the wide area 
network (WAN) infrastructure for a National Grid software-defined wide 
area network (SD-WAN) architecture located at 100 US National Grid 
Corporate sites.   T​his project will lead to better Wide Area Network 
(WAN) performance and enable better control over the network 
environment. Moving to SD WAN will also enable the use of multiple 
network transport options and will support traffic routing based on real 
time conditions of the network, leading to increased flexibility of access 
choice and the way in which applications are routed. ​
The benefits of SD-WAN include:
1. Centralized orchestration and policy control (leading to faster provisioning 
of network policy changes);
2. Better performance for cloud based applications:
3. Better visibility into applications traversing the network (SD WAN recognizes 
over 3000 applications by name through Deep Packet Inspection (DPI));
4. SD WAN continuously monitors the performance of each WAN transport 
(Multi-Protocol Label Switching (MPLS) or internet) and routes traffic over the 
appropriately performing transport, based on application policy (latency, jitter, 
packet loss). ​
INVP 6053
EoL Migration
The project will migrate a specified list of End of Life (EoL) servers onto 
supported platforms.   More specifically, the project will reduce 
technical debt by taking the appropriate action to decommission, 
replace, or upgrade 400 servers that have been dispositioned by the 
Asset Lifecyle Management initiative.  
The benefits include: 
1. Mitigation of security risk; 
2. Better performance of modern Operating Systems and Hardware; 
3. Reduces failure risk by remediating EoL assets 
4. Fully supported platforms and applications

CNI Infrastructure
This program will replace aged infrastructure within the Critical National 
Network (CNI) environment to ensure high availability and security.   
Security upgrades include: the refresh of firewalls (25) and routers (13),  
the encryption of communications at remote terminal sites, and a 
refresh of the intrusion detection system.  Telemetry upgrades include:  
circuit upgrades (600) and diverse wireless VPN modifications (800). 
Benefits include:
1. ​Reduction of assets that are end of service life within the National Grid CNI 
estate
2. Ensures regulatory compliance
3. Enhanced system security by replacing legacy devices with new kits 
containing enhanced security features; and 
4. Increase operational availability and vendor support
TBD
Operations Program
​This project will remediate critical end of life (EoL) software/operating 
systems/applications that are no longer vendor supported. These 
unsupported components can no longer be patched and are subject to 
known security vulnerabilities which cannot be remediated without the 
upgrade to the supported versions.  There is a significant risk that these 
unsupported services could not be recovered or that they experience 
extended  downtime during a failure.  
The upgrade to supported platforms will ensure vendor support is available 
should an incident occur.  Thereby, minimizing system downtime and the 
disruption to business operations.  The newer toolkits also provide the 
capability to:  implement automated monitoring and alerting, and  real-time 
asset inventory and visualization of infrastructure which will improve project 
delivery, decision making and cost transparency.
