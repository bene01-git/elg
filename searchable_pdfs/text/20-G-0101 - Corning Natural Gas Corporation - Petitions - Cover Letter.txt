Attachment A.2 The increases reflected for those two years are $845,142 and $680,913, 
respectively. Those three years of stand-alone increases would impact customer total bills by 
23.4%, 2.56% and 2.01%, respectively. In this filing, however, Coming proposes a rate plan 
with levelized increases over three years. The levelized increase would be $3,523,167 for each 
of the three Rate Years and would impact customer total bills by 10.93% in each year. The 
revenue requirement includes $684,359 of costs (local production deferral) that are being 
transferred to base rates. These costs are currently being paid by customers through the Delivery 
Rate Adjustment. 
In addition to the proposed three-year rate plan, the Company is open to developing a staged 
increase proposal for the 12 months ending January 31,2025 and January 31, 2026. Pursuant to 
the staged increase, Coming would recover the carrying costs (defined as pre-tax overall rate of 
return, depreciation expense and property taxes) on incremental plant additions over the previous 
year. As explained in the testimony filed herewith, as an alternative to the staged increase, the 
Commission could authorize a construction surcharge mechanism ("CSM") which would permit 
the Company to establish a surcharge factor to be applied to all delivery customers (except those 
SC 11 customers with contractually determined prices) that would cover the carrying costs 
(return, depreciation and property taxes) on projected infrastmcture investments. Coming would 
reconcile the carrying costs collected to the actual carrying costs based on actual investment 
made and any over- and under-collection would be included in the calculation of the following 
year's CSM. 
The multi-year approach that Coming proposes in this filing affords an excellent opportunity for 
all stakeholders, including the Commission, to realize substantial benefits, including rate 
certainty and stability, reduced need to devote resources to annual rate filings, and enhancement 
of the Company's ability to access financial markets on reasonable terms. In addition, staged 
increases or a CSM would provide contemporaneous review of the Company's capital 
expenditures, which is one of the main drivers of the increased revenues Coming requires. In 
short, a multi-year program would save costs that would otherwise be collected from customers 
and would not impair the Commission's oversight responsibilities. 
The rate increases Coming seeks are necessitated by a number of factors. The primary drivers 
are the need to recover costs associated with the recent decarbonization legislation and programs 
mandated by the Commission. For the Commission's convenience, Coming has prepared 
Attachment B to this letter summarizing the major drivers underlying the determination to make 
this rate filing. Full details of these drivers are contained in the exhibits being filed herewith.

In an effort to promote a prompt decision in this proceeding, Coming, as it did in 
Case 16-G-0369,3 has undertaken to provide substantially more information concurrently with 
this filing than is either required or typical in rate proceedings. Among other information, the 
Company is supplying rate case quality forecasts for the 12 months ending not only on 
January 31, 2022, but also for the 12 months ending January 31, 2023 and 2024. Coming is also 
providing to the Staff of the Department of Public Service ("Staff) the exhibits for this filing in 
an electronic form that includes all the interactive features of the model used by the Company. 
In addition, based on prior cases, Coming is providing an exhibit in this proceeding (Exhibit 
CNG-13), which summarizes the Company's electronic responses to "standard" Staff requests. 
Those electronic responses will be made available to Staff on a "Share Point" site that will be 
password protected and subject to certain confidentiality requirements. In addition, the Share 
Point site will include certain information from Coming's consultant, Management Applications 
Consulting, Inc. ("MAC"), supporting MAC's Cost of Service and Rate Design presentation. 
The Company will provide a separate data base with all vendor charges and payments for the 
historical test year {i.e., the 12 months ended September 30, 2019). Again, Staff will have 
electronic access to this information, which will be password protected and subject to certain 
confidentiality requirements. By providing this information at this stage, the Company believes 
that review of this filing will be enhanced. 
In accordance with 16 NYCRR § 4.5(d), Coming submits herewith the prepared written 
testimony and exhibits which will comprise its direct case in support of this gas rate filing. Also 
pursuant to Section 4.5(d) of the Commission's Regulations, an electronic copy of this filing is 
being served simultaneously on the Utility Intervention Unit of the Division of Consumer 
Protection of the New York Department of State. 
A notice to the public will be published in local newspapers regarding the revisions to the tariff 
in accordance with 16 NYCRR § 720-8.1 and a bill insert will be furnished to all customers as 
prescribed in 16 NYCRR § 720-9.1.
