RECOMMENDATION:  Further Suspension of the Effective Date of the 
 
 
 
  Major Rate Changes. 
 
 
 
On July 31, 2020, Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation 
d/b/a National Grid (Niagara Mohawk) filed amendments to its 
tariff schedules, P.S.C. Nos. 214 and 220 – Electricity and 
P.S.C. No. 219 - Gas, to increase the annual electric and gas 
delivery revenues for the rate year ending June 30, 2022. 
 
 
 
 
Niagara Mohawk requested an electric delivery revenue 
increase of approximately $100.4 million (4.9 percent increase 
in delivery revenues or 3.2 percent increase in total system

revenues).1  Niagara Mohawk’s requested increase in delivery 
revenues will result in a delivery bill increase of 
approximately $3.43 (6.0 percent increase on the delivery bill,

 For its gas business, Niagara Mohawk requested a 
delivery revenue increase of approximately $41.8 million (9.8 
percent increase in delivery revenues, or 5.2 percent increase 
in total system revenues).  Niagara Mohawk’s requested increase 
in delivery revenues will result in a monthly bill increase of 
about $4.53 (9.5 percent on the delivery bill, or a 6.0 percent 
increase on the total bill) for residential heating customer 
using an average of 82 therms per month. 
 
 
By Notice issued August 20, 2020, the proposed 
amendments were suspended through December 29, 2020.  The 
investigation of these proceedings is continuing.  On 
October 14, 2020, Niagara Mohawk updated its delivery revenue 
increase requests to $103.3 million and $37.1 million for its 
electric and gas business respectively. 
 
 
 
Pending the proceedings and decision on the proposed 
changes, the operation of these amendments is further suspended, 
and the use of the rates, charges, rules, and regulations are 
deferred through June 29, 2021, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission.  The rates, charges, rules, and regulations altered 
by the suspended amendments shall continue in effect until these 
proceedings are concluded or until the period of further 
suspension and any extended suspension expires, unless otherwise 
ordered by the Commission. 
 
Niagara Mohawk is directed to file with the 
Commission, no later than December 22, 2020, a consecutively 
numbered supplement announcing further suspension of the 
amendments. 
 
1  Total system revenue impacts for both electric and gas 
includes energy service company sales priced out at Niagara 
Mohawk’s commodity rates.
