THE REPORTER:  We're on the record. 
A.L.J. MORENO:  Thank you.  I call 
Cases 22-E-0064 and 22-G-0065.  Proceedings on the 
motion of the Commission as to the rates, charges, 
rules, and regulations of Consolidated -- 
Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. for 
electric and gas service. 
Good afternoon, everyone.  We are here 
today for a public statement hearing that was noticed 
on February 28th, 2022.  Consolidated Edison Company 
of New York Inc., which I will refer to as Con 
Edison, filed amendments to its electric and gas 
tariff schedules on January 28th, 2022, proposing to 
increase its annual electric and gas delivery 
revenues effective January 1st, 2023. 
Con Edison proposes to increase its 
electric delivery revenues by approximately $1.2 
billion, which is a 17.6% increase in base delivery 
revenue, or an 11.2% increase in total revenue.  And 
its natural gas delivery revenues is proposed to 
increase by approximately $500 million, which is 
about a 28.1% increase in base delivery revenue, or 
an 18.2% increase in total revenue.
