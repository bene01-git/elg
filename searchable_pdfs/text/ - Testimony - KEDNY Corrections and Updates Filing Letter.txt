Dear Secretary Burgess: 
 
The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (“KEDNY”) submits for 
filing corrections and updates to its gas base rate filing in Case 16-G-0059.  The attached filing 
includes the corrections and updates testimony and revised exhibits of the witnesses identified in 
Attachment A.   
 
Exhibit ___ (RRP-1CU) sets forth updates and a reconciliation of the differences in 
revenue requirements between KEDNY’s original filing and as adjusted for the corrections and 
updates presented herein.   
 
To facilitate the parties’ review of the corrections and updates, the Revenue 
Requirements Panel provides a summary page for each schedule to clearly identify applicable 
corrections and updates and links to supporting workpapers.   
 
 
Please contact me if you have questions or require further information.
