A. Security Guard Services 
2 
Q. 
Please describe how Con Edison uses security guard 
3 
services. 
4 
A. 
Security guard contractors provide Con Edison with a range 
5

6

7

8

9

10 
visitors enter each facility, and patrolling facilities 
11 
regularly to detect and prevent suspicious activity and 
12 
security breaches. These contractor guards are also trained 
13 
and prepared to respond and take appropriate action in the 
14 
event of security issues or incidents.

Q.  Is the Company proposing changes to its ECC Relocation 
1 
project? 
2 
A.  Yes. The project schedule reflected in the January 2025 
3 
filing was based on completing real estate acquisition 
4 
activities within RY1. Upon further evaluation, the Company 
5 
has increased the property acquisition timeframe to two 
6 
years, which will include both real estate acquisition and 
7 
engineering activities. As a result, the anticipated 
8 
construction start date will be deferred by a year (i.e., 
9 
to RY2).  
10 
Q. 
Why is the Company making these changes to the proposed ECC 
11 
Relocation project? 
12 
A. 
These changes will provide the Company with additional time 
13 
to acquire real estate for the relocated ECC. Given the 
14 
complexity of the property requirements, including parcel 
15 
size, setback distances, and employee accessibility, there 
16 
may be a limited inventory of properties available. As 
17 
such, the Company will need additional time for property 
18 
acquisition. 
19 
Q. 
Do these changes impact the Company’s projected capital 
20 
spending during the three rate years? 
21

Q. 
How will these changes affect the overall project budget? 
12 
A. 
In the long-term, these changes are expected to increase 
13 
the overall project budget by approximately $200 million. 
14 
Q. 
Please explain. 
15 
A. 
By deferring the construction start date by a year, the 
16 
Company is shifting more work into subsequent rate plans, 
17 
when costs are expected to be higher due to escalation. In 
18 
addition, some of the work in later years may need to be 
19 
completed on an accelerated basis, resulting in increased 
20 
costs.
