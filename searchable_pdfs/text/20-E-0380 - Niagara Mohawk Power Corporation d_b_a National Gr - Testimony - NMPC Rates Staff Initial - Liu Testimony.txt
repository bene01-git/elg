RECOMMENDATIONS ON FORECASTING WITH DERS 
1 
Q. 
What do you recommend to model DERs for the 
2 
electric deliveries forecast? 
3 
A. 
I recommend forecasting models developed from 
4 
actual deliveries data, with an independent 
5 
variable to recognize the growing impact of DERs 
6 
on energy use.  In other words, unlike the add-
7 
back approach, the left-hand side dependent 
8 
variable of a regression equation is not 
9 
adjusted for DER.  Rather, a DER variable is 
10 
included on the right-hand side along with other 
11 
independent variables.  This approach models the 
12 
actual energy use as it is and lets a DER 
13 
variable along with economic and weather 
14 
variables simultaneously determine their 
15 
respective effects on the historical variations 
16 
in electric energy usage. 
17

18 
for your forecasting models?

A. 
Yes, I have.  One of the alternatives is a time 
20 
trend variable.  In fact, the Company’s time 
21 
series for the estimated total DERs is highly 
22 
correlated with a time trend.  While the trend 
23 
variable is an objective variable representing 
24

Q. 
What is an ARIMAX model? 
6 
A. 
An ARIMAX model is a time series regression 
7 
model that contains two types of components, 
8 
structural and non-structural.  The structural, 
9 
or “X”, component relates the deliveries or 
10 
number of customers to a group of independent 
11 
variables, like a regression model.  The 
12 
independent variables may include, among other 
13 
things, economic variables, weather variables, 
14 
and seasonal binary variables. 
15 
Q. 
Please explain the non-structural component of 
16 
the ARIMAX model. 
17 
A. 
The non-structural, or “ARIMA”, component is 
18 
represented by a procedure that relates the 
19 
values and errors of a time series to its past 
20 
values and errors.  This component is included 
21 
to capture the remaining variations in the 
22 
dependent variable, such as trend, seasonality, 
23 
and serial correlations in residual errors.  It 
24 


--- Page 20 ---
Cases 20-E-0380 & 20-G-0381 
LIU 
 
 
19 
 
may also capture the influence of other 
1 
explanatory variables that may have not been 
2 
included in the model. 
3 
Q. 
How is the ARIMAX model estimated? 
4 
A. 
An ARIMAX model is estimated using historical 
5 
data.  The Company provided historical data 
6 
through August 2020 along with forecasted values 
7 
for the economic and weather variables that I 
8 
included in my model.  The ARIMAX model produces 
9 
a forecast for sales volume when predicted 
10 
values of the independent variables are input 
11 
into the model.
