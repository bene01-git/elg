Years 2 and 3.2  The increases reflected for the three Rate Years are $5,758,453, $1,643,355 and 
$989,061, respectively.  Those three years of stand-alone increases would impact customer total 
bills by 20.43%, 4.85% and 2.78%, respectively.  In this filing, however, Corning proposes a rate

More detail on customer impacts may be found in the accompanying Exhibit CNG-10, Schedule 
PMN-9, pages 1-30.  Schedule B to this letter also shows the delivery rate impacts of the 
proposed increase. 
In addition to the proposed three-year rate plan, the Company is open to developing a Safety and 
Reliability Charge (“SRC”) for the 12 months ending June 30, 2026 and June 30, 2027.  Pursuant 
to the SRC, Corning would recover the carrying costs (defined as pre-tax overall rate of return, 
depreciation expense and property taxes) on incremental plant additions over the previous year. 
The multi-year approach that Corning proposes in this filing affords an excellent opportunity for 
all stakeholders, including the Commission, to realize substantial benefits, including rate 
certainty and stability, reduced need to devote resources to annual rate filings, and enhancement 
of the Company’s ability to access financial markets on reasonable terms.  In addition, the SRC 
would provide contemporaneous review of the Company’s capital expenditures, which is one of 
the main drivers of the increased revenues Corning requires.  In short, a multi-year program 
would save costs that would otherwise be collected from customers and would not impair the 
Commission’s oversight responsibilities. 
The rate increases Corning seeks are necessitated by a number of factors.  The primary drivers 
are the need to recover costs associated with programs mandated by the Commission, recovery 
of regulatory amortization and increases in all other operating costs.  For the Commission’s 
convenience, Corning has included in Attachment B a summary of the major drivers underlying 
the determination to make this rate filing.  Full details of these drivers are contained in the 
exhibits being filed herewith.  The single largest factor driving the need for the proposed increase 
is growth in plant investment resulting principally from Commission mandates, including pipe 
replacement.  The Rate Year impact of such additional investment, as well as a listing of total 
capital expenditures by year is shown in Attachment B.  Further detail on the projects giving rise 
to these expenditures can be found in the accompanying Direct Testimony of Matt J. Cook (see
pp. 12-19) and the Exhibits referenced therein.

In an effort to promote a prompt decision in this proceeding, Corning, as it did in previous rate 
cases, including Case 20-G-0101,3 has undertaken to provide substantially more information 
concurrently with this filing than is either required or typical in rate proceedings.  Among other 
information, the Company is supplying rate case quality forecasts for the 12 months ending not 
only on June 30, 2023, but also for the 12 months ending June 30, 2024 and 2025.  Corning is 
also providing to the Staff of the Department of Public Service (“Staff”) the exhibits for this 
filing in an electronic form that includes all the interactive features of the model used by the 
Company.  In addition, based on prior cases, Corning is providing an exhibit in this proceeding 
(Exhibit CNG-13), which summarizes the Company’s electronic responses to “standard” Staff 
requests.  Those electronic responses will be made available to Staff on a “Share Point” site that 
will be password protected and subject to certain confidentiality requirements.  In addition, the 
Share Point site will include certain information from Corning’s consultant, Management 
Applications Consulting, Inc. (“MAC”), supporting MAC’s Cost of Service and Rate Design 
presentation.  The Company will provide a separate data base with all vendor charges and 
payments for the historical test year (i.e., the 12 months ended March 31, 2021).  Again, Staff 
will have electronic access to this information, which will be password protected and subject to 
certain confidentiality requirements.  By providing this information at this stage, the Company 
believes that review of this filing will be enhanced. 
In accordance with 16 NYCRR § 4.5(d), Corning submits herewith the prepared written

being served simultaneously on the Utility Intervention Unit of the Division of Consumer 
Protection of the New York Department of State.
