IV. 
Other Commission Audits 
1 
Q. 
Have there been any other Commission-initiated management or 
2 
operations audits of National Grid in the last several years? 
3 
A. 
Yes.  The Commission initiated the Gas Management Audit in 2013 along 
4 
with two statewide operations audits of New York utilities, the Data Audit 
5 
(2013) and Staffing Audit (2014).     
6 
 
7 
Q. 
Please provide a summary of the Gas Management Audit and its status. 
8 
A. 
In February 2013, the Commission commenced the Gas Management Audit 
9 
pursuant to PSL Section 66(19).  The Commission selected NorthStar 
10 
Consulting Group (“NorthStar”) to perform the audit.  In August 2014, 
11 
NorthStar released its audit report.  The report, which contained 31 
12 
recommendations, described generally strong performance by National 
13 
Grid’s gas business in a number of key functional areas, including gas 
14 
operations, load forecasting, supply procurement, and customer information 
15 
systems.   
16 
 
17 
On November 3, 2014, National Grid filed its implementation plan with the 
18 
Commission detailing plans to implement the audit recommendations.  The 
19 
implementation plan was approved by the Commission by Order dated May 
20 
14, 2015.  In the ensuing months, the Company worked collaboratively with

Q. 
Please discuss the status of the Staffing Audit. 
1 
A. 
In January 2014, the Commission instituted an audit of the internal staffing 
2 
levels and use of contractors for selected core utility functions at the major 
3 
New York utilities.  The Commission selected The Liberty Consulting 
4 
Group (“Liberty”) to conduct the audit.  In February 2017, the Commission 
5 
authorized the public release of Liberty’s audit report.  Liberty identified a 
6 
total of 27 recommendations aimed at enhancing National Grid’s staffing 
7 
and workforce management processes.  In March 2017, National Grid filed 
8 
its implementation plan, which was approved by the Commission in 
9 
December 2017.  In its latest implementation plan update filed with the 
10 
Commission, National Grid advised that all 27 recommendations had been 
11 
implemented and were pending Staff’s review and approval.
