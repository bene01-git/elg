Q. 
Please explain why the commercial charging station rebates proposed by the 
202 
Company are appropriate and necessary.  
203 
A. 
Particularly for DCFC installations, upfront costs of charging hardware and software can 
204 
be prohibitive for many site hosts. Although the Statewide Make-Ready Order does not 
205 
include a directive for hardware and software rebates, DCFC deployment is likely to be 
206

208 
experience in other service territories illustrates the prohibitive nature of upfront costs 
209 
well. In Rhode Island, the Company did not issue a single DCFC make-ready incentive in 
210 
rate year 1 of its program. In rate year two, the Company began coordinating with 
211 
Electrify RI, a program that leveraged VW funding to offer an incentive to offset the cost 
212 
of DCFC, and began to generate interest in its DCFC make-ready program. The 
213 
Company states that it expects to see increased participation going forward due to the 
214 
more comprehensive support offered through the combination of the charger rebate and 
215 
the make-ready program.9
