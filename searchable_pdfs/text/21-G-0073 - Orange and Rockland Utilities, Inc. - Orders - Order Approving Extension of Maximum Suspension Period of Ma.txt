BY THE COMMISISON: 
 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On January 29, 2021, Orange and Rockland Utilities, 
Inc. (Orange and Rockland or the Company) filed amendments to its 
tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 3 – Electricity and P.S.C. No. 4 - 
Gas, to increase the annual electric and gas delivery rates for 
the rate year ending December 31, 2022.  Orange and Rockland 
requested an increase in annual electric delivery revenues of 
approximately $24.5 million (inclusive of gross receipts tax,

5.8 percent increase in base delivery revenues, or 3.3 percent 
increase in total system revenues) compared to the revenues 
approved in Rate Year Three of the Company’s current rate plan.  
The requested increase in electric delivery revenues would 
result in monthly bill increase of approximately $6.09 (7.5 
percent increase on the delivery bill, or 4.9 percent increase 
on the total bill) for a typical residential customer using 600 
kilowatt hours per month.  According to the Company, the major 
cost drivers of the electric revenue request include 
infrastructure costs associated with return on rate base and 
depreciation on plant additions, reduced sales, and increases to 
labor expense and shared services costs. 
 
 
For its gas business, Orange and Rockland requested a 
delivery revenue increase of approximately $9.8 million 
(inclusive of gross receipts tax, 6.9 percent increase in 
delivery revenues, or 4.0 percent increase in total revenues).  
The requested increase in gas delivery revenues would result in 
a monthly bill increase of about $7.97 (9.1 percent increase on 
the delivery bill, or 5.8 percent increase on the total bill) 
for a typical customer using 100 hundred cubic feet per month.  
The Company asserts that the major cost drivers of the gas 
revenue request include infrastructure costs associated with 
return on rate base and depreciation on plant additions, 
increased shared services costs, and reduced sales. 
 
 
By Notices issued February 25, 2021, and May 13, 2021, 
the Secretary to the Commission, pursuant to authority delegated 
to her,1 extended the suspension period applicable to the 
effective date of the proposed tariff amendments through 
December 26, 2021, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
To accommodate the settlement process, by letter dated 
September 10, 2021, Orange and Rockland agreed to extend the 
maximum suspension period of these proceedings through May 25, 
2022.2  The request is subject to a make whole provision that 
would keep Orange and Rockland and its customers in the same 
economic position as it would have been absent the extension.  
Orange and Rockland states that for the purposes of these 
proceedings, under the make whole provision, it understands 
being “made whole” to mean that the Company would recover or 
refund any revenue under-collections or over-collections, 
respectively, including interest, resulting from the extended 
suspension period.  The Company would calculate any revenue 
adjustments as the difference between (i) sales revenues it 
would have billed at new rates during the extension of the 
suspension period, and (ii) the same level of sales revenues at 
current rates.  The revenue adjustments would include all 
applicable surcharges and would be subject to reconciliation in 
accordance with all applicable adjustment mechanisms (including 
revenue decoupling mechanisms, where applicable).  In addition, 
the amortization of net deferrals reflected in the Commission 
order will commence effective with the month of January 2022, on 
an earnings neutral basis. 
To facilitate the orderly conduct of these 
proceedings, the effective date of the tariff leaves filed by 
the Company is further extended through May 25, 2022, unless 
otherwise ordered by the Commission.

The Commission orders: 
1. 
The operation of the matters suspended by the 
Notices issued February 25, 2021, and May 13, 2021, in these 
proceedings, is further suspended and the use of the rates, 
charges, rules and regulations stated in said Notices be further 
extended through May 25, 2022, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission. 
2. 
Orange and Rockland Utilities, Inc. is directed 
to file with the Commission, on not less than five days’ notice 
on or before December 8, 2021, consecutively numbered 
supplements announcing the extension of the suspension period of 
suspended amendments.  
3. 
Orange and Rockland Utilities, Inc. shall be made 
whole for the period between the start of Rate Year One of the 
new rate plan and the date the Commission sets new rates, 
charges, rules and regulations for the new rate plan in the 
final decision in these proceedings. 
4.   These proceedings are continued.
