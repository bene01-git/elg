Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (“CLCPA”) 
3 
Q. 
What is the CLCPA and how does it affect the Company? 
4 
A. 
The CLCPA is New York State legislation, enacted in 2019, that is designed to reduce 
5 
greenhouse gas (“GHG”) emissions over the next several years.  The CLCPA 
6 
mandates, among many other things, the reduction of GHG emissions to 60% of 1990 
7 
levels by the year 2030, and to 15% of 1990 levels by the year 2050, with the 
8 
remaining emission reduction achieved by controlled offsets.
9 
Q. 
Has the CLCPA policy issue been addressed in previous Corning rate cases? 
10 
A. 
Yes.  In Case 20-G-0101 the Company proposed to shorten depreciation lives of plant 
11 
assets to correspond with the statutory date of the required reduction of New York 
12 
GHG emissions to 15% of 1990 levels, which, in Case 20-G-0101, was 30 years. 
13 
Q. 
Why did Corning make such a proposal? 
14 
A. 
The CLCPA, in conjunction with additional legislation proposed by the Governor, is 
15 
intended to reduce and then eliminate GHG emissions in New York State.  Over a now 
16 
much shorter period of time, this legislation will reduce the number of natural gas 
17 
customers and the volume of natural gas use, thus reducing customer revenues and 
18 
revenues per customer.  The CLCPA will thereby put pressure on the Company’s 
19 
access to external capital, thus increasing costs to safely deliver natural gas to its 
20 
customers.  The CLCPA poses a double edged threat to the Company. While its 
21 
greenhouse gas emission mandates will undeniably reduce its customer base and 
22 
customer revenues, this legislation, and the Commission’s orders, require the 
23
