A. Janitorial Services 
9 
Q. 
Please describe how Con Edison uses janitorial services. 
10 
A. 
Con Edison uses janitorial service contractors to perform a 
11 
range of essential cleaning tasks at 131 Company locations, 
12 
including plants, substations, and other buildings and 
13 
facilities. To provide a clean and hygienic work 
14

15

16 
vacuuming, wet mopping, toilet cleaning, disinfecting, and 
17 
dusting, among other tasks. The work performed by these 
18 
janitorial service contractors is crucial in maintaining a 
19 
clean, safe, and healthy environment for employees and 
20 
visitors at Company locations.
