Public Utility Law Project of New York, Inc. (PULP) 
Service Area PUMS_PUMA Data Procedures for Excel Users 
Excel users interested in re-producing median household incomes for the Central Hudson 
service area from the original source data (independent of evidence submitted by PULP in its 
December 22, 2020 direct testimony in Cases 20-E-0428 and 20-G-0429)1 can use the 
following procedures to do so. PULP’s analysis of the PUMAs that comprise Central Hudson’s 
service area2 will still be needed to subtract households in those PUMAs that are not entirely 
located within the service area. PULP identified in testimony that such an analysis would be 
require refinement by the Company in order to create a PUMA-based representation of service 
area households with a margin of error less than the 8% PULP estimated for its analysis. 
However, such a refinement is not necessary to complete the procedures below up to and 
including Step 24.   
Note: The recommended minimum system capabilities for these procedures are 100 gigabytes 
(“GB”) of free disk space, 1.3 gigahertz (“GHz”) of processing capacity, and 32 gigabytes 
(“GB”) of random access memory (“RAM”). 
Steps   
1.
Download the 2018 PUMS five-year ACS data for New York State households
(csv_hny.zip) from U.S. Census Bureau website (census.gov) using the following link:
https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs/data/pums/2018/5-Year/ 
2.
Extract the data from Step 1.
3.
Open the extracted “psam_h36.csv” in Excel.
4.

Workpapers - 2.xlsx”.
5.

b. “AE” through “BP”

d. “E” through “F”

14. Place the cursor in Cell “P2” on the “Service Area PUMA Data 1” in your newly named 
Excel file. 
15. Select the range from “P2” through “AT2”. 
16. Copy the range logic to the clipboard (Right-Click/Copy, or CTRL C). 
17. Place the cursor in Cell “P3”. 
18. In the “Name Box” just below “File” and “Home” at the top-left on the Excel main 
menu, replace “P3” with “P466235”. 
19. Hold the “Shift” key down and press “Enter”. 
20. Release the “Shift” and “Enter” keys and then press “Enter”. 
21. The logic from the range P2:AT2 will be copied for the range for the entire range 
P3:AT466235. (This process may take several minutes, depending on your computer’s 
processing and memory capacity.) 
22. With the range “P3:AT466235” still selected, copy the entire range’s logic to the 
clipboard (Right-Click/Copy, or CTRL C). 
23. Right-Click again and select Paste Values (the icon with the “123”). This will replace 
the logic of the range with values, freeing up substantial memory and disk space while 
retaining the logic necessary to re-create the data in range “P3:AT466235” intact in 
Row 2. 
24. Save your file. 
25. Place the cursor in Cell “P1” select “Data”, “Filter” from the Excel menu. 
26. Select the drop down that appears in Cell “P1” and uncheck the value “0”. 23,690 rows 
will be returned representing households in the Central Hudson service area. 
27. Place the cursor in Cell “A1” and select the entire range of the filtered data, which 
should be “A1:AT466209”. 
28. Copy the entire range to the clipboard (Right-Click/Copy, or CTRL C). 
29. Select the worksheet “Service Area PUMA Data 2”. Place the cursor in Cell “A1”. 
30. Right-Click and select Paste Values (the icon with the “123”). 
31. Right-Click again and Paste Formatting (the icon with the paint brush). 
32. Save your file. 
33. Select the range A1:AT23691, then select “Data”, “Sort” from the Excel main menu. 
34. Verify that the checkbox labeled “My Data Has Headers” is checked.
