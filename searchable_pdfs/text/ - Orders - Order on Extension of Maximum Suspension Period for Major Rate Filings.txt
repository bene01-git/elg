BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On January 31, 2025, Consolidated Edison Company of 
New York, Inc. (Con Edison or the Company) filed amendments to 
its tariff schedules, P.S.C. Nos. 10 and 12 – Electricity and 
P.S.C. No. 9 – Gas, to increase its annual electric and gas 
delivery revenues for the rate year ending December 31, 2026. 
 
 
Con Edison is requesting an increase in annual 
electric delivery revenues of approximately $1,612 million (an 
18.0 percent increase in base delivery revenues, or an 
11.4 percent increase in total revenues) compared to the

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
On June 24, 2025, the Company filed a letter agreeing 
to extend the maximum suspension period of these proceedings 
through February 28, 2026.3  The Company requested the extension 
to provide additional time for parties to continue settlement 
discussions in these proceedings.  The request is subject to a 
“make-whole” provision that the Company states would keep 
Con Edison and its customers in the same economic position as it 
would have been absent the extension.  The Company’s letter 
stated it understands “make-whole” to mean the Company would 
recover or refund any revenue under-collections or over-
collections, respectively, including interest, resulting from 
the extended suspension period.  The Company would calculate any 
revenue adjustments as the difference between sales revenues 
Con Edison would have billed at the new rates during the 
extension of the suspension period and the same level of sales 
revenues at current rates.  The revenue adjustments would 
include all applicable surcharges and would be subject to 
reconciliation in accordance with all applicable adjustment 
mechanisms (including revenue decoupling mechanisms, where 
applicable).  In addition, Con Edison proposes the amortization 
of net deferrals reflected in the Commission’s order will 
commence effective with the month of January 2026, on an 
earnings neutral basis. 
 
3  The Company filed a subsequent letter dated August 15, 2025,

Staff of the Department of Public Service filed a 
joint proposal on November 5, 2025, signed by various parties to 
these proceedings.  To facilitate the orderly conduct of these 
proceedings and to provide time for parties and the Commission 
to consider the joint proposal, the effective date of the tariff 
leaves filed by the Company is further extended through 
February 28, 2026, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.  
In addition, the Company will be provided a make whole provision 
for the period it requested. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The operation of the matters suspended by the 
Notices issued February 13, 2025, and June 3, 2025, in these 
proceedings, is further suspended and the use of the rates, 
charges, rules and regulations stated in said Notices be further 
extended through February 28, 2026, unless otherwise ordered by 
the Commission. 
2. Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. is 
directed to file with the Commission, on not less than one day’s 
notice on or before December 22, 2025, consecutively numbered 
supplements announcing the extension of the suspension period of 
suspended amendments. 
3. Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. shall 
be made whole for the period between January 1, 2026, and the 
date the Commission sets new rates, charges, rules and 
regulations in the final decision in these proceedings. 
4. These proceedings are continued.
