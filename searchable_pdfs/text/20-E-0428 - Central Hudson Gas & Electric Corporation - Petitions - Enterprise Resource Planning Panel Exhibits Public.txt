ERP Transformation: Directional Estimated Benefits   
The estimate of the quantitative annual benefits associated with implementation of 
SAP’s ERP S/4 solution to replace the existing Mainframe’s functionality for the EWAM 
capabilities considered potential savings in the forms of cost reduction, cost avoidance, 
and revenue enhancements.  These benefits were also identified as either potential 
capital or O & M savings.  These benefits are not expected to be realized immediately 
and Exhibit__ (ERPP-5) – ERP “Transformation: Estimated Benefits Timeline” provides 
the directional timeline of the transformation and the corresponding avoided 
investments and benefits that result. 
Key Improvement Drivers – Capital: $3,088,000 in benefits 
Capital Cost Reduction: $954,000 in savings 
Design, Estimation, and Construction Contracting Reduction: $573,000 

All work in one system leading to better visibility for work planning.

System based workload tracking and system based scheduling.

Standards based design leading to less re-work.
Field Clerk Overtime Reduction by Improved Material Reconciliation: $52,000 

Electronic receipt of materials used and marked for returns.










Contract Commercial Representative Reduction: $74,000 

