Regulations of Orange and Rockland Utilities  
for Gas Service. 
 
Proceeding on Motion of the Commission 
as to the Rates, Charges, Rules and                              Case 21-E-0074 
Regulations of Orange and Rockland Utilities 
for Electric Service 
 
Achievement of the goals outlined in the Climate Leadership and Community 
Protection Act (CLCPA) are a key concern of NY-GEO in all rate cases, along with 
concerns for rate equity among all customers. Because of the forward movement in 
several issues described below by Orange and Rockland Utilities, NY-GEO supports 
this settlement. NY-GEO also notes several focus areas related to, but not addressed 
in this settlement document, which should receive attention if New York State is to 
meet CLCPA mandated goals. 
 
The reduction in gas sales volume by 1.5% builds on % reduction targets in the last 2 
major utility rate cases before the Commission. This momentum is crucial as we move 
forward to fully comply with CLCPA goals. Subsequent rate cases will require stronger 
targeted reductions in gas usage and sales and we urge the Commission to provide 
guidance to staff on the necessary pace of ramping up billed gas usage reductions on 
a glide path that is consistent with necessary greenhouse gas reduction goals.  
 
Modification of website, customer mailings, emails and marketing materials to remove 
promotion of natural gas is welcomed.  Accurate and accessible information about gas 
alternatives will be important for English and non English speaking customer 
education and adoption.  NY-GEO has informed the Company about some existing 
website information (cost comparison calculator and expanded geothermal contractor 
listing) that needs to be corrected and is confident it will be changed. A commitment to 
rebates for induction stoves should be included on the Company’s on-line store.

We applaud the Company’s immediate commitment to 100% of it’s light duty vehicles 
being electric and the intent to move medium and heavy duty vehicles to non fossil 
fuels. The addition of EV charging infrastructure is also appreciated.  
 
Reducing the required regulatory allowances for mains, service lines and appurtenant 
facilities for residential customers is another positive first step, hopefully initiating the 
continuous reduction of these allowances to only statutory levels.  
 
“Considering” non pipeline alternatives (NPA’s) for projects involving the construction 
of a new gas pipeline or the replacement or expansion of an existing pipeline is not the 
same as making a firm commitment with numerical targets. NY-GEO would like to see 
a stronger commitment to a specific target(s) included in this rate case. 
 
While the points above led us to sign the JP, we do have several reservations on 
aspects of the settlement where we would like to see the Commission instruct staff to 
push for more positive resolution in future rate cases. These include:  
 
Depreciation. While NY-GEO is pleased with the Company’s increased focus on 
depreciation and hopefully the problem of stranded assets, we are disappointed that 
the commitment is open ended with no deadline. An improvement to this effort would 
be to thoroughly investigate depreciation this rate term.  
 
An emissions inventory specific to Company activity/facilities does not go far enough 
to be of real value to future decisions.  Total lifecycle emissions encompassing those 
created by the production, transport, distribution and combustion of fossil fuels 
purchased and sold by Orange and Rockland Utilities provides the most meaningful 
and CLCPA aligned information.  In addition to both direct and indirect emissions, the 
20 year methane impact should be used when calculating emissions and EAM’s.  
 
Rates have an impact on fossil fuel use and customer equipment purchases.  In the 
previous rate case a more equitable rate that would benefit geothermal customers  
was included in the settlement document, yet still awaits Commission approval. NY-
GEO would like to see this rate approved and implemented for the upcoming rate 
term.
