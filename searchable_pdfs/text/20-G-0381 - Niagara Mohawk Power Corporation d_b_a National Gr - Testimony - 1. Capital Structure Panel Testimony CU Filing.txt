III. 
Corrections and Updates 
1 
Q. 
Please describe the corrections and updates that are reflected in Exhibit __ (CSP 
2 
1CU). 
3 
A.  
Schedule 1 of Exhibit __ (CSP-1) originally indicated debt issuances of $500,000,000 on 
4 
October 31, 2022, and $300,000,000 on June 30, 2024.  Now the Company is planning on 
5 
issuances of $300,000,000 on March 31, 2022, $500,000,000 on October 31, 2022, and 
6 
$500,000,000 on June 30, 2024.  These issuances are set forth on Schedule 1 of Exhibit __ 
7 
(CSP-1CU).  We have incorporated the effects of these more recent assumptions across the 
8 
revised exhibit. 
9 
 
10 
Q. 
Please explain the reason for these changes. 
11 
A. 
The changes reflect revised assumptions concerning the optimal timing of new issuances 
12 
and reflect the Company’s effort to take advantage of favorable financial conditions in the 
13 
long-term debt market.  Securing more long-term, permanent financing at favorable interest 
14 
rates will reduce the Company’s reliance on borrowing short-term debt from the money 
15 
pool, which would be refinanced at a future date.  Ensuring the Company has appropriate 
16 
cash reserves during the current uncertain economic environment will reduce future 
17 
financing risks. 
18
