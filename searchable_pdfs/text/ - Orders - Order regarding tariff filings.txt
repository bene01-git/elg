SUMMARY 
 
 
 
On November 27, 2013, the Brooklyn Union Gas Company 
d/b/a KeySpan Energy Delivery New York (KEDNY) and KeySpan Gas 
East Corporation d/b/a KeySpan Energy Delivery Long Island 
(KEDLI) (collectively, the Companies) filed tariff amendments, 
effective on a temporary basis as of January 1, 2014, in 
compliance with Commission Orders issued December 21, 2007, 
November 28, 2012 and June 13, 2013.1  The majority of the tariff 
amendments are designed to implement delivery rate changes for 
KEDNY and KEDLI for the seventh rate year (January 1, 2014 
through December 31, 2014).  These changes do not increase base 
rate revenue but instead continue the gradual shift of revenue 
recovery from the delivery block rates to the monthly minimum 
charges.  The remaining tariff amendments update the service 
classification specific Site Investigation and Remediation (SIR) 
Recovery Surcharges for both KEDNY and KEDLI to recover deferred 
balances stemming from their respective SIR activities. 
Staff has reviewed the Companies’ tariff amendments 
and finds that they are in compliance with the relevant 
Commission Orders.  Therefore, Staff recommends that the tariff 
amendments be allowed to become effective on a permanent basis 
upon the issuance of this Order.

BACKGROUND 
KEDNY and KEDLI were directed to file tariff changes 
as are necessary to effectuate the terms of the 2007 Rate Order 
provided that such amendments were to become effective on a 
temporary basis on or after January 1, 2012, but would not be 
made permanent until approved by the Commission.  The 2007 Rate 
Order required annual, revenue neutral, delivery rate changes to 
better align the minimum charges with the cost of service.   
In the Deferred Balances Order, the Commission took 
action with regard to the level of SIR expenses incurred and 
deferred by the Companies.  The 2007 Rate Order already 
established a level of SIR recovery, through the Delivery Rate 
Adjustment, of $25 million for KEDNY and $40 million for KEDLI.  
However, under the 2007 Rate Order, these surcharges were set to 
expire at the end of 2012.  The Commission’s Deferred Balances 
Order continues, at the same level, the company-specific 
recovery mechanisms beginning January 1, 2013, resulting in no 
net impact to customer bills.  The Companies were each directed 
to implement the new surcharge, which would operate in the same 
manner as the 2007 Rate Order’s expiring mechanism, collected 
from each firm service classification based on the forecasted 
percentage of each service classification’s contribution to 
total firm delivery revenues.2 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The November 27, 2013 tariff amendment filings made to 
KEDNY’s and KEDLI’s gas tariffs, P.S.C. Nos. 12 and 1 – Gas, 
respectively, were made in compliance with the 2007 Rate Order, 
the Deferred Balances Order and the 2013 Order.  Staff has
