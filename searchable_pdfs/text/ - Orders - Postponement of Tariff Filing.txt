National Grid NY – Tariff Filing Regarding Phase Two 
Power Generation Collaborative.   
 
SUMMARY OF 
RECOMMENDATION:  Staff recommends approval of the postponements 
to June 21, 2018. 
 
 
 
On January 23, 2018, KeySpan Gas East Corporation 
d/b/a National Grid (KEDLI) and The Brooklyn Union Gas Company 
d/b/a National Grid NY (KEDNY) (together, the Companies) filed 
amendments to their gas tariff schedules, KEDLI P.S.C. No. 12 
and KEDNY P.S.C. No. 1.  The Companies proposed revisions 
pursuant to Phase Two Report of the Power Generation 
Collaborative regarding the elimination of the additional

penalty charge of $10 per dekatherm applied to certain customer 
imbalances in the daily balancing charges.  The proposed 
amendments have an effective date of May 21, 2018.  
 
The Companies have agreed to postpone the effective 
date of their proposed amendments to June 21, 2018, to provide 
additional time for review of the filings.  On April 25, 2018, 
the Companies filed supplements to implement the postponements 
and requested that the supplements be allowed to go into effect 
on less than statutory notice on May 21, 2018.  The Companies 
also requested waivers of the requirements of newspaper 
publication of the change proposed by the supplements.   
   
         It is recommended that special permission be granted: 
1. authorizing Supplement No. 77 to KEDLI’s Schedule 
P.S.C. No. 1 – Gas and Supplement No. 88 to KEDNY’s 
Schedule P.S.C. No. 12 - Gas to go into effect on 
less than statutory notice on May 21, 2018; and 
 
2. waiving the requirements of Public Service Law 
§66(12)(b) and 16 NYCRR §720-8.1 as to newspaper 
publication of the change proposed by the 
supplements.
