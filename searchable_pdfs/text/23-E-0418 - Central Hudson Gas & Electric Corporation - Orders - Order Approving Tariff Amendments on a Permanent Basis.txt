suspension period of the electric and gas rate proceedings by 
one month, the Commission approved a make whole mechanism.5  The 
make whole mechanism consists of a surcharge or credit that 
allows the Company and ratepayers to recoup revenues that would 
have been collected or refunded as a result of the new rates 
absent the one-month delay in the issuance of the 2024 Rate 
Order.  The 2024 Rate Order requires that the revenue 
differences be collected or refunded over a ten-month period, 
starting September 1, 2024.

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), and 66(1) and (12), the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the proposed tariff 
leaves, as well as modify, reject or approve such filed tariffs 
to ensure that the rates and services governed by those tariffs 
are just and reasonable and otherwise consistent with law.  As 
such, the Commission has the legal authority to review and 
approve tariff amendments on a permanent basis, as prescribed in 
this Order.

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
The proposed tariff amendments filed by Central 
Hudson, containing changes in both tariff language and customer 
rates, are consistent with the directives of the 2024 Rate 
Order.  The make whole provisions for both gas and electric 
service that went into effect on September 1, 2024 also properly 
capture the revenue differences caused by the extension of the 
suspension period.  
The Commission notes that several of Central Hudson’s 
proposed tariff amendments related to its electric rate 
proceeding are on electric tariff leaves that were previously 
modified by the Company on December 15, 2023, and April 15, 
2024, in compliance with the Standby Service Order in Case 15-E-
0751.  These leaves are Leaf Nos. 272.6.1, 232, 272.3.2, and 
231.  These leaves, because they are related to the Standby 
Service Order, will remain in effect on a temporary basis until 
acted upon by the Commission.  Accordingly, the Commission 
authorizes the tariff amendments listed in the Appendix to go 
into effect on a permanent basis, except for Leaf Nos. 272.6.1, 
232, 272.3.2, and 231, as those amendments are related to the 
Standby Service Order in Case 15-E-0751. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by Central Hudson Gas & 
Electric Corporation and listed in the Appendix to this Order 
are authorized to become effective on a permanent basis upon the 
date of issuance of this Order, provided that Leaf Nos. 272.6.1, 
232, 272.3.2, and 231, which are related to Case 15-E-0751, 
shall remain in effect on a temporary basis until acted upon by 
the Commission, as discussed in the body of this Order.
