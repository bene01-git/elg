II. 
Purpose of Testimony 
13 
Q. 
What is the purpose of the Panel’s supplemental direct testimony? 
14 
A. 
The purpose of the Panel’s supplemental direct testimony is to discuss the 
15 
potential impacts to the gas capital programs described in KEDLI’s April 
16 
2019 rate filing if the Company is unable to obtain incremental natural gas 
17 
supplies from Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line, LLC’s Northeast Supply 
18 
Enhancement (“NESE”) project, which would provide 400,000 
19 
dekatherms/day of incremental gas supply to KEDNY and KEDLI’s gas 
20 
distribution systems to meet forecast demand for natural gas and enhance 
21 


--- Page 4 ---
Page 2 of 8 
 
reliability for existing customers.  In our direct testimony, the Panel 
1 
identified certain proposals that would be affected if the NESE project were 
2 
not placed in service by the winter of 2020/2021.  As discussed more fully 
3 
by Company Witness Elizabeth D. Arangio, recent decisions by the New 
4 
York State Department of Environmental Conservation (“DEC”) and the 
5 
New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection regarding required 
6 
water permits create uncertainty as to whether and when the NESE project 
7 
may proceed with permitting and construction.  While the Company 
8 
remains cautiously optimistic the NESE project will go forward, and 
9 
therefore is not proposing any changes to its April 2019 filing at this time, 
10 
the Panel addresses the impacts that gas supply constraints would have on 
11 
KEDLI’s forecast capital additions and certain gas operations and safety 
12 
programs should the NESE project not be available during the proposed rate 
13 
plan period.

III. 
Potential Impacts of the NESE Project and Gas Supply Constraints 
10 
Q. 
Did the Panel’s direct testimony discuss the possibility that the NESE 
11 
project might not be available and that this could affect the Company’s 
12 
filing? 
13 
A. 
Yes.  While the Company’s filing was prepared assuming the NESE project 
14 
would be completed by the winter of 2020/2021, the Panel’s direct 
15 
testimony (at 118-120) identified the possibility that the NESE project 
16 
would not be available as planned and explained that KEDLI’s capital 
17 
investment plan would be impacted in that scenario.  Specifically, the Panel 
18 
explained that several planned capital programs and projects could be 
19 
eliminated, modified, or deferred, including the Customer Connections and 
20 
Gas System Reinforcement programs.  Further, without the planned 
21 


--- Page 6 ---
Page 4 of 8 
 
capacity from the NESE project, the Company would be unable to proceed 
1 
with the planned maintenance and upgrades to the Holtsville LNG facility. 
2 
 
3 
Q. 
Please explain the impact to the Company’s capital investment plan if 
4 
the NESE project is not approved.     
5 
A. 
Exhibit ___ (GIOP-1S) is an illustrative capital investment plan reflecting 
6 
the impact in the Rate Year and Data Years should the NESE project not be 
7 
completed on time, including reductions to the Company’s Customer 
8 
Connections, Reinforcements, and LNG programs.  As stated by Company 
9 
Witness Arangio, without the incremental capacity from the NESE project, 
10 
KEDLI will not be able to procure sufficient gas supplies to meet the 
11 
forecast demand from new firm customers, such that connecting additional 
12 
firm load would jeopardize the Company’s ability to provide safe and 
13 
reliable gas service to existing customers.   Therefore, investments in the 
14 
Customer Connections programs associated with serving new firm load 
15 
would not be necessary, including the installation of new main and the 
16 
purchase and installation of new meters and associated AMR devices, as 
17 
well as investments in the low-to-moderate income gas conversion program 
18 
that was proposed by the Company’s Future of Heat Panel.  Similarly, the 
19 
Gas System Reinforcements program would be reduced to remove projects 
20 


--- Page 7 ---
Page 5 of 8 
 
no longer needed to serve incremental firm load in KEDLI’s service 
1 
territory.   
2 
 
 
3 
 
Finally, without the NESE project, the Company is unable to take the 
4 
Holtsville LNG Tank out of service to perform planned maintenance and 
5 
upgrades.  The Company intends to perform some engineering work for this 
6 
project for the purpose of conducting studies and design work for potential 
7 
limited upgrades to the tank that would not require taking it out of service.       
8

page shows the adjustments to each impacted program.  Exhibit __ (GIOP-

1S) provides a revised version of the total capital forecast showing the

adjusted programs.

Q. 
Would the supply constraints affect KEDLI’s forecast of capital and 
2 
O&M expenses for any of the proposed gas safety programs? 
3 
A. 
Yes.  The Company’s Contractor Inspector Safety Program, which will add 
4 
contractor inspectors over three years to reach a one-to-one ratio of 
5 
inspectors to crews, would also be adjusted to reflect reductions to the 
6 
capital work plan.  KEDLI would reduce its total incremental FTE proposal 
7 
by 13 inspectors and one supervisor.  The revised FTE forecast is provided 
8 
in Exhibit __ (GIOP-8S).  Associated reductions to the capital forecast and 
9 
non-labor O&M forecast for the Contractor Inspector Program are set forth 
10 
in Exhibit __ (GIOP-1S) and Exhibit (GIOP-7S), respectively.
