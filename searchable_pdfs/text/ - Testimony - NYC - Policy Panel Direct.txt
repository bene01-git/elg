8 
CONSIDERATIONS. 
9 
A. 
Given the dense vertical urban environment in New York City, with an increasing 
10 
number of high-rise developments, electricity has become a critical safety need as 
11 
well as a basic human need.  It is well-established that the continuous provision of 
12 
electricity is critical to preserving public health and safety.  New York City has one 
13 
of the most reliable electric systems in the world, and that reliability should not be 
14 
diminished. 
15 
 
 
Our changing climate has placed growing emphasis on the need for highly 
16 
resilient utility systems.  Severe storms, previously considered very infrequent 
17 
occurrences, are now occurring every few years.  The severity of storms is 
18 
increasing, and climate science indicates that future extreme weather will worsen.  
19 
As sea levels rise, flooding and coastal storms will become stronger, damage will 
20 
extend deeper into New York City,  and greater numbers of residents will be 
21 


--- Page 18 ---
Cases 25-E-0072 and 25-G-0073 
NYC POLICY PANEL 
17 
impacted.  Future heat waves will occur more frequently, last a week or more, and 
1 
occur during what, in the past, were considered shoulder seasons.  Climate change 
2 
projections also indicate that future climatic events will include compound impacts, 
3 
such as high heat combined with high humidity, which increase grid stress while 
4
