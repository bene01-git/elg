Q.   
Is the minimum size distribution system method and zero intercept method the 
6 
same thing? 
7 
A.  
In concept, yes they are the same.  The minimum size system approach identifies the 
8 
minimum sized distribution main needed to serve customers and then classifies that 
9 
portion of distribution mains as customer-related.  The zero intercept method uses 
10 
regression analysis to identify the cost of a hypothetical “zero sized” main, the cost of 
11 
which is necessary to serve customers connected to the system whether or not they 
12 
place any demand on the system.  While there may be subtle differences between the 
13 
two methods, both recognize that certain distribution main costs should be classified 
14 
as customer-related and allocated based on the number of customers not on peak 
15 
demand.     
16 
 
17

cost of distribution mains?

A. 
The result is a gross misallocation of costs that fails to allocate proper cost

This point can be illustrated with a simple example.  Assume that there is a single
