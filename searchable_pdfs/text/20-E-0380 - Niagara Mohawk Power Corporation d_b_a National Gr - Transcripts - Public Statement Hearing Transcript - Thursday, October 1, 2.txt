today for a public statement hearing that was noticed 
on September 8th, 2020.  Niagara Mohawk filed 
amendments to its electric and gas tariff schedules

million dollars, a 4.9 percent increase in base 
delivery revenues or a 3.2 percent increase in total 
revenues and its natural gas delivery revenues by 
approximately 41.8 million dollars, which is 
approximately 9.8 percent increase in base delivery 
revenues or 5.2 percent increase in total revenues.
