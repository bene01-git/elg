What surcharges in addition to rates are included in the attached 
document 
Perplexity AI Search 
The attached Joint Proposal document for Consolidated Edison in Cases 25-E-0072 (electric) and 
25-G-0073 (gas) includes several surcharges and pass-through mechanisms that are applied in 
addition to standard base rates. These surcharges cover a wide range of expenses, true-ups, 
reconciliation processes, and regulatory adjustments. 
Key Surcharges and Mechanisms Included 
• 
Monthly Adjustment Clause (MAC): This includes specific adjustments and a NYPA 
Surcharge for supply and supply-related charges. 
• 
 
Additional Items 
• 
Deferral and True-Up Mechanisms: Many operational, regulatory, or capital expense 
categories feature deferrals, reconciliations, and—where eligible—future recovery via 
surcharges to customers (e.g., storm recovery costs, taxes, pension funding above 
forecasts). 
• 
 
For most surcharges, annual caps are imposed (often no more than 0.5% total bill impact for items 
like property tax reconciliation or uncollectible accounts), and any excess is deferred for future 
recovery. 
 
This structure results in many bill components beyond standard delivery and supply rates, enabling 
the utility to recover or credit costs based on actual expenses, regulatory changes, and events 
during the rate plan period. 
 
Revenue Decoupling Mechanism (RDM): Regular recalculations and adjustments to account for 
differences between forecast and actual usage.PJM OATT Charges: Related to transmission. 
Major Storm Cost Reserve Annual Surcharge: Covers major storm recovery costs exceeding 
annual rate allowances, with caps defined for electric service in each rate year (between $43–$47 
million depending on the year). 
Uncollectible Expense and Late Payment Charge (LPC) Surcharge: Annual true-up and 
surcharges for late payment charges and uncollectible accounts, with a cap at 0.5% of the total bill 
per commodity. 
Property Tax Surcharge/Credit: Variations between projected and actual property taxes are 
recovered or credited through a surcharge mechanism, with an annual bill impact cap.

Pensions/OPEBs Surcharge: Reconciliation of pension and other post-employment benefits 
expenses, deferred above or below projected values. 
Environmental Remediation Surcharge: Reconciles costs of site investigations and 
environmental remediation that differ from the forecast. 
Municipal Infrastructure Support Surcharge: Adjusts for non-labor municipal infrastructure 
support costs that vary from the set base. 
Congestion Tolling Program Surcharge: Recovery of new congestion charges as they are 
incurred. 
Major Storm Reserve: Annual funding and surcharges for major storm-related costs above the 
base reserve, subject to caps. 
Safety and Reliability Surcharge (Gas only): For specific pipeline and system safety 
improvements. 
Energy Efficiency Surcharge: Certain energy efficiency program costs are now recovered through 
a separate surcharge rather than base rates. 
Other Variable Surcharges: Various mechanisms enable surcharges for adjustments such as 
taxes, regulatory changes, or other unforeseen expenses as detailed in the proposal. 
Appendices Detail: Specific formulae, annual caps, and true-up calculations are detailed in the 
document's appendices for each surcharge or adjustment scenario.
