Request: 
 
 
1.  
In the Joint Proposal Appendix 1 the Companies forecast a capital expenditure of 
$12,264,000 for KEDNY LNG Blanket in FY‘24, a large increase from the previous 
years and $9,499,627 more than forecasted in the Companies’ December 2019 Second 
Supplemental Testimony. 
 
The Companies forecast a capital expenditure of $5,754,000 for KEDLI LNG Blanket in 
FY‘24, a large increase from the previous years and $4,609,781 more than forecasted in 
the Companies’ December 2019 Second Supplemental Testimony. 
 
a. 
Please explain the changes in the LNG Blanket Program for both KEDNY & 
KEDLI leading to increased capital expenditures. 
 
2.  
Appendix 6 “Capital Capacity Projects Summary of Estimated Revenue Requirement 
Impact” shows the total Capex Impact of Vaporizers 13 & 14 from FY’20-FY’24 to be 
$4,329,000 higher than the Companies originally forecasted in their Direct Capital 
Expenditures in the December 2019 Second Supplemental Testimony. 
 
b. 
Please explain the increased costs for Vaporizers 13 & 14. 
 
Response: 
 
 
1. 
As described in the Companies’ GIOP testimonies, the LNG Blanket program provides 
funding for near-term and emergent capital projects needed to maintain safety and 
reliability at its LNG facilities.  The LNG Blanket budget is based on historical averages 
and typically increases 2% annually (adjusting for inflation).  In FY24, the base capital 
expenditure for the LNG Blanket in KEDNY and KEDLI is $2,764,373 and $1,144,219, 
respectively.  The FY24 figures for the LNG Blanket in Appendix 1, Schedule 5 and

The initial estimate for Vaporizers 13 and 14 was developed primarily based on a similar

construction bids received, and risks-based estimates, and serves as a more accurate 
depiction of potential costs based on the specifics of the project.
