Q 
WHAT FACTORS CAUSE A UTILITY TO INVEST IN AN ELECTRIC DISTRIBUTION 
1 
NETWORK? 
2 
A 
The purpose of the electric distribution network is to deliver power from the 
3 
transmission grid to the customer, where it is eventually consumed.  Thus, the central 
4 
roles of the distribution network are to: 
5 
 
Provide access to a delivery-ready power grid (i.e., a customer-related 
6 
cost); and 
7 
 
Meet customers’ peak electrical power needs (i.e., a demand-related 
8 
cost). 
9 
Providing grid access requires not only a physical connection, but also the voltage 
10 
support, which is provided by the distribution network infrastructure.  Clearly, these 
11 
costs are related to the existence of the customer.  This is why classifying a portion of 
12 
the distribution network as customer-related is consistent with cost causation.  In other 
13 
words, investments that must be made solely to attach a customer to the system are 
14 
clearly customer-related.   
15

16 
DISTRIBUTION MAINS?

customer’s expected design day demand.  Further, this investment must be capable 
20 
of sustaining the appropriate operating pressure to support the delivery of natural gas.  
21 
To the extent that this component of distribution main cost is a function of the 
22 
requirement to connect the customer and support the deliverability of natural gas, 
23 
regardless of the customer’s size, it is both appropriate and necessary to allocate the

WHY WOULD CLASSIFYING ALL DISTRIBUTION NETWORK COSTS TO
