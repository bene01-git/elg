Dear Ms. Magnis: 
 
The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (“KEDNY”) and KeySpan 
Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“KEDLI”) (collectively, the “Companies”) 
respectfully request that the information in the attached documents be treated by the Public 
Service Commission (“Commission”) and the Department of Public Service as confidential 
pursuant to Public Officers Law §§ 86(5), 87(2)(f), and 89(5)(a)(1-a), and the Commission’s 
implementing regulation, 16 NYCRR § 6-1.3.  The documents for which confidential 
treatment is requested are reports regarding the Companies’ plans to upgrade the Greenpoint 
and Holtsville liquified natural gas (“LNG”) facilities, which enhance system reliability and 
provide backup gas supplies on the Companies’ gas systems (the “Attachments”).  The 
Attachments contain information related to specific LNG asset vintages and conditions, 
operational information, and facility limitations.  This information is critical energy 
infrastructure information regarding systems, assets, places or things, so vital to the state that 
the disruption, incapacitation or destruction of such systems, assets, places or things could 
jeopardize the health, safety, welfare or security of the state, its residents or its economy.  
Redacted versions of the Attachments have been filed with the Commission’s Secretary.  
Unredacted copies of the Attachments are attached hereto and have been marked with the 
following designation: “This document contains Critical Energy Infrastructure Information 
(CEII).  DO NOT DISTRIBUTE or COPY.” 
 
Accordingly, the Companies respectfully request that the confidential information 
included in the Attachments be exempted from public disclosure in accordance with applicable 
law and Commission regulations. 
Kara J. Krueger, Esq. 
Senior Counsel I
