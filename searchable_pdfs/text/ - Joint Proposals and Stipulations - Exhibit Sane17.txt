SUBJECT: Greenpoint KNG Facility Vaporizers
 
Request: 
 
 
The Companies have proposed several improvements and capacity projects for the LNG facility 
on Newtown Creek in Greenpoint, Brooklyn. These include replacing antiquated LNG 
vaporizers, adding new LNG vaporizers, and developing LNG truck loading and unloading 
capacity. 
1. Please list by their numbers all of the LNG vaporizers currently in operation at the 
Greenpoint facility, and the years when each were constructed. 
2. According to the technical consultant Burns and McDonnell, two antiquated LNG 
vaporizers were replaced this year, with work completed in June 2020. Please list the 
numbers of those vaporizers and clarify if those two vaporizers were “in-flight” and 
covered by this rate case, or if they are associated with the previous rate case.  
3. How many entirely new vaporizers are planned, and what are their reference numbers 
(such as. 7 & 8, 13 & 14)?  
4. Please provide the capacity of each of the active and proposed vaporizers by their 
numbers, in terms of tons per hour.  
5. What is the energy source used for vaporizing the LNG at the Greenpoint facility?  
      6.   How much regasified LNG was injected into the system for Brooklyn customers in 
            winter 2018-19 and winter 2019-20? What percentage of total capacity is this?  
      7.   Please specify how much regasified LNG can be used as "on system supply" in terms of 
            1) how much gas can be drawn on a daily basis (including from storage) to mitigate peak 
            demand; and 2) how much gas can be or is drawn for non-peak uses.  
      8.   What is the capacity for storing LNG at the Greenpoint facility? What percent of the 
            stored LNG is currently liquefied on site, and what percent is delivered by truck?  
      9.   How many tanker trucks, with what capacity and at what frequency by day, are expected 
            to deliver LNG to the Greenpoint facility in winter 2020-21 and 2021-22?  
    10.   When LNG is trucked to Greenpoint, where will it be trucked in from? Please list all the 
             source locations. 
    11.   Will LNG be trucked from the Greenpoint facility for vaporization or storage elsewhere? 
             If yes, please list all destination facilities outside Brooklyn, and the corporate owners of

Response: 
 
 
1. The LNG vaporizers currently in operation at the Greenpoint facility, and the years when 
each were constructed are as follows: 
 
• Vaporizer 3 - 1969 
• Vaporizer 4 - 1969 
• Vaporizer 7 - 1980 
• Vaporizer 8 - 1980 
• Vaporizer 9 - 1986 
• Vaporizer 10 - 1986 
 
2. Vaporizer Project 11 and 12 is in construction and the new units are planned to be in-
service in September 2021 and June 2021, respectively.  These vaporizers will replace 
existing Vaporizers 3 and 4, which will be taken out of service and removed from the 
site.  The Vaporizer 11 and 12 Project was approved in a prior rate case with rates in 
effect April 2016.  
 
3. There are two vaporizer projects.  Vaporizer 11 and 12 project is in construction and will 
replace Vaporizers 3 and 4.  The Vaporizer 13 and 14 Project adds two vaporizers to the 
facility and is planned to start construction of the new asset in January 2021.  This is not 
an expansion of the Greenpoint LNG facility insofar as the project does not change the 
existing storage capacity of the LNG facility, rather it allows the Company to achieve 
faster delivery of LNG into the system when needed on peak days.  
 
4. The capacity of each of the active and proposed vaporizers by their reference number, in 
terms of capacity are as follows: 
• Vaporizer 3 – 60 MMSCFD 
• Vaporizer 4 - 60 MMSCFD 
• Vaporizer 7 - 60 MMSCFD 
• Vaporizer 8 - 60 MMSCFD 
• Vaporizer 9 - 60 MMSCFD  
• Vaporizer 10 - 60 MMSCFD 
• Vaporizer 11 - 60 MMSCFD 
• Vaporizer 12 - 60 MMSCFD 
• Vaporizer 13 - 60 MMSCFD (proposed) 
• Vaporizer 14 - 60 MMSCFD (proposed) 
 
The current rated facility capacity is 291,200 DTH and this accounts for vaporizer 
equipment redundancy (i.e., spare vaporizer equipment) to ensure facility reliability.  The 
current rated facility capacity does not include Vaporizer 13 and 14.

Gas is utilized for heating.

7. The volume of regasified LNG that can be injected into the system during peak periods of 
demand is 291,200 DTH.  The number is the same during non-peak periods if the system 
conditions permit. 
 
8. As stated in the Company’s Exhibit GIOP-5, the LNG facility at Greenpoint has a tank 
capacity of 1.6 BSCF.  100% of the LNG is liquefied with equipment onsite. 
 
9. None.  Applicable regulations do not allow LNG to be transported via tanker truck in 
New York City. 
   
10. There is no plan to truck LNG to the Greenpoint LNG facility.  The proposed Truck 
Station project increases the reliability of the Greenpoint LNG facility by allowing tanker 
trucks to offload LNG and refill the LNG tanks under emergency conditions and with 
FDNY approval only.  The asset provides a back-up option to the primary liquefaction 
capabilities at the plant exclusively used to fill the tanks.  
 
11. No.  There is no plan to truck LNG from the Greenpoint facility for vaporization or 
storage elsewhere.
