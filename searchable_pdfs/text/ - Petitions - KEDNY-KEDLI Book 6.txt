National Grid’s overall approach to compensation is to ensure that (i) a

18

achieving goals that are tied to the attainment of customer satisfaction, 
20 
safety and reliability objectives.  To provide safe, reliable and efficient 
21

1 
Q.

2 
A. 
In 2015, the Commission issued an Order that adopted a new definition of 
3 
“service line” in New York.  Under the former definition in New York’s 
4

5

10

will require additional training, qualifications and testing.  This is a 
15 
paradigm shift that makes KEDNY and KEDLI responsible for inspecting 
16 
more customer-installed, inside facilities.  In urban areas with high 
17 
concentrations of multi-unit buildings, such as KEDNY’s service territory, 
18 
work volume and the need for additional resources will increase 
19 
significantly.
