MUNICIPAL INTERFERENCE OVERVIEW 
9 
Q. 
Please explain municipal interference and why 
10 
Con Edison incurs costs related to municipal 
11 
interference. 
12 
A. 
Municipal interference occurs when Con Edison’s 
13 
facilities are within, or very close to, the 
14

15 
on the project scope, Con Edison must relocate 
16

19

20 
generate interference costs for Con Edison

22 
and repair of water mains, installation and 
23 
repair of sewers and drainage facilities, and

3 
interference costs? 
4 
A.  The Company’s O&M costs are primarily driven by 
5 
indirect interference, which are expenses
