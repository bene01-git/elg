* New York Geothermal Energy Organization signs this Joint Proposal with respect to the
electric service provisions (Case 22-E-0064) only and not for the gas service provisions (Case
22-G-0065). NY-GEO  believes that the proposal contains provisions in furtherance of the
objectives of the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act only for the electric service
and does not agree the gas service is in furtherance of the CLCPA and requests any public
announcements referencing NY-GEO make this distinction clear.
