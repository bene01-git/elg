Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
 
The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (“KEDNY”) and KeySpan 
Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“KEDLI”) (KEDNY and KEDLI collectively the 
“Companies”) hereby submit to the New York Public Service Commission (“Commission”) a 
Petition Seeking Approval of a Non-Pipeline Alternative (“NPA”) BCA Process Framework, 
Cost Recovery Framework, Customer NPA Incentive, and Performance Incentive, For Current 
and Future NPA Projects. 
 
Specifically, the Companies request Commission approval of five core elements: 
 
1. A streamlined NPA Benefit-Cost Analysis (BCA) process allowing NPA projects with an 
SCT score ≥ 1.0 to proceed upon filing of the BCA with the Commission; 
 
2. An NPA cost recovery framework, including creation of a ten-year regulatory asset 
amortization period and a surcharge mechanism for incremental NPA costs; 
 
3. A customer NPA incentive for an implemented New Connection NPA at a residential 
development in KEDLI’s service territory; 
 
4. A shareholder performance incentive framework applicable to NPAs; and 
 
5. A performance incentive for the implemented KEDLI New Connection NPA for the 
number of homes built to date.  
Bess Beikoussis Gorman 
Assistant General Counsel
