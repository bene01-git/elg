2 
      I hereby oppose the Joint Proposal because it fails to adequately 
address the need  for Orange and Rockland to proactively lead its 
service territory in a transition to  clean energy alternatives in order to 
meet national climate change prevention goals.  
      As to rates, I join in the opposition of PULP, Inc. and support their 
statement of opposition. As I stated in my testimony ,others are better 
equipped than I to address these issues. 
      As to Climate Change, the Joint Proposal shows that the company is 
relying on passive incentives and existing efficiency programs to 
promote positive climate change in tis territory. While I support the 
continuance of the existing incentives to the public, there is no 
evidence to support the proposition that there will not be some 
diminishing returns from this program. Changing O&R small vehicles to 
electric vehicles will take years and produce little resulting emissions 
savings overall.
