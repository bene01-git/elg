Allocated Cost of Service Study 
2 
Q. 
Would you briefly define an Allocated Cost of Service Study?
3 
A. 
The cost to serve the customers of any utility company consists generally of operating 
4 
expenses and return.  For a historical test period, these costs are on record and the overall 
5 
cost to serve the collective customers of the utility may be readily established.  On the 
6 
other hand, the unique cost to serve customers of the various service classifications is 
7 
much less apparent.  Costs can vary significantly between customer classes depending 
8 
upon the nature of their demands upon the system and the facilities required to serve 
9 
them.  The purpose of an Allocated Cost of Service Study is to assign or allocate each 
10 
relevant component of cost on an appropriate basis in order to determine the proper cost 
11 
to serve.  The result is a cost matrix displaying for each cost category the detailed costs 
12 
of serving each customer class in this study. 
13

19 
Expense in detail and to assign or allocate each item to the customer classes.  The
