Q.    
Why is the Company centralizing Distribution System Operations? 
11 
A. 
The distribution grid is rapidly evolving.  One-way power flow via central 
12 
station generators is no longer the norm.  DERs continue to proliferate 
13 
across the state, and as a result power flow has become much more 
14 
complex, with a two-way flow interaction.  As regulations change and 
15 
DERs like large solar fields or aggregators are able to bid into the 
16 
wholesale market, centralized control becomes necessary to effectively 
17 
operate an increasingly complex distribution network.  Undertaking grid 
18 
modernization allows Central Hudson to continue its support of the wide 
19 
adoption of DERs within its service territory and help meet the State’s 
20 
clean energy policy.  With more devices on the system providing visibility 
21 
and control, the Company can better manage voltage variations and
