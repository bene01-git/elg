I. 
Introduction 
New York State has declared a state of emergency in response to the coronavirus 
(“COVID-19”) global pandemic rapidly spreading across the country.  The government has 
directed the closure of schools statewide and limited public gatherings.  Many businesses, 
including gyms, theaters, and casinos, have been directed to close and restaurants may only serve 
food on a delivery or take-out basis.  Other essential businesses, such as grocery stores, gas 
stations, pharmacies, and medical facilities, are operating on limited hours.  These precautions, 
while necessary for public health and safety, will financially impact residents across the State, 
including those who are paid hourly for their services.  Indeed, many residents are temporarily

II. 
Background and Discussion 
A. Postponement of Niagara Mohawk Rate Increases

signed by numerous parties.  The Joint Proposal set forth the comprehensive terms and conditions 
of a three-year rate plan for Niagara Mohawk’s electric and gas operations beginning April 1, 2018 
and continuing through March 31, 2021.5  To promote rate stability and mitigate bill impacts to 
customers, the Joint Proposal provided a gradual transition to full cost-of-service rates by phasing 
in the delivery rate increase over the term of the three-year rate plan.6  Beginning April 1, 2020, 
the Joint Proposal provides for a net revenue increase of $89.636 million for electric and $21.531

III. 
Request for Emergency Approval 
Pursuant to Article II, Section 202(6)(a) of the State Administrative Procedures Act 
(“SAPA”), National Grid requests that the Commission waive the notice requirements of the 
SAPA and approve the instant petition on an emergency basis as it is necessary for the public 
health, safety, and general welfare.  If approved, the petition will help customers offset the 
financial hardship experienced as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic and related activities.  
Absent waiver of the notice requirement, the proposals made in this petition could not be 
implemented on a timely basis. 
IV. 
Waiver of Newspaper Publication 
Niagara Mohawk has already filed revised tariff leaves with Rate Year 3 rates to become 
effective April 1 in accordance with the 2018 Niagara Mohawk Rate Order.  In addition, Niagara 
Mohawk has made the required newspaper publications.  As the rate increases are being postponed, 
and publications are expensive, Niagara Mohawk respectfully requests waiver of any further 
newspaper publication.  Niagara Mohawk will issue a press release to notify customers about the 
suspension.  In addition, Niagara Mohawk’s communications, media, and customer teams will 
work to notify customers through the website, customer interactions, and social media channels 
about the suspension and subsequent rate change.
