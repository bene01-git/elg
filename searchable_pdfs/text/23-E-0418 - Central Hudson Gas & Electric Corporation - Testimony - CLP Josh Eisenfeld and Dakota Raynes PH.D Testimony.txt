II. Purpose of Testimony 
5 
Q-8.  What is the purpose of your testimony? 
6 
A-8.  We are providing testimony to share pertinent information from research relevant 
7 
to RSG rate cases. We urge the Public Service Commission to reject any proposed rate increases 
8 
including certified gas, responsibly sourced gas, differentiated gas, or so called optimized gas 
9 
with lower emissions. Our thorough and published research into gas certification programs (or 
10 
other similar schemes) shows that none of these programs has the capability to guarantee lower 
11 
emissions that would justify passing on higher prices to consumers (the full report is included as 
12 
Exhibit__JEDR-02). Their inability to guarantee lower emissions stems from four important 
13 
details.  
14 
The first detail is that monitors in the field consistently fail to operate and capture 
15 
emissions. Our research shows that for multiple reasons - including operator error, mechanical 
16 
error, and technological failures - monitors used by certifiers like Project Canary consistently 
17 
failed to capture pollution events.   
18 
The second detail is that there is no body that regulates or verifies that RSG certification 
19 
programs are what they claim they are or that emissions measurements are accurate and 
20 
substantiated.  
21 
The third detail is that Project Canary has no independent and peer reviewed studies to 
22 
substantiate the claims that their monitors consistently work as described in the field or, when 
23

5 
 
working, can capture acute but prolonged emissions events that make up a substantial portion of 
1 
emissions throughout the  oil and gas supply chain.   
2 
The fourth and final detail is that while certifiers like Project Canary claim they have 
3 
independent studies (not peer reviewed) to show the efficacy of their products those studies vary 
4 
from how we have observed monitor set up and design in the field. For instance, at one site in

Project Canary and other monitors were consistently placed outside of 60 ft sound walls (see

III. Responsibly Sourced Gas and Certification 
5 
Q-10.  Based on your knowledge, experience, or observation, what does the term 
6 
Responsibly Sourced Gas (RSG) refer to, and are there any other terms that are 
7 
synonymous with it? 
8 
A-10.  There is no one definition for RSG. There are no defined levels of 
9 
“responsibility.” Project Canary’s Trustwell certification has no government endorsements, in 
10 
fact, we have only heard serious doubts about the platform's integrity from former and current 
11 
government officials as well as former employees of the company. One former employee, who 
12 
wishes to remain anonymous for safety concerns, told our research team that companies could 
13 
only receive the highest level (Platinum) certification if they agreed to purchase Project Canary 
14 
monitors. I’m not sure what that has to do with responsibility.  
15 
The term has been blurred and watered down to mean very little. And it is important to 
16 
remember that operators pay companies like Project Canary and MiQ to put their stamp of 
17 
approval on the gas. Other names that could be synonymous with RSG are certified gas, 
18 
differentiated gas, next gen gas, or so-called optimized gas. There may be others as well.
