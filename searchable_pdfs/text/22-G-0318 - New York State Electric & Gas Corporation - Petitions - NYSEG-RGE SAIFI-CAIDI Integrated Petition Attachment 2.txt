Preliminary Notice of Non-Utility Controlled Outage Events – October 2023 
Director Wisely: 
Pursuant to the terms of Appendix K of the Joint Proposal (“JP”) adopted by the State of New York 
Public Service Commission the (“Commission”) in its October 12, 2023 Order Adopting Joint Proposal, 
in the above-captioned proceedings, New York State Electric & Gas Corporation (“NYSEG”) and 
Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation (“RG&E, jointly the Companies”)  are required to provide 
preliminary notice and supporting documentation for annual report exclusions, other than major storms to 
the Director of the Office of Resilience and Emergency Preparedness (OREP) for review within 45 days 
of the event.  In keeping with these requirements, the Companies.hereby provide notice of the attached 
supporting documentation for SAIFI and CAIDI non-utility controlled outage exclusions occurring during 
the month of October, 2023.  
In order to safeguard confidential information contained in the attachment, the confidential version will 
be transmitted to the Commission’s Records Access Officer, along with a request for confidential 
treatment. 
If you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me at the address or telephone below.
