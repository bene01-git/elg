to its electric and gas tariff schedules on January 
31st, 2025, proposing to increase its annual electric 
and gas delivery revenues for the 12-month period 
ending December 31st, 2026.

electric delivery revenues by approximately $1.6 
billion, which represents an 11.4 percent increase in 
total revenues, and it proposes to increase its 
natural gas delivery revenues by approximately $440 
million, which represents a 19.1 percent increase in 
total revenues.

proposed changes on any particular customer class 
would vary based upon revenue allocation and rate 
design.  Under New York State law, the Public Service 
Commission must consider a utility's proposal and it
