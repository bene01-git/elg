Q. 
Has the Commission historically employed “tolerance bands” 
1 
around the system rate of return in developing class revenue 
2 
responsibilities? 
3 
A. 
Yes.  Based on past practice, class revenue responsibility has 
4 
been measured with respect to a ±10% tolerance band around the 
5 
total system rate of return.  Classes would not be considered 
6 
“surplus” or “deficient” if the class ECOS rate of return falls 
7 
within this tolerance band.  Classes that fall outside this 
8 
range would be either surplus or deficient by the revenue 
9 
amount, including appropriate state and federal income taxes, 
10 
necessary to bring the realized return to the upper or lower 
11 
limit of the band.  We propose to continue this practice in 
12 
this case.

23 
for example, to differentiate distribution-demand related costs

3 
A. 
During the process of functionalization, all costs are 
4

11 
any customer’s particular level of usage.
