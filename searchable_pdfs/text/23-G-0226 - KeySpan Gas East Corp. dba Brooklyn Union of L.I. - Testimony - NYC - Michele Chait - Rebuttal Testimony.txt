PURPOSE OF REBUTTAL TESTIMONY 
1 
Q. 
WHAT IS THE PURPOSE OF YOUR REBUTTAL TESTIMONY? 
2 
A. 
I have reviewed the Utility Intervention Unit (“UIU”) Direct Testimony of Danielle Panko 
3 
and the Prepared Testimony of the Department of Public Service Staff Gas Rates Panel.  
4 
My rebuttal testimony addresses 1) cost allocation; 2) bill impacts; and 3) the non-firm 
5 
demand response (“NFDR”) customer gas cost reconciliation proposal.   
6 
Q. 
ARE YOU SPONSORING ANY EXHIBITS WITH YOUR TESTIMONY?  
7 
A. 
No.  
8 
Q. 
PLEASE SUMMARIZE YOUR CONCLUSIONS AND RECOMMENDATIONS.  
9 
A. 
I recommend that due to the resulting cost shifts, UIU’s proposal for each service 
10 
classification to receive a uniform rate increase not be accepted.   
11 
 
I recommend that the Companies be directed to conduct a nuanced analysis of potential 
12 
rate designs for each customer class, with the objectives of mitigating adverse bill impacts 
13 
and ensuring that no customers are unduly impacted.   
14 
 
Due to cost shift concerns, I recommend that over-/under-collection of gas costs from firm 
15 
and NFDR customers be modified so that it is not accomplished via a single volumetric 
16 
rate.

Q. 
DO YOU SUPPORT THIS PROPOSAL? 
1 
A. 
No. It is worth noting that the interclass revenue allocation proposed by the Companies 
2 
already was tempered to soften the impacts on those classes that are being subsidized.  The 
3 
UIU approach would under-allocate costs to under-contributing firm service classifications 
4 
and over-allocate costs to over-contributing firm service classifications, thereby 
5 
perpetuating existing interclass subsidies.    
6 
Q. 
DO YOU HAVE ANY OTHER OBSERVATIONS REGARDING THIS 
7 
PROPOSAL? 
8 
A. 
Yes, I have two.  First, across firm service classifications, UIU’s proposed methodology 
9 
effectively allocates the distribution revenue increase per the delivery revenue at present 
10 
rates rather than according to the Companies’ cost of service studies in the instant 
11 
proceeding. 
12 
Q. 
WHAT IS THE SECOND OBSERVATION? 
13 
A. 
Although Ms. Panko requests that the Companies perform ECOS studies using a 100% 
14 
demand classification of distribution mains costs, such a study does not appear to form the 
15 
basis of UIU’s revenue allocation proposal. In any event, for the reasons cited in my direct 
16 
testimony, I support the Companies use of the minimum distribution system to allocate the 
17 
cost of distribution mains, as does the Staff Rates Panel.
