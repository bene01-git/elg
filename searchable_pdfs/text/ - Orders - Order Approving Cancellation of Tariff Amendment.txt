INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On March 1, 2019, The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a 
National Grid NY (KEDNY or the Company) filed proposed tariff 
revisions to its gas tariff schedule, P.S.C. 12, to revise the 
paperless billing credit for customers that choose paperless 
billing in accordance with the Commission’s 2016 Rate Order in 
this proceeding.1  Subsequently, the Company filed a supplement, 
by which it proposed to withdraw this tariff amendment.  By this 
Order, the Commission grants the Company’s request to withdraw

BACKGROUND 
 
 
The 2016 Rate Order required the Company to implement 
a paperless billing credit of $0.49 per service period for all 
customers who receive their bill electronically.  The 2016 Rate 
Order also allowed for KEDNY to make a tariff filing to revise 
the credit to reflect changes in its electronic billing options.  
KEDNY’s March 1, 2019 filing sought to revise the credit to 
reflect changes as contemplated.  Specifically, KEDNY proposed 
to adjust the paperless bill credit from $0.49 per service 
period to $0.41 per service period to reflect the increased 
electronic billing system costs.  On May 3, 2019, KEDNY 
submitted proof of publication of the proposed tariff revisions. 
 
 
On April 30, 2019, KEDNY filed proposed tariffs 
constituting a major rate change in Case 19-G-0309.2  Following 
discussions with Staff, the Company proposes to address this 
issue in the current rate proceeding in Case 19-G-0309.  
Accordingly, on July 12, 2019, KEDNY filed a supplement to 
implement the cancellation of the previously filed tariff 
amendment (see Appendix).  KEDNY requests a waiver of the 
requirement of newspaper publication of the change proposed by 
the supplement. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
As KEDNY has a currently pending major rate 
proceeding, it is appropriate to consider changes to the 
paperless billing credit in the context of that proceeding.  
Accordingly, the Commission grants KEDNY’s request to withdraw

proposed by the supplement is waived. 
3. This proceeding is closed.
