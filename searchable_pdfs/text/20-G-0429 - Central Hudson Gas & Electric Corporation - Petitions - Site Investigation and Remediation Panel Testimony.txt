7

9 
The NYSDEC Remediation Division administers the laws and regulations 
10 
pertaining to the investigation and remediation process for all 
11

12

13

14

15

16 
The NYSDEC regulates programs for Central Hudson sites, under a 
17 
NYSDEC Consent Order, the Brownfield Cleanup Program, or hazardous 
18 
waste storage permits.  The SIR process applicable to each respective 
19 
site, however, is carried out in accordance with a NYSDEC-issued 
20 
guidance document entitled “DER-10/Technical Guidance for Site 
21 
Investigation and Remediation” or provisions within the USEPA Resource
