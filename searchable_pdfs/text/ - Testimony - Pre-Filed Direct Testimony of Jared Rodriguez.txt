Is there anything you would like to bring to the Commission's attention 
relating to the fact that the Buildings are purchasing gas as TC Service 
customers of National Grid?

limiting Estates' ability to achieve conservation goals and to better serve

for well over two decades or more.

DISCUSSION 
Q. 
What concerns does Estates have regarding the future of TC 
Service? 
A. 
Estates entered into an agreement, dated December 19, 2014, with 
Brooklyn Union ("6M5 Agreement") for transportation services under the 
SC-18 6M5 Rate. The term of the 6M5 Agreement ends on March 31, 
2017. Although the term of the 6M5 Agreement is automatically extended 
on a month to month basis, that "evergreen" provision is subject to the fact 
that it can be terminated by Brooklyn Union on 30 days' advance written 
notice. Brooklyn Union has informed us that it is uncertain how much 
longer it will be offering TC Service. For those reasons, we have no 
assurance from Brooklyn Union that the current 6M5 Rate under which 
Estates is taking service will be continued beyond the calendar year 2017. 
How does this disadvantage Estates? 
Current natural gas prices are at historic lows. Estates would like to be 
able to procure gas at the currently prevailing low prices but is not in a

Please comment on the relative economics as between Brooklyn Union 
and 6M5 Rate customers. 
The 6M5 Rate confers a significant advantage upon Brooklyn Union by 
providing flexibility which enables it to maintain gas throughput at a 
revenue level commensurate with SC-3 Firm Service. Conversely, the 
cost for such transportation services (and therefore the overall cost of fuel 
procurement) is significantly higher for Estates than it is for a similarly 
situated SC-3 Firm Service customer. In addition, the alternate fuel, No. 2 
fuel oil (required by Brooklyn Union Tariff Leaf: 381.2 during low 
temperature conditions) is currently trading at a multiple of 2 to 3 times 
the value of delivered natural gas when the value of each fuel is calculated 
on a cost per therm basis.
