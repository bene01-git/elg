5 
A.

6 
time in response to State policy. We will manage this transition 
7 
and continue to provide safe, reliable and resilient service to 
8 
our 1.1 million existing customers. We will explain how our Gas 
9

10

12

14

6 
Q. 
Why is the Company’s gas infrastructure important to its 
7 
customers and its service territory? 
8 
A. 
The Company’s gas infrastructure is essential for ensuring a 
9 
reliable and safe energy supply, supporting economic stability 
10 
while also preparing for future energy transitions. By 
11 
maintaining and replacing this infrastructure as needed, the 
12 
Company can reduce methane emissions, which aligns with broader 
13 
environmental goals and helps pave the way for integrating more 
14 
sustainable energy sources in the future.

Q. 
Please provide a high-level overview of the Company’s natural gas 
16 
transmission and distribution system. 
17 
A. 
A gas distributor since 1823, Con Edison currently provides 
18 
natural gas service to more than 1.1 million customers in 
19 
Manhattan, the Bronx, parts of Queens, and Westchester County.  
20 
Con Edison manages a large, complex underground natural gas 
21 
transmission and distribution system.  This system contains 
22 
approximately 4,450 total miles of gas main with approximately 
23 
381,000 service pipes that transport more than 339 million 
24 
dekatherms of natural gas each year. And on January 20th, 2024, we 
25 
broke a record in peak throughput on the system demonstrating the
