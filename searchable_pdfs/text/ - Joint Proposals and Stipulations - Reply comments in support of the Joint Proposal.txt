Margot Spindelman
June 14, 2021
Reply Letter to State of New York Department of Public Service Staff Statement in Support of the 
Joint Proposal in Case 19-G-0309 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, 
Charges, Rules and Regulations of The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY 
for Gas Service Case 
19-G-0310 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, Charges, Rules and 
Regulations of KeySpan Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid for Gas Service
In Staff’s June 2, 2021 Statement in Support of the Joint Proposal (pg 12), they refer to the Long-
Term Capital Capacity Projects, including the vaporizers at the Greenpoint LNG facility, saying, “The 
Companies’ asserted that these projects are necessary.” They continue: “Staff also included these projects 
in its recommended capital expenditure budgets. This reflects Staff’s judgment that the projects are indeed 
needed.” 
Also, when referring to the new position of “Independent” Consultant (pg 12), they specify that the 
Independent Consultant will be working “at the direction of Staff.”
When Staff claims the Independent Consultant’s investigation will be at their direction, and Staff has 
already supported The Companies’ claims of need in litigation, how are we to trust that this Independent 
Consultant will come to any conclusion other than one that supports the Long Term Capital Capacity 
Projects? The Consultant is independent of the Companies, but under the authority of Staff, who have 
publicly supported the Companies’ claims. This independence seems a distinction without a difference, 
and it is hard to have faith that any conclusion other than a confirmation of need would be forthcoming.
Staff also notes (pg 16), pointedly, that, since they are not responsible for permitting nor licensing of the 
Long-Term Capital Capacity Projects (“the Commission’s adoption of the Joint Proposal only authorizes 
cost recovery through rate mechanisms. Indeed, other state agencies and entities do have permitting 
authority regarding the Long-Term Capital Capacity Projects, including the DEC, and various agencies 
of the City of New York.”), their role is only to authorize cost recovery. Essentially, they say “not on us,” 
even though they are the ultimate gateway through which these projects must pass. They state that this 
“Joint Proposal presents the Companies with aggressive targets, thus at least setting KEDNY and KEDLI 
on a path to avoiding the need for similar infrastructure in the future.” 
“At least.”
This is a jaw-dropping abdication of responsibility in THIS three year (+ one year stay-out) period. 
The climate timeline says we have less than a decade left to avoid the worst impacts of a heating planet, 
and instead we hear this agency, tasked with supporting the State’s climate law, give this pallid offering 
toward some sort of future achievement. We simply cannot afford to continue an expansion of fossil fuel 
infrastructure, justifying it with the hope that by turning the ocean liner toward an unspecified future path 
we will avoid another iceberg. Quoting Asad Rehman, “They can see that our house is on fire, and yet, 
frankly, they’re still discussing what color to paint the door.”
