Dear Secretary Philips: 
 
 
Attached please find an emergency petition by Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a 
National Grid (“Company”) seeking an additional one-month waiver of the approved electric and 
gas rate increases such that the increases would take effect on August 1, 2020 instead of July 1, 
2020.  To implement this request, the Company also seeks a temporary waiver of Rule 57.2 of the 
Company’s Electric Tariff PSC 220, which provides for an interim revenue decoupling mechanism 
(“RDM”) adjustment if the RDM reconciliation in any month exceeds a certain threshold.  Finally,  
Niagara Mohawk requests an additional one-month delay in the implementation of the updated, 
reduced low-income discount levels such that the new discount would also take effect on August 1, 
2020.   
 
National Grid submits that this emergency petition will help with the financial hardships 
experienced by our customers and communities.

Petition of National Grid for Approval on an Emergency Basis to Further Postpone 
Approved Rate Increases and the Reduction to the Low-Income Credit to Lessen the 
Economic Impact of COVID-19 on Customers 
I. 
Request for Waiver 
In the wake of the COVID-19 outbreak, Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a 
National Grid (“Niagara Mohawk” or “Company”) and its downstate New York affiliates, The 
Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (“KEDNY”) and KeySpan Gas East 
Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“KEDLI”),1 took several steps to mitigate the impact of the 
pandemic on customers.  Among other things, National Grid filed an emergency petition on March 
24, 2020 seeking to (i) suspend until July 1, 2020 the gas and electric rate increases previously 
approved for Niagara Mohawk in Cases 17-E-0238 et al.;2 (ii) postpone Niagara Mohawk’s annual

National Grid’s petition was granted by a one-Commissioner Order dated March 25, 2020.4  
The March 2020 Order directed Niagara Mohawk to (i) file revised tariff leaves on not less than 
30 days’ notice to become effective on July 1, 2020, (ii) file revised electric and gas low-income 
credit tariff statements on not less than three days’ notice to become effective on July 1, 2020, and 
(iii) provide customers with notification prior to new rates going into effect on July 1, 2020.   
While New York is proceeding with the gradual reopening of its economy, and the future 
looks brighter every day, Niagara Mohawk recognizes the financial hardship that many customers 
still bear from the unprecedented pandemic.  To that end, Niagara Mohawk submits this petition 
for an additional one-month waiver of the approved electric and gas rate increases such that the 
increases would take effect on August 1, 2020 instead of July 1, 2020.  In addition, Niagara 
Mohawk requests an additional one-month delay in the implementation of the updated, reduced 
low-income discount levels such that the new discount would also take effect on August 1, 2020.  
Given that the tariff leaves would need to be filed by May 29, 2020 pursuant to the March 2020 
Order and the notification to customers would also need to be submitted to the Company’s billing 
department at that time to ensure inclusion in June bills, Niagara Mohawk respectfully requests 
that the Commission approve the petition on an emergency basis.

The Company proposes to recover the electric and gas revenue shortfalls resulting from the 
postponement in the same manner approved by the Commission in the March 2020 Order.  
Specifically, Niagara Mohawk will file revised tariff leaves with an effective date of August 1, 
2020 (instead of July 1, 2020), no later than July 1, 2020, which would provide electric and gas 
base delivery rates designed to recover the Rate Year 35 revenue increases over the remaining eight 
months (instead of nine months) of Rate Year 3.  Consistent with the March 2020 Order, Niagara 
Mohawk will design electric and gas base delivery rates for this eight-month period by dividing 
the total net revenue for each service classification scheduled to receive an increase in Rate Year 
36 by the forecast of volumes to be delivered between August 1, 2020 and March 31, 2021.  In this 
way, customers will continue to avoid a rate increase for four months and Niagara Mohawk will 
be made whole by collecting the Rate Year 3 increase over the period August 1, 2020 through 
March 31, 2021.   
Niagara Mohawk will not design rates to collect any more than the annual revenues 
approved in its rate plan; however, due to compressing the twelve months of revenue increases 
into eight months now, the rates will have to be proportionately higher.7  Notwithstanding, Niagara 
Mohawk submits that this continues to be a reasonable outcome that benefits customers under 
these unique circumstances. 
In addition, Niagara Mohawk requests a temporary waiver of Rule 57.2 of the Company’s 
Electric Tariff PSC 220, which provides for an interim revenue decoupling mechanism (“RDM”)

adjustment if the RDM reconciliation in any month exceeds a certain threshold.  Specifically, Rule 
57.2 states: 
57.2 
If in any month, the total of the cumulative monthly reconciliation balance, 
based on monthly actual billed delivery service revenue and an allocation of the 
[annual target revenues] for the applicable RDM reconciliation period to the months 
in that period, for any of the Reconciliation Groups in Rule 57.1.2 above, is greater 
than 1.5% of the Company’s [annual target revenues] for that Reconciliation Group 
for that year, the Company will file an interim RDM adjustment for each affected 
Reconciliation Group for the remainder of the calendar year.  
 
The Company is requesting waiver of this provision to provide flexibility in the application 
of this rule for the remainder of the RDM reconciliation year (twelve months ending March 2021) 
to allow the Company to assess whether an interim adjustment is appropriate and necessary on a 
month to month basis in the event that changes in customer usage trigger an interim RDM 
surcharge or sur-credit. 
For similar reasons, Niagara Mohawk proposes to delay implementation of the updated, 
reduced low-income discount levels, until August 1, 2020, and Niagara Mohawk, KEDNY, and 
KEDLI propose to continue the waiver of tariff fees in connection with the suspension of 
collections activities and non-essential services such as manual meter reads, for so long as National 
Grid’s suspension of collections activities remains in effect.   
II. 
Request for Emergency Approval 
Pursuant to Article II, Section 202(6)(a) of the State Administrative Procedures Act 
(“SAPA”), Niagara Mohawk requests that the Commission waive the notice requirements of the 
SAPA and approve the instant petition on an emergency basis as it is necessary for the public 
health, safety, and general welfare.  If approved, the petition will help customers offset the 
financial hardship experienced as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic and related activities.
