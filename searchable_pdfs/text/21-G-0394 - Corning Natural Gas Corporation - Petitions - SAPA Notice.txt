Pursuant to the provisions of the State Administrative Procedure Act (SAPA), NOTICE is 
hereby given of the following proposed rule: 
1. 
Proposed action:
The Public Service Commission is considering whether to approve or reject or modify, in whole 
or in part, a proposal filed by Corning Natural Gas Corporation to make various changes in the 
rates, charges, rules and regulations contained in its schedule for gas service – P.S.C. No. 7.  The 
proposed rate year is the 12 months ending June 30, 2023 and the Company is proposing that 
rates be set for two additional 12-month periods ending June 30, 2024 and June 30, 2025. 
2. 
Statutory authority under which rule is proposed:
Public Service Law, Sections 65(1) and 66(12). 
3. 
Subject of rule:
Natural gas rate case. 
4. 
Purpose of rule:
To consider whether to increase annual gas revenues by $5,758,453 (or 20.43 percent on an 
overall bill basis) in the 12 months ending June 30, 2023, by $1,643,355 (or 4.85 percent) in the 
12 months ending June 30, 2024, and by $989,061 (or 2.78 percent) in the 12 months ending June 
30, 2025 or, alternatively, to increase rates in all three years by a levelized amount of $3,591,855 
(or 11.14 percent) in each year, and to consider increases for certain limited expenditures for the 
12-month periods ending June 30, 2026 and June 30, 2027.

Rate Case by Corning Natural Gas Corporation 
5. 
Terms of Rule (Substance of Proposed Action): 
Corning Natural Gas Corporation has filed proposed tariff amendments to produce an increase 
in annual gas revenues of $5,758,453 (or 20.43 percent on an overall bill basis) in the 12 
months ending June 30, 2023, by $1,643,355 (or 4.85 percent) in the 12 months ending June 
30, 2024, and by $989,061 (or 2.78 percent) in the 12 months ending June 30, 2025 or, 
alternatively, to increase rates in all three years by a levelized amount of $3,591,855 (or 11.14 
percent) in each year.  Corning suggests possible additional scenarios in which certain 
increased costs would be permitted to be recovered in rates for the 12-month periods ending 
June 30, 2026 and June 30, 2027, thereby reducing the need for the Public Service 
Commission, the Company and other parties to devote time and resources to more frequent 
rate filings.
