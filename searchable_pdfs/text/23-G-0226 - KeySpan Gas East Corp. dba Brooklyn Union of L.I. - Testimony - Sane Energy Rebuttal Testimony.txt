I. INTRODUCTION AND OBJECTIVE
Q. Please state your name and occupation
A: Kim Fraczek, Director, Sane Energy Project
Q. Did you submit direct testimony in this proceeding?
A. Yes, on September 1, 2023
Q: What Testimonies are you rebutting in this Testimony?
A:
1. Staff Pipeline Safety Panel
2. Staff Gas Reliability & Supply Panel
3. Staff Policy Panel
Q. What is the objective of your rebuttal testimony?
A: The objective of this rebuttal is to address the testimony presented by the Staff Pipeline
Safety Panel of the Department of Public Service and the concerns that it raises about the
alignment of our state's actions with climate laws and commitments.
I will address Staff’s attendance to National Grid’s request for over $2Billion in Leak Prone
Pipe replacement in the Pipeline Safety Panel. I will also address Staff’s lenient
recommendations to the Greenpoint Energy Center Infrastructure.
This rebuttal is particularly timely as it coincides with the final day of Climate Week 2023,
aimed at galvanizing action towards a sustainable future1.

Q: What is your purpose in submitting this rebuttal testimony?
A: My purpose in submitting this rebuttal testimony is to appeal to our state government and
regulators to be conscientious objectors to National Grid’s proposal, and adhere to the global
echo to stop investment in fossil fuels. As stated in the closing remarks of the United Nation
Climate Ambition Summit just two days ago, be the “first-doers and first-movers to align our
strategies and portfolios with a 1.5 degrees strategy”. Given this nation built out about 1.5million
miles of the 3 million miles of gas pipelines in a decade in the mid-20th century2, we can
certainly do anything we put our minds to including retiring this aged-system instead of
replacing it invest in Thermal Energy Networks and Energy Efficiency as stated in our Initial
Testimony.

KEDNY’S GREENPOINT INFRASTRUCTURE PROGRAM, AS WELL
AS THE GREENPOINT MASTER PLAN EXECUTION PROJECT.
Q: Do you agree with Staff assessments of the importance for further investments in LNG
facilities?
A: No. On Page 21 in the Testimony of Staff Gas Reliability & Supply Panel, they state that
“The Panel supports the Companies’ proposed capital programs associated with the LNG
facilities due to the very significant amount of peak day supply that each LNG facility provides
for their respective service areas and the fact that other supply sources cannot be used as a
substitute for the LNG facilities due to ongoing capacity constraints in each Company’s service

Q: Do you agree with the Staff recommendations to decrease spending on Greenpoint
Energy Center?
A: No, I implore Staff to adopt an even more assertive approach in slashing funding to

Q: What do you recommend?
A: I strongly recommend that we seize this pivotal moment in time to transcend the isolated
