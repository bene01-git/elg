Response: 
 
1. 
Please see the Company’s response to DPS-029 for the latest KEDNY TWU Local 101 
and IBEW Local 3 union contracts supporting the associated wage increases used in the 
Rate Year labor forecast. 
2. 
The KEDNY TWU Local 101 contract expires on October 15, 2019   and given the 
timing, it was reasonable to assume that the new bargained contract would, at a 
minimum, include a 1.5% wage increase for October 2019 as the terms of the recently 
expired contract included wage increases of 1.5% each April and October of 1.5%.  
Therefore, the Company included an assumption of a 1.5% wage increase for October 
2019 in its Rate Year labor forecast.   
Notwithstanding the foregoing, on April 27, 2019 National Grid and TWU Local 101 
negotiated a new three-year contract.  The contract term, which commences on October 
16, 2019 and ends on October 15, 2022, includes a wage increase of 2.75% effective 
October 16, 2019, a 3.0% wage increase effective on October 16, 2020 and a 3.0% wage 
increase effective on October 16, 2021.  Please see Attachment 1 for the latest 
memorandum of agreement between National Grid and TWU Local 101 dated April 27, 
2019.    The increases resulting from this memorandum will be included in the 
Company’s Corrections and Updates filing.
