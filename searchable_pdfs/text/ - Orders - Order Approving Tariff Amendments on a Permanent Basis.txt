(Issued and Effective June 23, 2025) 
 
 
BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On February 25, 2025, The Brooklyn Union Gas Company 
d/b/a National Grid NY (KEDNY) and KeySpan Gas East Corporation 
d/b/a National Grid (KEDLI) (together, the Companies) filed 
tariff amendments to their tariff schedules P.S.C. Nos. 12 – Gas 
and P.S.C. No. 1 – Gas, respectively, as required by the

BACKGROUND 
 
 
The 2024 Rate Order adopted the terms of the Joint 
Proposal, with minor modification and corrections, and 
established a three-year rate plan for gas service for the 
period beginning April 1, 2024, through March 31, 2027.2  The 
approved rate plans provide for a RY 2 revenue increase of 
$287.52 million for KEDNY and $161.14 million for KEDLI. 
 
 
The 2024 Rate Order directed the Companies to file, on 
not less than 30 days’ notice, tariff amendments, as necessary, 
to effectuate the RY 2 terms to become effective on 
April 1, 2025, on a temporary basis.  The amendments set forth 
in the compliance filings would not become effective on a 
permanent basis until approved by the Commission.

TARIFF FILINGS 
 
 
On February 25, 2025, the Companies filed amendments 
to their tariff schedules to implement the RY 2 rates as 
required by the Commission’s 2024 Rate Order.  The tariff 
amendments also include a minor housekeeping revision to KEDLI’s 
Leaf No. 78, in P.S.C. No. 1 – Gas, to correct a typographical 
error, which erroneously notated section “I” instead of “(c)” in 
the definitions section list for normal heating degree days.  
The February 25, 2025 filings became effective on a temporary 
basis on April 1, 2025. 
 
 
The Companies complied with the requirements of 
newspaper publication pursuant to Public Service Law (PSL) 
§66(12)(b) and Title 16 of the New York Codes, Rules and 
Regulations (NYCRR) §720-8.1 for their February 25, 2025 
filings. 
The Appendix to this Order contains a list of the 
tariff amendments the Companies have filed and the dates of 
newspaper publication.  In the 2024 Rate Order, the Commission 
stated that parties wishing to comment on the tariff amendments 
may do so by filing such comments with the Secretary to the 
Commission within ten days of service of the tariff amendments.3  
No comments were filed in response to the Companies’ compliance 
filings. 
 
LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), and 66(1) and (12), the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the proposed tariff 
leaves, as well as modify, reject or approve such filed tariffs 
to ensure that the rates and services governed by those tariffs 
are just and reasonable and otherwise consistent with law.  As 
such, the Commission has the legal authority to review and

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The Companies’ proposed tariff amendments properly 
reflect the directives of the 2024 Rate Order, including the 
rates and charges necessary to effectuate RY 2.  Further, the 
proposed revision to KEDLI’s Leaf No. 78, in P.S.C. No. 1 – Gas, 
to correct a typographical error, is housekeeping in nature.  
Therefore, the Commission authorizes the tariff amendments filed 
on February 25, 2025, and listed in the Appendix, to become 
effective on a permanent basis as of the date of issuance of 
this Order. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by The Brooklyn Union 
Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY and KeySpan Gas East 
Corporation d/b/a National Grid, and listed in the Appendix to 
this Order, are authorized to become effective on a permanent 
basis upon the date of issuance of this Order. 
2. These proceedings are continued.
