Q. 
Please provide an overview of the typical 
7 
management and operations audit process. 
8 
A. 
The Commission first initiates a proceeding that 
9 
provides for the issuance of a Request for 
10 
Proposals (RFP).  The RFP details the scope of 
11 
work and expected audit schedule.  The 
12 
Commission selects a consultant to conduct the 
13 
audit.  The chosen consultant, the Department, 
14 
and the utility or utilities subject to the 
15 
audit then enter into a multi-party agreement 
16 
that governs the conduct of the audit.  The 
17 
consultant develops a work plan, interviews 
18 
company personnel, performs its analysis, and 
19 
submits an audit report to Staff.  After Staff 
20 
review, the Commission releases the report to 
21 
the public. 
22

A.  When the Commission issues a management and 
1 
operations audit report, PSL §66(19)(b) requires 
2 
the audited utilities to submit their proposed 
3 
plan to implement the audit recommendations to 
4 
the Commission.  The Commission may require the 
5 
utility to modify its implementation plan.  Upon 
6 
the Commission’s approval, or approval with 
7 
modification, the utility’s implementation plan 
8 
becomes enforceable.  Staff is responsible for 
9 
monitoring the utility’s implementation of the 
10 
audit recommendations, pursuant to the 
11 
Commission-approved implementation plan.  
12 
Utilities submit regular updates on the 
13 
implementation of audit recommendations and must 
14 
provide Staff with evidence that demonstrates 
15 
implementation.  Staff reviews this information 
16 
and determines if each recommendation was 
17 
implemented per the approved implementation 
18 
plan.  The implementation status of audit 
19 
recommendations generally falls into one of 
20 
three categories: In Progress; Pending Review, 
21 
which is reported as complete by the utility but 
22 
under review by Staff; and Completed.
