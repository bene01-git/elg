The additional scope of WFP cloud effort will enable the workforce planning framework within

implementation which add further efficiencies to users.  They will also improve data archival and 
seeding processes for budget and forecast scenarios.   
 
Justification Summary:  
 
ePBCS is the first of several Oracle Cloud Finance implementations.  Defining standard data 
structures results in a significant increase in user experience, transparency across tools, and a 
reduction effort required to implement each subsequent tool.  Doing this project now eliminates a 
significant amount of future rework and retrofit, while providing immediate increases in user 
experience, cost savings, and transparency in reporting and analysis.   
 
The EPBCS implementation and the additional scope of WFP Cloud, eDMCS, and data 
governance phases of the project will: 
 
• Provide capability to leverage actual data when developing budgets and forecasts to 
generate analysis and trending that will reduce or eliminate hours activities that do not add 
value, such as: 
o Data transformation and manipulation, 
o External spreadsheet modeling, 
o Multiple iterations of effort to achieve relatively low impact reductions. 
• Enable meaningful budget to actual analysis by being able to understand the root cause of 
variances, which are complicated today by mappings and data movements 
• Enable users to spend more time focusing on analysis and providing insights to the business 
teams 
• Enable quarterly forecasting in a single tool which will lead to eventual trending and 
analytics 
• Reduce the level of effort required to produce budgets and forecasts 
• Reduce the effort required to produce reports and analysis 
• Reduce the effort required to maintain multiple data structures across multiple systems 
• Enable the organization to transition other applications to Oracle Cloud more easily

Summary of Financial Benefits (if applicable) and Costs:  
 
o This enhancement phase has no on-going additional O&M costs 
o Benefits and costs relating to total Budget Systems Enhancement Program: 
 
Ongoing annual software cost of $165K per year (through Oracle License and 
Maintenance Agreement). 
 
FTE reduction of 1 per year beginning in 2020 through 2024 for a total reduction of 4 
FTE.  Each FTE represents $100K per year in cost reduction.  The total cumulative cost 
reduction is $1million.   
 
Elimination of the costs for ongoing hardware/software upgrades, as well reducing the 
resources required for testing.  Typical upgrade cycles can cost approximately 
$1million.  While testing will still be required, the elimination of hardware purchases 
and software development costs will be eliminated.  Hardware costs have typically been 
approximately $300K and software and data migration resources of at least $500K over 
a six-month to one-year period.
