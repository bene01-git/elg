A. 
Hydrofluorocarbon (HFC) refrigerants used in C&I building refrigeration systems are 
1 
highly potent, “super” GHGs. They have an outsized environmentally deleterious effect 
2 
on climate change because of their chemical composition and relatively short 
3 
atmospheric lifetimes. These chemicals frequently leak from C&I refrigeration systems 
4 
and into the atmosphere. Grocery stores alone leak an average of 25 percent of their total 
5 
refrigerant charges, or the equivalent of approximately 2.5 million pounds of carbon 
6 
dioxide (CO2) per year from an average store. CO2 is the primary GHG emitted into the 
7 
atmosphere from human activity, accounting for 80 percent of all GHGs. 
8 
Recommendation: Offer a refrigerant management initiative within the C&I 
9 
energy efficiency portfolio to capture both energy and GHG savings. Expanding 
10 
refrigerant management in O&R’s energy efficiency program portfolio would help the 
11 
utility achieve its current energy savings goals. A dedicated refrigerant management 
12 
program could comprise incentives, contractor training, and technical assistance in (1) 
13 
refrigerant leak detection and repair in existing systems, (2) converting existing systems 
14 
to less harmful “drop-in” refrigerants, and (3) promoting natural refrigerants in new 
15 
systems. O&R can use the experience of Efficiency Vermont as a framework for 
16 
designing successful programs, developing measure characterizations for energy and 
17 
direct GHG savings, and evolving to include a measurable, achievable GHG metric. 
18

Q. 
What is the scope of traditional refrigerants in the general economy, and what role do 
10 
they play in contributing to GHGs and / or the environment generally?  
11 
A. 
These substances are used in applications nationwide—from cold storage of food and 
12 
other perishables to manufacturing applications, to water and space heating and cooling 
13

14 
manufactured refrigerant chemicals such as hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), which were the 
15

16

17

18

19

20

21 
impact of these chemicals is of concern because all HVACR systems that use refrigerants 
22

23 
potent GHGs directly into the atmosphere. Certain sectors, particularly grocery stores and 
24 
food processing companies, have large amounts of refrigerant and long piping runs; their

16

17 
removing, disposing of, and replacing high-GWP refrigerants with lower-GWP 
18

19 
efficiency natural refrigerants in new refrigeration equipment.
