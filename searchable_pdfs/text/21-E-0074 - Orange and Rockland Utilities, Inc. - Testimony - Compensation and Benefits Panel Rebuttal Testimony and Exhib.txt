In response to DPS-12-378, the Company stated, “[the] response to DPS 6-295, Question 2, 
included the Operating Budget with the 20 Key Performance Measures under the broad 
Customer Service Goals category rather than Financial Goals because they provide direct 
benefits to the customers.” 
Quantify the benefits ratepayers received from the achievement of the Operating Budget goal for 
the previous four years. 
Response 
The Company’s ability to control costs on a Company-wide basis provides benefits to all 
customers.  The ATIP’s allocation of 25 percent of the award for performance against the 
Operating Budget is a tangible way for all employees to focus on controlling costs. Over the last 
three performance periods (i.e., 2018, 2019 and 2020) the actual Operating Budget results were 
$8.4 million below the budget. The incremental increase in the ATIP due to the favorable 
Operating Budget performance was approximately $500,000 over the same three-year period.
