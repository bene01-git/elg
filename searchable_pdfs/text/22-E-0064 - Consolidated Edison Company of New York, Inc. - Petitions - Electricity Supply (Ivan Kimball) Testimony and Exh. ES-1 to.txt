14

15 
day.   
16 
Options typically provide a financial benefit to 
17 
the option holder when the contracted parameters, such 
18 
as short-term price, temperature, or both, exceed 
19 
prior agreed-upon thresholds.  The premiums or 
20 
purchase costs of such options are related to the 
21 
volatility of the underlying product, the length of
