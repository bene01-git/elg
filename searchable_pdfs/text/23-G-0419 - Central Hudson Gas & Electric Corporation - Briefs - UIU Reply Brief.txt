I. INTRODUCTION 
The Utility Intervention Unit (“UIU”) of the New York State Department of State’s 
Division of Consumer Protection submits this Reply Brief to address a mischaracterization by 
Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corporation (“Central Hudson” or the “Company”) in their 
Initial Post-Hearing Brief regarding the Customer Experience Capital Stipulation.1

by the Company, Department of Public Service Staff (“Staff”), and UIU. Page one of the

Subsequently, page two of the stipulation states, “The following capital projects . . . will not be

On Page 137 of the Company’s Initial Post-Hearing Brief, the Company stated: 
[C]ertain other capital and operations projects initially sought by the 
Company were agreed to be pushed out beyond the Rate Year as part 
of the Stipulation Regarding Customer Experience Capital Projects (the

upgrades, CIS/CX emergent, Customer Bill Redesign, More Online 
Energy calculators and Kubra Replacement - Payment Experience 
vendor, eBill, Bill Presentment and Bill Print. Exhibit 515, page 2. 
(Emphasis added).  
 
In this statement, the Company represents that their proposed projects were agreed to be “pushed 
out beyond the Rate Year.” The Company’s assertion is a material mischaracterization of the 
stipulation. The Company appears to imply that there is an agreement or expectation among the 
signatories that these programs might be implemented or considered on a future date after 
conclusion of the Rate Year. No language in the stipulation supports this inference and, more 
significantly, agreements affecting future rate cases is beyond the scope of this proceeding. 
Hence, page five of the stipulation provides, “The agreements set forth in this Stipulation are 
solely for the purpose of the above-captioned proceedings and nothing in this Stipulation restricts 
any signatories to this Stipulation from taking any position or lawful action or making any filing
