II. 
LANSING AREA MORATORIUM 
1 
Q.  
Do you wish to address the Staff Panel’s observation that the moratorium for Lansing is 
2 
to remain in place despite the potential to reduce peak demand through Non-Pipes 
3 
Alternatives (“NPA”) projects in the area? 
4 
A.  
Yes.  Although the Commission-approved NPA portfolio for the Lansing area facilitates 
5 
a significant reduction in gas demand to improve distribution system operating pressures, 
6 
it does not provide sufficient demand reduction to allow for additional gas demand added 
7 
to the system.  The actual improvements to system operating pressures will be reviewed 
8 
annually as the implementation of the NPA portfolio programs progress.

3

4

VI. 
GAS COST INCENTIVE MECHANISM 
3 
Q.  
Do you wish to address the Staff Panel’s comments on the Gas Cost Incentive 
4 
Mechanism (“GCIM”)? 
5 
A. 
Yes.  We have proposed a change to the sharing mechanism percentages under the 
6 
GCIM.  Specifically, in our direct testimony we proposed that all categories included in 
7 
GCIM activities, including non-migration capacity release, off-system sales net of gas 
8 
costs, and optimization transactions, be shared at 80% customers / 20% shareholders.  
9 
The Staff Panel indicated that we did not provide adequate justification to amend the 
10 
sharing percentages, and further indicated that the current percentages have been in place 
11 
for many years and are industry standards in New York State.  
12 
Q.  
Do the Companies agree with Staff’s recommendation that the current split of sharing 
13 
percentages should remain unchanged? 
14 
A.  
No.  Due to the de-contracting of long-haul capacity assets in recent years, combined 
15 
with the ever-increasing complexity of the natural gas industry, significant resources are 
16 
expended by the Companies to bring value to the Companies’ commodity customers. 
17 
Amending the sharing percentages to an 80% customers / 20% shareholders split better 
18 
reflects the efforts required to optimize NYSEG and RG&E’s portfolios. 
19 
Q. 
Does this conclude the Panel’s Rebuttal Testimony at this time? 
20 
A. 
Yes, it does.
