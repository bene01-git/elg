Background  
Appendix K of the 2023 Joint Proposal sets forth the specific metrics, targets and 
associated NRAs as well as the reporting requirements associated with the Company’s Electric 
Reliability Performance Measures. The 2023 Joint Proposal and Appendix K state that the 
Company measures performance on a calendar year basis and, if RG&E fails to meet any of the 
established reliability performance targets during any calendar year, it is subject to the associated 
electric system reliability NRAs established in the 2023 Joint Proposal and Appendices I and K 
of the 2023 Joint Proposal.  
The 2023 Joint Proposal also recognizes that factors beyond the Company’s control (non-
utility control outages) could adversely affect the Company’s ability to meet the established 
Electric Reliability Performance Measure targets. The 2023 Joint Proposal, Appendix K defines 
Non-Utility Control Outages to include outages due to the following:

provides that the Company does not waive and expressly retains the right to petition the

targets as a result of the above-identified factors beyond the Company’s control. RG&E also may 
petition the Commission to request that Non-Utility Control Outages be exempt from SAIFI 
calculations.  
Qualifying Event  
As noted above, the 2023 Joint Proposal provides that the Company’s SAIFI reliability 
performance metrics are measured on a calendar year basis and the Company may petition to 
exempt certain events beyond the Company’s control from the calculation of SAIFI results. 
During each calendar year, RG&E identifies and tracks service interruptions occurring on its 
systems and reports the information by class of events pursuant to the definitions contained in 16 
NYCRR § 97 and identified above, including those that are outside the Company’s control 
(defined as “Class 6 Events”). RG&E monitors the impact of these events and the Company’s 
ability to meet the year-end SAIFI targets.4 
On September 26, 2025, the Company experienced an outage event, which falls within 
the definition of Non-Utility Control Outages. This outage was due to a loss of foreign utility
