tariff amendments to their gas tariff schedules, as directed by 
the Commission.1  On April 25, 2023, the Companies filed further

TARIFF FILINGS 
 
 
On March 2, 2023, the Companies filed gas tariff 
amendments to effectuate the Stay Out period rates pursuant of 
the Commission’s 2021 Rate Order.  The proposed tariff 
amendments update the delivery rates for the Stay Out period. 
The tariff amendments, provide for overall Stay Out period 
revenue increases of 0.5 percent for KEDLI and 3.9 percent for 
KEDNY.  The tariffs amendments also incorporated several 
housekeeping revisions which include: 1) updating the Companies’ 
addresses and contact information, 2) eliminating tariff 
provisions that are no longer in effect, and 3) clarifying that 
the Research and Development Surcharge is applicable to service 
class No. 17 customers.   
 
 
On April 25, 2023, the Companies filed further tariff 
amendments to update the Stay Out period revenue targets for the 
Revenue Decoupling Mechanism (RDM), the Merchant Function 
Charge, the Non-Firm Demand Response Revenue Reconciliation 
(NFRR) Surcharge, and the Newtown Creek Revenue Reconciliation. 
 
 
The Appendix to this Order sets forth a list of the 
amended tariff leaves filed by the Companies.  The Companies 
complied with the requirements of newspaper publication pursuant

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSC §§5, 65(1) and (8), and 66(1) and 
(12), the Commission has the legal authority to review the 
proposed tariff leaves, as well as modify, reject, or approve 
and make effective tariff amendments, as prescribed in this 
Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The Commission finds the Stay Out Period delivery 
rates specified in the tariff amendments are overstated and 
would result in KEDNY over collecting the revenues by $0.227 
million.  KEDNY’s delivery revenues for the Stay Out Period 
should increase by $53.6 million as the surcredit used to 
levelize Rate Year 3 delivery rates ended.2  However, KEDNY’s 
proposed delivery rates and corresponding RDM targets would 
result in a delivery revenue increase of $53.875 million, an 
over collection of $0.227 million. In a response to an 
information request, the Company also confirmed that KEDNY’s 
delivery rates were overstated by $0.227 million.  KEDNY stated 
that it would need to redesign delivery rates for all KEDNY 
customers, republish new rates in the newspaper, update and test 
the billing system, and update numerous tariff leaves to reflect 
the revised rates and revenue targets.  Instead of revising the 
delivery rates and RDM targets, the Company proposed to record a 
deferred lability for the $0.227 million with accrued carrying 
charged at the Company’ pretax Weighted Average Cost of Capital 
for future refund to customers.  The Commission agrees that
