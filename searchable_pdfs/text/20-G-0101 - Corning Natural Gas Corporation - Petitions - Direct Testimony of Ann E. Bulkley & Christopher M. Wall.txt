REGULATORY GUIDELINES AND FINANCIAL

Please describe the guiding principles to be used in establishing the cost 
13 
of capital for a regulated utility. 
14 
The United States Supreme Court’s precedent-setting Hope and Bluefield
15 
cases established the standards for determining the reasonableness of a 
16 
utility’s allowed ROE.  Among the standards established by the Court in 
17 
those cases are:  (1) consistency with the returns on equity investments in 
18 
other businesses having similar or comparable risks; (2) adequacy of the 
19 
return to support credit quality and access to capital; and (3) an 
20 
understanding that the means of arriving at a fair return are not controlling, 
21 
only that the end result leads to just and reasonable rates.3

IV.
  CAPITAL MARKET CONDITIONS 
Why is it important to analyze capital market conditions? 
1 
The ROE estimation models rely on market data that are either specific to 
2 
the proxy group, in the case of the DCF model, or the expectations of market 
3 
risk, in the case of the CAPM.  The results of the ROE estimation models 
4 
can be affected by prevailing market conditions at the time the analysis is 
5 
performed.  While the ROE that is established in a rate proceeding is 
6 
intended to be forward-looking, the analyst uses current and projected 
7 
market data, specifically stock prices, dividends, growth rates and interest 
8 
rates in the ROE estimation models to estimate the required return for the 
9
