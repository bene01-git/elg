Q.  Before explaining the Company’s O&M request, please explain 
2 
the types of costs in the O&M category. 
3 
A. 
IT O&M requests fall into two main categories: a capital 
4 
project with an O&M tail and incremental O&M for existing 
5 
projects whose costs increase over time.   
6 
Q. 
Please explain what is meant by an O&M tail. 
7 
A. 
Simply put, an O&M tail represents the ancillary costs 
8 
associated with owning and operating an asset. For example, 
9 
when the Company purchases a “capital” vehicle, to operate 
10 
that vehicle, it must have gas, have routine maintenance 
11 
performed on various systems, have a driver, and need 
12 
certain parts replacement as appropriate.  The costs for 
13 
the gas, maintenance, driver, and repairs are the O&M tail. 
14 
Q. 
Please explain the O&M tail as it relates to IT projects. 
15 
A. 
Most IT projects are capital projects as they are initially 
16 
developed and implemented.  Once in production, operating 
17 
costs, including subscriptions or licenses and maintenance 
18 
costs, are incurred.   
19 
Q. 
Do you have an example of an IT O&M tail? 
20 
A. 
For Outage Management platforms, the O&M tail is comprised 
21 
of vendor license costs, labor cost for deploying patches, 
22 
labor cost for testing (functional testing, stress testing, 
23
