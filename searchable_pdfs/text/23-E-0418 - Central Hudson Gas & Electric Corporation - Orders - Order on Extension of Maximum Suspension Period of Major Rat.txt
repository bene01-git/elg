BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On July 31, 2023, Central Hudson Gas & Electric 
Corporation (Central Hudson or the Company) filed amendments to 
its tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 15 – Electricity and P.S.C. 
No. 12 – Gas, respectively, to increase its annual electric and 
gas delivery revenues for the rate year ending June 30, 2025 
(Rate Year). 
 
 
Central Hudson requested an increase in annual 
electric delivery revenues of approximately $139.5 million 
(31.6 percent increase in base delivery revenues, or

13.3 percent increase in total system revenues).  The requested 
increase in electric delivery revenues would result in an 
average residential monthly bill increase of $30.12 
(31.9 percent increase on the delivery bill, or 16.4 percent 
increase on the total bill) for an average of 660 kWh per month 
customer. 
 
 
For its gas business, Central Hudson requested an 
increase in annual gas delivery revenues of approximately 
$41.5 million (29.8 percent increase in base delivery revenues, 
or 14.2 percent increase in total system revenues).  The 
requested increase in gas delivery revenues would result in a 
monthly bill increase of about $30.13 (29.2 percent on the 
delivery bill, or 19 percent increase on the total bill) for an 
average residential heating customer using 780 ccf per year. 
 
 
The Secretary to the Commission issued a Recommended 
Decision by the assigned Administrative Law Judges on May 1, 
2024, which would establish new electric and gas rates for the 
Rate Year.  If adopted by the Commission, the Recommended 
Decision would provide an increase in electric delivery revenues 
by $75.5 million, or by 16.7 percent; and an increase in gas 
delivery revenues by $29.6 million, or by 21.8 percent.  The 
actual bill impacts of the proposed changes on specific customer 
classes will vary based upon revenue allocation and rate design.   
 
 
By Notices issued August 11, 2023, and November 21, 
2023, the Secretary to the Commission, pursuant to authority 
delegated to her,1 suspended the captioned proceedings through 
June 30, 2024.  By this Order, the Commission extends the 
suspension of the captioned proceedings through July 31, 2024.

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
By letter dated March 14, 2024, the Company filed its 
consent to extend the maximum suspension period of these 
proceedings through July 31, 2024.  The Company requested the 
extension to accommodate a briefing schedule that provides 
additional time for parties to respond to the Recommended 
Decision issued in these proceedings.  Further, the Company made 
its request to extend the suspension period subject to a “make 
whole” provision that would keep the Company and its customers 
in the same financial position as they would be absent the 
extension.  The Company’s letter stated they understand “make 
whole” to mean that the Company would recover or refund any 
revenue under-collections or over-collections in sales revenue 
resulting from the extended suspension period.  The Company 
would calculate any revenue adjustments as the difference 
between sales revenues the Company would have billed at new 
rates compared to sales revenues at current rates during the 
period for which the suspension was extended.  The revenue 
adjustments would include all applicable surcharges and carrying 
charges and be subject to reconciliation in accordance with all 
applicable adjustment mechanisms.  In addition, the amortization 
of net deferrals reflected in the final Commission order 
approving rates in these proceedings would commence effective 
with the month of August 2024 on an earnings neutral basis. 
 
 
At this time, the effective date of the tariff leaves 
filed by the Company is further extended through July 31, 2024, 
unless otherwise ordered by the Commission, to facilitate the 
orderly conduct of these proceedings.  In addition, the Company 
will be provided a make whole for that period identified for 
what is essentially a one-month extension.

The Commission orders: 
1. The operation of the matters suspended by Notices 
issued August 11, 2023, and November 21, 2023, in these 
proceedings, is further suspended and the use of the rates, 
charges, rules and regulations stated in said Notices be further 
extended through July 31, 2024, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission. 
2. Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corporation is 
directed to file with the Commission, on not less than one day’s 
notice, on or before June 26, 2024, consecutively numbered 
supplements announcing the extension of the suspension period of 
suspended amendments. 
3. Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corporation shall be 
made whole, as defined in this Order, for the period between 
June 30, 2024, and the date the Commission sets new rates, 
charges, rules and regulations in the final decision in these 
proceedings. 
4. These proceedings are continued.
