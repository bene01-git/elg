(1) How much added revenue is in the JP to support the “Climate Leadership and Community
Protection Act (‘CLCPA’) Goals” as referred to on page 2 of the Summary of the JP?
(2) How much added revenue is in the JP to “enhance the electric system in anticipation of
transportation and building electrification” as referred to on page 2 of the Summary of the JP?
(3) How much added revenue is in the JP to “facilitate the clean energy transition,” as referred to
on page 2 of the Summary of the JP?
(4) How much added revenue is in the JP to “encourage the development of alternative heating
options, including ground- and air-source heat pumps” as referred to on page 3 of the Summary
of the JP?
(5) How much added revenue is in the JP to “enhance Information Technology systems and
processes supporting the adoption and integration of renewable energy resources,” as referred to
on page 3 of the Summary of the JP?
(6) The Joint Proposal contains rate increases to pay for hundreds of millions of dollars of
spending by Con Ed to provide for delivery of additional electric service to support building and
vehicle electrification.  Simultaneously the State has mandated closure of multiple existing
natural gas power plants during the three-year rate period, and NYISO has warned that there will
be insufficient generation to support even existing levels of usage.  What is the basis to believe
that there will be sufficient electricity generation capacity to support the building and vehicle
electrification projects in the "Interference - Electric", “New Business Capital”; “Non-Network
Resiliency Cutout” that are included in Appendix 11 of the JP.

benefits that cannot be properly tracked and segregated by the category requested.
