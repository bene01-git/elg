2 
Q. 
Why is it urgent to reduce GHG emissions?  
3 
A. 
Scientific evidence overwhelmingly demonstrates that climate change is causing 
4 
immediate, devasting impacts, and that these harms will worsen dramatically as 
5 
greenhouse gas pollution continues to rise.2 The Intergovernmental Panel on Climate 
6 
Change (“IPCC”) stated that “[h]uman influence has warmed the climate at a rate that is 
7 
unprecedented in at least the last 2000 years.”3 Cutting GHG emissions now is critical 
8 
because “there is a near-linear relationship” between human-caused GHG emissions and 
9 
related global warming, meaning that each additional increment of global warming 
10 
exacerbates changes in extreme weather events.4 Preventing the worst impacts of climate 
11 
change “requires steep and sustained reductions in fossil fuel production and use,” in 
12 
addition to measures that reduce production-cycle emissions.5 
13 
Q. 
Why is it important to reduce methane emissions?  
14 
A. 
Methane, the primary component of natural gas, is itself a potent greenhouse gas; and 
15 
when combusted, methane further contributes to warming by releasing the GHG carbon 
16 
dioxide (CO2). Compared with CO2, methane’s warming potential is ~84 times higher

14 
downstream activities (e.g., customer use of sold products, such as natural gas). 
15
