TARIFF FILINGS 
 
 
On December 2, 2024, Con Edison filed amendments to 
its electric and gas tariff schedules to implement the RY 3 
terms as required in the 2023 Rate Order.  The proposed tariff 
amendments became effective on January 1, 2025, on a temporary 
basis.  The Company complied with the requirements of newspaper 
publication pursuant to Public Service Law (PSL) §66(12)(b) and 
Title 16 of the New York Codes, Rules and Regulations (NYCRR) 
§720-8.1 for its December 2, 2024 filings.  The Appendix to this 
Order sets forth a list of the tariff amendments the Company has 
filed and the dates of newspaper publication.  In the 2023 Rate 
Order, the Commission directed parties wishing to comment on the 
tariff amendments to file such comments with the Secretary to 
the Commission within ten days of service of the tariff 
amendments.5  No comments were filed in response to Con Edison’s 
compliance filings.

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), 66(1), and 66(12), the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the proposed tariff 
leaves, as well as modify, reject, or approve such filed tariffs 
to ensure that the rates and services governed by those tariffs 
are just and reasonable and otherwise consistent with law.  As 
such, the Commission has the legal authority to review and 
approve tariff amendments on a permanent basis, as prescribed in 
this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
Con Edison’s proposed tariff amendments properly 
reflect the terms and provisions approved in the 2023 Rate 
Order.  Therefore, by this Order, the Commission authorizes the 
tariff amendments listed in the Appendix to become effective on 
a permanent basis as of the date of issuance of this Order.  
This Order does not address amendments to tariff provisions 
proposed by Con Edison in compliance with the Standby Service 
Order, and those proposed tariff provisions shall remain in 
effect on a temporary basis until acted upon by the Commission. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by Consolidated Edison 
Company of New York, Inc. and listed in the Appendix to this 
Order are authorized to become effective on a permanent basis on 
the date of issuance of this Order, except for tariff provisions 
related to Case 15-E-0751, which shall remain in effect on a 
temporary basis until acted upon by the Commission.
