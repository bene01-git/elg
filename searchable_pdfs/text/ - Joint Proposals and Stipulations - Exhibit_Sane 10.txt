Request: 
 
 
1. In SANE-20, Response 1, the Companies state that they did not obtain a certificate of 
            environmental compatibility and public need for the Metropolitan Reliability 
            Infrastructure (MRI) Project, pursuant to Article VII of the Public Service Law, “because 
            it was not required for the project.” Why was this certificate, in the Companies’ view, not 
            required for the MRI Project? 
 
      2.   In SANE-20, Response 2, the Companies state that they “did not perform a SEQRA 
            review” for the MRI Project “because it was not applicable under the circumstances.” 
            Why was SEQRA review, in the Companies’ view, “not applicable under the 
            circumstances”? What were the circumstances that, in the Companies’ view, rendered 
            SEQRA review inapplicable? 
 
       3.  In DPS-1091, Response 1c, the Companies state “Tie in work is planned to support 
            having Phases 1-4 in service by October 2020.” for the MRI Project. 
               a. Was that anticipated in service date met? 
               b. If not, when do the Companies anticipate Phases 1-4 of the MRI Project will be in 
                   service? 
 
       4.  Provide the amount of capital expenditures the Companies spent on the MRI Project in 
            2020. 
 
 
 
Response: 
 
 
1. A certificate of environmental compatibility and public need (“CECPN”) is only required 
for the siting of major utility transmission facilities under Article VII of the Public 
Service Law (“PSL”).  PSL § 120 defines a “[m]ajor utility transmission facility” and 


--- Page 2 ---
Form 103 
explicitly excludes from the definition “ any such transmission line which is located 
wholly underground in a city or wholly within the right of way of a state, county or town 
highway or village street as those terms are defined in article one of the highway law and 
article six of the village.”  As this project was within the public right of way, neither an 
Article VII nor a CECPN were required.  
 
2. 6 NYCRR § 617.5(c) lists Type II Actions that do not require further review under 
SEQRA.  This project did not require a discretionary decision by any state or local 
agency and therefore was exempt from SEQRA review under 6 NYCRR § 617.5(c)(25). 
 
3.  
a. The anticipated in-service date of October 2020 was not met. 
b. MRI Phases 1-4 were placed in service in November 2020. 
 
4. Capital expenditures for the MRI Project totaled $48.052 million for the period April-
November 2020.
