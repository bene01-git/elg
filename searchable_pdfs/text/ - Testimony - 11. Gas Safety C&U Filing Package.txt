II.   
Corrections and Updates 
1 
A. Inside Service Line Inspections Program 
2 
Q. 
Please describe the update to the forecast of costs for the Inside Service Line 
3 
Inspections Program. 
4 
A. 
The forecast of costs for the Inside Service Inspections Program has been updated to 
5 
include non-labor O&M costs to seal points of entry (inside wall penetrations) in cases 
6 
where no inside seal is observed during an inside service line inspection.  This is a program 
7 
enhancement developed in collaboration with Staff.  The annual costs for point-of-entry 
8 
sealing are approximately $0.73 million and $0.65 million for KEDNY and KEDLI, 
9 
respectively, during the Rate Year and Data Years.         
10 
   
11 
B.   Enhanced Inactive Accounts Program 
12 
Q. 
Please describe the update to the Enhanced Inactive Accounts Program forecast. 
13 
A. 
As described in the Panel’s direct testimony, the Companies proposed a phased approach 
14 
to shorten the end-to-end process for addressing inactive accounts to a 45-day process by 
15 
the end of the Rate Year and to 30 days by the end of Data Year 2.  The Companies re-
16 
evaluated current resources and the enhanced program requirements and determined that 
17 
KEDNY’s initial forecast of incremental FTEs was overstated.  Specifically, KEDNY’s 
18 
incremental FTE forecast of 95 CMS representatives is being reduced by 25 to a total of 
19 
70 CMS representatives.  The revised KEDNY FTE hiring plan is provided in KEDNY 
20 
Exhibit __ (GIOP-9CU) and Exhibit__(RRP-11) Workpapers to Exhibit (RRP-3), 
21 
Schedule 27, Workpaper 2.   
22

Q. 
Are there any changes to the non-labor forecast for the Enhanced Inactive Accounts 
1 
Program? 
2 
A. 
Yes.  The Companies’ non-labor forecasts failed to include costs of uniforms and fleet 
3

4

5

6 
are no non-labor adjustments for this program in the Data Years.

C.   
I&R Distribution Station Overpressure Protection and Control Line Survey 
9 
and GPS Mapping Programs 
10 
Q. 
Please describe the corrections to the forecast for the I&R Distribution Station 
11 
Overpressure Protection and Control Line Survey and GPS Mapping Programs. 
12 
A. 
The Companies inadvertently excluded costs for these two programs related to enhancing 
13 
overpressure protection and safety at distribution stations.  First, KEDLI’s capital forecast 
14 
for the Distribution Over Pressure Protection Program did not include the costs of adding 
15 
relief valves at distribution stations of approximately $1.2 million in each of the Rate Year, 
16 
Data Year 1, and Data Year 2.  There are no costs added for Data Year 3 because relief 
17 
valve installation will be complete by the end of Data Year 2.  Installation of relief valves 
18 
will serve as an additional layer of overpressure protection for distribution stations.    
19 
 
20 
Second, KEDNY and KEDLI’s non-labor O&M forecasts for the Control Line Survey and 
21 
GPS Mapping programs are being adjusted to include the costs of reviewing as-built 
22 
transmission station drawings against the physical facilities to ensure the accuracy of the 
23

1

2

3 
2.  There are no costs in Data Year 3 because the document review will be complete by the
