Introduction
By this letter, Sane Energy Project (“Sane”) respectfully requests that your Honors recommend
to the Public Service Commission (“the Commission”) to reject the Joint Proposal (“JP”) in the
above referenced proceedings filed with the Commission’s Secretary on May 14, 2021 by the
Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (“KEDNY’), KeySpan Gas East
Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“KEDLI’) (KEDNY and KEDLI are collectively referred to as the
“Companies”) and the Department of Public Service Staff (“Staff”).
In a case with unprecedented participation1, where there is far more opposition than support2, it
is important to note that of the very small number of Parties that did support the JP, the only
environmental group and renewable energy trade group that signed on had to qualify their
support, saying the JP did not go far enough to comply with New York State and New York City
climate laws.
The Environmental Defense Fund (“EDF”) said, “There is an outstanding and urgent need to
align gas utility operations with the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act
(“CLCPA”), its implementing policies, and New York City Local Law 97.” 3 NY-GEO said New
York State is “not on track” to meet the legally mandated goals of the CLCPA and “The terms of
this rate case fall far short of the reductions in billed gas usage that need to start as part of
meeting the 40% reduction in GHGs by 2030.”4

The broad opposition to this proposal can best be seen though in the thousands of public
comments that ratepayers from all walks of life have submitted in this case and provided orally

members throughout the Companies’ service territory.
