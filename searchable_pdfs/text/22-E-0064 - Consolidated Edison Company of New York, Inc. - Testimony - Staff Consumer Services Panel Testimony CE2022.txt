Customer Service Performance Incentive Mechanism 
17 
Q. 
What is the general purpose of customer service 
18 
performance incentive mechanisms? 
19 
A. 
Service quality incentive mechanisms help to 
20 
align shareholder and ratepayer interests by 
21 
providing earnings consequences related to the 
22 
quality of service that a utility provides to 
23 
its customers.  The mechanism links earnings 
24 


--- Page 18 ---
Cases 22-E-0064 & 22-G-0065      STAFF CONSUMER SERVICES PANEL  
 
 
17 
 
directly to a utility’s performance regarding 
1 
specific measures of customer service.  Based on 
2 
historical performance, target levels are 
3 
established for the various metrics during 
4 
utility rate cases.  Currently, such mechanisms 
5 
are in effect for most of New York’s major 
6 
investor-owned utilities, including Central 
7 
Hudson, Con Edison, Corning, KEDLI, KEDNY, O&R, 
8 
NYSEG, and RG&E. 
9 
Q. 
Why are service quality mechanisms needed? 
10 
A. 
As monopoly providers of utility delivery 
11 
services, investor-owned utilities, referred to 
12 
as IOUs, such as Con Edison, do not have a 
13 
profit-based incentive to provide adequate 
14 
customer service because its customers cannot 
15 
select another utility based on the quality of 
16 
service received.  However, obtaining adequate 
17 
service is extremely important to customers and 
18 
is in the public interest.  A service quality 
19 
mechanism establishes an incentive for utilities 
20 
to provide adequate levels of customer service. 
21 
Q. 
Are there other methods of monitoring adequate 
22 
customer service? 
23 
A. 
Yes, another method that the Department uses to 
24 


--- Page 19 ---

18 
 
measure and monitor customer service is through 
1 
Performance Indicator, referred to as PI, 
2 
reports. 
3 
Q. 
Please elaborate on what PI reports entail. 
4 
A. 
PIs are a standardized set of measures for 
5 
customer service performance that are reported 
6 
by all major IOUs.  These measures are reported 
7 
on a frequent and consistent basis facilitating 
8 
comparative analysis of customer service.  This 
9 
allows Staff to identify trends in utility 
10 
performances throughout the year. 
11 
Q. 
What is the difference between the service 
12 
quality mechanisms and PIs? 
13 
A. 
Service quality mechanisms are, typically, 
14 
reported annually.  They are established, in a 
15 
utility’s rate case and have earnings 
16 
incentives, also known as Negative Revenue 
17 
Adjustments, or NRAs, tied to a utility’s 
18 
performance results.  PIs are reported monthly 
19 
and do not have set targets that are associated 
20 
with earnings incentives.  Generally, Staff uses 
21 
the monthly filed PI reports to verify the 
22 
accuracy of the annually filed reports regarding 
23 
Customer Service Performance Incentive
