COMPARABLE RETURN STANDARD 
Q. 
Are authorized returns in other jurisdictions a relevant benchmark 
9 
that investors consider? 
10 
A. 
Previous authorized ROEs can provide some insight as a basic test of 
11 
historical reasonableness and a benchmark that investors consider in 
12 
comparing the authorized ROE to the returns available from other regulated 
13 
utilities with comparable risk. However, it is important to screen this data 
14 
to consider only the returns that have been authorized for risk-comparable 
15 
companies.  Further, it is important to consider the overall market conditions 
16 
that existed at the time that the decisions were made to ensure that the 
17 
conclusions drawn from the data are consistent with the comparable return 
18 
standard.  The Hope and Bluefield decisions require that authorized ROEs 
19 
must be comparable to other investments of commensurate risk.
