offset base gas delivery rates during the Stayout Period set

BACKGROUND

Proposal establishing a three-year rate plan for electric and

June 30, 2024.3  The 2022 Rate Order contains terms to apply 
during the Stayout Period in which Niagara Mohawk would not file 
for new rates beginning July 1, 2024, and ending on the earlier 
of either the effective date of revised base rates for the 
Company, or March 31, 2025.  The 2022 Rate Order provided that, 
if the Company did not file for new rates to be effective 
April 1, 2025, the base gas delivery rates in effect during the 
Stayout Period would continue, unchanged, however, the

TARIFF FILING 
 
 
On March 31, 2025, Niagara Mohawk filed tariff 
amendments to its Gas Tariff to effectuate the Post-Stayout 
Period gas rates pursuant to the 2022 Rate Order, which became 
effective on a temporary basis on May 1, 2025.  The tariff 
amendments update the gas rates to end the use of deferred 
credits partially offsetting base gas delivery rates for the 
Stayout Period, including updating: (1) Empire Zone Rider rates, 
applicable to service classification (SC) No. 2 – Small General 
Service, SC No. 5 – Firm Gas Sales and Transportation Service, 
SC No. 7 - Small Volume Firm Gas Sales and Transportation 
Service, and SC No. 8 - Gas Sales and Transportation Service 
with Standby Sales Service; (2) Excelsior Jobs Program rates, 
applicable to SC No. 2, SC No. 5, SC No. 7 and SC No. 8; and (3) 
delivery rates for SC No. 1 – Residential Service, SC No.2, SC 
No. 5, SC No. 7, SC No. 8, and SC No. 12 - Distributed 
Generation Service – Non-Residential.  The Post-Stayout Period 
rates were to begin immediately following the end of the Stayout 
Period, which would have been April 1, 2025, however, the 
Company submitted its tariff revisions late, resulting in the 
effective date being May 1, 2025.6

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65, and 66(1) and (12), the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the proposed tariff 
leaves, as well as modify, reject, or approve such filed 
tariffs.  As such, the Commission has the legal authority to 
review the Company’s filing and approve and make effective 
tariff amendments on a permanent basis, as prescribed in this 
Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
Niagara Mohawk’s proposed tariff amendments to its Gas 
Tariff effectuate the terms of the Commission’s 2022 Rate Order 
and implements gas rates to recover the approved revenue 
requirement for the Post-Stayout Period, which reflects the 
discontinuation of deferral credits that were being applied to 
partially offset gas rates during the Stayout Period.  As stated 
above, the Company submitted its tariff revisions late, 
resulting in the effective date being May 1, 2025, instead of 
the date immediately following the end of the Stayout Period, 
April 1, 2025.  The late submission of the Company’s tariff 
revisions did not harm customers, as the Stayout rates that were 
in effect are lower than the Post-Stayout rates that went into 
effect on May 1, 2025.  The Commission authorizes the tariff 
amendments listed in the Appendix to become effective on a 
permanent basis as of the date of issuance of this Order.
