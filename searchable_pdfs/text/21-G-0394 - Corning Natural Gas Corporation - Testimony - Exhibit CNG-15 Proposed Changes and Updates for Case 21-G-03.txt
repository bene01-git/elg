DPS-22-325
Cook
The number of miles of proposed LPP replacement for 2022-
2024 was 9 miles.  In his testimony, Mr. Cook had stated that, 
to mitigate the Company’s revenue requirement increase, 
the Company could reduce the LPP program requirement 
from 9 to 7.5 miles.  The Company inadvertently included 
data based on 7.5 miles of LPP replacement in the 2022 – 
2024 model. The model has been corrected to reflect the 9 
miles of LPP replacement.
