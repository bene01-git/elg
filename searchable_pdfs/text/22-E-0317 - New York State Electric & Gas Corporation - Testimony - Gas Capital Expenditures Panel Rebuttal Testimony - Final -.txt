Q. 
Why were detailed cost estimates not developed for many projects? 
1 
A. 
Most of the projects are in the Preliminary Engineering phase, which only includes high 
2 
level conceptual cost estimates, as detailed engineering has not been advanced.  Our 
3

4 
is consistent with past rate case filings and with the preparation of the 5-year capital plan.

6 
scope, then scaled/adjusted based on several factors, including:  the general scope of the 
7

inflation.  Consistent with Appendix M of the 2020 Joint Proposal, we complete a more

detailed cost estimate as part of comprehensive NPA process and BCA analysis, which 
12 
for large projects is performed prior to detailed engineering, permitting, and construction 
13 
and before more than five percent of the total project cost has been spent, not at the 
14 
Preliminary Engineering phase.
