BILLING 
1 
Q. 
What types of problems do NYPA Governmental Customers experience with the 
2 
Company’s current customer services procedures with respect to billing?  
3 
A. 
NYPA Governmental Customers experience several problems, including billing delays 
4 
and slow response times to requests for estimated reads, which are calculated estimations 
5 
of customer usage for a particular billing period where an actual meter reading is not 
6 
available.  NYPA Governmental Customers have also experienced issues with non-
7 
billing of accounts that lead to large monthly adjustments.  The causes of these billing 
8 
issues include, but are not limited to, broken meters, the failure of the Company’s 
9 
automated metering infrastructure (AMI) connectivity functions, and meter access issues, 
10 
such as when the Company cannot physically access a meter but fails to relay this issue to 
11 
NYPA as necessary for NYPA to work with the relevant Governmental Customer to 
12 
resolve the access issue. 
13 
Q. 
How would the creation of a dedicated NYPA customer operations team at the 
14 
Company address these concerns?  
15 
A. 
As detailed in the Company’s IR response to NYPA Set 5 Question 19, Company 
16 
employees in new senior customer service representative roles will “review accounts 
17 
daily” for unbilled and/or estimated billing and will address the “underlying root causes” 
18 
of billing issues by promoting timely automated billing based on actual meter readings.  
19 
See Exhibit __ (EB-1) at 3. 
20 
Q.  
Based on the Company’s response, what are your recommendations?  
21 
A.  
In view of Con Edison’s commitments to address NYPA billing issues in the manner 
22 
described in this IR response, the Commission should ensure that the Company’s

DISTRIBUTED ENERGY RESOURCES 
5 
Q. 
What are the issues that NYPA Governmental Customers experience with the 
6 
Company’s current customer services procedures with respect to distributed energy 
7 
resource (DER) projects?  
8 
A. 
DER projects must go through the interconnection process in order to connect to the grid. 
9 
NYPA Governmental Customers often face delays when advancing DER projects 
10 
because the interconnection process is not streamlined and further hindered by the 
11 
Company’s personnel capacity constraints.   
12 
Q. 
How would the creation of a dedicated NYPA customer operations team at the 
13 
Company address these concerns?  
14 
A. 
As detailed in the Company’s IR response to NYPA Set 5 Question 17, the Company 
15 
seeks to establish a new managerial role that would oversee the entire interconnection 
16 
process used to accommodate DER projects, including pre-interconnection activities, 
17 
assisting with collection and review of necessary forms, inspecting the final setup before 
18 
granting permission to connect to the grid, and post-interconnection tasks like confirming 
19 
that the correct account and rate configurations are in place and that the account bills 
20 
accurately and on time.  See Exhibit __ (EB-1) at 1.
