7.    
Page 33-34 of the Joint Proposal states: “In Case 19-G-0678, the Commission approved a 
settlement agreement wherein the Companies agreed to identify  potential  solutions  for    
addressing the  gap  between  the  Companies’ supply of natural gas and the forecast of 
firm demands of customers.  As part of  a  portfolio  of  solutions  to  meet  peak  
demand,  the  Companies  have  identified  opportunities to implement certain 
incremental non-infrastructure solutions (i.e., demand response and incremental energy 
efficiency programs).  As discussed in Section IV.5.3 of this  Joint  Proposal,  the  
Companies  are  also  considering certain  Long-Term Capital Capacity Projects to 
address the supply/demand gap.”   
a. 
Please quantify the “gap between the Companies’ supply of natural gas and the 
forecast of firm demands of customers.” Please quantify this gap for each of the 
next 5 years, including 2021. 
b. 
Please describe the assumptions made when calculating this gap, including but not 
limited to: the impact of new customer connections, the impact of the companies’ 
numerous commitments under the “Future of Heat” section of the Joint Proposal, 
and the requirements of the New Efficiency New York order. 
Response: 
 
 
Please see the Companies’ “Supply and Demand Analysis Related to Service Areas with Known 
Supply Constraint Vulnerabilities,” filed on July 17, 2020, in Case 20-G-0131.    
 
 Section 2 provides an overview of the Companies’ service territory.  It generally 
describes the geographic areas and number of customers the Companies’ serve, the 
portfolio of supply sources, and a description of how the Companies procure and deliver 
various supply resources to customers through a combination of owned infrastructure and 
contracts with third parties.
