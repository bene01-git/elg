Customer Service Performance Indicators 
7 
Q. 
What is the purpose of annual customer service 
8 
performance indicator mechanisms? 
9 
A. 
Customer service performance indicators, or 
10 
CSPIs, are mechanisms that help to align 
11 
shareholder and ratepayer interests by providing 
12 
earnings consequences related to the quality of 
13 
service that a utility provides to its 
14 
customers.  The mechanism links earnings 
15 
directly to a utility’s performance on specific 
16 
measures of customer service.  Based on 
17 
historical performance, target levels are 
18 
established for metrics during utility rate 
19 
cases.  Currently, such mechanisms are in effect 
20 
for most of New York’s major investor-owned 
21 
utilities, including Central Hudson, Con Edison, 
22 
Corning, KEDLI, KEDNY, Niagara Mohawk, and 
23 
Orange and Rockland. 
24 


--- Page 15 ---
Cases 22-E-0317, et al. 
 
SCSP 
 
 
15 
 
Q. 
Why are CSPIs necessary? 
1 
A. 
As monopoly providers of utility delivery 
2 
services, investor-owned utilities, such as 
3 
NYSEG and RG&E, do not have a profit-based 
4 
incentive to provide adequate customer service 
5 
because their customers cannot select another 
6 
utility based on the quality of service 
7 
received.  However, obtaining adequate service 
8 
is extremely important to customers and is in 
9 
the public interest.  A CSPI mechanism 
10 
establishes an incentive for utilities to 
11 
provide adequate levels of customer service. 
12 
Q. 
Are there other methods of monitoring adequate 
13 
customer service? 
14 
A. 
Yes, another method that Department Staff uses 
15 
to measure and monitor customer service is 
16 
through the monthly CSPI reports. 
17 
Q. 
Please elaborate on what the monthly CSPI 
18 
reports entail. 
19 
A. 
The monthly CSPIs are a standardized set of 
20 
measures for customer service performance that 
21 
are reported by all major IOUs.  These measures 
22 
are reported on a frequent and consistent basis, 
23 
facilitating comparative analysis of customer 
24 


--- Page 16 ---
Cases 22-E-0317, et al. 
 
SCSP 
 
 
16 
 
service across the utilities.  This allows 
1 
Department Staff to identify trends in utility 
2 
performances throughout the year. 
3 
Q. 
What is the difference between the annual and 
4 
monthly CSPIs? 
5 
A. 
The annual CSPIs are reported once per year, 
6 
along with being established, modified, and 
7 
approved within a utility’s rate case and have 
8 
earnings incentives, also known as NRAs, which 
9 
are tied to a utility’s performance results.  
10 
CSPIs that are reported monthly, do not have set 
11 
targets that are associated with earnings 
12 
incentives.  Generally, Department Staff uses 
13 
the monthly filed CSPI reports to verify the 
14 
accuracy of the annually reported CSPI results. 
15
