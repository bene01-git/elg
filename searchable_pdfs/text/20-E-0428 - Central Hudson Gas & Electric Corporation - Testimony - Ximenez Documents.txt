Subject: 
 
 
Question: 
 
Please report the number of customers who have identified themselves as 
Spanish-speaking, and describe the process by which the company collects this 
information. Please also report the number of calls to the company’s consultants 
that were conducted in Spanish. If available, please identify the topics of concern 
and any actions that were taken.  
 
Response: 
 
As of November 30, 2020, the company has 6,757 active customer accounts 
identified as Spanish Speaking.  
 
Our CSRs are trained to collect this information manually and record this 
preference in our Customer Information System (CIS). Capturing this information 
can happen on any contact but usually occurs during an application for new 
service when the customer establishes communications, billing and payment 
preferences with Central Hudson.  
 
Spanish speaking calls are handled only by Central Hudson CSRs and if a 
Spanish speaking customer routes to our third-party call center, they will transfer 
the call back. Should an English speaking CSR receive a request to interact in 
Spanish, and one of our Bilingual CSRs is not available, they will utilize the 
Company's interpretation services.  These services bring a third party fluent in 
the customer’s native language into the contact for translations purposes to 
complete the interaction. Please see CLP-008 Attachment 1 for 2020 Spanish 
interpretation volume from January 1, 2020 through November 30, 2020. 
 
Spanish speaking customer’s topics of concerns are not captured separately.
