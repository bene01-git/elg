Q. 
What customer service performance measures are included in the 
2

3 
A.   
Currently, KEDNY and KEDLI both have the following customer service 
4 
performance measures: (1) annual Commission complaint rate per 
5 
100,000 customers (PSC Complaint Rate); (2) Residential Customer 
6

8 
(Direct Testimony of Shared Services Panel p. 52). In their Rate Plans, the 
9

10 
for each metric, and if a Company fails to meet the performance target(s), 
11 
then it will incur a negative revenue adjustment(s) (NRAs). (See Direct 
12 
Testimony of Shared Service Panel p. 53 for a list of the Companies’ 
13 
current targets and NRAs for each metric). I further note that while the 
14 
Companies’ performance on each metric is measured monthly, the target 
15 
threshold(s) are applied to the average of each Company’s twelve-monthly 
16 
scores. (See Cases 16-G-0058 & 16-G-0059 Joint Proposal (filed 
17 
September 7, 2016) p. 42 (2016 JP)).  
18

Q. 
Please further describe these customer service performance measures. 
20 


--- Page 8 ---
7 
 
A. 
The PSC Complaint Rate measures the number of complaints per 
1 
100,000 customers “charged” to the Company on a monthly basis, but the 
2 
metric’s target is based on the average of twelve-monthly scores. Under 
3 
the Companies’ current rate plans, a complaint is considered “charged” 
4 
when a customer, after first contacting the Company to express 
5 
dissatisfaction with a Company action or practice, contacts the 
6 
Commission to express their dissatisfaction with the Company’s resolution 
7 
of their complaint. (See 2016 JP at pp. 42-43 (KEDNY) and pp. 91-92 
8 
(KEDLI)).  
9 
 
The Residential Customer Transaction Satisfaction Index measures the 
10 
satisfaction ratings resulting from a random survey of residential 
11 
customers who have contacted the Company (minimum 100 completed 
12 
surveys a month). Under the Companies’ current rate plans, the 
13 
satisfaction level is measured based on the number of customers rating 
14 
the Companies between “6” and “10” on a ten-point satisfaction scale.  
15 
(See 2016 JP at p. 44 (KEDNY) and p. 93 (KEDLI)). 
16 
 
The Telephone Answer Rate measures the percentage of total calls that 
17 
are answered within 30 seconds by a Company representative. (See 2016 
18 
JP at p. 45 (KEDNY) and p. 94 (KEDLI)). 
19 


--- Page 9 ---
8 
 
 
The Adjusted Customer Bills metric measures the percentage of total 
1 
customer bills that later require adjustment due to an error by the 
2 
Company.  (2016 JP at KEDNY p. 45-46 and KEDL p. 94-95).   
3 
 
4 
Q. 
What is the function of customer service performance measures?  
5 
A. 
Customer service performance measures provide utilities with powerful 
6 
incentives to provide acceptable levels of customer service. Quality 
7 
service is important to customers and serves the public interest. Due to 
8 
the monopolistic nature of utility service, customers cannot select another 
9 
delivery service provider if they are not satisfied with the quality of service 
10 
they receive from KEDNY or KEDLI. Meaningful measures associated with 
11 
appropriate financial risk are required to ensure that utility companies 
12 
provide adequate service to their customers. Structured correctly, 
13 
customer service performance measures provide utilities with powerful 
14 
incentives that should result in the high level of service to which customers 
15 
are entitled.
