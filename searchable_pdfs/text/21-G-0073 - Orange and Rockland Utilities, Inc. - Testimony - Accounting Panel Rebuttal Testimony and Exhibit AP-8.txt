Subject:  Intercompany Shared Services 
1.
In Exhibit AP-E3 and AP-G3, the Company applied its inflation factor of 4.28% to the
HTY and Program Changes to reach the RY level of Intercompany Shared Service expenses
for electric and gas. The program change amounts of $803,000 and $396,000, respectively,
however, are based on the difference between the Company’s forecasted costs for RY1 and
the Company’s HTY as detailed in the response to DPS-6-287, “DPS-6-287-Attachment 1”.
As the Company’s Program Change adjustments already reflect the expected changes in costs
expected to occur in 2022/RY1, does the Company believe that applying inflation on top of
that results in a double count. Explain in detail why or why not?
2.
Refer to tab “Forecast” of “DPS-6-287-Attachment 1” which provides an Intercompany
Shared Services expense forecast of $16,593,793, $16,522,837, and $16,752,247 for O&R
electric and gas combined for calendar years 2022, 2023, and 2024, respectively.
Has the affiliated billing credit referred to in the Company’s response to DPS-6-287 been
reflected in these forecasted amounts? If yes, for each year, show where and provide all
workpapers and documentation supporting the Company’s response. If no, explain why the
RY1, RY2, and RY3 forecasts included on exhibit AP-E3 and AP-G3 should not reflect the
combined forecasts of $16,593,793, $16,522,837, and $16,752,247, respectively shown on
this tab.
Response 
1. The Company agrees that the program changes already reflect expected cost increases,
and that inflation should not have been applied.
2. The affiliated billing credit referred to in the Company’s response to DPS-6-287 has not
been reflected in the forecast amounts for Intercompany Shared Services because the
