A. 
Ongoing improvement in electric system reliability continues to be an

Central Hudson is primarily measured utilizing the SAIFI and CAIDI 
17 
indices, long used by the Commission to measure reliability.  These 
18 
standard Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers indices are 
19 
defined as follows: 
20

Total # of Customers Interrupted

1 
Sum of Customer Interruption Duration 
2 
 
 
 
Total # of Customers Interrupted 
 
3 
In accordance with Commission determinations, for the purpose of 
4 
tracking reliability performance in relation to its SAIFI and CAIDI 
5 
performance indices, Central Hudson excludes major storm outage data.  
6 
The Commission’s regulations, at 16 NYCRR § 97.1, define a major storm 
7 
as “a period of adverse weather during which service interruptions affect 
8 
at least ten percent of the customers in an operating area and/or result in 
9 
customers being without electric service for durations of at least 24 hours.”   
10 
 
SAIFI metrics are inherently tied to the planning, design, 
11 
construction standards and overall system topology/conditions (i.e., 
12 
weather events, tree coverage), whereas CAIDI is more closely tied to 
13 
crew availability, weather events, number of concurrent outages and 
14 
response/repair times.  The root cause of the interruption has a significant 
15 
impact on the repair time.
