Q. 
WHAT IS THE BIGGEST CHALLENGE FOR A WEATHER-DEPENDENT  
1 
ELECTRIC SYSTEM? 
2 
A. 
The Independent Intervenors believe that the biggest challenge facing electric resource  
3 
planners for the PSL 66-P renewable energy program is the necessity to provide electric 
4 
energy at all times.  Weather-dependent resources vary in time and space and planners must 
5 
account for the impacts on resource availability.  It is expected that peak loads will shift to 
6 
the winter in response to CLCPA building and transportation mandates.  Unfortunately, New 
7 
York solar resources are weakest in the winter due to the latitude and persistent lake-effect 
8 
clouds and snow.  The biggest challenge will be for the gap when there are extended periods 
9 
of light winds in the winter.  New York electric planning experts agree that a new category 
10 
of generating resources called Dispatchable Emissions-Free Resources (DEFR) is necessary. 
11 
Independent Intervenor Exhibit 5 includes a summary of a highly technical analysis by 
12 
Professor Lindsay Anderson of Cornell that explains why DEFR is needed.   
13 
There are ramifications of this challenge on the Company rate case.  Acknowledging 
14 
the problem is only the first step in resolving it.  Electric planners are still trying to figure 
15 
out how to incorporate the need for DEFR into reliability planning criteria.  Determining
