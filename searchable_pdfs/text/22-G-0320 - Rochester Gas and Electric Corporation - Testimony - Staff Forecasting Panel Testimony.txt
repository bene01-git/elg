1 
an econometric modeling methodology to estimate 
2 
the relationship between a dependent variable, 
3 
in this case the monthly billed electric 
4 
delivery volumes by customer class, and certain 
5 
independent, explanatory variables.  Econometric 
6 
modeling was used to develop the electric

delivery volume forecasts for the Companies’

9 
and street and area lighting classes.  The

13 
of actual use. 
14 
Q. 
Please explain econometric modeling further. 
15 
A. 
An econometric model for electric deliveries is 
16 
a regression model that relates customer demand 
17 
for electricity to a set of independent 
18 
variables such as the number of customers, price 
19 
of electricity, a key economic variable such 
20 
personal income or employment, and weather.  An 
21 
econometric model for customer forecasting is a 
22 
regression model that relates the number of 
23 
customers to an economic or demographic 
24 


--- Page 14 ---
Cases 22-E-0317 et al. Staff Forecasting Panel 
 
 
13 
 
variable, among others.  In addition, an 
1 
econometric model may also include one or more 
2 
autoregressive, or AR, and/or moving average, or 
3 
MA, terms because the customer or delivery data 
4 
is a time series.  Autocorrelation is the 
5 
situation where the current value of a variable 
6 
is significantly related to its own values in 
7 
the recent past.  The econometric model 
8 
coefficient estimates will only be efficient and 
9 
unbiased if any autocorrelation in the model’s 
10 
error term is corrected.  The AR and MA terms 
11 
are included to correct the problem of 
12 
autocorrelation and reduce forecasting errors.  
13 
The AR and MA terms may also capture some of the 
14 
effect of other unknown or unmeasurable 
15 
variables that may have not been included in the 
16 
model. 
17 
Q. 
How is an econometric model estimated? 
18 
A. 
An econometric model, or an equation with 
19 
estimated coefficients of the relationships 
20 
between the dependent and independent variables, 
21 
is estimated using a regression analysis with a 
22 
statistical package to best fit the equation on 
23 
actual historical data for both the dependent 
24 


--- Page 15 ---
Cases 22-E-0317 et al. Staff Forecasting Panel 
 
 
14 
 
and independent variables.  The coefficients of 
1 
AR and MA terms, depending on data patterns over 
2 
time, are statistically determined during the 
3 
model estimation process.
