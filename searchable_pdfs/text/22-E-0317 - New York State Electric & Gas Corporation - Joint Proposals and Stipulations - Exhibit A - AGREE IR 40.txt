Page 5 of the Joint Proposal states, “The Proposal contains provisions consistent with, supportive 
of, and in furtherance of the objectives of the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act 
(“CLCPA”).”  
 
a.  What analysis did the signatory parties conduct on the greenhouse gas emissions 
reductions or increases associated with the capital investments and programs described in 
the Joint Proposal?  Please provide this analysis. 
 
b. What analysis did the signatory parties conduct on the increases or reductions to the 
pollution burdens in disadvantaged communities associated with the capital investments 
and programs described in the Joint Proposal? Please provide this analysis. 
 
c. For each Company, what is the total of clean energy and energy efficiency investments 
contained in the Joint Proposal? For each Company, what is the percentage of those 
investments that will benefit disadvantaged communities?

Response: 
a. The proposed capital investments contain many aspects which are aligned in achieving 
CLCPA greenhouse gas targets, specifically; 
• Funding for improved gas reliability through, for example, the continuation of the 
Companies’ Leak Prone Main Replacement programs, which reduces GHG 
emissions; 
• Funding for gate and regulator stations that will modernize these critical pieces of 
infrastructure, thereby increasing resilience and helping to mitigate climate 
impacts by reducing methane emissions; 
• Continuation of a residential methane detection (“RMD”) program that will allow 
for quicker discovery and repairs of any potentially leaking pipes thereby 
addressing both human exposure/safety issues and unwanted emissions concerns; 
• Proposed electric system upgrades included in CLCPA Phase 1 which will enable 
greenhouse gas emission reductions by significantly increasing available electric 
system capacity for new emission-free generation development. 
• Funding for improved electric reliability (e.g., those projects and programs that 
are needed to address immediate and long-term system needs such as 
thermal/capacity overloads, voltage, and loss-of-load violations) and resiliency 
(e.g., those projects and programs that are needed to improve system restoration 
capabilities, improve overall system flexibility and provide system operators with 
the appropriate visibility and field infrastructure to quickly restore power and 
minimize customer exposure following a contingency event);  
 
b. The proposed Capital Investments contains many aspects which are aligned in achieving the 
pollution reductions as described in part a.  Additionally, the Joint Proposal provides capital 
funding for investments and maintenance necessary to maintain safe and reliable service for 
all communities, including disadvantaged communities.  These system improvements along 
with the reduction in GHG emissions would provide positive benefits, and not 
disproportionately burden disadvantaged communities. 
 
c. The Companies and Staff objects to this request on the grounds that it is overly burdensome.

projects like those included CLCPA Phase 1 will create benefits for all New Yorkers by 
facilitating the achievement of the State’s clean energy and decarbonization goals, which
