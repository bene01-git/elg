Staff’s adjustment to the Company’s labor distribution, which reflects a

Company’s base level headcount.

or approximately 47% of the Company’s requested staffing increase.

17 
proposed reductions are within customer experience and are necessary to 
18 
implement monthly meter reading and collections activities while meeting 
19 
targeted customer service performance levels..  As discussed in the 
20 
rebuttal testimony of many of the Company’s Panels including the

Workforce Compensation and Benefits, Customer Experience, and
