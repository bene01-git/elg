DISCUSSION 
3 
Q. 
Describe the Companies’ proposed RAM. 
4 
A. 
The RAM would allow the Companies to recover existing reconciliations, and the 
5 
associated deferral amounts booked from January 1, 2023, through March 31, 2024.  
6 
The collection for the RAM would begin on June 1, 2024. 
7 
 
8 
Q.  
What is the DPS Staff’s position on the proposed RAM by the Companies? 
9 
A. 
As stated on page 20 of DPS Staff’s Policy Panel, DPS Staff does “not agree that 
10 
implementing a RAM is appropriate in the context of a one-year rate proceeding.”  
11 
 
12 
Q.  
Do you agree with DPS Staff? 
13 
A. 
Yes, we agree that a RAM should not be implemented in a one-year rate plan. 
 
14 
 
15 
Q. 
Are there any recommendations that DPS Staff is proposing in the event the 
16 
Commission approves the implementation of a RAM? 
17 
A. 
Yes, DPS Staff is recommending that in the event the Commission adopts a RAM, 
18 
the reconciled items be limited to the existing property tax deferral, Energy 
19 
Affordability Program (EAP) deferral, and incremental energy efficiency program 
20 
costs.  Additionally, should the Commission implement a RAM cap, DPS Staff 
21 
recommends that “a cap of 2.5 percent of total revenues is reasonable in the absence 
22 
of other caps on specific reconciliations.” 
23

1 
Q. 
Do you agree with DPS Staff’s recommendation to the Commission regarding the 
2 
Companies’ proposed RAM? 
3 
A. 
No. DPS Staff alternatively recommends on page 21 of their Policy Panel 
4 
testimony, if the Commission authorizes a RAM, that a cap of 2.5 percent on total 
5 
revenues for KEDNY and KEDLI would be reasonable.  We disagree with any 
6 
consideration of a RAM and thus disagree with that aspect of Staff’s proposal. The 
7 
revenue increases proposed by Staff in their Revenue Requirements Panel 
8 
testimony is $389.5 million for KEDNY (Exhibit__(SRRP-1), at 1) and $219.9 
9 
million for KEDLI (Exhibit__(SRRP-2), at 1) for the rate year ending March 31, 
10 
2025.  This equates to a total revenue increase of 13.9 percent for KEDNY and 11.5 
11 
percent KEDLI, respectively. If the Commission adopts a RAM to begin on June 
12 
1, 2024, being recovered in a surcharge, assuming a 2.5 percent RAM cap, it is 
13 
essentially like increasing delivery rates by an additional $24.2 million for KEDNY 
14 
and $45.0 million for KEDLI, just two months after new rates go in effect, which 
15 
is not in the interest of ratepayers. The RAM values for KEDNY and KEDLI were 
16 
provided by the Companies in response to Staff’s IR 433, which is attached as 
17 
Exhibit__(UIU-RAM-1).
