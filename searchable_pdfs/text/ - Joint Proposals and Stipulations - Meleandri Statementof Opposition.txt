Case 
19-G-0310 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, Charges, Rules and 
Regulations of KeySpan Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid for Gas Service 
Dear Secretary Phillips: 
Please ﬁnd my statement of opposition to The Company’s Joint Proposal for ﬁling in the above 
mentioned cases.
I am a lifelong New Yorker, an artist, an activist and a homeowner who cares deeply about the 
future of the city and the people who live and work here. It is my belief that we have to stop 
looking at the challenges that face us as separate issues and treat the City holistically.  Solving 
our energy needs must take into account all aspects of our goals as New Yorkers which include: 
sustainable answers to climate change, environmental justice and quality of life for all New 
Yorkers. This Joint Proposal in no way reﬂects any of those imperatives. The fact that the City of 
New York has not signed on to this proposal seems to me to be an afﬁrmation that we have to 
do more. I oppose the Joint Proposal and encourage the Public Service Commission to ﬁnd 
better solutions. 
Respectfully submitted, 
Nina Meledandri
cc: Active Parties in Cases 19-G-0309 and 19-G-0310
