BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On July 20, 2023, the Commission adopted the terms of 
a Joint Proposal filed on February 16, 2023, in these 
proceedings, subject to additional requirements which 
established three-year (January 1, 2023, to December 31, 2025) 
electric and gas rate plans (Rate Plans) for Consolidated Edison 


--- Page 2 ---

-2- 
Company of New York, Inc. (Con Edison or the Company).1  Among 
the provisions of the Rate Plans is the continued use of revenue 
decoupling mechanisms (RDM).2  The purpose of an RDM is to ensure 
that the utility is not disincentivized in encouraging its 
customers to be efficient and reduce their consumption of 
electric and gas.  As such, the RDM reconciles the Company’s 
actual electric and gas delivery revenues to the forecasted 
level of revenues established in its Rate Plans. 
 
 
In a petition filed November 6, 2023, Con Edison 
states that the RDM targets for Rate Year 1 (January 1, 2023, to 
December 31, 2023) are correct.  However, Rate Year 2    
(January 1, 2024, to December 31, 2024) and Rate Year 3  
(January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025) RDM targets in 
Appendices 4 and 5 of the Joint Proposal reflect errors in their 
calculation.  The Company indicates that its proposed 
corrections will align the Rate Years 2 and 3 RDM targets with 
Rate Years 2 and 3 revenue requirements established by the 
Commission in the 2023 Rate Order. 
 
 
By this Order, the Commission directs that the RDM 
targets for Rate Years 2 and 3 contained in Appendices 4 and 5 
of the Joint Proposal be corrected, that the Company file tariff 
amendments to make the corrections and waives newspaper 
publication of these amended tariffs. 
 
BACKGROUND 
 
 
As the Company explains in its petition, the electric 
revenue requirements established by the Commission in the 2023 
Rate Order were correctly set at a $442.3 million increase for

Rate Year 1, a $517.3 million increase for Rate Year 2, and a 
$382.2 million increase for the Rate Year 3.  The Company 
further states that the components of the revenue requirements, 
rate increases, and sales revenue forecasts for both the

Con Edison has over or under-recovered its electric and gas 
delivery revenues for each rate year, were incorrectly set. 
 
 
Under the 2023 Rate Order, the Company calculates the 
over or under recoveries of delivery service revenues by 
comparing the forecasted level of revenues to the RDM targets 
for the applicable RDM reconciliation period (the six-months 
ending June 30 for electric and the six-months ending December 
31 for gas).  This variation is either recovered from or 
credited to customers in a subsequent period.3  
The RDM Target Error 
 
 
According to Con Edison, the components of the revenue 
requirements, rate increases, and sales revenue forecasts for 
its electric and gas businesses for each rate year are correct 
in the Joint Proposal.  However, the Company discovered that for 
Rate Years 2 and 3, an error was made in setting the RDM 
targets.  Con Edison states that the sales forecast changes 
embedded in the revenue requirements were not correctly 
translated to the sales forecast changes used to set the RDM 
targets of the Joint Proposal.  For Rate Year 1, the starting 
point for the revenue requirements and RDM target models

To correct these errors and align the revenue 
requirements with the RDM targets for Rate Years 2 and 3, the 
Company allocated the revenue shortfall for each Rate Year to 
the service classes based on the allocations used to set the RDM 
targets in the Joint Proposal.  The allocated revenue shortfall 
for each service class was then added to the existing RDM 
targets to arrive at the corrected RDM targets that align with 
the revenue requirements as approved by the Commission in the 
2023 Rate Order.

NOTICE OF PROPOSED RULE MAKING 
 
 
Pursuant to the State Administrative Procedure Act 
(SAPA) §202(1), a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking was published in 
the State Register on December 6, 2023 [SAPA No. 22-E-0064SP3].  
The time for submission of comments pursuant to the Notice 
expired on February 5, 2024, and no comments were received. 
 
LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Public Service Law (PSL) §66(12) provides the 
Commission with broad ratemaking powers, including the ability 
to correct the RDM targets calculated in the Joint Proposal and 
adopted in the 2023 Rate Order.  In addition, pursuant to PSL 
§§5, 65(1), 66(1) and (12), the Commission has the legal 
authority to review proposed tariff leaves, as well as to 
modify, reject or approve such tariff amendments and to waive 
newspaper publication of such amendments. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The error associated with the electric and gas RDM 
targets for Rate Years 2 and 3 have been confirmed and the 
proposed corrections and tariff amendments have also been 
reviewed for accuracy and we have determined that they correctly 
align the revenue requirements with the RDM targets for Rate 
Years 2 and 3.  Therefore, the Commission grants Con Edison’s 
request to correct the RDM targets in Appendices 4 and 5 of the 
Joint Proposal for Rate Years 2 and 3 as reflected in the 
Appendix to this Order.  Further, the Company shall file tariff

corrected tariff leaves will not result in any change to the
