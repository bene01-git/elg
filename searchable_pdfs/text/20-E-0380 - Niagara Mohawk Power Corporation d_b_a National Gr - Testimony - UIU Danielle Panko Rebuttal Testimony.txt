Q.  
What is your response to this proposal? 
10 
A. 
One of the challenges of incorporating a minimum size system (or any other 
11 
method that classifies costs as customer-related) in a cost of service model (if one 
12 
choose to do so) is to determine how to appropriately classify costs as customer-
13 
related. When considering this, one must be aware that the more costs are 
14 
classified as customer related, the more utility costs will end up being paid by the 
15 
classes with the largest number of customers. There is no exact science of doing 
16 
so which creates disputes among parties in rate cases throughout the country. As 
17 
noted in my initial testimony, I do not agree with the theory behind a minimum size 
18 
system and I highlight there are professional documents that either do not 
19 
recommend or advise cauation against using a minimum system. In my intial 
20 
testimony, I note that the Company’s electric ECOS model assumptions have 
21 
significantly changed over the years  – the change to classify distribution costs as 
22 
customer-related may place a larger burden on smaller users within service
