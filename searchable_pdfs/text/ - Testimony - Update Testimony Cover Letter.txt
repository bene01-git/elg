In accordance with the March 8, 2022 ruling in these proceedings,1 Con Edison 
respectfully submits the attached testimony, whitepapers, and exhibits correcting or updating 
prior submissions by the following Company panels and witnesses: 
  
− Accounting Panel 
− Customer Energy Solutions Panel 
− Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act Panel 
− Electric Infrastructure and Operations Panel 
− Electric Forecasting Panel 
− Environment, Health, and Safety Panel 
− Gas Forecasting Panel 
− Gas Infrastructure, Operations and Supply Panel 
− Information Technology Panel 
− Property Tax Witness 
− Shared Services Panel  
 
As explained by the Accounting Panel in its attached update testimony, the Company’s 
proposed electric revenue requirement has decreased by approximately $161.1 million, and 
the Company is now seeking an overall electric revenue requirement increase of $1,037.8 
million.  The Company’s proposed gas revenue requirement has decreased by approximately 
$100.5 million, and the Company is now seeking an overall gas revenue requirement increase 
of $402.2 million.

In addition, the Company is withdrawing its proposed changes to the spoilage 
compensation provisions of General Rule 21.1 (Leaf 171) of its Electric Tariff.  Public 
Service Law section 73, which becomes effective April 21, 2022, establishes spoilage 
compensation requirements, and directs the Commission to promulgate rules to implement 
them.  The Company anticipates that the Commission-promulgated rules will apply during 
the proposed rate year and that it will make a tariff filing separate from this rate filing to 
implement the statute and any applicable Commission changes. 
 
The Company reserves its rights under all applicable law to make further updates as 
required, including on May 20, 2022, as set forth in the procedural schedule.
