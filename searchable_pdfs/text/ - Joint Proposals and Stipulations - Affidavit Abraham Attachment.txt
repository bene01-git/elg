Case 12-G-0544 – In the Matter of the Commission's Examination of The Brooklyn 
Union Gas Company d/b/a National GridNY's Earnings Computation Provisions and 
Other Continuing Elements of the Applicable Rate Plan 
 
Request For Information 
 
FROM: Department of Public Service Staff, [Ronald Calkins and George Abraham] 
 
TO: Frank Lombardo 
 
 
Request:   
 
1. Provide detailed backup work papers of the National GridNY’s Excess Earnings 
computation for 12 Months ended December 31, 2011. 
 
2. Provide the detailed backup work papers to support the computation the Revenue 
Requirement computation (return on Common Equity of  9.48% for 2013 and 9.40% for 
the year 2014) reflected in the Settlement Term Sheet dated 15, 2013.  
 
3. Provide detailed explanation and rationale, with proper back up to all normalizing 
adjustments made by the company to Operation and Maintenance Expenses and Taxes 
Other Than Income Taxes in the above filing. 
 
4. The recently passed American Taxpayer Relief Act of 2012 includes a provision for the 
continuation of Bonus Tax Depreciation at a rate of 50% for vintage year 2013 assets for 
federal tax purposes.  
a. Could a revised calculation of the 2013 tax depreciation including the Bonus Tax 
Depreciation (50%) be provided?  If so, please provide.  If not, please explain. 
b. Also provide the revised 2013 and 2014 accumulated deferred taxes, tax 
depreciation for federal income tax purposes and the impact on the forecasted rate 
of returns for 2013 and 2014 due to the lowering of the respective forecasted rate 
base balances.   
 
 
Response: 
 
1. Please refer to the detailed workpapers provided by the Company in support of the 
KEDNY Calendar Year 2011 Excess Earnings Filing that were filed on May 29, 2012.  
 


--- Page 2 ---
2. Please refer to Attachment 1 to DPS-3 (GA-1) for the detailed workpapers in support of 
the computation of the KEDNY CY 2013 and 2014 revenue requirement reflected in the 
term sheet dated January 15, 2013. 
 
3. The Company has provided detailed explanation of its CY2011, 2013 and 2014 O&M 
normalizing adjustments in response to RFC-1 in this proceeding.  
 
Normalizing adjustments to Taxes other than Income Taxes include labor inflation 
increases (3.4% in CY 2013 and 1.7% in CY 2014) to CY 2011 actual Federal Old Age 
Benefit (FICA) and Unemployment Insurance (SFU) amounts.  In addition, property and 
special franchise taxes were set at Rate Year Five levels ($145.5 million) per the Merger 
& Gas Revenue Requirement Joint Proposal adopted by the Commission in Case 06-M-
0878 on August 23, 2007.  The final normalizing adjustment to Public Utility Excise 
(Municipal Gross Revenue) Tax of $1.1 million includes a 2.3% growth factor in line 
with revenue increases. 
 
4. a.) Please see Attachment 3 to DPS-3 (GA-1) for the change in ADIT (2013 and 2014) 
resulting from inclusion of bonus depreciation.  The impact of the change is a reduction 
in rate base of $5.798m in CY 2013 and a reduction in rate base of $18.688m in CY 
2014.  
 
b.) Please see Attachment 2 to DPS-3 (GA-1).  The change in deferred taxes resulting 
from inclusion of bonus depreciation detailed in part (a) above would result in a change 
in ROE from 9.48% to 9.51% in CY 2013 and 9.41% to 9.50% in CY 2014.
