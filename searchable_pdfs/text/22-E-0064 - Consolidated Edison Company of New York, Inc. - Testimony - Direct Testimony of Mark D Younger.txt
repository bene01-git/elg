13 
eliminate the potential that investor-owned utilities could exercise vertical market 
14 
power to the detriment of wholesale competitive electricity markets and consumers.3  
15 
Vertical market power “occurs when an entity that has market power in one stage of 
16 
the production process leverages that power to gain advantage in a different stage of
