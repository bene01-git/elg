INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On March 25, 2020, the Order Postponing Approved 
Electric and Gas Delivery Rate Increases and Updated Reduction 
to the Low Income Discount Credit and Temporarily Waiving 
Certain Tariff Fees (Order) was issued in these proceedings,

approving a number of actions on an emergency basis to help 
provide some financial relief to the ratepayers of Niagara 
Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (Niagara Mohawk), 
The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (KEDNY)

On April 15, 2020, Notices of Proposed Rulemaking were 
published in the State Register pursuant to SAPA §202(1), 
soliciting comments on whether the Commission should adopt the 
emergency rule as a permanent rule [SAPA Nos. 17-E-0238SP8, 
17-E-0239SP4, 16-G-0058SP7, 16-G-0059SP7 and 14-M-0565SP12].  
The time for submission of comments pursuant to the Notices 
expired on June 15, 2020.  One public comment was received, 
requesting the Commission to reverse the scheduled rate 
increases for electric and gas delivery rates set for July 1, 
2020, due to the economic impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic.        
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The Order postponed previously approved increases in 
electric and gas delivery rates1 and a scheduled reduction to 
existing low income program discount levels for Niagara Mohawk

These measures were approved on an emergency basis to 
provide some immediate relief to National Grid’s customers, many 
of whom were temporarily without work or working reduced hours 
due to the declared state of emergency in New York State and the 
mandated closure of many businesses and schools.  The action was 
necessary to protect the general welfare of National Grid’s 
customers and to avoid inflicting any additional financial 
hardship during the critical months of the COVID-19 pandemic.  
The increases in electric and gas delivery rates and reduction 
to low-income program bill discount levels were scheduled to 
take effect on April 1, 2020, which required action on an 
emergency basis before compliance with standard SAPA notice and 
publication requirements.
