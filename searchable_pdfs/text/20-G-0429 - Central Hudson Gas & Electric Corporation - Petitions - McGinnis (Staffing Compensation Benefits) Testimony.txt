VI. 
SUPPLEMENTAL EXECUTIVE RETIREMENT PLAN 
1 
Q.    
Would you please provide an overview of Central Hudson’s Supplemental 
2 
Executive Retirement Plan (“SERP”)? 
3 
A. 
The SERP is designed to preserve a prior benefit formula for a closed 
4 
group of eligible executives. The SERP provides the executives who have 
5 
a minimum of 30 years of service at the age of 61 with 57% of their 
6 
pension-eligible earnings (defined as the highest 3 consecutive calendar 
7 
years during the 10 consecutive calendar years immediately preceding 
8 
termination of employment), less the amounts received under the 
9 
Retirement Income Plan and Benefit Restoration Plan. Individuals having 
10 
less service or retiring prior to age 61 have their benefits reduced 
11 
according to provisions in the SERP. 
12 
Q.    
Are you seeking rate recovery of costs associated with the SERP?  
13 
A. 
Yes. The Company believes this program is a key component of the 
14 
executive compensation program and delivery rates should be designed to 
15 
allow for recovery of this cost. Preserving prior benefit formulas for a 
16 
closed group of executives is a typical transition method and is an 
17 
accepted practice in the employment market and consistent with treatment 
18 
of this issue in past Central Hudson rate cases. Costs associated with 
19 
SERP were approved by the Commission in Cases 14-E-0318, 14-G-
20 
0319, 17-E-0459, and 17-G-0460. 
21
