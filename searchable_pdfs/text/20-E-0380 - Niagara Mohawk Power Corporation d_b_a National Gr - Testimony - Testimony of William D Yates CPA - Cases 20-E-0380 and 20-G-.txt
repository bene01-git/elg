The ECB of customers in the Niagara Mohawk service area can be calculated using 
3

4

5 
Community Survey (“ACS”),8 which asked a representative sample of Americans 
6

7

8

9 
and Hispanic origin, as well as estimates of total housing units. 
10 
The statistical geographical area used in the PUMS sample is referred to as a 
11 
“Public Use Microdata Area” or “PUMA”, which the Census Bureau defines as a 
12 
contiguous area in one state of at least 100,000 persons.9 As applied to the Company’s 
13 
service area, PUMAs may be comprised of geographical units as small as parts of cities 
14 
(e.g., Buffalo has more than one PUMA) or as large as groups of counties (e.g., the counties 
15 
of Clinton, Essex, Franklin, and Hamilton together form one PUMA). 
16 
The advantage of utilizing PUMA data as a means of measuring ECB is that it 
17 
includes the weighted responses of individual households10 to all ACS questions, including 
18 
information about household costs for electricity, utility natural gas, fuel oil, propane, and 
19 
other heating fuels. This information, together with household income reported by 
20 
respondents, makes it possible to summarize statistically weighted ECBs for the service 
21 
area. 
22 
In order to match PUMA areas (or parts of PUMA areas) with the Company’s 
23 
service area, it is necessary to reconcile the geographies of the two, and to subtract 
24 
households in the parts of PUMAs that are outside of the service area. This exercise gives 
25 
rise to a reasonable representation of the households in the Company’s service area.

What is “Excess Unprotected Accumulated Deferred Federal Income Tax”?
