I. 
Preliminary Statement 
Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“Niagara Mohawk” or the 
“Company”) hereby respectfully petitions the New York State Public Service Commission (the 
“Commission”) for a limited waiver of the Revenue Decoupling Mechanism (“RDM”) provisions 
included in Rule 57.1.3 and 57.1.3.3 of the P.S.C. 220 – Electric Service Tariff (“Electric Tariff”) 
to allow for the recovery of prior period reconciliation amounts.  The proposed modifications are 
necessary to enable the recovery of certain credits associated with Electric Energy Efficiency 
Transition Implementation Plan (“ETIP”) Costs that were inadvertently omitted from the RDM 
calculation from April 2018 through March 2021.  National Grid respectfully submits that the 
limited waiver, as described below, is in the public interest and should be approved by the 
Commission. 
II. 
Background 
The electric RDM in Rule 57 of the Electric Tariff allows the Company to reconcile actual 
billed delivery service revenues for the RDM reconciliation period to annual target revenues for 
delivery service as approved in the Company’s most recent rate case.  The amounts are reconciled 
for each customer group (“Reconciliation Group”) subject to the RDM.  The RDM reconciliation

determines the difference between actual billed delivery service revenues and the annual target 
revenues for any Reconciliation Group listed in Rule 57.1.11 in each annual RDM reconciliation 
period and forms the basis of the RDM adjustment for that Reconciliation Group. 
As part of the Company’s 2017 Rate Case (Case 17-E-0238), the Company’s electric ETIP 
costs were moved from the System Benefits Charge (“SBC”) surcharge rate to base delivery rates.  
As a result, customers with an allocation of energy from New York Power Authority (“NYPA”) 
or the Recharge New York (“RNY”) program began receiving ETIP credits April 2018.  The 
Company issued the ETIP credits to preserve the ETIP exemption that these customers receive on 
the NYPA or RNY portion of their load.  Because the NYPA ETIP credits are now included in 
base delivery rates, they should have been included in the Company’s RDM reconciliation.   
The Company subsequently determined that the billing charge types for the NYPA ETIP 
credits were not being included in the queries that capture actual delivery revenues for the RDM 
reconciliation.  As a result, the Company under recovered approximately $18 million from Service 
Classification (“SC”) 2 Demand (“SC-2D”), SC-3 and SC-3A customers for the period of April 
2018 through March 2021.2

III. 
Requested Relief 
A.  Limited Waiver of Rules 57.1.3 and 57.1.3.3 of the Electric Tariff 
Each year, pursuant to Rule 57 of the Electric Tariff, the Company calculates over/under 
recoveries of delivery service revenue for the RDM reconciliation period (i.e., twelve months 
ending June 30) by Reconciliation Group3 to be recovered from, or refunded to, customers in the 
following year over a twelve-month period beginning with the July billing month in the year 
following the RDM reconciliation period (“RDM Adjustment Period”).  A negative reconciliation 
balance (i.e., actual revenue was less than the forecast amount) will result in a surcharge to the 
applicable Reconciliation Group; a positive balance (i.e., actual revenue exceeded the forecast 
amount) will provide a credit to customer bills. 
The Company discovered that the NYPA ETIP credits, which are provided to customers 
who receive NYPA and RNY allocations, were not being included in the revenue that is used to 
calculate over or under recoveries for certain service classifications for the period of April 2018 
through March 2021.  Because the amounts are credits, the result was an RDM under recovery 
from the SC-2D, SC-3 and SC-3A service classifications, as shown in Table 1, above.  The total 
amount of NYPA ETIP credits (not including carrying charges) that were inadvertently excluded 
from the RDM calculation during this period is approximately $18,411,174. 
Pursuant to Rules 57.1.3 and 57.1.3.3 of the Company’s Electric Tariff, any imbalance 
between actual revenue and the revenue targets at the end of the “RDM reconciliation period” is

reconciled during the subsequent RDM Adjustment Period for each Reconciliation Group.  The 
RDM Reconciliation Period is the 12-month period for which actual billed delivery revenues are 
reconciled to the target revenues and the RDM Adjustment Period is the 12-month period for which 
any over or under recoveries are credited or surcharged, which commences July 1 immediately 
following the end of the RDM Reconciliation Period and continues through June 30 of the next 
year. 
The Company is requesting a limited waiver of Rule 57.1.3 to allow the Company to reflect 
the inadvertently omitted prior period balances in the RDM adjustment.  Additionally, the 
Company is requesting a limited waiver of Rule 57.1.3.3 to allow the Company to recover such 
prior period balances over a period longer than 12 months for SC-3 and SC-3A customers, as 
further described below.  
It should also be noted that Niagara Mohawk did not earn its allowed rate of return during 
the years for which the Company under-recovered RDM, as described in this petition.  In addition, 
the amounts the Company is requesting to recover through the RDM surcharge rates would not 
have put the Company into an earnings sharing position for those years. 
B. Recovery Proposal 
The Company considered several options for the recovery of the under-recovered delivery 
revenues with the intent of minimizing the impact on customers’ bills.4  The Company proposes 
different recovery plans due to the varying magnitude of under-recovered revenues for the three 
impacted Reconciliation Groups.  
Due to the relatively small dollar amount for the SC-2D Reconciliation Group, the 
Company proposes that the under recovery of $22,913 be included in the Company’s next annual

RDM reconciliation filing in June 2023.  With regard to SC-3 and SC-3A customers, the Company 
proposes an adjustment to the existing RDM rate as follows: 
 SC-3:  The Company proposes to recover the SC-3 under-recovery of 
$2,147,614 over a 15-month period, beginning in April 2023.  This would result 
in an incremental increase in the SC-3 RDM rate of $0.11 per kW per month 
during the 15-month period. Any principal amounts remaining at the end of the 
proposed recovery period due to over/under recoveries would then be rolled 
into the RDM imbalance to be recovered or refunded in the subsequent year. 
 SC-3A:  The Company proposes to recover the SC-3A under-recovery of 
$16,240,635 over a 27-month period, beginning in April 2023.  This would 
result in an incremental increase in the SC-3A RDM rate of $0.63 per kW per 
month during the 27-month period. Any principal amounts remaining at the end 
of the proposed recovery period due to over/under recoveries will be rolled into 
the RDM imbalance to be recovered or refunded in the subsequent year. 
The Company calculated estimated bill impacts for the SC-3 and SC-3A scenarios as 
shown in Exhibit 1 of this filing.  The calculation of the proposed RDM surcharge rates are shown 
in Exhibit 2 of this filing.

communications.

For the reasons set forth above, Niagara Mohawk respectfully requests that the
