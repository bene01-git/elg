Q. 
Has the Commission prohibited IOU ownership of generation because of a 
7

9

10 
market power concerns with utility ownership of generation.  Thereafter, the 
11 
Commission examined this issue in the specific context of IOU generation 
12

13 
their monopolies over the transmission and distribution systems in their service 
14 
territories.  The Commission adopted its VMP Policy Statement in Case 96-E-0900, 
15 
which established a rebuttable presumption of vertical market power and, unless 
16 
rebutted or otherwise mitigated and beneficial to ratepayers, requires the separation of 
17 
generation from utilities owning transmission to eliminate the potential that IOUs 
18 
could exercise vertical market power to the detriment of wholesale competitive 
19 
electricity markets and consumers.  As the Commission explained, vertical market 
20 
power “occurs when an entity that has market power in one stage of the production
