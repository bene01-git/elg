Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
Pursuant to the terms of Appendix P (Customer Service Provisions) to the Joint Proposal 
approved in the Order Adopting Joint Proposal, issued October 12, 2023 in Cases 22-E-
0317 et al. (the “2023 Joint Proposal”), New York State Electric & Gas Corporation 
(“NYSEG” or the “Company”) hereby petition the State of New York Public Service 
Commission (the “Commission”) to approve exemption of certain events from the Percent of 
Estimated Bills metric portion of the Company’s Customer Service Performance Indicators 
(“CSPI”) during January 2024.  Specifically, the Company requests authorization to exclude 
the events outside of the Company’s control as defined in Appendix P.I of the 2023 Joint 
Proposal occurring during the period January 10, 2024 through January 24,  2024.  As more 
particularly explained below, during this period, severe weather created abnormal operating 
conditions that affected NYSEG’s  ability to safely perform meter reading activities 
measured by the Percent Estimated Bills metric in NYSEG’s CSPI.   
 
Background 
Appendix P of the 2023 Joint Proposal sets forth the CSPI specific metrics, targets and 
potential associated negative revenue adjustments (“NRAs”) as well as the reporting 
requirements associated with the NYSEG and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation. The 
2023 Joint Proposal  states that performance is measured on a calendar year basis and, if 
NYSEG fails to meet any of the established reliability performance targets during any

calendar year, it would be subject to the associated NRAs established in the 2023 Joint

Appendix P.I to the 2023 Joint Proposal permits the Companies to seek exclusion of events 
outside of the Company’s control that could adversely affect the Company’s ability to meet the 
established customer service performance measure targets. The 2023 Joint Proposal  
provides: 
Exclusions - Abnormal operating conditions, including during any 
period of catastrophe, strike, natural disaster, major storm, or other 
unusual event or circumstance not in the Companies’ control 
affecting more than ten percent of the customers in an operating 
area. A “major storm” will have the same definition as set forth in 16 
NYCRR Part 97. In the event abnormal operating conditions affect 
the Companies’ ability to perform any activity that is measured by 
the below Customer Service Performance Indicators (“CSPI”) 
metrics, NYSEG and RG&E may petition for exclusion of the 
performance data associated with such an event. The petition shall 
include justification for the exclusion, as well as a detailed analysis 
of the Companies’ performance prior to and subsequent to the 
event.  
NYSEG addresses below each of the enumerated conditions that must be met for an event to 
qualify for an exemption from the CSPI metric.   
Qualifying Event 
As specified in the above provision of the 2023 Joint Proposal, exclusions are defined as 
abnormal operating conditions, including during any period of catastrophe, strike, natural 
disaster, major storm, or other unusual event or circumstance not in the Company’s control 
affecting more than ten percent of the customers in an operating area.  During the period  
January 10, 2024 through January 24, 2024, the Company faced two Major Storms, as that 
term is defined in 16 NYCRR Part 97. The first Major Storm starting on January 10, 2024, 
impacted multiple divisions across the Company’s service operating area, with peak outages 
reaching 70,000 customers throughout NYSEG with 106 internal wire guards on fulltime 
storm duty focusing their efforts on protecting life and property with 889 total wires down. 
The most significantly impacted divisions were Lancaster, Auburn, Geneva, Ithaca, as 
shown in the charts below, all available resources were focused on protecting life and 
property during this Major Storm event:

Additionally, from January 13th through January 18th  a rare bombogenesis (a rapid 
intensification of a cyclone or low-pressure system) moved over the Lancaster Division 
leaving over 5-6 feet of snow in parts of the territory. This rare Major Storm Event impacted 
all 171k customer in our Lancaster division, which is 14% of the total NYSEG customer 
base. As the extreme weather and conditions impacting the Company’s ability to read 
meters continued throughout the week, our ability to safely travel and read customers’ 
meters were severely restricted due to the heavy volume of snow in the area through 
January 24, 2024. 
Proposed Action  
For the above stated reasons, NYSEG requests that the Commission approve the exclusion 
of the identified events during the specified periods from the Company’s Percent of 
Estimated Bills metric from the CSPI as listed in Attachments 1 and 2 to this petition.  
Additionally, as part of such approval, the Company requests that the Commission 
specifically find that the exclusion exemption from the Percent of Estimated Bills calculation 
for the CSPI performance includes the major storm events for the following NYSEG 
divisions and dates and that by approving such exclusion, the identified events should not 
be included in the calculation of any potential NRA: 
 
• 
Lancaster – 1/10/2024 – 1/24/2024 
• 
Auburn – 1/11/2024 -1/12/2024 
• 
Geneva -  1/10/2024 – 1/12/2024
