RECOMMENDATION:  Further Suspension of the Effective Date of the 
Major Rate Changes. 
 
 
 
On January 28, 2022, Consolidated Edison Company of 
New York, Inc. (Con Edison or the Company) filed amendments to 
its tariff schedules, P.S.C. Nos. 10 and 12 – Electricity and 
P.S.C. No. 9 – Gas, to increase its annual electric and gas 
delivery revenues for the rate year ending December 31, 2023.  
 
Con Edison is requesting an increase in annual 
electric delivery revenues of approximately $1.2 billion (17.6% 
increase in delivery revenues, or 11.2% increase in total 
revenues).  The requested increase in electric delivery revenues 
results in an average residential monthly delivery bill increase 
of $20.90 (18.8% increase on the delivery bill, or a 13.2% 
increase on the total bill) for a 600 kWh per month customer.

For its gas business, Con Edison is requesting an 
increase in annual gas delivery revenues of approximately $500 
million (28.1% increase in delivery revenues, or 18.2% increase 
in total revenues).  The Company projects that the requested 
increase in gas delivery revenues will result in a total monthly 
bill increase of about $37.88 (26.8% increase on the delivery 
bill, or 19.1% increase on the total bill) for a typical 
residential heating customer using 100 therms per month.  
 
 
 
By Notice issued February 9, 2022, the proposed 
amendments were suspended through June 26, 2022.  The 
investigation of these proceedings is continuing.   
 
 
 
Pending the proceedings and decision on the proposed 
changes, the operation of these amendments is further suspended, 
and the use of the rates, charges, rules, and regulations are 
deferred through December 26, 2022, unless otherwise ordered by 
the Commission.  The rates, charges, rules, and regulations 
altered by the suspended amendments shall continue in effect 
until these proceedings are concluded or until the period of  
further suspension and any extended suspension expires, unless 
otherwise ordered by the Commission. 
 
 
 
Con Edison is directed to file with the Commission not 
later than June 21, 2022, consecutively numbered supplements 
announcing suspension of the amendments.
