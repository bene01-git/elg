Cost of Debt 
6 
Q. 
Please describe the Staff Finance Panel’s recommendations regarding the cost of 
7 
KEDNY’s planned issuance of long-term debt in the Rate Year. 
8 
A. 
The Staff Finance Panel recommends (at 48-49) an interest rate of 3.24 percent for 
9 
KEDNY’s planned issuance of $400 million of new long-term debt in the Rate Year, 
10 
compared to the 3.83 percent rate that I recommended in my direct testimony.  The Staff 
11 
Finance Panel does not agree with the proposed use of forecast Treasury yields to 
12 
determine the interest rate of the planned Rate Year debt issuance.  The Staff Finance 
13 
Panel’s recommended rate of 3.24 percent for this debt issuance is based on the June 
14 
2019 yield for 10-year Treasury debt of 2.07 percent, plus KEDNY’s proposed credit 
15 
spread of 1.17 percent.  The Staff Finance Panel recommends updating its proposed 
16 
approach to determining the cost of debt with more current data prior to the 
17 
Commission’s decision.

V. 
Service Company Asset Recovery Charge 
14 
Q. 
Do you agree with the Staff Information Technology Panel’s rate of return 
15 
recommendation for Service Company? 
16 
A. 
No,  I do not.  I disagree with the Staff Information Technology Panel’s conclusion that 
17 
the Companies’ weighted average cost of capital is the appropriate rate of return for 
18 
Service Company.  The Staff Information Technology Panel ignores the fact that Service 
19 
Company maintains a capital structure that is separate and distinct from KEDNY and 
20 
KEDLI.  Moreover, Service Company’s cost of long-term debt is lower than Niagara 
21 
Mohawk Power Corporation’s cost of long-term debt.  Therefore, the proposed rate of 
22 
return for Service Company is reasonable and conservative.
