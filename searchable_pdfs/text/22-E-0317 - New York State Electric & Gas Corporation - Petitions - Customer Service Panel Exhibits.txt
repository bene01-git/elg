Messaging for Customers – Overview
Background 
Upon the New York State Public Service Commission’s issuance of an order approving the 
Proposal by New York State Electric & Gas (NYSEG) and Rochester Gas and Electric (RG&E) 
on November 19, 2020, the companies agreed to file an updated Outreach Plan within 60 days. 
Winter weather can affect heating bills and COVID-19 also creates challenges for some 
customers. This plan outlines the ways the companies notify customers of potential assistance 
available to help with their utility bill, including COVID-19 relief. 
. The plan is presented with three areas of focus: 
-
Winter Heating Season and Energy Management
-
COVID-19 Relief
-
Payment Assistance
NYSEG and RG&E will continue to engage in customer advocate communications, including 
expanded community and agency outreach to ensure new groups of vulnerable populations are 
aware of internal and external assistance programs and payment options available.

credit is available to residential customers that participate in our low-income programs or

Community Outreach through our Customer Advocates 
With the COVID-19 pandemic and its economic impacts on our communities, NYSEG and 
RG&E’s Customer Advocates are working to reach our most vulnerable customers. The 
continued efforts and focus for this group is to provide education and resources to our most 
vulnerable customers.  
•
Community Relations:  With the use of technology, our Customer Advocates have set
up community action groups and inter-agency meetings to identify community needs. In
addition, agency lists have been created for every territory in both NYSEG and RG&E to
continue to strengthen the relationships with human service agencies within our service
areas.
•
Meetings and Focus Groups: The Customer Advocacy group will continue to organize
and attend virtual meeting and events. This includes establishing focus groups with the
human service agencies to improve customers’ opportunities to get the help they need.

Methodology for Determining FTE Requirements

NYSEG & RGE utilized Genesys WFM tool to calculate staffing requirements

Genesys is a world leader in contact center technology.  Their WFM software uses a
proprietary calculation to generate staffing requirements based on parameters including call
volume, agent handle time and answer rate requirements.

Historical arrival patterns for call volume and AHT were compiled from the ACD reporting
application

Daily and half-hour volumes

Normalizing of call volume and AHT for abnormal days (Holidates, storms, etc.)

Volume and AHT data is input into WFM software – Genesys WFM

WFM software provides interval and daily FTE requirements based upon the configured volume,
AHT and Service Level target parameters. These requirements are commonly referred to as base
or unshrunk requirements

A shrinkage factor is then applied the requirements to determine total staffing need.

Shrinkage defines time where representatives are unable to take phone calls. Shrinkage
accounts for time away from phones for reasons including, but not limited to planned or
unplanned absenteeism, meetings, training, and required breaks.
