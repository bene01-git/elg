Q.

2

3 
A. 
DRPs are programs designed to reduce customer 
4 
usage while maintaining adequate firm service.  
5 
Historically, the most successful DRP is

12

13 
reduction to their firm rates.  Central Hudson 
14 
already has this program.  In addition, new DRP 
15 
efforts are developing that can reduce gas use 
16 
of firm customers during cold weather.  Typical 
17 
programs are thermostat set-back programs that 
18 
allow a customer to reduce usage on certain 
19 
hours of the day when use is at a maximum and 
20 
commercial/industrial use reduction programs 
21 
where a large customer agrees to be paid to

reduce its usage when called upon while still
