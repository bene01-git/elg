"Commission") revised tariff leaves with an effective date of April 1, 2020 (the "Rate Filing"). 
Using as a base period the 12 months ended September 30, 2019, the revised tariff leaves would 
result in an increase in revenues from the Company's gas operations of $6,255,926 in the Rate 
Year ("Rate Year 1," the 12-month period ending January 31, 2022). The Company proposed a 
three-year rate plan encompassing the initial Rate Year (Rate Year 1) and the two succeeding 
Rate Years (the 12-month periods ending January 31, 2023 and 2024), with rate increases of
