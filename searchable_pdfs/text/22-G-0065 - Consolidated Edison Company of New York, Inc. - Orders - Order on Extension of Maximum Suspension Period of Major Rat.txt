BY THE COMMISISON: 
 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On January 28, 2022, Consolidated Edison Company of 
New York, Inc. (Con Edison or the Company) filed amendments to 
its tariff schedules, P.S.C. Nos. 10 and 12 – Electricity and 
P.S.C. No. 9 – Gas, to increase its annual electric and gas 
delivery revenues for the rate year ending December 31, 2023.  
Con Edison requested an increase in annual electric delivery 
revenues of approximately $1.2 billion (17.6 percent increase in

delivery revenues, or 11.2 percent increase in total revenues) 
and an increase in annual gas delivery revenues of approximately 
$500 million (28.1 percent increase in delivery revenues, or 
18.2 percent increase in total revenues).   
 
 
A Joint Proposal was filed in the above-captioned 
proceedings on February 16, 2023, which would establish new 
electric and gas rate plans for a three-year period from 
January 1, 2023, through December 31, 2025.  If adopted by the 
Commission, the Joint Proposal would provide levelized increases 
in electric delivery revenues of $457.5 million for each rate 
year, with a three-year cumulative total electric delivery bill 
revenue increase of 19.8 percent and total electric bill 
increase of 12.5 percent; and levelized increases in gas 
delivery revenues of $187.2 million for each rate year, with a 
three-year cumulative total gas delivery bill revenue increase 
of 31.3 percent and a total gas bill increase of 20.2 percent.  
The actual bill impacts of the proposed changes on specific 
customer classes will vary based upon revenue allocation and 
rate design.   
 
 
By Notices issued by the Secretary to the Commission 
dated February 9, 2022, and May 25, 2022, the Secretary pursuant 
to authority delegated to her,1 suspended the captioned 
proceedings through December 26, 2022.  By order issued 
November 21, 2022, the Commission, on consent of the Company, 
further suspended the captioned proceedings through April 25, 
2023.2  By this Order, the Commission extends the suspension of 
the captioned proceedings through July 24, 2023.

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
On November 1, 2022, December 22, 2022, and January 13, 
2023, the Company filed its consent to extend the maximum 
suspension period of these proceedings through July 24, 2023.  
The Company requested the extension to provide additional time 
for parties to continue settlement discussions and to consider 
the Joint Proposal filed on February 16, 2023, in these 
proceedings.  The request is subject to a “make whole” provision 
that the Company states would keep Con Edison and its customers 
in the same position as they would have been absent the 
extension.  The Company’s letters stated they understand “make 
whole” to mean the Company would recover or refund any revenue 
under-collections, respectively, including interest, resulting 
from the extended suspension period.  The Company would calculate 
any revenue adjustments as the difference between sales revenues 
Con Edison would have billed at the new rates during the 
extension of the suspension period and the same level of sales 
revenues at current rates.  The revenue adjustments would include 
all applicable surcharges and would be subject to reconciliation 
in accordance with all applicable adjustment mechanisms 
(including revenue decoupling mechanisms, where applicable).  In 
addition, Con Edison proposes the amortization of net deferrals 
reflected in the Commission order will commence effective with 
the month of January 2023, on an earnings neutral basis. 
 
 
To facilitate the orderly conduct of these 
proceedings, the effective date of the tariff leaves filed by 
the Company is further extended through July 24, 2023, unless 
otherwise ordered by the Commission.

The Commission orders: 
1. 
The operation of the matters suspended by order 
issued November 21, 2022, in these proceedings, is further 
suspended and the use of the rates, charges, rules and 
regulations stated in said Notices be further extended through 
July 24, 2023, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission. 
2. 
Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. is 
directed to file with the Commission, on not less than one day’s 
notice on or before April 24, 2023, consecutively numbered 
supplements announcing the extension of the suspension period of 
suspended amendments.  
3. 
Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. 
shall be made whole for the period between January 1, 2023, and 
the date the Commission sets new rates, charges, rules and 
regulations in the final decision in these proceedings. 
4. 
These proceedings are continued.
