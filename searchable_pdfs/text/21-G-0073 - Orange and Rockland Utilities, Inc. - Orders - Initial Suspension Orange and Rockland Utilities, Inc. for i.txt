On January 29, 2021, Orange and Rockland Utilities, 
Inc. (Orange and Rockland or the Company) filed amendments to its 
tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 3 – Electricity and P.S.C. No. 4 - 
Gas, to increase the annual electric and gas delivery rates for 
the rate year ending December 31, 2022.

Orange and Rockland seeks an increase in annual 
revenues for electric delivery of approximately $24.5 million 
inclusive of gross receipts tax, 5.8% increase in delivery 
revenues, or 3.3% increase in total revenues).1  The requested 
increase in electric delivery revenues results in an average 
residential monthly bill increase of $5.95 (7.5% increase on the 
delivery bill, or 4.9% increase on the total bill) for an 
average 600 kWh per month customer.2  The major cost drivers of 
the electric revenue request include infrastructure costs 
associated with return on rate base and depreciation on plant 
additions, reduced sales, and increases to labor expense. 
 
The Company seeks an increase in annual revenues for 
gas delivery of approximately $9.8 million (inclusive of gross 
receipts tax, 6.9% increase in delivery revenues, or 4.0% 
increase in total revenues).3  The requested increase in gas 
delivery revenues results in an average residential monthly bill 
increase of $7.81 (9.1% increase on the delivery bill, or 5.8% 
increase on the total bill) for an average 100 Ccf per month 
customer.4  The major cost drivers of this rate filing include 
infrastructure costs associated with return on rate base and 
depreciation on plant additions, and reduced sales. 5

- 3 -  
The proposal has an effective date of February 28, 2021. 
Orange and Rockland’s amendments require a hearing.  The Public 
Service Commission delegated its authority to suspend the 
effective date of the amendments to tariff schedules and to 
institute proceedings to the Secretary to the Commission.  Without 
answer or other formal pleading, proceedings are instituted 
concerning the propriety of the rates, charges, rules, and 
regulations contained in the amendments to Schedules P.S.C. No. 3 
– Electricity and P.S.C. No. 4 - Gas, listed in the Appendix to 
this Notice. 
 
 
     Pending the proceeding and decision on the proposed 
changes, the operation of these amendments is suspended, and the 
use of the rates, charges, rules, and regulations are deferred 
through June 26, 2021, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission.  The rates, charges, rules, and regulations altered 
by the suspended amendments shall continue in effect until this 
proceeding is concluded or until the suspension period 
(including the initial suspension and any extended suspension) 
expires, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission. 
 
 
     Orange and Rockland is directed to file with the 
Commission not later than February 26, 2021, consecutively 
numbered supplements announcing suspension of the amendments.
