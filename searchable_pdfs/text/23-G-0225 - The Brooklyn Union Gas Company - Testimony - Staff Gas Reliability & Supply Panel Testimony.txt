Q.

A. 
As stated in the U.S. Energy Information

Administration’s Glossary, renewable energy

resources are those that are naturally

24

action, and tidal action.  RNG supplies are

those produced from renewable biomass, such as

that is available at any point in time.

1

20

will have a lower explosive limit somewhere in

between that for hydrogen and natural gas.  In
