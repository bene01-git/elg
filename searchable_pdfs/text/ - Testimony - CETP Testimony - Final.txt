Q.  What are the CLCPA’s clean energy requirements related to 
1 
electricity? 
2 
A.  
The CLCPA requires 70% of statewide electric generation 
3 
to be renewable by 2030 and 100% of the statewide 
4 
electrical demand system to be zero-emissions by 2040. In 
5 
addition, the CLCPA requires the Commission to develop 
6 
programs to procure 9 GW of offshore wind by 2035, and 10 
7 
GW of distributed solar by 2030. While the CLCPA requires 
8 
that 3 GW of energy storage be procured by 2030, New York 
9 
State is working to achieve 6 GW of energy storage by 
10 
2030.  
11 
Q.  What are the CLCPA’s emissions reduction requirements? 
12 
A.  The CLCPA establishes statewide, economy-wide emissions 
13
