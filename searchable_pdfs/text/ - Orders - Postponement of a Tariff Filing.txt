SUBJECT: CASE 16-G-0059 – Proceeding on Motion of the

Regulations of The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a 
National Grid NY for Gas Service. 
 
 
POSTPONEMENT of a tariff filing by The Brooklyn Union 
Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY to Revise Its  
Paperless Billing Credit for Customers that Choose 
Electronic Billing. 
 
SUMMARY OF 
RECOMMENDATION:  Staff recommends approval of the postponement 
to September 1, 2019.       
 
 
 
On March 1, 2019, The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a 
National Grid NY (KEDNY or the Company) filed a proposed 
amendment to its gas tariff schedule, P.S.C. No. 12 – Gas, to 
revise the paperless billing credit for customers that choose 
paperless billing.  The Company is proposing to adjust the 
paperless bill credit rate from $0.49 per service period to 
$0.41 per service period to reflect the increased costs of 
KEDNY’s newer electronic billing system.  The proposed amendment 
has an effective date of July 1, 2019.

KEDNY has agreed to postpone the effective date of the 
proposed amendment to September 1, 2019, to allow additional 
time for review of the proposed changes.  On May 14, 2019, the 
Company filed a supplement to implement the postponement and 
requested a waiver of the requirements of newspaper publication 
of the change proposed by the supplement.   
   
         It is recommended that: 
1. Supplement No. 91 to Schedule P.S.C. No. 12 – Gas be 
allowed to go into effect on June 14, 2019; and 
 
2. special permission be granted waiving the 
requirements of Public Service Law §66(12)(b) and 
16 NYCRR §720-8.1 as to newspaper publication of the 
change proposed by the supplement.
