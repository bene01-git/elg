B. Inside Service Line Inspections Program 
13 
Q. 
Please describe the Inside Service Line Inspection Program and what is 
14 
required to implement the new mandated inside service line inspection 
15 
intervals. 
16 
A. 
In 2015, the Commission issued an Order that adopted a new definition of “service 
17 
line” in New York (Case 14-G-0357).  Under the former definition in New York’s 
18 
pipeline safety regulations (16 NYCRR section 255.3(29)), the service line 
19 
extended “to the first accessible fitting” inside the customer’s building where a 
20 
meter is located.  Under the new definition, the service line now extends “to the 
21 


--- Page 20 ---
 
 
 
18 of 64 
outlet of the customer meter or at the connection to a customer’s piping, whichever 
1 
is further downstream where a meter is located within the building.”  While the 
2 
Commission adopted the new service line definition in 2015, it stayed 
3 
implementation pending development of a plan to address the new leak survey and 
4 
corrosion inspection requirements for inside facilities.  In April 2017, the 
5 
Commission issued its “Order Establishing Statewide Inspection Schedules and 
6 
Procedural Requirements” in Case 15-G-0244 (“Inside Service Inspection Order”) 
7 
that established mandated leak survey and corrosion inspection intervals for inside 
8 
services.  Citing the 2015 explosion on 2nd Avenue in Manhattan, which involved 
9 
unauthorized tampering with utility gas facilities on the inside of a building, the 
10 
Inside Service Line Inspection Order set an aggressive schedule for utilities to 
11 
complete baseline leak surveys and inspections, and shorter continuous inspection 
12 
intervals thereafter.  For example, for inside services located in business districts, 
13 
leak surveys were to be completed by the end of CY 2018 and re-inspections are 
14 
required to be completed once every year thereafter.  For non-business districts, 
15 
baseline leak surveys are to be completed by April of 2020, and re-inspections must 
16 
occur every three years thereafter.  Baseline corrosion inspections, regardless of 
17 
location, also must be completed by April of 2020, and must be repeated on three-
18 
year intervals.  The Companies began to ramp-up implementation of the Inside 
19 
Service Line Inspection Program during CY 2017 and CY 2018.  Additional work 
20 
is required to timely complete baseline and ongoing inspections.     
21
