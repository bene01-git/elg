A.L.J. SAYRE:  I call case 20-G-0394 
proceeding on motion of the Commission as to the 
rates, charges, rules and regulations of Corning 
Natural Gas Corporation for gas service.  Good 
evening and welcome.  We are here this evening for a 
Public Statement Hearing that was noticed on October 
1, 2021.   
This Public Statement Hearing concerns 
a rate case filing by Corning Natural Gas Corporation 
on July 16th, 2021 and updated on September 30th.  As 
updated, the proposed annual increase would be 
approximately six point six million dollars for the 
rate year twelve months ending June 30th, 2023, and 
an additional one million for the next rate year 
ending June 30th, 2024 and an additional eight 
hundred and fifty thousand dollars in the third-rate 
year.   
And the company would not be permitted 
to file another rate case for the three-year period.
