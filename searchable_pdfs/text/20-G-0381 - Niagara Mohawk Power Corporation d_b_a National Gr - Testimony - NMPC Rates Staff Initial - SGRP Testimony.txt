Q. 
Please explain what an NPA is and how NPAs are 
2 
used for planning purposes. 
3 
A. 
NPAs are alternative solutions used to delay, 
4 
reduce, or eliminate the need for traditional 
5 
capital investments to reinforce or expand gas 
6 
distribution infrastructure, and planned and 
7 
implemented in order to meet future demand or 
8 
reliability needs of a gas utility system. 
9 
Q. 
Why should NPAs be a key component of the 
10 
Company’s strategy to maintain reliable service 
11 
for its firm customers? 
12 
A. 
Building new upstream capacity and efforts to 
13 
enhance or expand natural gas distribution 
14 
systems run counter to environmental concerns 
15 
associated with the combustion of fossil fuels 
16 
such as natural gas.  NPAs can be an opportunity 
17 
for a utility to provide alternative solutions 
18 
to typical traditional capital projects in order 
19 
to meet new customer energy demands.
