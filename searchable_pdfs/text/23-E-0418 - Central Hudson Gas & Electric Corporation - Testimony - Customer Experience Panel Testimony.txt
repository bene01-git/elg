Q.    
Why does the Monthly Meter Reading Plan propose a phased roll out? 
1 
A. 
A phased roll out of monthly meter reading is designed to protect 
2 
customers by limiting any programming defects discovered within the “real 
3 
world environment” to the smallest group of customers possible.  The 
4 
intent of the phased roll-out would be to first implement the program for a 
5 
very specific subset of customers, then to expand the program to an initial 
6 
meter reading route, and next to each of the Company’s five districts, until 
7 
full scale implementation is achieved.   
8 
Q.    
Please describe the incremental annual costs and resources required to 
9 
implement monthly meter reading. 
10 
A. 
The incremental annual costs equate to approximately $4.4 million.  These 
11 
incremental costs are comprised of recuring and one-time operating and 
12 
maintenance (“O&M”) costs as well the return on and of approximately 
13 
$1.8 million of capital expenditures.    
14 
 Annual O&M costs related to the transition to monthly meter reading 
15 
will be approximately $4.0 million.  The majority of this cost is 
16 
attributable to additional meter reading resources, with the small 
17 
remainder driven by maintenance costs of meter reading hardware 
18 
and maintenance and fuel costs for meter reading vehicles.  Meter 
19 
reading resources are assumed to be fulfilled by both external 
20
