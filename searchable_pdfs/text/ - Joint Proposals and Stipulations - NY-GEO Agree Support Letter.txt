New York Geothermal Organization (NY-GEO) Letter in Support of AGREE’s 
Motion for Post-Hearing Briefs and Sur-replies
Dear Administrative Law Judges Costello and Leary:
New York Geothermal Organization (NY-GEO) submits this letter in support of 
AGREE and Sane Energy’s joint motion seeking leave to file post-hearing briefs 
(and alternatively sur-reply statements in opposition to the Joint Proposal). In 
our letter supporting the joint proposal, NY-GEO identified grave concern that, 
relative to gas utilities, New York State is not currently on track to adequately 
contribute to meeting the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act 
(CLCPA) legal mandates to reduce carbon emissions by 40% by 2030 and 85% 
by 2050. AGREE’s request is related to NY-GEO’s concern and we believe 
additional information resulting from this motion will be important in this and 
subsequent cases. Given the many unique aspects of this KEDNY/KEDLI rate 
case NY-GEO believes the request is reasonable and will result in a more
