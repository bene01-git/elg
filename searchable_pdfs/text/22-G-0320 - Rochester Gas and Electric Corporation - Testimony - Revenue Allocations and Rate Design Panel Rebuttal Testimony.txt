B. Standby Rates 
1 
Q.    
Please summarize Nucor’s and MI’s concerns with the Companies’ recommendation to 
2 
extend the applicability of the RDM to Standby customers at both NYSEG and RG&E. 
3 
A. 
In regard to Standby customers being included in the RDM, Mr. Pollock, on behalf of 
4 
MI, states on page 31 of his testimony that  
5 
...the use of standby service, and the corresponding operating 
6 
revenues, will vary from month to month and from year to year. 
7 
Thus, standby service is non-reoccurring.  Therefore, standby 
8 
service is not conducive to an RDM, which requires accurate sales 
9 
and revenue forecasting.  It is even less conducive when the 
10 
corresponding parent service class is also prone to significant 
11 
forecasting errors.   
12 
Nucor generally agrees with the Companies’ recommendation to include standby 
13 
customers and their associated revenues in the respective class RDMs but not for SC 7-4. 
14 
Q.    
Please comment on these statements.   
15 
A. 
The Companies continue to propose extending the applicability of the RDM to Standby 
16 
Service customers.  Including Standby Service customers in the RDM is consistent with 
17 
other utilities that currently include Standby Service customers in the RDM (National 
18 
Grid, Central Hudson and Orange & Rockland) or have proposed to include Standby 
19 
Service Customers in the RDM (Consolidated Edison).  Furthermore, it is not equitable 
20 
for certain customers in a service class to be included in the RDM while others in the 
21 
same service class are not.  Under both Companies’ current tariffs, demand-metered 
22 
customers without on-site generation that opt to select the applicable rates under NYSEG 
23 
Service Class No. 11 – Standby Service or RG&E Service Class No. 14 – Standby 
24 
Service in lieu of the rates under their Otherwise Applicable Service Classification 
25

(“OASC”) are subject to the RDM Adjustment based on their OASC.  This provision is a 
1 
requirement of the Commission’s Order on Standby and Buyback Service Rate Design 
2 
and Establishing Optional Demand-Based Rates, issued and effective May 16, 2019 in 
3 
Case 15-E-0751.  All other customers, i.e., those with on-site generation, that are billed 
4 
under NYSEG Service Class No. 11 or RG&E Service Class No. 14 are excluded from 
5 
the RDM.  Including Standby Service customers in the RDM will provide consistency
