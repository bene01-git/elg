was adopted by the Commission in the Order Adopting Terms of Joint Proposal and Establishing 
Gas Rate Plans (issued and effective December 16, 2016) in the above-captioned matters.  The 
corrections are minor and do not impact the revenue requirements or terms of the Joint Proposal.   
 
Revised appendices are attached to this letter.  The corrections are as follows: 
 
KEDNY Appendix 6, Schedule 14 and KEDLI Appendix 7, Schedule 12 
 
• These appendices contain the rate allowance for the city/state construction deferral 
and examples of the reconciliation.  There was a formula error for calendar years 
2018 and 2019 on page 1 of the original appendices, resulting in only 11 months of 
depreciation expense in the columns labeled (c) and (j).  The revisions correct this 
error and include 12 months of depreciation expense.   
 
KEDLI Appendix 7, Schedule 7 
 
• On page 2, the column labeled (d) in the original appendix did not correctly total 
columns (a) through (c).  Column (d) has been corrected in the revised appendix. 
 
The Companies have notified the Signatory Parties of the proposed corrections and received 
no objection.
