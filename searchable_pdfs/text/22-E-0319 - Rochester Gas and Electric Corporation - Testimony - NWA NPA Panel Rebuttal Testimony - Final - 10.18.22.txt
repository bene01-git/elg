III. 
NON-WIRES ALTERNATIVES AND NON-PIPES ALTERNATIVES 
1 
Q.    
Staff’s Sustainable Energy Panel suggests on pages 89-91 of its testimony that there may 
2 
be situations which require Staff and the utility to agree on “reasonable certainty” of 
3 
NWA/NPA portfolio costs without an executed contract in place.  Do the Companies 
4 
have any concerns with respect to Staff’s position on this matter? 
5 
A. 
Yes.  The Companies’ position is that “reasonable certainty” of NWA/NPA portfolio 
6 
costs cannot be sufficiently established without a fully executed contract in place with 
7 
another party or parties (e.g., developer(s), end use customer(s), etc.).  In the event a 
8 
project requires a longer lead time or incremental procurements over multiple years, the 
9 
Companies would still execute a contract with another party prior to proceeding with 
10 
project implementation.  For staged procurements, the Companies would endeavor to 
11 
have a contract in place for the first phase or stage of the project before establishing 
12 
reasonable certainty of the project costs. 
13 
In the Companies’ experience, NWA/NPA project and/or portfolio pricing can 
14 
significantly change based on a number of factors leading up to contract execution 
15 
including, but not limited to, changing market conditions (e.g., product availability, 
16 
supply chain issues, inflation rates, etc.), and additional costs associated with the 
17 
developer’s requirements necessary to adhere to terms and conditions set forth in the 
18 
NWA/NPA agreement identified during the contract negotiation process.  It is unlikely 
19 
the Companies will have “reasonable certainty” of project costs and confirmation if the 
20 
project is advancing prior to execution of a contract with another party or parties (e.g., 
21 
developer(s), end use customer(s), etc.).
