Q.

7 
A.

14 
Q.

15 
A. 
For purposes of this measure, ‘unprotected’ 
16 
means that the pipe lacks adequate cathodic 
17 
protection, rendering it susceptible to 
18 
corrosion.  It is also commonly referred to as 
19 
‘bare steel’ pipe. Cathodic protection is a 
20 
method by which steel pipelines are protected 
21 
from corrosion by making the surface a cathode, 
22 
thereby inhibiting the formation of iron oxides 
23 
that can result in reduced wall thickness of the 
24 


--- Page 22 ---
Cases 20-E-0428 and 20-G-0429   Staff Gas Pipeline Safety Panel 
 
 
21 
 
pipe.  Unprotected pipe often has no surface 
1 
coating or inadequate coating, rendering efforts 
2 
to cathodically protect the pipe ineffective and 
3 
uneconomical.   
4 
Q. 
How does the leak prone pipe removal program add 
5 
to the safety of the Company’s gas system?  
6 
A. 
Leaks on underground piping can create safety 
7 
risks to the public and can potentially lead to 
8 
gas-related incidents, such as fires or more 
9 
serious events that could impact health and 
10 
safety, especially under frost conditions.  Leak 
11 
prone pipe generally leaks at a higher rate than 
12 
coated and cathodically protected steel or 
13 
plastic pipes.  The removal of leak prone pipe 
14 
reduces these safety risks.  
15 
Q. 
Explain the importance of removing unprotected 
16 
or bare steel and cast-iron pipe.  
17 
A. 
Unprotected or bare steel pipes are more prone 
18 
to leakage because they are more susceptible to 
19 
corrosion.  Removal of these pipes reduce risks 
20 
associated with leakage.  As for cast iron pipe, 
21 
it is brittle, susceptible to graphitization (a 
22 
form of corrosion), and has low beam strength, 
23 
meaning that the material can fail if it is 
24 


--- Page 23 ---
Cases 20-E-0428 and 20-G-0429   Staff Gas Pipeline Safety Panel

ground support, which makes the material

particularly susceptible to stresses resulting

disturbances include ground settlement, freeze-

catastrophic failure than cathodically protected

steel and plastic pipe.  Because cast iron was

potential for leaks and incidents resulting from

its failure.  This will in turn improve the

public safety.
