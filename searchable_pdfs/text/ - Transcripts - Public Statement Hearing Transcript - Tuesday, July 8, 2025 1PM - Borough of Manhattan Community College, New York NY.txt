background on these proceedings.  Consolidated  
Edison Company filed amendments to its electric and 
gas tariff schedules on January 31st, 2025, proposing 
to increase its annual electric and gas delivery 
revenues for the twelve months period ending December 
31st, 2026.  As of its April 10th, 2025, updated 
testimony, Con Edison proposes to increase its 
electric delivery revenues by approximately $1.6 
billion, an 11.3 percent increase in total revenues 
or 17.9 percent increase in base delivery revenues.  
And its natural gas delivery revenues by 
approximately $349 million, a 10.5 increase in total 
revenues or 14.9 percent increase in base delivery 
revenues.  The actual bill impacts of these proposed 
changes on any particular customer class will vary 
based upon revenue allocation and rate design. 
On or about May 30th, 2025, direct 
testimony and exhibits responding to Con Edison's 
rate requests were filed by DPS staff, City of New 
York, assembly member Chris Burdick, assembly member 
MaryJane Shimsky, assembly member Dana Levenberg, 
Amtrak, Alliance for a Green Economy, Consumer Power
