Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
 
In an email dated June 23, 2021 (Request), administrative law judge (ALJ) 
Maureen F. Leary required certain information of The Brooklyn Union Gas Company 
d/b/a National Grid NY (KEDNY), KeySpan Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid 
(KEDLI) (collectively, National Grid or the Companies), and Trial Staff of the Department 
of Public Service (Staff).  This letter provides the information required of Staff. 
 
On May 14, 2021, National Grid filed, on behalf of itself, Staff, and other 
Signatory Parties, a Joint Proposal accompanied by numerous appendices.  As stated 
in the Request, during the June 21, 2021 evidentiary hearing in the above-captioned 
proceedings, some necessary revisions to previously-filed appendices to the Joint 
Proposal were identified.  In order to ensure a clear record, the Request requires

National Grid to file an “entire set of final appendices and schedules.”  The Request also 
requires that National Grid and Staff “review the amended/corrected schedules and 
each separately affirm that they are correct.” 
 
Prior to filing the complete set of revised appendices, the Companies provided a 
copy to Staff for review.  Staff has completed its review and affirms that the complete 
set of appendices filed in DMM by the Companies on July 9, 2021 are correct to the 
best of the Staff team’s knowledge.  Additionally, Staff affirms that the following 
corrections have been made to the appendices and schedules. 
 To remove the erroneous inclusion of $1.9 million of capital spending for 
Metropolitan Reliability Infrastructure (MRI) Phase 5 in rate base: 
1. Appendix 1, Schedule 5, Page 2 has been amended to remove $1.9 
million of capital expenditures associated with MRI Phase 5 in Fiscal Year 
20 (i.e., the 12 months ended March 31, 2020); 
2. Appendix 1, Schedule 3, Page 3 has been added to show the calculation 
of the revenue requirement impacts of the error (i.e., approximately 
$82,000 in Rate Year One, $163,000 in Rate Year Two, and $227,000 in 
Rate Year Three); and 
3. Appendix 1, Schedule 3, Pages 1 and 2 have been amended to decrease 
the deferred credits so that they align with the calculated decrease in the 
revenue requirements. 
 To reflect a September 1, 2021 effective date of new rates: 
1. Appendix 3, Schedule 3 (Summary of Rates) and Schedule 3.1 (Rate 
Design) for KEDNY; 
2. Appendix 3, Schedule 4.1 (Monthly Bill Comparison – RY1 v RY2) and 
Schedule 4.2 (Monthly Bill Comparison - RY2 v RY3) for KEDNY; 
3.  Appendix 3, Schedule 13.2 (Illustrative Example of the Make Whole 
Merchant Function Charge Calculation) for KEDNY; 
4. Appendix 4, Schedule 3 (Summary of Rates) and Schedule 3.1 (Rate 
Design) for KEDLI;  
5. Appendix 4, Schedule 4.1 (Monthly Bill Comparison – RY1 v RY2) and 
Schedule 4.2 (Monthly Bill Comparison - RY2 v RY3) KEDLI; and 
6. Appendix 4, Schedule 13.2 (Illustrative Example of the Make Whole 
Merchant Function Charge Calculation) KEDLI.
