Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“Niagara Mohawk” or 
“Company”) submit for filing corrections and updates to its July 31, 2020 electric and gas base 
rate filings in the above matters.  The attached filings include the corrections and updates testimony 
and revised exhibits of the witnesses identified in Attachment A. 
 
The net impact of the corrections and updates is an increase of $2.913 million to the 
revenue requirement for the electric business and a decrease of $4.684 million to the revenue 
requirement for the gas business.  The primary drivers of the changes to the electric and gas 
revenue requirements are updates to the Company’s revenue forecasts, capital investment plans, 
and O&M expenses.   
 
Exhibit ___ (RRP-1CU) sets forth the updates and reconciliation of the differences in 
revenue requirements between the Company’s original electric and gas filings and as adjusted for 
the corrections and updates presented herein.   
 
To facilitate the parties’ review, the Revenue Requirements Panel provides a summary 
page for each schedule to clearly identify updates and links to supporting workpapers.
