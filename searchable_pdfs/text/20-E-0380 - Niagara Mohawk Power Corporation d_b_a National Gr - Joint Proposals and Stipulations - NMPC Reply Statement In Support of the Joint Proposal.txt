III. 
Contrary To The Suggestions Of Certain Parties, The JP Is Fully Consistent With 
The Intent And Purpose Of The CLCPA And Will Help The State Achieve Its 
Emissions Reductions Goals 
Section 75-0107 of the Environmental Conservation Law sets statewide greenhouse gas 
emission limits of 60 percent of 1990 emission levels by 2030 and 15 percent of emission levels 
by 2050.20  Section 7 of the CLCPA requires New York state agencies to evaluate the impact of 
their administrative determinations on the goals and policies outlined in the Act.21  Specifically, 
Section 7(2) requires agencies to consider whether their administrative decisions “are inconsistent 
with or will interfere with the attainment of the statewide greenhouse gas emissions limits 
established in article 75 of the environmental conservation law.”22  Section 7(3) provides that 
administrative approvals and decisions shall not disproportionately burden disadvantaged 
communities.”23     
In the 2021 KEDNY/KEDLI Rate Order, the Commission determined that the CLCPA 
requires the Commission to evaluate proposed rate plans for consistency with the CLCPA’s 
emissions limits in the context of the Commission’s “core responsibility of ensuring that electric 
and gas corporations maintain safe and adequate service.”24  The Commission recognized that 
“[t]he CLCPA is still a nascent law whose implementation remains a work-in-progress in the State, 
albeit an important one,”25 and that the path that New York will take to implement the CLCPA 
awaits the development of regulations by the New York State Department of Environmental
