To initiate a management and operations audit,

The RFP details the scope of work, deliverables,

and expected audit schedule.  A consultant is

1 
The chosen consultant, the Department, and the 
2 
utility or utilities subject to the audit then 
3 
enter into a multi-party agreement which governs 
4 
the conduct of the audit.  Typically, the 
5

6

report to Staff.  The Commission then releases

the report to the public.
