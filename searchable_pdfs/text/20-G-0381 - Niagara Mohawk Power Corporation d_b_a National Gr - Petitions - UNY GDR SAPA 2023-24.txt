NYS DEPARTMENT OF STATE 
NOTICE OF PROPOSED RULEMAKING 
 
Proposed Gas Demand Response Program 
 
PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION 
 
 
 
Pursuant to the provisions of the State Administrative Procedures Act, NOTICE is hereby 
given to the following proposed rule: 
 
1. 
Proposed Action: 
The Public Service Commission (“Commission”) is considering a 
petition filed by Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (the “Company”) 
regarding modifications to its firm gas demand response programs. 
 
2. 
Statutory Authority Under Which the Rule is Proposed:  Public Service Law Sections 
5, 65, and 66. 
 
3. 
Subject of the Proposed Rule:  Proposed revisions to the Company’s firm gas demand 
response programs for the 2024 - 2025 winter season and going forward. 
 
4. 
Purpose of the Proposed Rule:  To determine whether to authorize the Company’s 
proposed modifications to its firm gas demand response programs and associated tariff leaves. 
 
5. 
Substance of the Proposed Rule:  The Commission is considering a petition (“Petition”) 
filed by Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (the “Company”) on June 15, 
2024 in Case 20-G-0381. The Petition requests approval of modifications to the Company’s firm 
gas demand response programs. The Petition includes draft tariff leaves, which would amend the 
Company’s gas tariff schedule, P.S.C. No. 219. The filing of any modifications to the 
Company’s firm gas demand response program was required by the Commission’s Order 
Authorizing Tariff Amendments Regarding Gas Demand Response Programs For Firm 
Customers (“DR Authorization Order”), issued in Case 20-G-0381 on October 13, 2022. Under 
the firm gas demand programs, the Company can call upon firm customers (i.e., large 
commercial, industrial, and multi-family customers, selected at the Company’s discretion) to 
curtail load when needed. Participants voluntarily choose to enroll in the firm gas demand 
response programs and agree to curtail their natural gas consumption for a defined time-period in 
exchange for an economic incentive. The Company proposes several modifications to the 
elements of its existing 2023-2024 firm gas demand response programs. The proposed 
modifications are set forth in the 2023-2024 Firm Gas Demand Response Annual Report filed 
with the Commission on June 15, 2024 as required by the DR Authorization Order and submitted 
together with the Petition. The Company also submitted draft tariff leaves to effectuate the firm 
gas demand response programs for the 2024-2025 winter season. The Company requests that the 
Commission address the Petition and allow the implementation of the tariff amendments by 
November 1, 2024 for the 2024-2025 winter season. Additionally, the Company requests waiver 
of the newspaper publication requirements under Public Service Law Section 66(12) and 16 
NYRR Section 720-8.1 regarding the ultimate filing of the tariff leaves. The full text of the 
Petition and the full record of the proceedings may be reviewed online at the Department of 
Public Service web page: www.dps.ny.gov. The Commission may adopt, reject, or modify, in 
whole or in part, the action proposed and may resolve related matters.
