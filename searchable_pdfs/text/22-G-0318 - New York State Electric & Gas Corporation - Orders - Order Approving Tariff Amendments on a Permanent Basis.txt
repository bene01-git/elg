BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On October 27, 2023, New York State Electric & Gas 
Corporation (NYSEG) and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation 
(RG&E) (together, the Companies) filed tariff amendments to

Proposal (JP) establishing three-year electric and gas rate 
plans that provided annual revenue requirement increases.2  For

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§ 5, 65(1) and (8), and 66(1) and 
(12), the Commission has the legal authority to review the 
proposed tariff leaves, as well as modify, reject, or approve 
such filed tariffs.  As such, the Commission has the legal 
authority to review the Companies’ filings and approve and make 
effective tariff amendments, as prescribed in this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The Companies’ proposed October 27, 2023, electric and 
gas tariff amendments, as supplemented by the Companies’ further 
revisions filed on November 14, 2023, December 27, 2023, and 
March 18, 2024, properly reflect the directives of the 2023 Rate 
Order.  The filings include the rates, charges, rules, and 
regulations necessary to implement RY 1, as well as rate design 
and other changes described in the 2023 Rate Order.  In 
addition, no comments were received from the parties in these 
proceedings regarding the compliance tariff amendments.  As 
such, the Commission authorizes the tariff amendments listed in

date of issuance of this Order. 
 
 
The Companies assert that the changes made in their 
supplemental filings are either housekeeping in nature or 
clarify language; we agree.  As such, the Commission grants the 
requests to waive the newspaper publication requirements of PSL 
§66(12)(b) and 16 NYCRR §720-8.1 for the filings made on 
November 14, 2023, December 27, 2023, and March 18, 2024. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by New York State 
Electric & Gas Corporation and Rochester Gas and Electric 
Corporation and listed in the Appendix are authorized to become 
effective on a permanent basis upon the issuance of this Order. 
2. The requirements of Public Service Law §66(12)(b) 
and 16 NYCRR §720-8.1 as to the newspaper publication of the 
changes proposed by the November 14, 2023, December 27, 2023, 
and March 18, 2024, filings are waived. 
3. These proceedings are continued.
