III. 
FINANCIAL INTEGRITY 
8 
Q.    
What are Central Hudson’s financial integrity objectives? 
9 
A. 
Central Hudson defines financial integrity as maintaining a financial profile 
10 
that supports a strong credit rating in the “A” category, which in turn 
11 
supports adequate access to capital at reasonable prices.  Central 
12 
Hudson has a long-standing objective and commitment to maintaining a 
13 
sound, creditworthy financial structure.  This commitment is not only 
14 
ideological, but also practical as it balances two key responsibilities: the 
15 
obligation to provide safe and reliable service to our customers and to do 
16 
so at a reasonable price. 
17 
Q.    
Is access to sufficient financial resources during varying economic 
18 
climates critical to the Company’s ability to provide service?  
19 
A. 
Yes.  As we noted, the Company has an obligation to provide safe and 
20 
reliable service to our customers.  In order to meet this obligation, the 
21 
Company must have access to sufficient cash and credit at all times

Q.    
Do you have examples demonstrating why access to cash and credit are 
3 
critical for Central Hudson? 
4 
A. 
Yes.  Central Hudson continues to purchase large quantities of electricity 
5 
and natural gas on behalf of its customers who have not elected to receive 
6 
their supply from an energy service company.  The quantities can vary 
7 
based on customer usage and are often driven by weather-related 
8 
(temperature) end-uses like air conditioning and heating.  The price of 
9 
electricity and natural gas can also vary considerably in response to a 
10 
range of factors, including but not limited to market supply and demand 
11 
conditions.  In the past two years, energy prices have increased by 132% 
12 
and based on market forecasts, are expected to remain above historic 
13 
levels for the foreseeable future.  The supply cost increases negatively 
14 
impact cash flows as the Company pays for supply in advance of the 
15 
collection of receipts from its customers.  Therefore, Central Hudson must 
16 
maintain adequate access to cash or credit to meet its unpredictable and 
17 
varying daily supply needs.  The Company must also maintain financial 
18 
flexibility to serve all customers as the utility is the required provider of last 
19 
resort.  Therefore, the Company must have the financial flexibility to serve 
20 
customers who terminate supply from energy service companies or where 
21 
an energy service company fails, resulting in customers defaulting back to 
22 
supply from Central Hudson.  Additionally, the Company must have 
23

Q.    
How do storms impact Central Hudson’s financial integrity? 
6 
A. 
When Central Hudson expects to experience a severe storm event that 
7 
causes significant service interruptions, it begins mobilization of mutual 
8 
assistance to restore service to its customers as quickly and safely as 
9 
possible.  It is imperative that the Company has immediate and ready 
10 
access to cash to fund these restoration activities.  Any delay in 
11 
restoration caused by limited or a total lack of access to financial 
12 
resources is an unacceptable situation.  Moreover, the ability to 
13 
temporarily finance such outlays at a reasonable cost is critical since 
14 
recovery is not immediate.  Recovery has historically lagged because 
15 
expenditures have significantly exceeded the rate allowance.  Although 
16 
the RAM as approved in the Order in Cases 17-E-0459 and 17-G-0460 
17 
has provided some near term recovery, more timely recovery of these 
18 
operating costs is needed to support financial integrity and prevent further 
19 
deterioration in the Company’s credit ratings.
