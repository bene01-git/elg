INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On July 31, 2020, Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation 
d/b/a National Grid (Niagara Mohawk or the Company) filed 
amendments to its tariff schedules, P.S.C. Nos. 214 and 220 – 
Electricity and P.S.C. No. 219 – Gas, to increase the annual 
electric and gas delivery revenues for the rate year ending 
June 30, 2022.  
Niagara Mohawk requested an electric delivery revenue 
increase of approximately $100.4 million (4.9 percent increase

about $4.53 (9.5 percent on the delivery bill, or a 6.0 percent 
increase on the total bill) for a typical residential heating 
customer using an average of 82 therms per month.

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
On July 21, 2021, and August 13, 2021, the Company 
filed its consent to extend the maximum suspension period of 
these proceedings.  In its August 13, 2021 filing, the Company 
consented to an extension through and including February 28, 
2022.  The Company requested the extension to provide additional 
time to accommodate settlement discussions.  The request is 
subject to a make whole provision that would keep Niagara Mohawk 
and its customers in the same economic position as they would 
have been absent the extension. 
To facilitate the orderly conduct of these 
proceedings, the effective dates of the tariff leaves filed by 
the Company are further extended through and including 
February 28, 2022, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.  
 
The Commission orders: 
1.   The operation of the matter suspended by Order  
issued June 22, 2021, in these proceedings, is further suspended 
and the use of the rates, charges, rules and regulations stated 
in said Order be further extended through February 28, 2022, 
unless otherwise ordered by the Commission. 
2.   Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid  
is directed to file with the Commission, on not less than one 
day’s notice on or before November 23, 2021, consecutively 
numbered supplements announcing the extension of the suspension 
period of suspended amendments.  
3.   Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid 
shall be made whole for the period between July 1, 2021, and the 
date the Commission sets new rates, charges, rules and 
regulations in the final decision in this proceeding.
