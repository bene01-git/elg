BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
On April 28, 2023, The Brooklyn Union Gas Company 
d/b/a National Grid NY (KEDNY) and KeySpan Gas East Corporation 
d/b/a National Grid (KEDLI) (collectively, the Companies) filed 
amendments to their tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 12 – Gas, and 
P.S.C. No. 1 - Gas, respectively, to increase their annual gas 
delivery revenues for the twelve months ending March 31, 2025, 
or the Rate Year. 
KEDNY requested an increase in annual gas delivery 
revenues of approximately $414 million (28 percent increase in

for the Rate Year.  The requested increase in gas delivery

total bill) for an average residential heating customer. 
KEDLI requested an increase in annual gas delivery

the Rate Year.  The requested increase in gas delivery revenues 
results in an average monthly bill increase of about $28.52 
(25.1 percent on the delivery bill or 16.3 percent on the total 
bill) for an average residential heating customer. 
 
 
By Notices issued May 10, 2023, and September 6, 2023, 
the Secretary to the Commission, pursuant to authority delegated 
to her,1 suspended the captioned proceedings through March 31, 
2024, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.  By this 
Order, the Commission extends the suspension of the above-
captioned proceeding through August 31, 2024. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
By letters dated September 27, 2023, December 7, 2023, 
January 18, 2024, and February 16, 2024, KEDNY and KEDLI filed 
their consent to extend the maximum suspension period of these 
proceedings, ultimately through August 31, 2024.  The Companies 
requested the extension to provide additional time for parties 
to continue settlement negotiations in these proceedings.  The 
requests are subject to a “make whole” provision for the period 
beginning April 1, 2024, through August 31, 2024, that would 
keep the Companies and their customers in the same financial

position as they would have been absent the extension.  KEDNY’s 
and KEDLI’s letters stated it understands “make whole” to mean 
that the Companies would recover or refund any revenue under-
collections or over-collections, respectively, including 
interest, resulting from the extended suspension period.  KEDNY 
and KEDLI would calculate any revenue adjustments as the 
difference between sales revenue the Companies would have billed 
at new rates during the extension of the suspension period and 
the same level of sales revenues at current rates.  The revenue 
adjustments would include all applicable surcharges and carrying 
charges and would be subject to reconciliation in accordance 
with all applicable adjustment mechanisms.  Financial true-up 
targets established in the Commission’s order resolving the rate 
filings in these proceedings, as well as depreciation and 
amortization expense, would be applied to the extension of the 
suspension period.  All accounting/ratemaking would be pursuant 
to the new rate plan and be retroactive to April 1, 2024. 
 
 
To facilitate the orderly conduct of these 
proceedings, the effective date of the tariff leaves filed by 
the Companies is further extended through August 31, 2024, 
unless otherwise ordered by the Commission. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The operation of the matters suspended by the 
Notices issued May 10, 2023, and September 6, 2023, in these 
proceedings, is further suspended and the use of the rates, 
charges, rules and regulations stated in said Notices be further 
extended through August 31, 2024, unless otherwise ordered by 
the Commission. 
2. The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid 
NY and KeySpan Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid are 
directed to file with the Commission, on not less than one day’s
