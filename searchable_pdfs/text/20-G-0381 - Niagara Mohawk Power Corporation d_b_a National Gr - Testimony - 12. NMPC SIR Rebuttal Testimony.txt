Company’s significant underspending in SIR expense in CYs 2017

12 
Environmental Conservation (“DEC”) experienced changes to program 
13

16 
environmental health concern, known as Emerging Contaminants.  
17 
Emerging Contaminants is a broad term that covers a number of 
18 
contaminants including 1,4-dioxane (utilized as a solvent in certain 
19 
applications or as a chemical stabilizer or corrosion inhibitor in 
20 
conjunction with chlorinated solvent use) and per- and polyfluoroalkyl
