Q 
WHAT ARE MS. PANKO’S APPARENT CONCERNS WITH THE PROPOSED AMI 
5 
COST ALLOCATION FRAMEWORK? 
6 
A 
Ms. Panko’s objection appears to be based on an assertion that the proposed AMI 
7 
cost allocation framework fails to reflect the benefits of full AMI deployment.4  In other 
8 
words, Ms. Panko apparently believes that AMI costs should be allocated based on 
9 
some form of a “beneficiaries’ pay” approach rather than on the time-tested practice 
10 
of cost causation. 
11 
Q 
ARE THE PRINCIPLES OF COST CAUSATION AND BENEFICIARIES’ PAY THE 
12 
SAME? 
13 
A 
No.  Cost causation means allocating costs in a manner that recognizes the specific 
14 
drivers that lead to the incurrence of a particular cost.  By contrast, beneficiaries’ pay 
15 
requires identifying and quantifying all of the direct and indirect benefits from a 
16 
particular cost and using system allocations to apportion the costs to all customer 
17 
classes, including customer classes that do not directly benefit from the cost.
