Q.

17 
the CAPM.  
18 
A. 
The traditional CAPM essentially has three inputs, and the 
19 
formula is as follows: Cost of Equity = Risk-free Rate + Beta 
20 
* Equity Risk Premium. Staff’s estimated cost of equity under

the traditional CAPM using these inputs is as follow: 4.5

8 
Q. 
Please summarize what the ERP is and how it can be estimated. 
9 
A. 
The ERP is the required return on the market portfolio less 
10 
the risk-free rate. Essentially, it is an investor required 
11 
return for investing in the entire U.S. equity market.  
12 
Another way to state this is that the ERP is the cost of 
13 
equity of the U.S. equity market. There are several ways the 
14 
ERP can be estimated. In addition, since the ERP is an 
15 
important figure and data point for all investors and 
16 
managers, there are objective sources which publish their 
17 
estimates of the ERP. Expert surveys are also conducted in 
18 
which respondents are asked about their expert opinions on 
19 
the ERP. 
20 
 
21
