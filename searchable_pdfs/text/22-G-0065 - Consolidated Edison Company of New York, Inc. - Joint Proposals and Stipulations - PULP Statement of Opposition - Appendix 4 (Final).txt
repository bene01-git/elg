Response to PULP Interrogatories – Set PULP-1-Settlement 
Date of Response: March 17, 2023 
Responding Witness:  
 
 
Question No. :142 
 
Please state the part of the 9.25% authorized return on equity proposed by the settling parties 
that represents a “stay-out” premium for the proposed rate term. 
 
 
 
Response 
 
The response provided here was prepared jointly by the Company and Staff and reviewed by the 
settling parties. 
 
The settlement ROE does not a have a specific breakdown between “stay out premium” and 
Staff’s finance methodology.  The 9.25% ROE is reflective of current market conditions, the 
multi-year nature of the rate plan, and the other terms of the Joint Proposal.  In this context, the 
ROE appropriately balances the return requirements of the Company’s investors with customers’ 
expectations of safe and reliable service at just and reasonable rates.  In addition, the Company 
and Staff note that New York has one of the most ambitious clean energy goals in the Country. 
Achievement of these clean energy goals will require substantial Company investment over the 
three-year term of the rate plan (and beyond) that can only be accomplished if the Company can 
attract capital at reasonable rates.  Weak investment signals, such as ROEs that are well below 
industry averages, will impair the Company’s cash flow and credit metrics, which risks the 
degradation of the Company’s credit ratings.  All of these factors negatively impact the 
Company’s access to the capital markets and could cause liquidity pressures and higher 
financing costs, which would ultimately adversely affect customers through higher rates.
