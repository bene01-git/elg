II. 
Purpose of Testimony 
11 
Q. 
What is the purpose of the Panel’s second supplemental testimony? 
12 
A. 
The purpose of the Panel’s second supplemental testimony is to update 
13 
KEDLI’s revenue requirement to reflect the impacts of the recent settlement 
14 
agreement between the Companies and DPS Staff in Case 19-G-0678 
15 
concerning the gas supply constraints in downstate New York and related 
16 
moratorium on new customer connections (the “Settlement”).  The revenue 
17 
requirement also reflects an update to KEDLI’s cost of service resulting 
18 
from recently completed contract negotiations, as supported by the 
19 
supplemental testimony of the Companies’ GIOP.

III. 
Updates to the Revenue Requirement 
16 
Q. 
Please provide a brief overview of how the downstate supply constraint 
17 
affected the Company’s revenue requirement to this point in the 
18 
proceeding. 
19 
A.  
KEDLI’s direct and corrections and updates testimony and exhibits were 
20 
prepared under the assumption the NESE project would be in-service by the 
21 
winter of 2020/2021, thereby allowing the Company to continue connecting 
22 
new customers.  To provide the Commission with a full and complete 
23 
evidentiary record, KEDLI filed supplemental testimony on June 11, 2019, 
24 
reflecting a revenue requirement assuming a no-NESE project scenario 
25 


--- Page 5 ---
Page 3 of 9 
 
where the Company would not be connecting new customers in the Rate 
1 
Year and Data Years. 
2 
 
3 
 
Staff, in its direct testimony, took the position that the revenue requirement 
4 
should be adjusted to assume the unavailability of the NESE project.  
5 
Accordingly, in rebuttal, the Company adjusted the revenue requirement to 
6 
assume the NESE project would not be in service within the required 
7 
timeframe.  The adjustment resulted in a Rate Year revenue requirement of 
8 
$66.523 million, as shown in Exhibit ___ (RRP-1R), Schedule 1.  
9 
 
10 
Q. 
Please describe the Settlement and how it impacts the revenue 
11 
requirement. 
12 
A. 
As discussed in the GIOP’s supplemental testimony, on November 26, 
13 
2019, the Commission approved a settlement agreement between the 
14 
Companies and DPS Staff in Case 19-G-0678 that established a framework 
15 
for resolving all aspects of the Companies’ moratorium on new gas 
16 
connections.  Under the terms of the Settlement, the Companies agreed to 
17 
lift the moratorium for the next two winters (through September 1, 2021).  
18 
To enable customer connections notwithstanding the supply constraints in 
19 
the downstate service area, the Companies have developed an operational 
20 
plan consisting of new and unprecedented interim supply solutions, 
21 


--- Page 6 ---
Page 4 of 9 
 
including the expanded use of portable compressed natural gas injections to 
1 
maintain system pressure during peak conditions, as well as various 
2 
demand-side and energy efficiency measures to reduce peak demand.  The 
3 
Settlement also provides a framework for developing longer-term solutions 
4 
to address supply constraints.  
5 
 
6 
To incorporate these changes, the Company is updating its capital and O&M 
7 
forecasts, delivery revenues, and cost of gas.  These updates are supported 
8 
by the supplemental testimonies of the GIOP, Elizabeth D. Arangio, and 
9 
Theodore Poe, Jr., and this Panel utilized information from these witnesses 
10 
to update the revenue requirement.   
11 
 
12 
Q. 
Does the revenue requirement reflect additional updates? 
13 
A. 
Yes.  As mentioned, the GIOP’s supplemental testimony supports an update 
14 
to the cost of service from recent contract negotiations unrelated to the 
15 
Settlement.  The Panel reflected this update in the revenue requirement.   
16 
 
17 
Q. 
What is the updated revenue requirement? 
18 
A. 
As shown in Exhibit ___ (RRP-1SS), Page 1, the updated Rate Year revenue 
19 
requirement is $60.724 million, a decrease of $5.799 million from the 
20 
revenue requirement set forth in the Company’s rebuttal filing.   
21

Q. 
Please explain the changes to the revenue forecast.    
6 
A. 
The Company’s rebuttal filing reflected a Rate Year revenue forecast at 
7 
present rates of $1,131,732,134.  To adjust for the impacts of the Settlement, 
8 
the Company computed base delivery revenue, billing service fees, late 
9 
payment charges, merchant function charge revenue, commodity revenue, 
10 
and gross revenue taxes after accounting for the changes to the sales 
11 
forecast, as supported by Company Witness Poe.  The Company also 
12 
computed the commodity cost forecast to reflect the changes discussed by 
13 
Company Witness Arangio.  In addition, the Company made corrections to 
14 
its ETIP revenues to equal the estimated fiscal year ETIP budget.  As shown 
15 
in Exhibit ___ (RRP-3SS), Page 1, the updated revenue forecast at present 
16 
rates is $1,154,002,596 in this supplemental filing, an increase of 
17 
$22,270,462 from the rebuttal filing, which, after adjustment for associated 
18 
incremental gas costs, results in an increase in gross margins of $13.704 
19 
million. 
20 
 
21

expenses associated with the expansion of the Glenwood CNG facility;

(iii) include KEDLI’s allocated portion of the O&M costs associated 
11 
with the new CNG filling station to be located on Staten Island; (iv) 
12 
include O&M expense associated with two new CNG facilities; and (v) 
13 
include KEDLI’s allocated portion of the capital costs associated with 
14 
the proposed Staten Island CNG filling station, which is recorded as a 
15 
capital investment on KEDNY’s books (the calculation of which is 
16 
shown in Exhibit ___ (RRP-2SS)), but which will benefit both KEDNY 
17 
and KEDLI.  In addition, the Company made an adjustment to update

for damage prevention work, which is unrelated to the Settlement.
