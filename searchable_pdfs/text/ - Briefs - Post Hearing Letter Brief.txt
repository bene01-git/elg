Re: 
Cases 25-E-0072 and 25-G-0073 Consolidated Edison, New York Rate Cases 
 
Dear Secretary Phillips and Administrative Law Judges Costello, Kersey and Planty: 
 
 
The Westchester Municipal Consortium (“WMC”), consisting of forty municipalities 
within Westchester County, submits this letter in lieu of a post-hearing brief on the Joint 
Proposal filed in the above-referenced proceedings.  The evidentiary hearing held on December 
3, 2025 did not change the position of WMC as set forth in our Comments on the Joint Proposal 
previously submitted.   
 
We would point out the responses of Con Edison to certain WMC Information Requests 
were admitted into evidence as exhibits to assist the Commission in better understanding the 
respective costs of distributing electricity through an Underground Grid Network compared to an 
Overhead Radial System (Exhibit 1237a.) as well as the relative levels of investment in New 
York City and Westchester County (Exhibit 1237b).  These two issues are addressed in the Joint 
Proposal through providing data in conjunction with the next ECOS study and annual meetings 
on investments with WMC.  
 
In recognition of the material reduction of the proposed increases that brings the results 
substantially closer to the rate of inflation and the provisions within the JP addressing 
Westchester County issues, WMC is not opposing the Joint Proposal.  To avoid the impact of 
rate compression, WMC requests that the Commission consider the JP as soon as practical.
