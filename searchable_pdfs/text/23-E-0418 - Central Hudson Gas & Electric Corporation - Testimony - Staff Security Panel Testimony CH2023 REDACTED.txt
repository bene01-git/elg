Q. 
Why does the Panel agree with the Company’s 
8 
proposed network segregation activities? 
9 
A. 
Network segregation, in and of itself, is a 
10 
cybersecurity best practice and an important 
11 
part of building a multi-layered cybersecurity 
12 
defense.  Network segregation can be viewed as 
13 
the equivalent of putting up walls to close off 
14 
parts of the network from each other, only 
15 
allowing access to those parts by individuals 
16 
with proper permission.  The Company is also 
17 
increasing its network resilience by ensuring 
18 
endpoints on its WAN have redundant connections.  
19 
In the event that one connection is interrupted 
20 
or blocked, the redundant pathway will allow the 
21 
endpoint to continue to share the network 
22 
instead of suffering a loss of communications.
