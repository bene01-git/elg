IV. 
Revenue Allocation 
4 
Q. 
Please explain the Companies’ revenue allocation process. 
5 
A. 
The Companies’ revenue allocation proposals move the returns produced by 
6 
each service class closer to unity (the system average rate of return) while 
7 
mitigating extreme impacts, a concept known as gradualism.  For both 
8 
Companies, a tolerance band of 25 percent was used to determine which 
9 
service classifications had a rate of return at present rates close to the overall 
10 
rate of return at present rates.  Classes with returns at present rates above the 
11 
tolerance band were given smaller percentage increases than the average 
12 
increase, and classes with returns below the tolerance were given larger 
13 
increases.  Some adjustments were made for outlier classes and to promote 
14 
certain goals (i.e., supporting interruptibility).   
15
