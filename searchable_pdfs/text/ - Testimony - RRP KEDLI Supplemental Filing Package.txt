II. 
Purpose of Testimony 
12 
Q. 
What is the purpose of the Panel’s supplemental direct testimony? 
13 
A. 
The Panel’s supplemental direct testimony discusses the impact to KEDLI’s 
14 
April 2019 rate filing if the Company is unable to obtain incremental natural 
15 
gas supplies from Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line, LLC’s Northeast Supply 
16 
Enhancement (“NESE”) project.  As discussed more fully in the 
17 
supplemental testimony of Company Witness Elizabeth D. Arangio, recent 
18 
decisions by the New York State Department of Environmental 
19 
Conservation and the New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection 
20 
regarding required water permits create uncertainty as to whether and when 
21 


--- Page 4 ---
Page 2 of 9 
 
the NESE project may proceed with permitting and construction.  While the 
1 
Company remains cautiously optimistic the NESE project will go forward, 
2 
and therefore is not proposing any changes to its April 2019 filing at this 
3 
time, the Panel addresses the impact that continued gas supply constraints 
4 
would have on KEDLI’s revenue requirements.

III. 
Impact of the Unavailability of the NESE Project 
20 
Q. 
What is the impact to KEDLI’s revenue requirement if the NESE 
21 
project is not available as planned?  
22 
A. 
The April 2019 filing reflected a Rate Year revenue requirement that was 
23 
$49.4 million greater than revenue at present rates.  Assuming the NESE 
24 
project is unavailable, the impact to the revenue requirement is an increase 
25 
of $15.2 million, to $64.6 million, as shown in Exhibit __ (RRP-1S), 
26 
Summary.

Q. 
Please describe Exhibit ___ (RRP-1S). 
1 
A. 
Exhibit ___ (RRP-1S), Summary, presents the calculation of KEDLI’s 
2 
operating incomes for the Historic Test Year and Rate Year assuming the 
3 
NESE project is not approved.  Schedule 1 presents the update revenue 
4

5 
 
6 
Q. 
Please explain the changes to the revenue forecast under a no-NESE 
7 
project scenario.    
8 
A. 
The April 2019 filing reflected a Rate Year revenue forecast at present rates 
9 
of $1,207.805 million (including ETIP).  The Company calculated base 
10 
delivery revenue, billing service fees, late payment charges, merchant 
11 
function charge revenue, commodity revenue, and gross revenue taxes, after 
12 
accounting for the changes to the sales forecast and the customer counts 
13 
under a no-NESE project scenario, as supported by Company Witness Poe.  
14 
In addition, the Company computed the commodity cost forecast to reflect 
15 
the changes under a no-NESE project scenario, as discussed by Company 
16 
Witness Arangio.  As shown in Exhibit ___ (RRP-1S), Schedule 1, the 
17 
updated revenue forecast at present rates is $1,132.097 million (including 
18 
ETIP) under a no-NESE project scenario, a decrease of $75.708 million 
19 
from the April 2019 filing, which includes a decrease to commodity revenue 
20 
and gross revenue taxes of $52.624 million.   
21 


--- Page 8 ---
Page 6 of 9 
 
Q. 
Did the Company compute the bill impacts to customers under a no-
1 
NESE project scenario for a typical residential heat customer?   
2 
A. 
Yes.  The Company first computed the billing determinants (deliveries in 
3 
dekatherms and customer counts) derived from the updated sales forecast.  
4 
To develop revenue targets for each rate class, the revenue increase assigned 
5 
to each rate class in the April 2019 filing was increased by 26.9 percent, 
6 
which produced the total revenue increase needed under the no-NESE 
7 
project scenario.  The rate design for the no-NESE project scenario includes 
8 
the same customer charges as proposed in the April 2019 filing; the 
9 
volumetric rates were changed as needed to produce the revenue targets.  In 
10 
the April 2019 filing, the proposed delivery increase for a residential heating 
11 
customer using an average of 83 therms per month was 6.92 percent.  Under 
12 
a no-NESE project scenario, the proposed delivery increase for that same 
13 
customer would be 8.8 percent.   
14 
   
15

Please explain the impact to KEDLI’s O&M expense forecast if the 
16 
NESE project is not available.   
17 
A. 
Exhibit ___ (RRP-3S) presents a summary of KEDLI’s forecast O&M 
18 
expenses for the Rate Year and Data Years assuming the NESE project is 
19 
not available followed by expense schedules that would be impacted under 
20 
a no-NESE project scenario.  The net impact to O&M expense is a Rate

Year decrease of $2.357 million from the April 2019 filing, consisting of 
1 
the following:  
2 
• 
Schedule 27 (Other Initiatives) – adjustment to remove costs associated 
3 
with the Enhanced Contractor Safety Inspections Program as a result 
4 
of the forecast reductions to the capital work plan.  The adjustment is 
5 
discussed in the GIOP’s supplemental direct testimony.

•

7

•

9

conversion program.  As discussed in the supplemental testimonies of 
11 
the GIOP and Company Witness Arangio, the Company will be unable 
12 
to procure sufficient gas supplies to serve new firm customers under a 
13 
no-NESE scenario.  Therefore, this program would not be feasible.

Will the unavailability of the NESE project affect KEDLI’s forecast of

and taxes are assessed.  The changes occur in the Data Years.
