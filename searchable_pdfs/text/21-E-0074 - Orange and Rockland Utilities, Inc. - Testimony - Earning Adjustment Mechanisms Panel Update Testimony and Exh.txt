1.1 Electric Peak Reduction 
The Electric Peak Reduction metric is an outcome-based 
metric that incentivizes Orange and Rockland (“O&R” or the 
“Company”) to reduce peak load in its service territory. To 
the extent the weather-normalized peak load for the 
Company’s service territory is below the targets 
established for the EAM metric, the Company will receive an 
incentive. 
Metric 
Metric will be based on a reduction in the weather-
normalized New York Control Area (“NYCA”) coincident peak 
load for the Company’s service territory each rate year 
measured in Megawatts (MW) as published annually in 
December by the NYISO through their Business Issues 
Committee, Load Forecasting Task Force.

The reduction in the weather-normalized NYCA coincident 
peak load will be determined as follows:

1.2 Storage Roadmap 
The Storage Roadmap metric is an outcome-based metric which 
has been designed to achieve reliability benefits and is 
focused on improving selected circuit load factors that 
provide significant benefits to isolated areas.  
Metric 
The metric measures the average load factor, as compared to 
the system load factor, of six selected circuits in 
Blooming Grove and Warwick where there are significant 
customer and system benefit opportunities.  
Measurement 
The metric is based on percentage change in the Circuit to 
System Load Factor ratio (LF Ratio) compared to the prior 
year.

demand and average of top 5 summer firm demand during the 
period.
