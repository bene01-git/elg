4 
Q.

5 
A. 
As stated in the U.S. Energy Information 
6 
Administration’s Glossary, renewable energy 
7 
resources are those that are naturally 
8 
replenishing but flow-limited.  RNG is virtually 
9 
inexhaustible in duration but limited in the 
10 
amount of energy that is available per unit of 
11 
time.  Renewable energy resources include 
12 
biomass, hydro, geothermal, solar, wind, ocean 
13 
thermal, wave action, and tidal action.  RNG 
14 
supplies are those produced from renewable 
15 
biomass, such as the gas product from landfills 
16 
and anaerobic digesters.
