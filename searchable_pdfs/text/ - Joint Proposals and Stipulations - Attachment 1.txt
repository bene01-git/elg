Response to PULP Interrogatories – Set PULP-14 
Date of Response: November 20, 2025 
Responding Witness: Electric / Gas Rate Panels 
 
 
Question No. :98 
 
Please provide the delivery bill percentage impacts for average usage customers for each tier of 
EAP (for heating and non-heating residential customers) not already included in Appendices 15-
16. 
 
 
 
Response 
 
The attached files, CECONY Electric_PULP_14_Settling_Parties_98_Att.1 and CECONY 
Gas_PULP_14_Settling_Parties_98_Att.1, show both the delivery and total bill impact tables by 
usage levels for each tier of the EAP for both heat and non-heat customers. 
 
Please note that the tiered discounts are subject to change based on each rate year compliance 
filing.
