INTERNATIONAL BROTHERHOOD OF ELECTRICAL WORKERS, LOCAL UNION 97 
STATEMENT IN SUPPORT OF JOINT PROPOSAL FOR SETTLEMENT 
 
 
I. BACKGROUND  
The Joint Proposal (“Joint Proposal” or “JP”) that was made on the 24th day of 
September 2021, by and among Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid 
(“Niagara Mohawk” or “Company”), New York State Department of Public Service Staff 
(“Staff”), Multiple Intervenors, New York Power Authority (“NYPA”), Direct Energy Services 
LLC, Marathon Power LLC, New York State Office of General Services (“OGS”), the

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local Union 97, and Walmart, Inc. 
(collectively, the “Signatory Parties”), establishes a three-year rate plan for Niagara Mohawk’s 
electric and gas businesses.  It contains provisions that apply in a Stayout Period in the event the 
Company does not file to establish new delivery rates for the period immediately following the 
rate plan, and either resolves or establishes a framework for resolving all issues raised in Cases 
20-E-0380 and 20-G-0381 (“Rate Cases”).  In addition, the Joint Proposal resolves all issues in 
Case 19-M-0133; a proceeding in which the Company sought authority to defer certain actuarial 
expense losses associated with lump sum pension payments under its pension plan. 
On July 31, 2020, Niagara Mohawk filed tariff leaves and supporting testimony and 
exhibits for new rates and charges for electric and gas service to be effective September 1, 2020. 
The new tariffs were designed to increase electric and gas delivery revenues by approximately 
$100.4 million and $41.8 million, respectively, for the twelve months ending June 30, 2022. 
Niagara Mohawk filed corrections and updates testimony and exhibits on October 14, 
2020, increasing the electric revenue requirement to approximately $103.3 million and 
decreasing the gas revenue requirement to approximately $37.1 million.  On November 
25, 2020, eighteen parties, including Staff, filed direct testimony and exhibits addressing 
the Company’s filing. Niagara Mohawk, Staff and eight other parties each filed rebuttal 
testimony and exhibits on December 16, 2020. 
Settlement negotiations were held on January 5, March 2, April 13, and 20, May 14, June 
3, July 13 and 23, August 4, and September 17 and 22, 2021.  Additional, numerous settlement 
breakout sessions were held to discuss discrete issues.  All settlement conferences were duly 
noticed to the active parties and held virtually via video conference, which included the option to 
participate via telephone.

The Joint Proposal is the product of the active parties’ settlement negotiations and was 
developed pursuant to, and in accordance with, the Commission’s settlement procedures. 
 
II. STATEMENT OF SUPPORT  
I.B.E.W, Local Union 97 supports the Joint Proposal following discussions with 
Company representatives based on commitments of those representatives for the Company to 
maintain the current levels of represented employees and the Company to hire the additional new 
represented employees encompassed in each rate year of the Joint Proposal.  It is Local Union 
97’s opinion that the JP would be consistent with the result of litigation in these proceedings.   
 
III. THE JOINT PROPOSALS MEETS THE COMMISSION’S SETTLEMENT STANDARD FOR ADOPTION  
Local Union 97 believes that the JP is consistent with the New York State Public Service 
Law (“PSL”) and Commission policy.  The JP is supported by the record in these proceedings 
and produces a result that is rational and well within the range of reasonable outcomes had these 
proceedings been fully litigated.  Thus, there is a rational basis for the Joint Proposal.  Given 
these factors, the Joint Proposal clearly meets the Commission’s public interest standard and 
should be adopted by the Commission in its entirety without modification.  
 
IV. CONCLUSION  
For the reasons stated above, International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 
Union 97, recommends that the JP should be adopted by the Commission in its entirety without 
modification.
