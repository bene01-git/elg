The Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC) supports the provisions of the Joint Proposal related to 
electric service, primarily due to the Customer Energy Solutions Provisions contained in Section J, which 
support and will further the objectives of the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act 
(CLCPA); however, NRDC opposes the provisions of the Joint Proposal related to gas service because 
they contain significant imprudent investments in new gas infrastructure that are the primary driver of 
an unreasonable rate increase and are inconsistent with objectives of the CLCPA.  NRDC requests any 
public announcements referencing our position on the Joint Proposal make clear the distinction 
between our support for the electric service positions and opposition to the gas service provisions.
