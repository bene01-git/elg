Q: 
Do you have additional thoughts regarding EV rate design? 
1 
A: 
Yes. As Ms. Harris discussed in her testimony, increased development of high-powered 
2 
EV charging (e.g., DCFC and densely-clustered Level 2) has been challenged by the impact 
3 
of demand charges imposed by existing utility tariffs.14 Based on ChargePoint’s experience 
4 
in the EV charging market, demand charges represent one of the biggest financial 
5 
challenges facing EV charging providers. Demand charges can be triggered if multiple 
6 
drivers plug into a bank of DCFCs at the same time, or if just one driver plugs into a higher-
7 
powered DCFC. Studies show that demand charges can increase EV charging station utility 
8 
bills by thousands of dollars per month.15 In some markets, demand charges can account 
9 
for as much as 90% of total electricity costs for an EV charging provider.16 The structural 
10 
problems with traditional, demand-based C&I rates are not necessarily mitigated by higher 
11 
utilization, as the total cost share of demand charges at DCFCs that experience five 
12 
charging sessions per day can still range from 30 to 80 percent relative to total energy 
13 
costs.17  
14 
 
Q:  
What do you recommend regarding demand charges?   
15 
A:  
Implementing appropriate rate designs that eliminate, defer, or reduce demand charges is 
16 
key to unlocking increased investment in the EV charging infrastructure needed to support 
17 
EV drivers in New York as well as those transiting through the state. I recommend that the 
18 
Commission direct the Company to evaluate alternatives to traditional, demand-based rates 
19 
and file one or more options for review and approval within 90 days of a final order in this 
20 
proceeding.    
21 
In evaluating which alternatives to demand charges are appropriate for different 
22 
vehicle use cases, the Company can adopt or modify models established by utilities in other 
23 
states. Models that have been employed by utilities in other states include:
