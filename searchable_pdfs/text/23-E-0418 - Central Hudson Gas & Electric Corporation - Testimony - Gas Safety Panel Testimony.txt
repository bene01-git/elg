III. 
LEAK MANAGEMENT 
5 
Q.    
Does Central Hudson have a gas leak management program? 
6 
A. 
Yes. 
7 
Q.    
How does Central Hudson define its leak management program? 
8 
A. 
The Company defines its leak management program as a system wide 
9 
program to identify, respond to and mitigate underground gas leaks in a 
10 
safe and cost-effective manner.  Gas leaks result from unplanned releases 
11 
of natural gas from the Company’s transmission and distribution 
12 
infrastructure.  The causes for these releases may include damage by 
13 
excavation activities or outside forces such as frost induced ground 
14 
movement, obsolete joint designs such as those associated with cast iron 
15 
gas mains, material failures and obsolete construction practices such as 
16 
the installation of bare steel gas mains and services. 
17 
Q.    
Please describe the Company’s current safety performance measures for 
18 
leak management. 
19 
A. 
The Company monitors two performance measures for leak management: 
20 
1) a Total Leak Backlog Performance Measure; and 2) Hazardous (used 
21 
interchangeably with “Repairable”) Leak Backlog Performance Measure.   
22

Q.    
What is the Total Gas Leak Backlog Performance Measure? 
1 
A. 
The Total Gas Leak Backlog Performance Measure tracks the total 
2 
number of active leaks, including Hazardous Leaks, at Year End.  “Year 
3 
End” is defined as any time during the last ten days of the calendar year 
4 
not including any above grade leaks. 
5 
Q.    
What is the Hazardous Gas Leak Backlog Performance Measure? 
6 
A. 
Similar to the Total Gas Leak Backlog Measure, this measure tracks the 
7 
total number of Hazardous Leaks at year end.  Any above grade leaks are 
8 
excluded from the Hazardous Leaks reported at the end of the year for 
9 
this metric. 
10 
Q.    
Are all gas leaks considered ‘hazardous’? 
11 
A. 
No.  16 NYCRR Part 255 codifies which leaks are considered hazardous.   
12 
Pursuant to this regulation, all below grade leaks are assigned a 
13 
classification of Type 1, 2A, 2 or 3.  These leaks are documented, tracked, 
14 
monitored, and re-evaluated at regular intervals.  Hazardous or potentially 
15 
hazardous leaks are classified as a Type 1, 2A or 2 and have a set repair 
16 
timeframe ranging from immediate for a Type 1 classification to within 12 
17 
months for a Type 2 classification.  Type 3 leaks do not have an 
18 
associated repair timeframe and are considered to be ‘non-hazardous’ 
19 
due to the location and magnitude of below grade natural gas readings.  
20 
Type 3 leaks are re-evaluated during the next required leakage survey or 
21 
annually, whichever is sooner; to verify that the leak hazard level has not 
22

changed.  The Company’s total leak backlog is comprised primarily of

Type 3 leaks. 
2 
Q.    
Please describe Central Hudson’s leak management program. 
3 
A. 
As leaks are identified through the Company’s inspection program, by 
4 
customers or by the public, Central Hudson investigates, classifies, 
5 
prioritizes, and schedules the repair by severity.  Typically, all hazardous 
6 
leaks are repaired until eliminated.  The repair prioritization of non-
7 
hazardous leaks is based on review of the leak parameters, as well as 
8 
experience and coordination with the Company’s LPP elimination 
9 
program.  Central Hudson utilizes both Company and contractor resources 
10 
to maintain an overall leak backlog consistent with the targets established 
11 
in Case 20-G-0429 measured at any day during the last ten days of the 
12 
year. 
13

Central Hudson’s performance with respect to these metrics? 
15 
A. 
Yes.  External factors such as extreme cold causing ground movement or 
16 
third-party excavation practices may increase the total number of leaks 
17 
identified or found in a given year.

A. 
Emergency Response Time is the elapsed time between the Company

6 
45 minutes, and less than 60 minutes.  Reports from Company personnel 
7 
or its qualified agents are excluded from the metric calculation.
