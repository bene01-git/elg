BACKGROUND  
 
 
The 2022 Rate Order adopted the terms of the Joint 
Proposal establishing three-year rate plans for gas and electric 
service for the period beginning January 1, 2022, through 
December 31, 2024.  RY 3 delivery rates have been set in a 
manner to recover incremental revenues of $9,082,133, or a 5.2 
percent increase, on gas delivery revenue and $20,875,640, or a 
5.8 percent increase, on electric delivery revenue.  The 2022 
Rate Order directed O&R to file, on not less than 30 days’ 
notice, gas and electric tariff amendments, as necessary, to 
effectuate the RY 3 rates, which became effective on a temporary

TARIFF FILINGS 
 
 
On December 1, 2023, O&R filed tariff amendments to 
its gas and electric tariff schedules to implement the RY 3 
rates as required in the 2022 Rate Order.  On January 5, 2024, 
O&R filed further revisions that the Company contends are 
housekeeping in nature to P.S.C. No. 3 – Electricity, which 
combines revisions made to similar leaves filed on December 1, 
2023, in compliance with the 2022 Rate Order and those filed on 
December 15, 2023 in compliance with the Standby Service Order 
in Case 15-E-0751.  No comments were received regarding the 
filings made on December 1, 2023, or January 5, 2024.   
 
 
O&R complied with the requirements of newspaper 
publication pursuant to Public Service Law (PSL) §66(12)(b) and 
Title 16 of the New York Codes, Rules and Regulations §720-8.1 
for its filings made on December 1, 2023.  O&R requests waiver 
of the newspaper publication requirement for the further 
revisions filed on January 5, 2024, as the revisions are, 
according to the Company, housekeeping in nature.  The Appendix 
to this Order sets forth a list of the tariff amendments filed 
by the Company and the dates of the newspaper publication.  
 
LEGAL AUTHORITY 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), and 66(1) and  
(12), the Commission has the legal authority to review the 
proposed tariff leaves, as well as modify, reject or approve 
such filed tariffs to ensure that the rates and services 
governed by those tariffs are just and reasonable and otherwise 
consistent with law.  As such, the Commission has the legal

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The proposed tariff amendments, not including those 
filed in compliance with the Standby Service Order, properly 
reflect the directives of the 2022 Rate Order.  No party 
objected to the amendments as proposed.  The Commission 
authorizes the amendments and revisions listed in the Appendix 
that effectuate RY 3 to become effective on a permanent basis.  
The tariff amendments that were filed on December 15, 2023, and 
January 5, 2024, to incorporate the Commission directed tariff 
amendments from the Standby Service Order, will remain in effect 
on a temporary basis until the proposed amendments are acted 
upon by the Commission in Case 15-E-0751.  Further, the 
Commission approves O&R’s request for waiver of the newspaper 
publication requirement for the tariff amendments filed by the 
Company on January 5, 2024, as they are, in fact, housekeeping 
in nature as O&R only combines amendments that have been 
previously filed.  Furthermore, the RY 3 filings have been 
subject to newspaper publication and the Commission waived the 
newspaper publication requirement for the tariff filing 
submitted for the Standby Service Order. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by Orange and Rockland 
Utilities, Inc. and listed in the Appendix to this Order are 
authorized to become effective on a permanent basis on the date 
of issuance of this Order, except for tariff amendments related 
to Case 15-E-0751, which will remain in effect on a temporary 
basis until acted upon by the Commission.
