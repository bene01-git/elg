Michael J. Rieder, being duly sworn, deposes and says: 
 
1. 
I am employed as a Utility Supervisor in the Major 
Utility Rates Section of the Office of Electric, Gas and Water 
(OEGW) for the New York State Department of Public Service 
(Department).  I have worked for the Department since 1991.  
While with the Department I have prepared, analyzed, and 
reviewed reports and studies involving operating revenues, sales 
forecasts, operation and maintenance (O&M) expenses, capital 
budgets, marginal and embedded costs, mortality and net salvage, 
revenue allocation and rate design.  My current duties include 
the review and evaluation of electric and gas utility capital 
and O&M budgets and the engineering analyses of electric and gas 
utility rate, pricing, and tariff proposals. 
2. 
I have testified before the Public Service Commission 
in numerous proceedings regarding issues related to electric and 
gas utility sales, revenues, expenses, capital construction 
programs, cost studies, depreciation, revenue allocation and 
rate design. 


--- Page 2 ---
3. 
I offer this affidavit in support of a Joint Proposal 
filed with the Commission in the above captioned matter on 
February 22, 2013. 
4. 
Upon information and belief, the Brooklyn Union Gas 
Company d/b/a National Grid NY (KEDNY) has been operating under 
a rate plan established via various Commission Orders since 
2007.  See Joint Proposal at page 2.  The Joint Proposal 
modifies certain otherwise continuing provisions of the rate 
plan established under Case 06-G-1186 (2007 Rate Plan) for an 
additional two years.  See Joint Proposal at page 1. 
5. 
As part of Staff’s examination of KEDNY’s overearnings 
related to the 2007 Rate Plan, the OEGW assigned a team to 
review the Company’s Capital Expenditures (CapEx) Budget.  The 
OEGW team consisted of Staff members Michael Rieder, Marco 
Padula, Brian Fisher, and Chris Stolicky.  
6. 
As part of its review, Staff met with KEDNY 
representatives on December 11, 2012 in the Company's Albany 
offices.  The Company was represented by L. Brown, T. Small, J. 
Stavrakas, G. Lundquist, S Khan, M. Kern, S. Mongan, S. Greco, 
M. Zerella, P. DiGiglio, and C. Connolly.  At this December 11th 
meeting, Staff reviewed KEDNY's entire Capital Budget for fiscal 
years 2014 and 2015, including the following projects/programs: 
• Gas System Reinforcement Program 
• Brooklyn Queens Interconnect Project 
• Gas System Reliability 
• Flushing Meadow Project 
• Verrazano Narrows Upgrade 
• Metropolitan Reliability Infrastructure 
• Gas System Remote Control Valve Program 
• City Sate Construction Program 
• Local Law 30 
• Main and Service Replacement Programs 
• Transmission Integrity Management Program 
• System Growth

• Gas Pressure Regulating Facilities 
• Atmospheric Corrosion Program 
• Meter/AMR CapEx 
• Water Intrusion 
• Gas Distribution Valve Program 
7. 
Additionally, during the course of Staff’s 
examination, KEDNY responded to 13 Information Requests 
submitted by Staff regarding KEDNY’s forecasted capital projects 
and programs over the Company’s 2014 and 2015 fiscal years. 
8. 
Part of the CapEx program includes a rate year 
allowance for City/State Construction.  See Joint Proposal at 
page 7.  Appendix 1, the Statement of Capital Forecast shown on 
Schedule 9 to the Joint Proposal, shows the expected City/State 
Construction amounts on a fiscal year basis, as well as the 
remainder of KEDNY’s forecasted CapEx. 
9. 
The forecasted levels of investment reflected in the 
Joint Proposal has been increased as compared to the 2007 Rate 
Plan.  The increases in forecasts are primarily due to 
City/State Construction, leak prone pipe replacement, and the 
construction of two major projects, the Flushing Meadow 
Reinforcement and the Brooklyn Queens Interconnect. 
10. 
Over the past several years, the Company has invested 
more capital than what was provided for it in the 2007 Rate 
Plan.  The Staff team was satisfied with the KEDNY’s 
explanations supporting the increased level of spending beyond 
what was embedded in rates. 
11. 
Based on my experience, and in consultation with the 
other members of the Staff team, the Capital Investment levels 
that have been forecast in the Joint Proposal are reasonable.  
The Company has demonstrated the need for each of the projects 
and programs and has described it's budgeting and cost control 
processes. 


--- Page 4 ---
12. 
Staff believes that there is no indication that the 
Company will not pursue its full Capital budget during the term 
of the rate plan.  If, however, circumstances arise that impede 
the Company's infrastructure investment progress, ratepayers are 
protected from any under-spending by the net plant 
reconciliation mechanism contained in Section 3.2 of the Joint 
Proposal. 
13. 
Finally, I make note of one specific change made to 
the Temperature Control revenues of the 2007 Rate Plan that 
specifically addresses the Company’s recent earnings filings.  
14. 
Under the 2007 Rate Plan the Temperature Control 
revenues were projected to be $85 million with the Company 
retaining 100% of any amounts above this threshold.  Upon 
information and belief, KEDNY’s Temperature Control revenues 
were well in excess of $85 million during every year of the 2007 
Rate Plan.  
15. 
KEDNY, when determining whether it had achieved excess 
earnings during the period of 2008-2011, voluntarily included 
any additional Temperature Control revenues above the threshold 
within the earnings calculation.

16. 
The Joint Proposal changes the $85 million imputation 
such that the 2013 (Rate Year 6) and 2014 (Rate Year 7) rate 
years include Temperature Control revenues of $100.0 million and

$102.3 million, respectively. 
Additionally, the Joint Proposal 
includes a sharing mechanism such that any revenues exceeding 
the threshold within the rate years will be shared with 90% 
being deferred for the benefit of customers and 10% being 
retained by the Company, as well as a downward share in the 
unanticipated event that the Temperature Control revenues 
experience a severe drop off from their recent amounts.
