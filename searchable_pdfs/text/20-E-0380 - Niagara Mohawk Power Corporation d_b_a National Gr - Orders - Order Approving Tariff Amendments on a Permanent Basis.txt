BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On May 30, 2024, Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation 
d/b/a National Grid (Niagara Mohawk or the Company) filed tariff 
amendments to its gas schedule, P.S.C. No. 219 – Gas (Gas 
Tariff), and on May 31, 2024, the Company filed tariff 
amendments to its electric schedules, P.S.C. No. 214 – 
Electricity (Outdoor Lighting Tariff), P.S.C. No. 220 – 
Electricity (Electric Tariff), as directed by the Commission’s

BACKGROUND 
 
 
The 2022 Rate Order adopted the terms of the Joint 
Proposal establishing a three-year rate plan for electric and 
gas service for the period beginning July 1, 2021, through 
June 30, 2024.3  The 2022 Rate Order also contains terms to apply 
in a Stayout Period in which Niagara Mohawk would not file for 
new rates, beginning July 1, 2024, and ending on the earlier of 
either the effective date of revised base rates for the Company, 
or March 31, 2025.  The Company did not file for new rates to 
become effective prior to March 31, 2025.

TARIFF FILINGS 
 
 
On May 30, 2024, Niagara Mohawk filed tariff 
amendments to its Gas Tariff, and on May 31, 2024, the Company 
filed tariff amendments to its Outdoor Lighting Tariff and 
Electric Tariff to effectuate the Stayout Period in compliance 
with the 2022 Rate Order.  More specifically, the tariff 
amendments update rates, a gas deferral surcredit, and revenue 
decoupling mechanism (RDM) targets as directed by the 2022 Rate 
Order.  On July 9, 2024, the Company filed a further 
housekeeping revision to its Electric Tariff to incorporate the 
Commission-approved language from the Standby Service Order. 
 
 
The Company fulfilled the newspaper publication 
requirement in accordance with Public Service Law (PSL) 
§66(12)(b) and Title 16 of the New York Codes, Rules and 
Regulations (16 NYCRR) §720-8.1 for its proposed tariff 
amendments filed on May 30, 2024, and May 31, 2024, and 
requested waiver of the requirements of newspaper publication 
for its July 9, 2024 revision given it is housekeeping in 
nature.  The Appendix to this Order sets forth a list of the 
tariff amendments filed by the Company and the dates of 
newspaper publication. 
 
LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), and 66(1), the Commission 
has the legal authority to review the proposed tariff leaves, as 
well as modify, reject, or approve such filed tariffs to ensure 
that the rates and services governed by those tariffs are just 
and reasonable and otherwise consistent with law.  As such, the 
Commission has the legal authority to review and, approve tariff 
amendments on a permanent basis, as prescribed in this Order.

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
Niagara Mohawk’s proposed tariff amendments to its 
Outdoor Lighting Tariff, Electric Tariff, and Gas Tariff, 
properly reflect the requirements of the 2022 Rate Order and 
include the rates, charges, rules and regulations necessary to 
effectuate the rates and provisions of the Stayout Period.  The 
tariff amendments made on July 9, 2024, to incorporate the 
Commission-approved language from the Standby Service Order are 
reasonable and will provide customers with greater clarity.  The 
Commission authorizes the tariff amendments listed in the 
Appendix to become effective on a permanent basis as of the date 
of issuance of this Order. 
 
 
Niagara Mohawk fulfilled the newspaper publication 
requirement in accordance with PSL §66(12)(b) and 16 NYCRR §720-
8.1 for the tariff amendments filed on May 30, 2024, and May 31, 
2024.  The Commission waives the requirements for newspaper 
publication requirements for the Company’s July 9, 2024 tariff 
filing as the tariff modifications are housekeeping in nature. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by Niagara Mohawk Power 
Corporation d/b/a National Grid, and listed in the Appendix to 
this Order, are authorized to become effective on a permanent 
basis upon the date of issuance of this Order. 
2. The requirements of Public Service Law §66 (12)(b) 
and Title 16 of the New York Codes, Rules and Regulations §720-
8.1 as to the newspaper publication of the changes proposed in 
the July 9, 2024 tariff filing are waived.
