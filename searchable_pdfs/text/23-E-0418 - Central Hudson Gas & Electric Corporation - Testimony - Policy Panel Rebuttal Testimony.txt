Q.    
Why does the Company disagree with Staff’s methodology for determining 
1 
the Company’s labor distribution? 
2 
A. 
As further described in detail in the rebuttal testimony of the Revenue 
3 
Requirements Panel, the use of a historical three-year average does not 
4 
reflect how the existing workforce allocated their time between expense 
5 
and capital, and it ignores the projected effect that incremental employees 
6 
will have on the overall labor distribution. 
7 
Q.    
Why does the Company disagree with Staff’s application of a vacancy 
8 
rate? 
9 
A. 
As further described in the rebuttal testimony of the Revenue 
10 
Requirements Panel, within these rate proceedings the determination of a 
11 
vacancy rate should be premised on a comparison of actual headcount to 
12 
that provided for in rates, since that represents the headcount for which 
13 
funds are being provided by customers.  As such, Staff’s calculation of a 
14 
vacancy rate that is based on the Company’s historic attrition rate is 
15 
fundamentally flawed.  In contrast when the historical vacancy rate is 
16 
calculated based on the headcount allowed in rates, it shows that the 
17 
Company was staffed at a level above the headcount allowed in rates, 
18 
which obviates the need for Staff’s recommended vacancy rate.  Finally, 
19 
within the forecasted period the Company staggered the hiring of 
20 
incremental employees across the Rate Year resulting in an impact very 
21 
similar to a vacancy rate imputation making Staff’s imputation redundant 
22

VI.
SIGNIFICANT AND UNACCEPTABLE RISK ASSYMETRY
4 
Q.
Please describe the increased risk associated with the level of NRAs
5 
proposed by SCSP.
6 
A.
The SCSP recommends a 414% increase in the one-year maximum
7 
potential value for CSPI NRAs.
8 
Q.
Please describe the Panel’s concerns with the SCSP recommendation.
9 
A.
The SCSP’s proposed potential maximum CSPI NRA level is excessive
10 
and overly punitive.  Additionally, as discussed within the rebuttal
11 
testimony of the Company’s Customer Experience Panel, the CSPI
12 
metrics are strongly correlated with one another, and it is inappropriate to
13 
triple the NRA level for failure of more than one metric in the same year
14 
given the relationship between the metrics.  The SCSP recommended
15 
NRA levels in combination with existing potential electric and gas
16 
operations NRAs produce a total maximum NRA level of 355 basis points
17 
(“bps”) or 39% of Staff’s proposed 9.2% return on equity (“ROE”).  This
18 
maximum NRA level represents significant financial uncertainty, which, as
19 
discussed within the rebuttal testimonies of the Company’s Finance Panel
20 
and Company Witness Nowak, is likely to lead to higher financing costs for
21 
customers.
