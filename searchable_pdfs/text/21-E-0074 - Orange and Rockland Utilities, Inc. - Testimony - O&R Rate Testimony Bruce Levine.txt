3        A.  My name is Bruce M. Levine and I am an active party. I have

6        Rockland County Legislator. My testimony is meant to propose a  
7        the inclusion of a requirement that Orange and Rockland Utilities

9        government parking lots within its territory during the four year  
10      rate period. The cost could be borne by the ratepayers within  
11.    amount requested by Orange and Rockland by reducing the rate  
12     of return requested by the company in this rate proceeding.  
11    Q. What are you specifically requesting in this testimony?     
12    A. I request that Orange and Rockland enter into agreements with  
13    municipalities within its service territory to install for electric 
14    automobile charging stations in every parking lot operated by said  
15    local governmental entities as a way to help create a green energy

3      Q. How many local government entities are located within Orange    
4      and Rockland’s service territory?    
5     A. Orange and Rockland’s territory contains approximately 50 said  
6     entities. These consist of 3 counties, at least 15 towns and 30 
7     villages. All of these entities have municipal buildings for employees 
8      and almost all , if not all, have parks and recreational facilities that   
9      are frequented by the public. Many also have special operations 
10    facilities for Departments of Public Works and Sewer districts. 
11    I am requesting the Commission to require Orange and Rockland  
12    Utilities to enter into agreements with each of the entities to  
13   build and operate electric car charging stations in their parking 
14  lots for employees and visitors/residents to charge electric cars. 
15. Q. How many charging stations are you recommending at each such 
16 site?

1    A. I recommend that 3 to 5 electric car charging stations be   
2    located at each municipal site. The total would be 200 charging 
3    stations to be located and operating within a four year period. 
4    In year one, Orange and Rockland can negotiate and enter into  
5    agreements with each municipal entity to create and locate said  
6    charging stations and conduct site location and engineering.  
7    Where feasible,  Orange and Rockland can add a solar panel array to  
8    help provide some of the energy needed.  
9    In years two, three and four of the rate case period, Orange and  
10   Rockland can install approximately 60 to 75 groups of electric   
11   charging stations in cooperating municipalities. 
12   Q. What would the cost of this program be over the four year rate  
13   period? 
14   A. The approximate additional cost to the ratepayers per year over   
15   this three year implementation period is about $300,000. I am not 
16   aware of any tax breaks available to Orange and Rockland for this
