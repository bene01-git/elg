  Major Rate Changes. 
 
 
 
On January 29, 2021, Orange and Rockland Utilities, 
Inc. (Orange and Rockland or the Company) filed amendments to its 
tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 3 – Electricity and P.S.C. No. 4 - 
Gas, to increase the annual electric and gas delivery rates for 
the rate year ending December 31, 2022. 
 
 
 
Orange and Rockland seeks an increase in annual 
revenues for electric delivery of approximately $24.5 million 
(inclusive of gross receipts tax, 5.8% increase in delivery
