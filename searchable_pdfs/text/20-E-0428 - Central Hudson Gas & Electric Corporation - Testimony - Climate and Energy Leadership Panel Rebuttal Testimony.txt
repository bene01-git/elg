Q.    
Will Staff’s proposed delays to the Grid Modernization program impact the 
1 
Company’s ability to support state policy goals as outlined by the CLCPA

3

14 
expenditures prevent further real-time monitoring and control of the 
15 
distribution system, delaying completion of the Grid Modernization 
16 
initiative until 2026 and thus impacting the Company’s ability to support 
17 
CLCPA goals.
