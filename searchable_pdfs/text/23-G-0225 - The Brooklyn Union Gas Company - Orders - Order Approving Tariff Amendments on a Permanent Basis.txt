National Grid NY (KEDNY) and KeySpan Gas East Corporation d/b/a 
National Grid (KEDLI) (together, the Companies) filed tariff 
amendments to their tariff schedules P.S.C. No. 12 – Gas and 
P.S.C. No. 1 – Gas, respectively, as required by the

TARIFF FILINGS 
 
 
On March 2, 2026, the Companies filed amendments to 
their tariff schedules to implement the RY 3 rates as required 
by the Commission’s 2024 Rate Order.  Language regarding the 
“Late Payment Charges and Other Waived Fees,” “Gas Safety and 
Reliability Surcharge,” and “Demand Capacity Surcharge 
Mechanism” have also been discontinued and removed from the 
tariffs, per the 2024 Order.7  The filings became effective on a 
temporary basis on April 1, 2026. 
 
 
The Companies complied with the requirements of 
newspaper publication pursuant to Public Service Law (PSL) 
§66(12)(b) and Title 16 of the New York Codes, Rules and 
Regulations §720-8.1 for their tariff filings.

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), 66(1), and 66(12), the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the proposed tariff 
leaves, as well as modify, reject, or approve such filed tariffs 
to ensure that the rates and services governed by those tariffs 
are just and reasonable and otherwise consistent with law.  As 
such, the Commission has the legal authority to review the 
Companies’ filings and approve tariff amendments on a permanent 
basis, as prescribed in this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The Companies’ proposed tariff amendments properly 
reflect the directives of the 2024 Rate Order, including the 
rates and charges necessary to effectuate RY 3 and the removal 
of all language associated with obsoleted fees and surcharges.  
Therefore, the Commission authorizes the tariff amendments filed 
on March 2, 2026, and listed in the Appendix, to become 
effective on a permanent basis as of the date of issuance of 
this Order. 
 
 
This Order does not address amendments to tariff 
provisions proposed by the Companies in compliance with the 
Section 73 Order or the Rehearing Order.  Such proposed tariff
