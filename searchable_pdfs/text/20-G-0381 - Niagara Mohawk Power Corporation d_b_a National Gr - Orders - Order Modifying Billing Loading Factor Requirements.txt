Commission established in its rate order adopting the terms of 
the Joint Proposal in Cases 20-E-0380 and 20-G-0381 (2022 Rate

BACKGROUND 
 
 
When Niagara Mohawk bills third parties for services 
rendered, it adds overhead costs to the labor, materials, and 
other costs incurred.  These overhead costs include items such 
as administrative and general overheads, fringe benefits (e.g., 
medical care, pensions, other post-employment benefits, etc.), 
and payroll taxes.  The Company uses its billing loading 
factors, or burden rates, to determine the amount of overhead 
costs to charge a third party.  These burden rates fluctuate 
over time.  Appendix 16, Section 2.5, of the Joint Proposal 
adopted in the 2022 Rate Order limits the frequency with which 
Niagara Mohawk may adjust the billing loading factors to twice 
per year, in April and September, unless there is a change in a 
loading factor of greater than 20 percent, in which case an 
additional adjustment is allowed. 
 
 
The Petition states that the Company implemented an 
automated burden rates clearing process in its SAP financial 
system in December 2021.  The new automated process allows the 
Company to reconcile the burden loading rate differences between 
forecasted amounts applied by the loading factors and actual 
incurred costs.  However, the new automated process occurs more

LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§65 and 66 the Commission has the 
legal authority to review, as well as to modify, reject, or 
approve, in whole or in part, tariff amendments.  As such, the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the Company’s 
filings and approve and make effective tariff amendments, as 
prescribed in this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
Allowing Niagara Mohawk to adjust the billing loading 
factors quarterly will provide benefits to both the Company and 
billed third parties by matching costs more accurately with the 
work performed.  Although the adjustments would happen more 
frequently, the change would still allow for relative stability

Additionally, the automated burden clearing process provides 
several benefits, including efficiencies in labor costs, 
improved project forecasting and budgeting, improved billing 
accuracy, increased internal efficiencies and improved 
accounting.2  Moreover, the proposed modification to the 
Company’s third-party billing loading factors requirement does 
not substantively alter the balance of the terms of the Joint 
Proposal adopted in the 2022 Rate Order.  None of the signatory 
parties to the Joint Proposal opposed the requested 
modification, nor did the Commission receive any public 
comments.  Given the benefits of more frequent adjustments, the 
Commission authorizes Niagara Mohawk to adjust its third-party 
job order billing loading factors on a quarterly basis. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The petition of Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation 
d/b/a National Grid for authorization to adjust its third-party 
billing loading factors on a quarterly basis is granted. 
2. These proceedings are continued.
