Q.    
How does Central Hudson know how much gas supply to purchase for an 
3 
upcoming season? 
4 
A. 
Central Hudson annually reviews the normal and peak day 5-year sales 
5

6

7 
send-out volumes.  As a supplement to this information, Energy 
8 
Resources calculates a 3-year and 5-year historical average monthly 
9 
send-out to determine the monthly volumes expected for the upcoming 
10 
winter season.  Assumptions are made regarding the monthly volumes of 
11 
storage to be used based on historical patterns and timing of contract 
12 
ratchet requirements.  Base volumes of supply are procured to fill as much 
13 
FT space as needed for each month; any remaining incremental 
14 
requirements provide the basis for peaking services.  Separately, a similar 
15 
review of historical send-outs is performed prior to summer season supply 
16 
RFPs.  The seven-month summer season volumes are typically 
17 
consistently low and predictable but  they are similarly analyzed to 
18 
determine requirements and maintain system reliability.  Lastly, a matrix is 
19 
created for storage refills based on winter season ending balances, 
20 
achievement of 95% full by October 31st, and spreading refill volumes over 
21 
the seven-month timeframe based on a dollar-cost-average strategy.
