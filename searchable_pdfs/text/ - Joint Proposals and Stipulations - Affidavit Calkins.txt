Ronald Calkins, being duly sworn, deposes and says: 
 
1. 
I am employed as a Supervisor Utility Accounting, 
Audits and Finance for the New York State Department of Public 
Service.  I have worked for the Department of Public Service 
since June, 1969.  I work in the Office of Accounting, Audits 
and Finance where my responsibilities include acting as a lead 
in specific areas of inquiry, supervising staff members and 
fostering teamwork, overseeing work projects, being versed in 
the inter-relationships of cost of service elements, develop and 
review financial information, analyze and critique utility 
regulatory theory and departmental policies, communicate and 
convey complex matters in an understandable manner, and 
preparing the necessary testimony and exhibits. 
2. 
I have testified before the Public Service Commission 
in Case 05-G-1268 Corning Natural Gas Corporation, Cases 06-G-
1185/86 KeySpan Energy Delivery, New York and KeySpan Energy 
Delivery, Gas East Corporation, Case 11-G-0280 Corning Natural 
Gas Corporation.  The subjects of my previous testimony have 
included the development of staff’s forecasted rate year

projections for operating expenses, other taxes, Federal and New 
York State income tax calculations, working capital, earnings 
base capitalization, and any associated adjustments.  
3. 
I offer this affidavit in support of a Joint Proposal 
filed with the Commission in the above captioned matter on 
February 22, 2013. 
4. 
Upon information and belief, the Brooklyn Union Gas 
Company d/b/a National Grid NY (KEDNY) has been operating under 
a rate plan established via various Commission Orders since 
2007.  See Joint Proposal at p. 2 (2007 Rate Plan).  The Joint 
Proposal was entered into to modify certain continuing 
provisions of the rate plan for an additional two years.  See 
Joint Proposal at p. 1. 
5. 
I have reviewed KEDNY’s earnings history and forecast 
as was included in the 2007 Rate Plan and was involved in 
discussions regarding elements to be addressed in the Joint 
Proposal. 
6. 
In accordance with the 2007 Rate Plan, KEDNY submitted 
to Staff and the Commission the computation of its gas rate of 
return on common equity for each of the rate years ended 
December 31, 2008 through 2011, inclusive.  KEDNY’s allowed ROE 
in the 2007 Rate Plan is 9.8 percent. 
7. 
In such filings, KEDNY reported an earned return 
onROE) of 11.02 percent for 2008, 10.49% for 2009, 13.98% for 
2010 and 14.10% for 2011.  In addition to such results being in 
excess of its allowed ROE of 9.8 percent in every year, these 
results also are in excess of the 10.5% threshold above which 
earnings for 2008, 2010 and 2011, and are to be shared between 
KEDNY and its customers. 
8. 
In accordance with the 2007 Rate Plan, $4.66 Million 
has been deferred to the Balancing Account for the benefit for 
the customers in 2008.  This amount represents 50% of the 


--- Page 3 ---
revenue equivalent of KEDNY’s calendar year 2008 earnings that 
were in excess of the 10.5% earnings threshold.   In 2009, KEDNY 
did not generate any earnings that are subject to sharing with 
customers.  In 2010 and 2011, $34.0 Million and $ 34.9 Million, 
respectively, have been deferred to the Balancing Account for 
the benefit of the customers. 
9. 
In 2012, George Abraham and I assisted the Commission 
and Staff in identifying the components of the KEDNY financial 
operating results for the rate years 2008, 2009, 2010 and 2011 
that produced the company’s additional earnings, as compared to 
the Commission’s forecasted revenue requirements adopted under 
the 2007 joint proposal. 
10. 
To perform this task, we made certain adjustments to 
the KEDNY forecasted rate year data shown in 2007 joint proposal 
to produce forecasted revenue requirement schedules for the rate 
years 2008 through 2011.  We then compared the forecasted 
revenue requirement data with the actual operating results 
submitted by the Company in its annual excess earnings filings 
for the rate years 2008 through 2011 and identified the 
variances which produced the additional earnings. 
11. 
Although KEDNY could have filed for rates in 2012, it 
opted not to file. 
12. 
KEDNY filed for the year 2011 a report showing its 
actual earnings, revenues, Operation and maintenance Expenses, 
depreciation, amortization, taxes other than income taxes, rate 
base.  KEDNY also provided detailed work papers showing the 
computation of the forecasted the earnings for the year 2013 and 
2014. 
13. 
We reviewed these documents prior to entering into 
negotiations with KEDNY to extend the 2007 Rate Plan in detail 
and checked the company’s work papers and examined the Company’s 
books and accounts.  We examined the normalizing adjustments to 


--- Page 4 ---
operation and maintenance expenses.  In addition, we examined 
company’s operation and maintenance expense projection for the 
year 2013 and 2014 and reviewed the reasonableness of the 
Company’s forecast methodology. 
14. 
In our review, in addition to various informal 
requests to the Company, the Accounting Staff made formal 
requests in the following areas:   
• To provide detailed work papers of KEDNY’s Excess Earnings 
computation for 12 Months ended December 31, 2011. 
• To provide detailed work papers to support the computation 
the company’s Revenue Requirement for the years 2013 and 
2014. 
• To explain, with proper back up, all normalizing 
adjustments made by the company to Operation and 
Maintenance Expenses and Taxes Other Than Income Taxes. 
• To explain whether any other abnormal expenses or charges 
were included in the Base year 2011 Operation and 
Maintenance Expenses, other than that the normalizing 
adjustments made by the company’s filing, and if so, to 
identify and quantify those charges. 
• To explain, with proper backup, the normalizing adjustments 
made by the company in its filing. 
• To provide a computation, with proper backup work papers, 
for the development of the General and Labor inflation 
factors for the years 2013 and 2014. 
• To provide detailed work papers to support the actual Taxes 
Other Than Income Taxes expense for the year 2011 and the 
estimates for the year 2013 and 2014; and 
• With reference to the recently passed American Taxpayer 
Relief Act of 2012, which includes a provision for the 
continuation of Bonus Tax Depreciation at a rate of 50% for 


--- Page 5 ---
vintage year 2013 assets for federal tax purposes, to 
provide a revised 2013 and 2014 calculation for accumulated 
deferred taxes, tax depreciation for federal income tax 
purposes, detailing the impact on the forecasted rate of 
returns for 2013 and 2014 due to the lowering of the 
respective forecasted rate base balances. 
15. 
Using the above information we were able to make 
certain changes in the Joint Proposal that update KEDNY’s rate 
plan for rate years six and seven (2013 and 2014) that have no 
material effect on its overall revenue requirement, but provide 
a forecast whereby the company’s anticipated returns will not 
exceed its allowed ROE to the extent that they have in the past 
years 2008 through 2012. 
16. 
Finally, we also reviewed KEDNY’s General and Labor 
inflation factor that it used for its 2013 and 2014 projections 
which appears to be reasonable.  With regard to labor expense, 
the Company has forecasted labor expense for the 2013 and 2014 
rate years by using the 2011 calendar year labor expense and 
reflecting wage increases for 2012 through 2014. Also, the 
Company has reflected a 1 percent productivity adjustment (labor 
expense plus payroll taxes) and US Restructuring labor savings 
for both the 2013 and 2014 rate years. 
17. 
For KEDNY’s 2007 Rate Plan, its labor expense was 
forecasted using the 2005 historic test year labor expense of $ 
108.6 million with a KEDNY employee count 1285 as a starting 
point and then reflected wage increases to provide a forecasted 
labor expense for the 2011 rate year of $124.6 million.  The 
same level of labor expense was forecasted for the 2012 rate 
year.  In addition the company reflected a labor productivity 
adjustment that reduced its operating expenses by $3.5 million 
in the 2011 and 2012 rate years. 


--- Page 6 ---
18. 
KEDNY’s forecasted labor expense for the 2013 and 2014 
rate years utilized the 2011 calendar year labor expense of 
$107.5 million with a KEDNY employee count 1221 as a starting 
point and reflects wage increases for a forecasted labor expense 
for the 2013 and 2014 rate years at $113.2 million and $116.2 
million respectively without any productivity adjustment. 
19. 
The Company then reflected a 1 percent productivity 
adjustment of $2.1 million and US Restructuring Labor savings of 
$7.4 million for a combined reduction in operating expenses of 
$9.5 million for its 2013 forecast. For 2014, the Company 
reflected a further 1 percent productivity adjustment of $3.2 
million and US restructuring labor savings of $7.4 million for a 
combined reduction in operating expenses of $10.6 million. 
20. 
The 2011 KEDNY labor count (1221) used for forecasting 
in the Joint Proposal is 64 employees lower when compared to the 
2005 KEDNY labor count (1285).  The forecasted labor expense of 
2011/2012 at $124.6 million was lowered to $113.2 million in 
2013 and $116.2 million in 2014 for the Joint Proposal.  The 
forecasted labor productivity adjustment of 2011/2012 of $3.5 
million was increased for the Joint Proposal to productivity 
adjustments of $9.5 million in 2013 and $10.6 million in 2014. 
21. 
The net effect is that the Joint Proposal’s labor 
expense is lower by $17.4 million in 2013 and $15.5 million in 
2014 rate years as compared to the 2011/2012 forecasted rate 
years. 
22. 
These updated labor and operating expenses are 
reasonable as they more properly reflect the current state of 
the company for measuring its earnings over the term of the rate 
plan extension as contained in the Joint Proposal. 
23. 
Finally, attached hereto is Staff information request 
DPS-001 where KEDNY provides information related to normalizing 
adjustments made to its 2011 Operation and Maintenance Expenses
