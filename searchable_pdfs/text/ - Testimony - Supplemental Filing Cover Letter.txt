Dear Secretary Burgess: 
 
The Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National Grid NY (“KEDNY”) and KeySpan 
Gas East Corporation d/b/a National Grid’s (“KEDLI”) (collectively, the “Companies”) hereby 
submit supplemental testimony discussing the revenue requirement and other impacts to the 
Companies’ rate filings in the event the Transcontinental Gas Pipe Line LLC’s Northeast Supply 
Enhancement (“NESE”) project is not available during the proposed rate plan period.  The 
supplemental testimony consists of the following: 
 
• Supplemental Testimony of the Revenue Requirements Panels 
• Supplemental Testimony of the Gas Infrastructure and Operations Panels 
• Supplemental Testimony of Elizabeth D. Arangio 
• Supplemental Testimony of Theodore Poe, Jr. 
 
As discussed in the testimony, the NESE project is needed to address critical gas supply 
constraints in downstate New York that are currently preventing the Companies from adding new 
customers in the affected areas of their service territories.  The net impacts to KEDNY and 
KEDLI’s revenue requirements should the NESE project not go forward as planned are increases 
of $5.7 million and $15.2 million, respectively.  Items impacting the Companies’ revenue 
requirements include delivery revenues, capital expenditures, and O&M expenses.   
 
 
The Companies are not proposing to modify their April 2019 filings at this time, as the 
expectation is that the NESE project will be approved.  However, as agreed with Department of 
Public Service Staff, and discussed with the other parties at the Procedural Conference held on
