BY THE COMMISSION: 
 
 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
 
On November 26, 2014, the Brooklyn Union Gas Company 
d/b/a KeySpan Energy Delivery New York (KEDNY) and KeySpan Gas 
East Corporation d/b/a KeySpan Energy Delivery Long Island 
(KEDLI) (together, the Companies) filed tariff amendments to 
their gas tariff schedules, which became effective on a 
temporary basis on January 1, 2015.  The Companies’ tariff 
filings addressed three changes:  (1) compliance filing to make 
revenue neutral delivery rates changes, (2) compliance filing to 
make delivery rate changes to KEDNY’s low income program, and 
(3) revisions to Site Investigation and Remediation (SIR) 
Delivery Rate Surcharges to recover deferred balances stemming 
from the Companies SIR activities. 
We have reviewed the tariff amendments and will allow 
them to become effective on a permanent basis upon the date of 
issuance of this Order. 
 
TARIFF AMENDMENTS 
Pursuant to an Order issued December 21, 2007,1 the 
Commission adopted a Joint Proposal2 that approved a five-year 
rate plan for KEDNY and KEDLI, where Rate Year 5 ended December 
31, 2012.  Appendix C and D of the 2007 Joint Proposal 
established a delivery rate design for the stay-out years by

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
The 2007 Rate Order required revenue neutral changes 
to the monthly minimum charges and the block rates to better 
align the delivery rates with the cost of service.  In the 
filing under consideration herein, KEDNY and KEDLI made 
amendments to their tariff to implement the stay-out rate design 
adopted in the 2007 Joint Proposal.  We have reviewed the tariff 
amendments and note that both KEDNY and KEDLI have achieved 
their targeted delivery rate goals, as set forth in Appendix C 
and D, respectively, of the 2007 Joint Proposal.  The delivery 
rates will remain in effect until new delivery rates are 
established in a future rate proceeding. 
KEDNY also filed tariff amendments in compliance with 
a Commission Order issued June 13, 2013, that required an update 
to the monthly minimum charges and the block delivery rate of 
its reduced rate S.C. Nos. 1AR and 17-1AR (Residential Non-
Heating reduced rate) and S.C. Nos. 1BR and 17-1BR (Residential 
Heating reduced rate).  We have reviewed the low income rates 
and find that they comply with the 2012 Joint Proposal. 
 
 
On November 28, 2012, we issued the Deferred Balances 
Order allowing KEDNY and KEDLI to continue the company-specific 
SIR recovery mechanism, capped at the same level as existed at 
the end of Rate Year 5.  The Companies were directed to 
implement the new surcharge collected from each firm service 
classification based on the forecasted percentage of each 
service classification’s contribution to total firm delivery 
revenues.  We have reviewed the proposed SIR surcharges for Rate 
Year 8 and find that they were accurately calculated.
