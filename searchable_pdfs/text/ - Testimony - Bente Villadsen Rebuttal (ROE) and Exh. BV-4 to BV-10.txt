A. OVERALL APPROACH

Q8: How does Staff approach estimating the cost of equity for CECONY? 
3 
A8: Staff estimates the cost of equity for CECONY using three models – a multi-stage DCF

11 
they then weight 2/3 (DCF) and 1/3 (CAPM) to arrive at their recommendation.  Staff does 
12 
not use standard financial techniques to calculate the impacts of financial leverage in their 
13 
analyses.

10

11 
distribute to investors, but this is not captured in this approach. The reliance on cash flow or 
12 
earnings instead of dividends ensure that over time all cash flow to shareholders are counted 
13 
as such.

18

19 
calculation, as I see no logical explanation why Value Line predicts an average ROE of close 
20 
to 11.0 percent while Staff estimate a ROE that is below 9.0 percent.
