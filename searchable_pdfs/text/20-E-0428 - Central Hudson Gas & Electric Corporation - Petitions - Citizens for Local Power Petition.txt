PETITION OF CITIZENS FOR LOCAL POWER (CLP) TO POSTPONE  
CENTRAL HUDSON GAS & ELECTRIC COMPANY’S GAS AND ELECTRIC RATE 
INCREASES, SCHEDULED FOR JULY 1, 2022, AND FUTURE SCHEDULED RATE 
INCREASES PENDING THE OUTCOME OF INVESTIGATIONS INTO THE COMPANY’S 
BILLING PROBLEMS AND RESPONSE TO STORM DAMAGE 
 
Preliminary Statement 
 
Citizens for Local Power (CLP), also doing business as Communities for Local Power (CLP), 
respectfully petitions the Public Service Commission (“the Commission”) to place a hold on the 
increase in electric and gas delivery rates that Central Hudson Gas & Electric Company (“the 
company”) is scheduled to impose on July 1, 2022, and future scheduled rate increases, until all 
open investigations into the company’s recent activities by the New York State Senate and the 
Department of Public Service (“DPS”) are concluded.  
 
Ongoing investigations into Central Hudson’s recent actions and practices include: 
 
• 
The New York Senate Committee on Investigations and Government Operations 
investigation into the billing practices by NYS utility companies including Central Hudson, 
launched by a Senator who resides in the company’s territory.  
 
• 
The Department of Public Service (DPS) investigation of Central Hudson's Customer 
Information System implementation and resulting widespread billing errors. (Public comments 
on the DPS website are at 3,596 and counting.) 
 
• 
The DPS investigation of Central Hudson’s preparations for and response to the 
February ice storm that left more than 67,000 homes and businesses in the company’s territory 
without electricity for multiple days. 
 
Central Hudson customers have not been well served by the Company at a time when they 
have experienced severe disruptions of their economic and social lives – disruptions that made 
them especially dependent on the competency, reliability, and basic capability of their regulated 
monopoly utility to provide gas and electric service at just and reasonable rates. In addition to 
the apparent failures being investigated by the New York Senate and the PSC itself, Central 
Hudson has recently charged wildly inconsistent rates, which, in the case of electricity, 
increased by 46% in February, only to be followed by a company announcement that rates 
would decrease 46% in March. The “bill surge,” on top of the billing fiasco, has galvanized 
public resistance and led to a loss of confidence in the company as the monopoly provider of an 
essential public service.

As a result of the Covid-19 pandemic and the already sharp and pervasive inequalities that 
characterize our society, many Central Hudson customers face utility arrears that they cannot 
pay. The company’s most recent report revealed that ca. 48,000 residential customers and ca. 
7,000 businesses face arrears of 60+ days. Most of them became subject to shutoffs after April 
15, with all of the damaging consequences of that action, including health impacts, social 
isolation, eviction and potential homelessness, and reduced ability to borrow. To reward Central 
Hudson with a rate increase, even one that was properly negotiated and approved under the 
auspices of the Commission, risks further harm to communities that depend on Central Hudson 
for essential services and that are, also properly, dissatisfied and perplexed by the evident 
failures of a government-regulated monopoly to offer the reliable public service it exists to 
supply. 
 
Requested Relief 
 
On behalf of the approximately 307,000 residential customers of Central Hudson Gas & Electric 
Company, and with particular concern for the approximately 48,000 such customers who are 
currently vulnerable to shutoff because they have accumulated arrears of more than 60 days, 
we request that the company be enjoined from raising its rates as scheduled on July 1, 2022, 
and that no rate increase be permitted prior to the satisfactory conclusion of investigations into 
the company’s billing and storm response currently being carried out by the New York State 
Senate and the Department of Public Service, and the company’s satisfaction of any resulting 
requirements.  
 
Closing Statement 
  
CLP participated as a Party in the recent rate case that this Petition addresses. We are familiar 
with the negotiation process that resulted in the decision to lower Central Hudson’s electric rates 
by 0.25 percent in 2021 and to raise them by 1.3 and 1.4 percent in 2022 and 2023, 
respectively, while gas rates were to rise by 1.8%, 2.1%, and 0.8% over the three years. Our 
decision not to sign on to the negotiated settlement was driven by factors other than the size of 
the negotiated rate increases. But what seemed reasonable in November of 2021 no longer 
seems reasonable in light of the company’s cascading problems and recent unmistakable 
operational failures. These failures should not be rewarded with a default to “business as usual” 
at a time when business as usual seems like a distant memory to all but the most jaundiced 
observers. We appreciate the opportunity to have our concerns heard and to submit this Petition 
on behalf of Central Hudson customers.
