On May 26, 2022, New York State Electric & Gas 
Corporation (NYSEG) and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation 
(RG&E) filed amendments to their tariff schedules, P.S.C. Nos. 
119, 120 and 121 – Electricity, P.S.C. Nos. 87, 88 and 90 – Gas, 
and P.S.C. 18 and 19 – Electricity and P.S.C. No. 16 – Gas, 
respectively, to increase their annual electric and gas delivery 
revenues for the rate year ending April 30, 2024.  
 
NYSEG is requesting an increase in annual electric 
delivery revenues of approximately $274 million (31.2% increase 
in delivery revenues, or 16.8 % increase in total revenues) for 
the rate year ending April 30, 2024.  NYSEG projects that the 
requested increase in delivery revenues will result in a total 
monthly bill increase of about $18.31 or a 22.2% increase to the 
total bill for a residential customer using 600 kWh/month. 
 
 
 
For its gas business, NYSEG is requesting an increase 
in annual gas delivery revenues of approximately $43.4 million 
(20.7% increase in delivery revenues, or 9.8% increase in total 
revenues).  NYSEG projects that the requested increase in 
delivery revenues will result in a total monthly bill increase 
of about $14.94 or a 15.6% increase to the total bill for a 
residential heating customer using 83 therms per month. 
 
RG&E is requesting an annual increase in electric 
delivery revenues of approximately $93.8 million (19.0% increase 
in delivery revenues, or 11.3% increase in total revenues).  
RG&E projects that the requested increase in delivery revenues 
will result in a total monthly bill increase of about $12.95 or 
a 15.0% increase to the total bill for a residential customer 
using 600 kWh/month. 
 
For its gas business, RG&E is requesting an increase 
in annual gas delivery revenues of approximately $37.7 million 
(20.9% increase in delivery revenues, or 9.7% increase in total 
revenues).  RG&E projects that the requested increase in 
delivery revenues will result in a total monthly bill increase 
of about $9.62 or a 12.8% increase to the total bill for a 
residential heating customer using 83 therms per month. 
 
 
 
The proposals have an effective date of June 24, 2022.  
NYSEG’s and RG&E’s amendments require a hearing.  The Public 
Service Commission delegated its authority to suspend the 
effective date of amendments to tariff schedules and to 
institute proceedings to the Secretary to the Commission.

Without answer or other formal pleading, proceedings 
are instituted concerning the propriety of the rates, charges, 
rules, and regulations contained in the amendments to NYSEG’s 
Schedules P.S.C. Nos. 119, 120 and 121 – Electricity, P.S.C. 
Nos. 87, 88 and 90 – Gas, and RG&E’s P.S.C. Nos. 18 and 19 – 
Electricity and P.S.C. No. 16 – Gas, listed in the Appendix to 
this Notice. 
 
 
 
Pending the proceedings and decision on the proposed 
changes, the operation of these amendments is suspended, and the 
use of the rates, charges, rules, and regulations are deferred 
through October 21, 2022, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission.  The rates, charges, rules, and regulations altered 
by the suspended amendments shall continue in effect until these 
proceedings are concluded or until the period of initial 
suspension and any extended suspension expires, unless otherwise 
ordered by the Commission. 
 
 
 
NYSEG and RG&E are directed to file with the 
Commission, not later than June 21, 2022, consecutively numbered 
supplements announcing suspension of the amendments.
