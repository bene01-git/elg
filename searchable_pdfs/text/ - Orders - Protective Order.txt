RULING ADOPTING PROTECTIVE ORDER 
(Issued March 24, 2016) 
DAVID R. VAN ORT, Administrative Law Judge: 
On February 26, 2016, KeySpan Gas East Corp. (KEDLI) 
and The Brooklyn Union Gas Company (KEDNY) (collectively, 
"companies"), submitted a proposed protective order for adoption 
in the above-referenced cases. 
At the February.29, 2016 
procedural conference, several parties indicated that they did 
not have an opportunity to review the draft protective order 
because they had not yet or only recently filed their requests 
for party status in the Department of Public Service's Document 
and Matter Management system (DMM). 
On March 3, 2016, the 
companies sent me an e-mail, with an attached draft protective 
order, advising that the proposed protective order was sent to 
all parties after the procedural conference and the version sent 
to me includes the comments of the Department of Public Service 
staff (DPS Staff), City of New York and Long Island Power 
Authority. 
The e-mail further noted that the other parties 
expressed no objections to the draft protective order. 
The proposed protective order appeared to have some 
ambiguities or internal inconsistencies. 
In particular, the 
order identified protected information as information qualifying
