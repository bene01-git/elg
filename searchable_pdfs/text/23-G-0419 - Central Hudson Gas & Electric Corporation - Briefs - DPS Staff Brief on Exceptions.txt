STAFF BRIEF ON EXCEPTIONS 
 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On July 31, 2023, Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corporation (Central Hudson, 
or the Company) filed for major electric and gas rate increases as defined under Section 66(12) 
of the Public Service Law (PSL).  After parties filed testimony, an evidentiary hearing was held 
on January 24, 2024 through February 6, 2024 pursuant to a litigation schedule established by 
Administrative Law Judges (ALJs) Ashley Moreno and James Costello.  Parties filed initial 
briefs on March 1, 2024, and reply briefs on March 11, 2024, in advance of the Recommended 
Decision (RD) issued by ALJs Moreno and Costello on May 1, 2024.   
 
 
Staff of the Department of Public Service (Staff) generally concurs with the 
recommendations contained in the RD and urges the New York State Public Service 
Commission (Commission) to adopt those aspects of the RD unchanged for the reasons 
established in the RD and Staff’s briefs.  However, the RD is not without error, and Staff 
respectfully submits its exceptions where the RD inadvertently misstates facts, or where factual 
errors, from whatever source, found their way through to the outcomes recommended in the 
RD.1  Staff submits that the outcomes recommended in the RD, modified as recommended in 
this brief, constitute a well-supported and reasonable resolution to all the issues raised in these 
cases.

D.  Technology Capital 
4.  Security     . 
Staff excepts to the RD’s recommendation that the Company not be required to 
provide cybersecurity-related reporting to Staff, and to the RD’s acceptance of Central Hudson’s 
arguments that such reporting requirements are “unnecessary,” “burdensome,” and “could

more burdensome, since it would require creating an agenda and aggregating and collating the 
necessary information monthly, instead of a less frequent schedule.  Central Hudson has also 
asked for a historically large number of FTEs, and Staff reiterates the importance of being able to 
track how the Company utilizes this addition to headcount. 
The RD also appears to fundamentally misunderstand the type of information 
Staff seeks.  Staff clarifies that it seeks data similar to what the Company already publicly 
provides as part of its annual 5-year capital investment plan (e.g., project name, budget, and a 
brief description and justification).  This information is currently not included as part of Central 
Hudson’s quarterly and annual reporting.  If Staff’s reporting requirements are adopted, this 
information will enable Staff to monitor the progress of Central Hudson’s cybersecurity program 
implementation to address evolving cybersecurity concerns.

E.  Net Plant Reconciliation Mechanism 
Staff takes exception with the RD recommendation concerning a downward-only 
net plant reconciliation mechanism.  While the RD correctly identifies that such a mechanism 
“protects ratepayers if net plant additions and depreciation expense are less than forecasted,” it 
nonetheless recommends against such a mechanism in the Rate Year (RD at 217).  The RD 
specifically asserts that the parties are not agreeing to a multi-year settlement where forecasts 
may be expected to become less reliable as the plan progresses.  Staff notes, however, that the 
Commission recently approved the continuation of a downward-only reconciliation mechanism
