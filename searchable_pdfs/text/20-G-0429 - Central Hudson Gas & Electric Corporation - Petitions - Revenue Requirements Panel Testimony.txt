Q.    
Please elaborate and expand upon the Commission’s 1977 Policy 
1 
Statement as it relates to the development and presentation of 
2 
expense projections.   
3 
A. 
The Commission was very explicit in how expense projections were to be 
4 
developed.  First, forecasts were to be developed for individual elements 
5 
of expense, whereby projections were predicated on a historical base 
6 
adjusted for changes in unit prices and in activity levels.  Further, all 
7 
assumptions in unit price because of inflation or other factors and changes 
8 
in activity levels due to modified work practices or other reasons were to 
9 
be separately developed and identified.

12
