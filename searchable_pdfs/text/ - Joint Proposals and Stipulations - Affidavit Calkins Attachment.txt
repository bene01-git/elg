Response:  
 
1. Operation and Maintenance Expense (O&M), as reported in the Company’s filed 2011 
Computation of its gas return on common equity, included two one-time items.  These items 
are Costs to Achieve (i.e., IS Transformation) of $5 million that are addressed in the 
Company’s normalizing adjustments and further discussed in the Company’s response to 
Item 2 f. below, and the reserve for energy efficiency costs of $7.4 million that represented 
the amount of energy efficiency expenditures that exceeded the System Benefits Charge 
(SBC) authorization.  The Company has a petition pending at the Commission that seeks 
recovery of these energy efficiency expenditures. While the Company believes that it should 
be permitted to fully recover these costs for the reasons stated in its petition, the Company 
booked a reserve of $7.4 million for these expenditures pending the outcome of its petition.  
In forecasting KEDNY's earned return for Calendar Year 2013, we believe it is appropriate at 
this time to reduce O&M expense levels by $7.4 million related to energy efficiency costs. 
 
2. a. & i.) Reclass Purchased Gas Expenses 2011: Expense Type 400, Expense Type 
Description: Other in the amount of ($624,427,614) plus Expense Type M11, Expense Type 
Description: Natural Gas & Odorant in the amount of ($212,062,677) plus Other Incentives 
in the amount of ($3,165,000) for a total adjustment of ($839,655,292).  The $839,655,292 
adjustment reflects the cost of gas that was reclassed from operations and maintenance 
expense to the Cost of Gas line item in order to separately identify these expenses for 
presentation purposes in the Company’s 2011 ROE computation filing.  Detail supporting 
this adjustment is included in Attachment 1 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - Reclass Purchased Gas 
Expense.  Attachment 1 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - Reclass Purchased Gas Expense: Section 1(Gas 
Cost Reclass from O&M) provides a subtotal of the reclass that rolls up into Section 2 
(Account Detail) bridging to the amount of gas costs recorded on the Company’s books.  The

a.) Normalizing Adjustment Rate Year 2013: Expense Type 400, Expense Type Description: 
Other in the amount of ($5,556,915) – is an adjustment that is required to fully offset the 
Energy Efficiency and PSC 18-A Assessment margins such that their impact on Operating 
Income is $0.   This O&M expense adjustment, however, should have been ($3,240,467) to 
achieve a zero impact on Operating Income and the Company is therefore modifying the 
original normalization adjustment by $2,316,448 from ($5,556,915) to ($3,240,467) to result 
in a net $0 impact on operating income. The end result is a proper offset to Energy Efficiency 
and PSC 18-A Assessment net margins and a reduction to the calendar year return as the 
adjustment adds back O&M expense.  Details supporting this adjustment are included in 
Attachment 2 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - Energy Efficiency and PSC 18-A Assessment net margins. 
 
b.) Normalizing Adjustment Rate Year 2013: Expense Type 500, Expense Type Description: 
Rent/Lease in the amount of $4,846,950 – reflects additional rent expense from the Service 
Company associated with National Grid’s US Foundation Program (implementation of the 
new financial management system) that did not exist in 2011.  The calculation of the rent 
expense reflects the same methodology incorporated in the pending settlement of the 
Company’s most recent Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation (“NMPC”) rate case. Details 
supporting this adjustment are included in Attachment 3 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - SAP.

meant to reduce actual pension and OPEB expenses to Rate Year Four rate allowances to

be normalized by the reclass entries reflected here.  Additional detail is provided in

e.) Normalizing adjustment Rate Year 2013: US Restructuring Savings – ($11,952,458) 
For the rate year ended December 31, 2011, KEDNY’s operating expenses reflected only a 
partial year of labor and non-labor savings resulting from the US Restructuring Program 
(National Grid’s organizational and efficiency restructuring initiative). This program was 
initiated during the 2011 rate year and resulted in achieved savings totaling approximately $8 
million in that year. The Company estimates that by March 2013 the US Restructuring 
Program will be generating approximately $19 million of savings on an annualized run rate 
basis, which includes the $8 million achieved in 2011, and results in net incremental savings 
of approximately $11 million (composed of approximately $4 million of non-labor savings 
and approximately $7 million of labor savings).  Please note these anticipated savings are 
estimates and assume that all of the initiatives identified will be implemented and 100% of 
the estimated savings are achieved. Also, normal business expense increases such as 
inflation, contractual wage increases and medical costs, will offset these savings to some 
extent. This analysis of US Restructuring Program savings is consistent with the 
methodology incorporated in the pending settlement of the recent NMPC rate case. 
Additional detail is included Attachment 5 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - US Non Labor Restructuring 
(Savings) and Attachment 6 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - US Labor Restructuring (Savings).  
 
f.) Normalizing adjustment Rate Year 2013: IS Transformation Costs – ($5,000,000) 
As noted in the Company’s response to DPS-1 (RFC-1), for the rate year ended December 
31, 2011, the one time non-recurring items reflected in KEDNY’s income statement included 
Costs to Achieve (i.e., IS Transformation) of $5 million. Therefore, this amount was removed 
for the purposes of forecasting 2013 O&M expense levels in the Company’s model. 
 
g.) Normalizing adjustment Rate Year 2013: Addl. O&M for Local Law 30 -$4,289,480 
This normalization adjustment reflects an increase in O&M expense to address New York 
City’s (NYC) Local Law 30 compliance requirements.  Local Law 30 is a provision of the 
NYC building code that requires every gas service line in NYC to have a gas service valve or 
other outside emergency shut-off device installed. All existing Tier 1 services (3-family or 
greater, commercial, governmental & industrial) were installed prior to January 1, 2010. All 
existing 1 and 2 family services (Tier 2) must be completed by January 1, 2020.  The 
incremental Calendar Year 2013 O&M dollars reflect the cost of installing 2,920 valves at an 
estimated cost of $1.469 per valve.  
 
h.) Normalizing adjustment Rate Year 2013: Change in Allocations - $2,237,431 
This normalizing adjustment reflects the impact of KEDNY’s March 31, 2012 filing to revise 
its cost allocation methodologies.  The methodology used to determine this adjustment is 
consistent with the methodology incorporated in the pending settlement of the NMPC rate 
case. The normalizing adjustment reflects a move to a common set of allocations as 
described in KEDNY’s March filing with the Commission. Under the revised allocations it is 
estimated that an additional $2,237,431 of costs will be allocated to KEDNY in 2013. Details 
supporting this adjustment are included in the Attachment 7 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - Allocations.

i.) Normalizing adjustment Rate Year 2011: Other Incentives – $5,694,000  
Add back 50% Net Synergy Savings per Rate Plan, Merger Joint Petition - Case 06-M-0878 -
- Appendix 6, Rate Year 4.  This adjustment excludes from income 50% of the net synergy 
savings from the merger as this is the shareholder’s portion of the assumed savings over five 
year rate period. This discrete adjustment stops at the end of Rate Year 2012.  Additional 
detail is included in Attachment 8 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - Synergy Savings.  
 
i.) Normalizing adjustment Rate Year 2011: Other Incentives – $192,000  
This normalization adjustment reflects the sum of all of the Company’s discrete adjustments 
to O&M expense that were made to properly compute the CY 2011 gas ROE. The $192,000 
adjustment represents the sum of: 
1. $3.2 million LAUF gas incentive add back to O&M expense in order to exclude 
shareholder incentive from earnings as per JP 2, page 20, Section E, subparagraph 
b. 
2. ($2.8) million - reclass of service company property tax allocations from O&M to 
property tax expense. 
3. ($0.1) million – 10% of SIR costs associated with Coney Island and Citizens gate 
stations that were absorbed by shareholders. 
4. ($0.1) million – removal of estimated Ex-Pat costs.  
 
 
i.) Normalizing adjustment Rate Year 2013: Other Incentives – $977,554 
This normalization adjustment reflects the increase to O&M expenses required to cover the 
incremental labor and overhead costs of supporting the US Foundation Program.  The 
calculations of these O&M adjustments are consistent with the calculations of the US 
Foundation adjustments incorporated in the pending settlement of the recent NMPC rate 
case.  Additional detail is included in Attachment 9 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - Incremental USFP 
Staff. 
 
3. Work papers detailing the calculations of general and labor inflation from calendar year 2011 
to calendar year 2014 are provided in the Attachment 10 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - General 
Inflation and Attachment 11 to DPS-1 (RFC-1) - Labor Inflation. The calculation of general 
inflation uses GDP Price Index data from September 2012 Blue Chip Economic Indicators.  
The calculation of labor inflation utilizes a 3% annual increase for management labor and a 
2.5% annual increase for represented labor.  The labor inflation also includes a productivity 
offset of 1%. A union wage increase schedule is also provided in Attachment 12 to DPS-1 
(RFC-1) - Wage Increase Schedule.
