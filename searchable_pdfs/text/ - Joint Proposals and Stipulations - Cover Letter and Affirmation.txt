James M. Molloy affirms the following under penalty of perjury:

My business address is 40 Sylvan Road, Waltham, Massachusetts 02451.  I am

Metropolitan Reliability Infrastructure Correction 
2. 
In response to questions concerning the Joint Proposal submitted by the Presiding 
Administrative Law Judges in these proceedings, the Companies identified an error in KEDNY’s 
rate base as presented in the Joint Proposal.  Specifically, KEDNY identified that it had 
erroneously included in rate base $1.9 million of capital expenditures associated with Phase 5 of 
the Metropolitan Reliability Infrastructure (“MRI”) Project that were incurred in the twelve 


--- Page 3 ---
2 
 
months ending March 31, 2020 – the Companies’ 2020 Fiscal Year (“FY 20”), a period during the 
term of KEDNY’s prior rate plan.  This amount was intended to be accounted for as interest bearing 
construction work in progress but was erroneously included in KEDNY’s rate base.  Under the 
Joint Proposal, the associated revenue requirement impacts of this error are approximately $82,000 
in Rate Year One, $163,000 in Rate Year Two, and $227,000 in Rate Year Three.  Consistent with 
the Joint Proposal, these amounts should be removed from KEDNY’s revenue requirements to 
ensure that MRI Phase 5 costs will not be funded by customers unless and until MRI Phase 5 is 
separately authorized by the Commission as set forth in Section IV.5.3.1 of the Joint Proposal.  
See Ex. 815. 
3. 
To correct for this error, an approach was developed to decrease the deferral credits 
to align with the calculated changes – the decreases in the revenue requirements.  Specifically, the 
following modifications to Appendix 1 to the Joint Proposal are being made to remove the revenue 
requirement impact of MRI Phase 5: 
(i) 
First, Appendix 1, Schedule 5, Page 2 is being amended to remove $1.9 million of 
capital expenditures associated with MRI Phase 5 in FY 20; 
(ii) 
Second, Appendix 1, Schedule 3, Page 3 is being added to show the calculation of 
the revenue requirement impacts of the error; and 
(iii) 
Finally, Appendix 1, Schedule 3, Pages 1 and 2 are being amended to decrease the 
deferred credits so that they align with the calculated decrease in the revenue 
requirements. 
4. 
In addition, to ensure that the true-up/reconciliation mechanisms set forth in the 
Joint Proposal are not affected by the MRI Phase 5 costs, the following modifications are being 
made to Appendices 1 and 6 of the Joint Proposal:

(i) 
Appendix 6, Schedule 7, Page 1 is being amended to remove the MRI Phase 5

(ii) 
Appendix 1, Schedule 4, Page 1 is being amended to reflect the lower Rate Year 
Three revenue requirement to correct the Net Utility Plant and Depreciation

Updates to Reflect a September 1st Effective Date 
5. 
The Joint Proposal contemplated that new rates would be effective August 1, 2021 
(e.g., for purposes of calculating customer bill impacts).  The Joint Proposal, at Section 2.3, 
Footnote 9, provides that if a Commission order adopting the terms of the Joint Proposal is not 
issued prior to August 1, 2021, and thus new rates are not effective on August 1, 2021, the 
calculations that assumes new rates become effective August 1, 2021 will be recalculated based 
on the actual date new rates go into effect.
