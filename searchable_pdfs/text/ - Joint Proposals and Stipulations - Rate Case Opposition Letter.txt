Kimberly Brown 
 
June 2, 2021 
 
 
Opposition to 
Case 19-G-0309 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the 
Rates, Charges, Rules and Regulations of The Brooklyn Union Gas 
Company d/b/a National Grid NY for Gas Service Case 
19-G-0310 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, 
Charges, Rules and Regulations of KeySpan Gas East Corporation d/b/a 
National Grid for Gas Service 
 
I’m a homeowner and I’ve proudly lived in NYC for nearly 30 years. I joined the rate 
case to support the health and wellbeing of everyone who lives here. The Joint Proposal 
agreed upon by National Grid and the PSC will do the contrary. By not structuring a plan 
that reduces gas, the health and wellbeing of New York’s citizens are actually 
threatened by this Proposal. We can no longer do business as usual and turn our backs 
on the people who live next to the LNG plant, and on top of the pipeline. I oppose this 
Joint Proposal.
