STATEMENT OF NEW YORKERS FOR CLEAN POWER REGARDING JOINT PROPOSAL 
 
New Yorkers for Clean Power (“NYCP”) is a statewide collaborative campaign to rapidly shift to a 
clean energy economy. Through research, education, advocacy, and organizing, the campaign seeks 
to advance a range of clean energy, building decarbonization, and clean transportation solutions as 
well as creating jobs in these industries for all communities in New York State (NYS). NYCP is a 
Project of the Catskill Mountainkeeper Inc. 
 
NYCP respectfully submits the following comments on the Joint Proposal (JP)  filed in the above 
referenced proceedings. 
 
NYCP participated in the negotiations leading to the JP, but neither supports nor opposes the JP. 
NYCP appreciates the responsiveness from Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. 
(Company) in attending to NYCP’s request for updating its website to include content related to heat 
pumps in a visible and easily accessible manner.  NYCP also applauds the agreement to study and 
eventually adopt fairer and simpler rates for heat pump customers along with an improved price 
guarantee program. NYCP appreciates the Company agreeing to improve its customer outreach and 
education on the current heat pump friendly rates, which currently have abysmally low adoption rates. 
These are the reasons why NYCP does not oppose the JP’s adoption despite its shortcomings.

We found the Company’s reluctance to include information on heat pump friendly rates on its website 
a bit perplexing. We hope that the Company will see the value of doing so to support its proposed 
improvement in customer outreach and education on these rates. 
 
NYCP remains concerned by the level of traditional pipe replacement agreed to in the JP that locks in 
massive future ratepayer liabilities as well as greenhouse gas emissions even when less expensive 
and more climate friendly options are available.  The failure by Company and DPS Staff to substitute 
even a relatively small fraction of planned pipeline replacement with non-replacement strategies, 
such as the Electric Advantage Program or another non-pipe alternative (NPA), relining, repairs, or 
deferral in lower-risk situations until a suitable NPA can be implemented is the reason why NYCP 
does not support the JP.
