3

16

17

18

the total aggregate supply offered, to ensure more

The Company uses financial hedge products to mitigate

20

- 11 - 
call options.  CFDs are typically traded on a peak or 
1 
“5x16” basis, meaning their value is computed over the 
2 
16 peak hours (7 AM to 11 PM, prevailing time) on non-
3 
NERC-holiday weekdays.  For example, a buyer of a CFD 
4 
will negotiate to give the seller of a commodity at 
5 
settlement a fixed price per unit of the commodity in 
6 
exchange for the seller giving the buyer the market 
7 
price per unit.  CFDs may also be traded on an “around 
8 
the clock” basis, priced at the arithmetic average of 
9 
all 24 hours in a day, or on an “off-peak” basis, 
10 
meaning their value is computed over eight off-peak 
11 
hours (11 PM to 7 AM) during weekdays, and all weekend 
12 
and NERC holiday hours.  CFDs may be procured bi-
13 
laterally with a supplier or through an RFP.  Call 
14 
options typically provide a financial benefit to the 
15 
option holder when the contracted parameters, such as 
16 
spot price exceed prior agreed-upon thresholds or 
17 
strike price.  The premiums or purchase costs of such 
18 
options are related to the volatility of the 
19 
underlying product, the length of time prior to 
20 
delivery and the agreed-upon strike price.
