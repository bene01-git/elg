Q. 
Please describe the impact of the updated gas sales forecast. 
11 
A. 
Overall, the impact of the updated gas sales forecast was minimal.  The 
12 
updated forecast increased the projected storage dollars by less than one 
13 
percent, and forecast variable gas expense by approximately 1.5 percent.  
14 
The marginal commodity cost of gas and estimated marginal capacity cost 
15 
of gas were also minimally impacted, and in fact saw decreases of one 
16 
percent and eleven percent, respectively.  The changes to the forecast 
17 
fixed costs are mainly due to the corrections and updates discussed further 
18 
below. 
19 
 
20 
Q. 
Please describe the changes included in the update to the forecast 
21 
fixed costs in Exhibit __ (EDA-7CU). 
22 
A. 
The following three corrections and updates were made to the forecast 
23 
fixed costs previously provided in my direct testimony: 
24

Corrections and Updates Testimony of Elizabeth D. Arangio 
Page 4 of 4 
• Exhibit __ (EDA-7) in my direct testimony inadvertently excluded the 
1 
impact of pipeline rate cases currently pending before FERC.  The 
2 
updated exhibit is corrected to include $59 million per year of 
3 
estimated pipeline rate increases, attributed mainly to the Transco and 
4 
Texas Eastern rate cases.  This correction will not increase rates in the 
5 
Rate Year because it was excluded only in Exhibit __ (EDA-7), but 
6 
included in the bill impacts submitted by the Rate Design Panels in the 
7 
Companies’ initial filings.  
8 
• Exhibit __ (EDA-7CU) also updates the estimates of city gate peaking 
9 
reservation charges used in my direct testimony with actual costs from 
10 
peaking agreements that have been executed since the initial filings; 
11 
• In the initial filings, Exhibit __ (EDA-7) inadvertently excluded 
12 
estimated fixed costs associated with the existing Millennium, Union, 
13 
and TransCanada contracts for some of the Data Years because the 
14 
contracts do not contain evergreen provisions.  However, the 
15 
Companies reasonably expect that the contracts will be extended to 
16 
maintain access to liquid supply points.  Therefore, Exhibit __ (EDA-
17 
7CU) includes these costs for all three Data Years.
