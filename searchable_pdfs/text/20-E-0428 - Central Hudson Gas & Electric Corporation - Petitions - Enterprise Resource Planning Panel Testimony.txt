A. 
As we noted previously, over the last 40 years ten major functional 
1 
modules have been developed in-house with over 300 file exchanges to 
2 
support business processes. During this timeframe data redundancy has 
3 
increased significantly and data accuracy and controls have become very 
4 
difficult to support. Because the data is not normalized, harmonized or in 
5 
one place, Microsoft Office’s Excel is commonly used to combine and 
6 
interpret data, making Excel a single source for interpretation. This data 
7 
complexity coupled with the old “Natural” Mainframe coding language 
8 
makes the system fragile when seeking to further enhance and support 
9 
business process changes. System enhancements are expensive, due to 
10 
the limited supply of consultants with knowledge of the Adabas/Natural 
11 
programming language and it takes a long time to gain proficiency of the 
12 
custom code as there is limited documentation. Enhancements such as 
13 
the need for a Work Management system as noted in Recommendation 
14 
28.2 of the Company’s most recent Management Audit are not possible 
15 
within the current ERP Mainframe solution due to the complex system 
16 
architecture. Such architecture also requires traditional, intensive system 
17 
monitoring for transactional process breakage.

19 
the Company.

A.  
Transactional breakage refers to an instance where a scheduled program 
21 
or process within the system fails due to a variety of reasons such as lack
