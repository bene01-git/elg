DISTRIBUTION MAIN REPLACEMENT 
7 
Q. 
WHAT IS THE PURPOSE OF DISTRIBUTION MAIN REPLACEMENT 
8 
PROGRAMS GENERALLY? 
9 
A. 
 The purpose of distribution main replacement programs is to replace leak-prone 
10 
distribution main piping.  LPP consists of cast-iron, wrought iron and unprotected 
11 
steel pipe.  These pipe types are subject to external corrosion, especially cast-iron 
12 
piping which is subject to graphitic corrosion when the piping is exposed to water.  
13 
Corrosion presents the possibility of leaks or failure under potential loading 
14 
conditions.  In March 2012, the U.S. Department of Transportation Pipeline and 
15 
Hazardous Materials Safety Administration (“PHMSA”) issued an advisory 
16 
bulletin related to the continued use of cast-iron pipe and to alert gas distribution 
17 
system owners and operators to potential issues with the integrity of cast-iron pipe.  
18 
This bulletin did three things: (1)  advised owners and operators to conduct a 
19 
comprehensive review of their cast-iron distribution pipelines and replacement 
20 
programs and accelerate pipeline repair, rehabilitation and replacement of high risk 
21 
pipelines; (2) requested state agencies to consider enhancements to cast iron 
22

replacement plans and programs; and (3) alerted owners and operators of the 
1 
pipeline safety requirements for the investigation of failures.  This PHMSA 
2 
directive provided Con Edison with additional guidance for defining the pace (and 
3 
therefore, investment) and prioritization for this program..  
4 
Q. 
ARE LPP ACTIVELY LEAKING NATURAL GAS?  
5 
A. 
No.  LPP is a classification of piping based on its material properties and potential 
6 
risk of leakage.  While piping is classified as LPP, this does not imply that a specific 
7 
run of piping contains leaks.  Con Edison tracks leaks in piping and is required to 
8 
take action to repair leaks right away.    The integrity management program also 
9 
tracks variables that determine pipe segments’ risk of leakage that are used to 
10 
prioritize and plan the replacement program activities.  Therefore, the piping that is 
11 
included in this program for replacement by 2040 does not have active leaks.  
12
