Q. 
Please describe the customer service performance measures. 
1 
A. 
The PSC Complaint Rate metric measures the number of complaints per 100,000 
2 
customers “charged” to the Company on a monthly basis, but the metric’s target is 
3 
based on the average of twelve-monthly scores. Under the Companies’ current 
4 
rate plans, a complaint is considered “charged” when a customer, after first 
5 
contacting the Company to express dissatisfaction with an action, practice, or 
6 
conduct of the Company, contacts the Commission to express their dissatisfaction 
7 
with the Company’s resolution of their complaint.  
8 
 
The Transaction Satisfaction metric measures the satisfaction ratings resulting 
9 
from a random survey of residential customers who have contacted either 
10 
KEDNY or KEDLI.  
11 
 
The Telephone Answer Response measures the percentage of total calls that are 
12 
answered within 30 seconds by a Company representative.  
13 
 
The Adjusted Customer Bills metric measures the percentage of total customer 
14 
bills that later require adjustment due to an error by the Company.  
15 
 
(See Cases 19-G-0309 and 19-G-0310, Joint Proposal, at 101-05 (filed May 14, 
16 
2021).) 
17 
 
18 
Q. 
What is the function of customer service performance measures?  
19 
A. 
Customer service performance metrics provide utilities with incentives to provide 
20 
acceptable levels of customer service. Quality service is important to customers 
21

Q. 
How do you typically arrive at your proposed service target levels for a 
1 
company’s customer service performance measures? 
2 
A. 
I review a company’s annual service quality performance data for the last five 
3 
calendar years. In this case, I referenced data that was provided by the Companies 
4 
in response to UIU IR No. 34. I calculate the five-year average and then apply 
5 
two standard deviations. I choose two standard deviations because the result is a 
6 
more reliably accurate reflection of the average. Application of two standard 
7 
deviations in this context is a well-established practice in this industry. The 
8 
Companies conducted a similar analysis and provided those results in the 
9 
response to the IR.  
10 
 
11 
Q.  
Please detail your recommendation regarding KEDLI’s PSC Complaint Rate 
12

13

substantially without incurring an NRA, it does not provide an effective incentive

for KEDLI to maintain or improve its present level of service quality. Therefore, I

propose a more stringent target for KEDLI’s PSC Complaint Rate. The standard

A. 
Yes, I recommend that the NRA value for each metric be measured in basis points 
1 
as opposed to a set dollar amount. In prior rate plans, including the 2019 rate 
2 
plans, the customer service performance measures’ NRAs have been measured in 
3 
a flat dollar amount for all three rate years. However, the Companies were 
4 
increasing its revenue requirement for each of the three rate years, so the flat 
5 
dollar amount chosen for each measure became a smaller percentage of the utility 
6 
rate base at risk over time. Assigning a basis point value to the NRAs associated 
7 
with customer service performance measures will allow the percentage of utility 
8 
rate base at risk to remain constant if the utility rate base increases.  
9 
 
In the past, the Companies’ revenue requirement has increased, yet the monetary 
10 
value of the customer service NRAs remained frozen. Thus, the Companies’ risk 
11 
associated with the NRAs has been reduced.  To ensure consistency and motivate 
12 
each Company to continually provide adequate service, the NRA or incentive to 
13 
provide high level customer service should coincide with the value of a basis 
14 
point from rate case to rate case.
