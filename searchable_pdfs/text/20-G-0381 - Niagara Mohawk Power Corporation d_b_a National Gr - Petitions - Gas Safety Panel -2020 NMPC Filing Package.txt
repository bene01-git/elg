A. Bundled Walking, Atmospheric, and Inside Inspections Program 
5 
Q. 
Please describe the Bundled Walking, Atmospheric, and Inside 
6 
Inspections Program and what is required to implement the 
7 
mandated inside service line inspection intervals. 
8 
A. 
In 2015, the Commission issued a “Memorandum and Resolution 
9 
Adopting Gas Safety Regulation Amendments” that adopted a new 
10 
definition of “service line” in New York (Case 14-G-0357).  Under the 
11 
former definition in New York’s pipeline safety regulations (16 NYCRR 
12 
section 255.3(29)), the service line extended “to the first accessible fitting” 
13 
inside the customer’s building where a meter is located.  Under the new 
14 
definition, the service line extends “to the outlet of the customer meter or 
15 
at the connection to a customer’s piping, whichever is further downstream 
16 
where a meter is located within the building.”  While the Commission 
17 
adopted the new service line definition in 2015, it stayed implementation 
18 
pending development of a plan to address the new leak survey and 
19 
corrosion inspection requirements for inside facilities.  In April 2017, the 
20 
Commission issued its “Order Establishing Statewide Inspection 
21 


--- Page 16 ---
Testimony of Gas Safety Panel 
Page 14 of 63 
 
Schedules and Procedural Requirements” in Case 15-G-0244 (“Inside 
1 
Service Inspection Order”) that established mandated leak survey and 
2 
corrosion inspection intervals for inside services.  The Inside Service Line 
3 
Inspection Order set an aggressive schedule for utilities to complete 
4 
baseline leak surveys and inspections and shorter continuous inspection 
5 
intervals thereafter.   
6 
 
7 
 
The Company’s rates approved by the Commission in the 2017 Rate Case 
8 
reflect the forecast costs to perform inside service and atmospheric 
9 
corrosion inspections.  The Company proposes to continue recovery of

B. Enhanced Inactive Accounts Program

Q. 
Please describe the Company’s current inactive accounts process. 
2 
A. 
The Company has implemented inactive account process improvements 
3 
that considerably mitigate the risks of inactive accounts (i.e., accounts 
4 
without an active customer of record), including a revised end-to-end 
5 
process that provides a more structured approach to accessing unlocked 
6

7 
scripts, and training; dedicated additional resources to support the inactive 
8 
account process; made investments in the Company’s website and 
9 
customer and work management systems to support the inactive account 
10 
process; established a legal replevin process to secure access to gas 
11 
facilities through court orders; and improved customer communications 
12 
regarding inactive accounts (e.g., occupant/owner letters, post cards, door 
13 
stickers).  Significantly, the Company performs a site analysis on every 
14 
visit to determine whether conditions, such as trespassing at a vacant 
15 
premises or other observable unsafe conditions that would put a premise at 
16 
risk, warrant the Company immediately securing the service line.  In such 
17 
instances, the service cut and cap is expedited.   
18 
 
19 
The Company’s current process addresses inactive accounts within 60 
20 
days through a combination of field visits, back office work, and new
