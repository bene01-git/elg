Q. 
Does the Panel have any specific concerns with the particular regression 
13 
analysis performed by the SITP to forecast IT capital expenditures in the

15 
A. 
Yes.  The linear regression analysis used by the SITP provides no useful 
16 
information concerning what the appropriate level of IT investment should be 
17 
in the Rate Year.  Linear regression is used to determine whether a linear 
18 
relationship exists in a data set such that the relationship can be used to predict 
19 
future results.  The data set is comprised of points that measure the 
20 
relationship between at least two variables:  an independent variable, which is 
21 


--- Page 15 ---
 
Rebuttal Testimony of the Information Technology Panel 
Page 13 of 28 
expected to be the causative variable, and a dependent variable, which is 
1 
supposed to be the effect.  There is no basis for concluding that a linear 
2 
relationship exists or should exist between fiscal years and IT capital 
3 
investment levels.  Indeed, the Companies’ proposed IT capital investment 
4 
levels fluctuate up and down year over year depending on the nature of 
5 
projected capital investments planned for a given year.  Therefore, a linear 
6 
regression analysis is simply not an appropriate tool to use to forecast future 
7 
investment levels or to develop appropriate levels of IT investment for 
8 
ratemaking purposes. 
9 
 
10 
 
Further, for a regression analysis of past performance to be used to predict 
11 
future results, there should be an expectation that future conditions will be 
12 
similar to the past conditions used for the analysis.  Here, however, the 
13 
regression analysis covers a period that does not include spending such as 
14 
proposed for the CIS or SAP S/4 HANA projects, and therefore is not 
15 
representative of the Company’s future investment expectations.
