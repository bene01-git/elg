II.
SIR Program Costs 
1 
Q.
Please describe the SSIRP’s proposal to forecast the Companies’ SIR 
2 
program costs. 
3 
A.
The SSIRP proposes (at 49-53) to forecast KEDNY and KEDLI’s SIR 
4 
costs using a two-year average of actual costs, comprised of calendar years 
5 
(“CYs”) 2021 and 2022, plus inflation.  The SSIRP’s methodology results 
6 
in $21.041 million and $2.015 million reductions to KEDNY and KEDLI’s 
7 
revenue requirements, respectively.   
8 
9 
Q.
Do you agree with the SSIRP’s proposal? 
10 
A.
No.  I believe the Companies’ method results in a more accurate forecast 
11 
of SIR costs.  Specifically, the Companies’ approach is tailored to address 
12 
the concerns and differences unique to KEDNY and KEDLI’s 
13 
environmental remediation sites.  Rather than use a simple two-year 
14 
average, the Companies’ method is based on a detailed site-by-site 
15 
analysis, utilizing the work schedules from the New York State 
16 
Department of Environmental Conservation (“DEC”) and the actual 
17 
hands-on experience and knowledge of the Companies’ project managers 
18 
regarding anticipated site work.  The Companies also appropriately 
19 
normalized the forecasts to account for anticipated delays and historic 
20 
underspend, with KEDNY and KEDLI’s Manufactured Gas Plant 
21

recoveries.

Q.
Are there additional reasons why the Companies’ approach is 
5 
preferrable to the SSIRP’s two-year average? 
6 
A.
Yes.  The results of the Staff’s forecast are completely at odds with the 
7 
likely level of work that is anticipated to occur in the Rate Year and Data 
8 
Years.  For example, work at the Gowanus Canal, which impacts KEDNY, 
9 
is ongoing and expected to significantly ramp up.  At the Fulton site, New 
10 
York City has begun construction of the combined sewer overflow tanks 
11 
to comply with an EPA order.  In addition, KEDNY is working with the 
12 
owners/developers of both the Fulton and Citizens MGP sites to meet the 
13 
June 2026 Brownfield tax credit redevelopment deadline.  Similarly, 
14 
KEDLI expects to conduct remediation at the Inwood site.  This 
15 
anticipated work will result in levels of SIR costs incremental to that which 
16 
occurred in the two-year average used by the SSIRP.

III. 
Vendor Audit Recommendations
10 
Q.
Do you agree with the SSIRP’s recommendation (at 14-18) to assess 
11 
whether it is prudent to continue using the Veolia Middlesex 
12 
Treatment, Storage, and Disposal Facility (“TSDF”)? 
13 
A.
The Companies agree that it is important to review and assess the status 
14 
and compliance of TSDFs.  To that end, the Companies have a robust 
15 
process to evaluate TSDFs and ensure compliance with state and federal 
16 
regulations.  Specifically, the Companies established an internal Vendor 
17 
Advisory Group to evaluate, pre-qualify, and regularly audit their waste 
18 
disposal vendors who operate TSDFs.  The evaluation and audit process 
19 
includes, among other things, an independent audit conducted by a non-
20 
profit association that evaluates and audits waste vendors.  The Companies 
21

have relied on this process to assess their use of the Veolia Middlesex 
1 
TSDF and will continue to do so as part of normal periodic reviews.  At 
2 
this time, based on the level of risk of exposure as determined by the 
3 
Companies’ audit, as well as the cost implications of using other facilities, 
4 
with the next closest facility located approximately 980 miles from 
5

PULP’s Recommendations 
8 
Q.
PULP witness Yates claims (at 55-56) that KEDNY shareholders 
9 
should bear a percentage of future SIR costs through the earnings 
10 
sharing mechanism.  In addition, he claims (at 55-56) that the 
11 
Commission should assess the amount of SIR costs attributable to 
12 
deviations by KEDNY from the Commission’s SIR Generic Order and 
13 
require shareholders to fund a portion of the deferral balance based 
14 
on that assessment.  Do you agree with PULP witness Yates’ claims? 
15 
A.
No, I do not.  While PULP witness Yates makes a passing reference to the 
16 
Commission’s 2019 Order adopting KEDNY and KEDLI’s current rate 
17 
plans, which, incidentally, included full funding of SIR costs together with 
18 
deferral treatment and approval of the SIR Recovery Surcharge, he cites 
19 
no evidence of any deviations or analysis to support that KEDNY’s cost
