RE: Case 21-G-0394 - Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to the Rates, Charges,

Revised Rebuttal Testimony of Charles Lenns and Revised Exhibit __ (CL-1)
Dear Secretary Phillips: 
It has come to our attention that there was an error in the formula used to calculate Corning 
Natural Gas Corporation’s (“Corning” or the “Company”) required rate increase for the Rate 
Year Ending June 30, 2023, which appears in the Company’s rebuttal testimony and exhibits 
filed in this proceeding on December 10, 2021.  Specifically, the formula failed to include the 
“Purchased Gas” line item in the amount of $379,857 shown on Schedule A of Exhibit ___ (CL-
1) under the column headed “Company Adjustment.”  That omission also impacts amounts 
appearing in the subsequent columns of that Schedule and amounts appearing on Schedule J.  In 
addition, the reference to the amount of the required revenue increase appearing on page CL-14, 
line 4, of the rebuttal testimony of Charles Lenns requires correction.  Accordingly, enclosed for 
filing please find revised copies of the rebuttal testimony of Mr. Lenns and Exhibit ___ (CL-1).  
The only change to Mr. Lenns’s testimony is the aforementioned correction at page CL-14 and 
the only changes to Exhibit ___ (CL-1) are to Schedules A and J, both of which are identified as 
“revised.” 
Copies of the aforementioned revised testimony and revised exhibit are being served 
electronically on Administrative Law Judge Gregg C. Sayre, as well as on the parties to this 
proceeding. 
If you should have any questions regarding this matter, please contact me.
