Response:   
1. The NYSEG-RGE Electric Capital Portfolio Evaluation-Prioritization Matrix was first
implemented in January 2022.  It should be noted that this tool was designed with the intent
of evaluating the electric capital portion of the portfolio, and the Companies are currently
working towards incorporating these evaluation criteria into the larger Investment Planning
Prioritization Process which is designed to inform decision making on the entire portfolio
including Common, Gas, and Electric.
2. In the last five years, the Companies have implemented different tools/processes to aid in the
decision making around portfolio prioritization.  Prior to 2021, the Companies relied largely
on qualitative decision making focusing on the near-term and long-term priorities of the
business.  Portfolio prioritization decisions considered factors such as the overall business

The Investment Planning process has been enhanced since NYSEG & RG&E’s last rate case 
in 2019. The improvements include a defined approach to project prioritization, which helps 
guide and inform the development of the capital budget along with a formal governance 
process to request and receive approval for changes to the budget as needed.  In June 2021 a 
formal capital project/program prioritization process was implemented to inform the 
development of the 2022 budget. This built upon the existing methodology and process. 
Projects are ranked by their strategic importance with consideration given to how they will 
directly impact NYSEG & RG&E’s top priority of continuing to provide safe and reliable 
service to its customers. The formal capital project prioritization is performed annually at the 
beginning of the budget cycle to help develop the next year’s capital plan.  A detailed 
description of this process can be found in Chapter 3 of the NYSEG and RG&E 5-Year 
Capital Investment Plan (Dated April 1st, 2022) and Section IX of the NYSEG and RG&E 
Direct Testimony of Electric and Common Capital Expenditures Panel (Dated May 26th, 
2022).   
As noted in the response to Q1 (above), the Companies are actively working towards 
enhancing the current Investment Planning Process and will look to include more evaluation 
criteria such as those included in the NYSEG-RGE Electric Capital Portfolio Evaluation-
Prioritization Matrix.

REGARDING THE PRIORITIES WORKSHEET 
16. What is the purpose of the Priorities worksheet? 
 
Response:    
 
The purpose of the NYSEG-RGE Portfolio Prioritization Excel document was to evaluate all 
electric projects and programs, across a quantitatively based criteria, focusing on system 
performance needs, project maturity, and source document robustness.  The outputs were then 
used to inform qualitative decision making around which projects/programs were most critical to 
advance and include in the Companies’ direct testimony electric capital proposal.
