A. 
The goal of the ROE calculation is to determine the return on investment 
1 
that Con Edison requires to raise capital from equity investors.  At a high 
2

3

6

7 
member of the proxy group using the DCF and CAPM methodologies.  The 
8 
third step is to calculate a weighted average of these results, with the DCF 
9 
result given a 2/3 weighting and the CAPM result given a 1/3 rating.  In 
10 
these proceedings, the Staff Finance Panel identified a proxy group of 28 
11 
utility holding companies with similar financial performance.
