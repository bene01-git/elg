14 
returns.6  The Commission’s inquiry considered the merits of a generic 
15

16 
robust, but standardized, approach to setting ROE such that allowed returns 
17 
would be commensurate with the risk of the individual company and would 
18 
not be skewed by the shortcomings of a single methodology.

How is the required ROE determined? 
10 
While the cost of debt can be directly observed, the cost of equity and the 
11

12

13

14

15

16 
determine where within the range of results the cost of equity for a company 
17 
falls.  As a general proposition, the key consideration in determining the 
18 
cost of equity is to ensure that the methodologies employed reasonably 
19

20
