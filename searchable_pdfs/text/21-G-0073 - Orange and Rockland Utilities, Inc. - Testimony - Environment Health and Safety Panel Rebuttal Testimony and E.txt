Question No. : 608 
Subject:  O&M Expenses: Variance SIR expense 
Pages 60 and 62 of the Company’s EH&S Testimony refers to EH&S Exhibit 1 ,where it shows 
that the Rate Year Site Investigation Remediation expense is $9,676,000, however, the 
Company’s electric and gas Revenue Requirement models show the total Rate Year SIR expense 
is  $9,667,000 (electric $6,470,000, gas $3,197,000). 
Explain why there is a variance as indicated above, and indicate which one is the correct amount. 
Response 
Exhibit__EHS-1 and Exhibit__EHS-2 indicate that the Company calculated Rate Year SIR 
expenditures of $9,676,000 on a forecast prepared on January 10, 2021. 
The Company had to lock in the proposed revenue requirement prior to January 10 in order to 
prepare its revenue allocation and rate design proposals.  Based on an earlier forecast, the rate 
case revenue requirement includes $9,667,000 of SIR expenditures. 
The net $9,000 increase is comprised of the following changes: Suffern increased by $2,000, 
Haverstraw by $1,000, Port Jervis by $2,000, Borne by $3,000 and Metal bank by $3,000, while 
Spring Valley decreased by $2,000.
