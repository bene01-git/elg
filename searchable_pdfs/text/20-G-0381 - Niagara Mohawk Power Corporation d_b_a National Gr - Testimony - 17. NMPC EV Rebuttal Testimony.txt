While the Phase 1 program has made significant progress on Level 2 station development, 
progress has been slower for DCFC stations. This program did not intend to have a high volume 
of DCFC stations enrolled; however, overcoming hurdles to DCFC station development, such as 
the high cost of DCFC EVSE, will be important to address range anxiety concerns and support 
widespread EV adoption in the Company’s territory.  
Installation and EVSE costs for DCFC stations are significantly higher than for Level 2 stations; 
this cost disparity represents a significant barrier to DCFC station development. DCFC stations 
also face additional barriers, such as demand charges, that make it difficult to develop a 
sustainable business case for these types of stations. The Company’s program staff are actively 
reviewing program progress and adjusting methods to target site hosts in under-represented 
segments and to encourage DCFC station development.
