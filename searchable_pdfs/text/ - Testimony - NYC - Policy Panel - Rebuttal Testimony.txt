Q. 
WHAT IS THE PURPOSE OF YOUR REBUTTAL TESTIMONY? 
2 
A. 
The following rebuttal is provided in response to the Direct Testimony filed by Department 
3 
of Public Service Staff (“Staff”).   
4 
The Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (“CLCPA”) establishes that New 
5 
Yorkers cannot continue business as usual if we are to succeed in the transition away from 
6 
fossil fuels for the vast majority of our buildings and infrastructure. The CLCPA provides 
7 
clear guidelines that any rate plan adopted by the Commission must achieve this by 
8 
including requirements to plan for and implement electrification and non-pipe alternatives 
9 
(“NPA”) programs that will ultimately provide a pathway for decommissioning gas 
10 
infrastructure.  
11 
The City respectfully submits that Staff’s testimony does not sufficiently address CLCPA 
12 
goals in its support for certain gas pipeline investments and depreciation rates that will 
13 
unnecessarily increase costs to ratepayers.  This testimony also specifically addresses the 
14 
City’s ongoing concerns related to affordability and the overall magnitude of both the 
15 
Companies’ requested rate increases, as well as Staff’s proposed increases.   
16 
While the City recognizes that there is a tension between the costs of decarbonization and 
17 
the ability to provide reliable and affordable power, it is incumbent on all stakeholders to 
18 
set utility rates to advance the mandate of the CLCPA in a way that is affordable for 
19 
ratepayers. The City proposes to leverage reducing investments in infrastructure and 
20 
lowering the annual depreciation expense to reduce rate increases.  Finally, we briefly
