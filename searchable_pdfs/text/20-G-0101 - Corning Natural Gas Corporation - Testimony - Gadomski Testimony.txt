Q. 
Please summarize Commission precedent regarding 
9 
cost recovery of utility incentive compensation 
10

11 
A. 
The Commission’s requirements for incentive 
12 
compensation cost recovery were set forth in the 
13 
June 17, 2011 Order Establishing Rates for 
14 
Electric Service in Case 10-E-0362, a rate 
15 
proceeding regarding Orange and Rockland 
16 
Utilities, Inc. (O&R).  I will refer to this 
17 
later in my testimony as the “2011 O&R Rate 
18 
Order.”  In that Order, the Commission explained 
19 
that a utility must demonstrate that its total 
20 
level of employee compensation, inclusive of 
21 
incentive pay, is reasonable relative to peer 
22 
companies.  The Commission explained that 
23 
utilities should demonstrate the reasonableness 
24

Case

1

companies.  However, even if the utility

4 
reasonably in line with that of its peers, the 
5 
Commission also stated that an incentive pay 
6 
plan will not be deemed reasonable if it

includes performance targets that adversely 
8 
affect customer interests or are inconsistent 
9 
with Commission policies.
