BACKGROUND 
 
 
Since 2003, National Grid has offered qualifying 
business customers grant assistance through a suite of non-rate 
incentive programs.2  The programs are designed to attract, 
retain, and expand businesses for the purpose of creating and 
retaining jobs, incentivizing private capital investment, and 
retaining and adding electric load in the Company’s service 
territory.  Customers seeking to participate in each of the non-
rate incentive programs are subject to meeting minimum 
eligibility requirements associated with each program.  
Additionally, the programs have a cap on the maximum grant 
allowance.  The Company also operates on an annual total budget 
for its suite of economic development programs, which the 
Commission establishes as part of each rate plan.  Pursuant to

DISCUSSION 
 
 
It is long-standing practice and policy for New York 
State utilities to offer economic development grants and rate 
incentives, and the Commission has regularly approved ratepayer-
funded economic development programs in recognition of the key 
role that utilities play in attracting and retaining businesses 
within their service territories.  Well-designed business 
retention and attraction programs offered by utilities can 
benefit all customers in the service territory by stabilizing 
energy sales and moderating volumetric rates, and they help 
ensure that utilities are engaged with other economic 
development authorities, such as ESD, in identifying and 
pursuing economic development leads.  These programs also 
endeavor to enhance job opportunities for utility customers 
within the service territory. 
 
 
National Grid’s economic development programs have 
leveraged significant capital investment, job creation and

CLCPA Analysis 
 
 
New York’s recently enacted CLCPA reflects an 
ambitious commitment to reduce GHG emissions and achieve net-
zero emissions economywide, increase renewable energy usage, and 
prioritize climate justice.  Among its provisions, CLCPA §§7(2) 
and 7(3) require state agencies to conduct analyses regarding 
certain types of decisions.  CLCPA §7(2) requires all state 
agencies to consider whether certain final agency actions are 
inconsistent with or will interfere with the attainment of the 
statewide GHG emission limits established under Environmental 
Conservation Law (ECL) Article 75.  CLCPA §7(2) further states 
that, if a decision is deemed to be inconsistent with, or to 
interfere with, the attainment of the statewide GHG emissions 
limits, the responsible agency, office, authority, or division 
must provide a detailed statement of justification as to why 
such limits may not be met and identify alternatives or GHG 
mitigation measures to be required.  In turn, CLCPA §7(3) 
provides that in considering and issuing permits, licenses, and 
other administrative approvals and decisions, state agencies, 
including the Commission, shall not disproportionately burden 
disadvantaged communities, as such are defined under state law.  
CLCPA §7(3) also requires that all state agencies prioritize 
reductions in GHG emissions and co-pollutants in disadvantaged 
communities.  As regulations to implement the economywide

CONCLUSION 
 
 
This Order authorizes National Grid to utilize its 
deferred credit balance to fund electric economic development 
grant programs.  The deferred credit balance resulted from 
underspending on such programs during the terms of prior rate 
plans.  This authorization will ensure that there are no delays 
in the Company’s ability to continue accepting economic 
development grant applications, approving economic development 
grant applications and dispersing economic development grant 
funding. 
 
The Commission orders: 
1. Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National 
Grid is authorized to access its electric economic development 
program deferred credit balance to fund additional electric 
economic development grants, consistent with the discussion in 
the body of this Order. 
2. This proceeding is continued.
