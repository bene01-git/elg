Q.

11 
A. 
I use the term “correction” to refer to material that was incorrect as presented in the 
12 
original filing on July 16, 2021.  That material may be incorrect because it contains the 
13 
wrong data, was calculated erroneously, or was improperly labeled or formatted in the 
14 
initial filing.  In contrast, I use the term “update” to refer to situations where the 
15 
original presentation was correct at the time of the filing, but where more current data 
16 
are now available and can be appropriately substituted for the original information.  
17 
Also included in the category of “updates” are changes to the Company’s original 
18 
position on policies or other issues.  In referring to corrections and updates 
19 
collectively or without distinguishing among them, I may use the term “changes” or 
20 
“modifications.”
