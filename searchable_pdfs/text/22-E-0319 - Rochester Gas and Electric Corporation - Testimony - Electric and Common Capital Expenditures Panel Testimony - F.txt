II. 
OVERVIEW 
5 
Q. 
What is the overall purpose of the Panel’s Rebuttal Testimony?  
6 
A. 
We rebut the New York State Department of Public Service Staff (“Staff”) Electric 
7 
Infrastructure and Operations Panel’s and Shared Services Panel’s significant downward 
8 
adjustments to the Companies’ overall capital expenditure levels by 53% at NYSEG and 
9 
31% at RG&E over the 2023-2026 period (with significantly higher reduction 
10 
percentages for the Asset Condition category).  Staff’s recommended reductions raise 
11 
significant concerns about the ongoing reliability of the Companies’ electric systems and 
12 
the overall safe and reliable operation of the Companies’ businesses.  More importantly, 
13 
if implemented, Staff’s proposed sharp reductions raise the real possibility of harm to 
14 
customers.  The Panel also rebuts Staff’s Climate Leadership and Community Protection 
15 
Act Panel’s (“CLCPA Panel”) recommendations to reduce the scope and budget for the 
16 
Companies’ proposed Phase 1 transmission investments, including recommendations to 
17 
reject certain projects or project components, and to eliminate all proposed Phase 2 
18 
transmission projects from these proceedings.  We also address certain positions and 
19 
statements made in the Staff Electric Infrastructure and Operations Panel and Staff 
20 
Shared Services Panel regarding common capital that appear to be mischaracterizations 
21 
based on Staff lacking the benefit of full context.
