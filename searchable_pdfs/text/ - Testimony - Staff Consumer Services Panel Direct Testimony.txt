Customer Service Performance Indicators 
12 
Q. 
What is the purpose of annual customer service 
13 
performance indicator mechanisms? 
14 
A. 
Customer service performance indicators, or 
15 
CSPIs, are data points used by the Office of 
16 
Consumer Services to measure the utilities’ 
17 
levels of customer service so the Commission can 
18 
determine whether the utilities are providing 
19 
adequate customer service to their customers.  
20 
These CSPI mechanisms help align shareholder and 
21 
ratepayer interests by providing earnings 
22 
consequences related to the quality of service 
23 
that a utility provides to its customers.  The 
24 


--- Page 47 ---
Cases 25-E-0072 and 25-G-0073     Staff Consumer Services Panel 
 
 
46 
 
mechanism links earnings directly to a utility’s 
1 
performance on specific measures of customer 
2 
service.  Based on historical performance, the 
3 
Commission establishes target levels for metrics 
4 
during utility rate cases.  Currently, such 
5 
mechanisms are in effect for all of New York’s 
6 
major investor-owned utilities, Corning Natural 
7 
Gas Corporation, Liberty Utilities (Saint 
8 
Lawrence Gas) Corporation, Liberty Water and 
9 
Veolia Water. 
10 
Q. 
Why are CSPIs necessary? 
11 
A. 
As monopoly providers of utility delivery 
12 
services, investor-owned utilities, or IOUs, 
13 
such as Con Edison, do not have a profit-based 
14 
incentive to provide adequate customer service 
15 
because their customers cannot select another 
16 
utility based on the quality of service 
17 
received.  However, a primary responsibility of 
18 
the utilities is to provide adequate customer 
19 
service and this is in the public interest.  A 
20 
CSPI mechanism establishes an incentive for 
21 
utilities to provide adequate levels of customer 
22 
service. 
23
