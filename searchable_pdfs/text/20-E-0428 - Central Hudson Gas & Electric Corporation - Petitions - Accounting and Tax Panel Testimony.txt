6 
Q. 
What is the general basis upon which property taxes levied upon the  
7 
Company have historically been determined? 
8 
A.  
Property taxes have been based generally on the “market value” of 
9 
property and include taxes on land and the structures and/or equipment 
10 
erected or affixed to the land.  Additionally, utilities also pay special 
11 
franchise taxes, i.e., property taxes on utility equipment located in public 
12 
right-of-way.  
13 
In New York State, public utility property is valued under a method known 
14 
as the “cost approach.”  Under this approach, the New York State Office of 
15 
Real Property Tax Services (“ORPTS”), and most of the local assessors in 
16 
the Company’s service territory, determine value by using a Reproduction 
17 
Cost New Less Depreciation (“RCNLD”) methodology for utility property.  
18 
RCNLD calculates what it would cost to reproduce property at current 
19 
construction costs based on original cost and a trending index, subtracts 
20 
an allowance for depreciation and obsolescence, if any, and adds the 
21 
value of land to arrive at a “value” for the entire property.  RCNLD is used 
22 


--- Page 55 ---
Case 20-E-____; Case 20-G-____

53 
 
only to value certain of the Company’s structures and all of its taxable 
1 
equipment.  The value of land and office buildings is determined by

comparable sales data, also known as the “market approach.”
