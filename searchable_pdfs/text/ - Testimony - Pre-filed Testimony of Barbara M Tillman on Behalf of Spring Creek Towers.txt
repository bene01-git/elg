Q. Please describe the physical plant at SCT. 
6 
A.  The SCT Power Plant is the sole source of electric service, domestic hot water 
7

8

10

11

12

13

14 
but upon the next renewal of its City permit, SCT will be required to use No.2 
15 
fuel oil as its back up fuel.

Q. What incremental costs are required to maintain fuel switching

capability? 
2 
A. There are numerous annual operating, maintenance, regulatory compliance and 
3 
administrative costs associated solely with the use of fuel oil during gas service 
4 
interruptions. These include: secondary spill containment, discharge prevention 
5 
measures, discharge or drainage controls, discharge countermeasures; tank 
6 
inspections, tests; personnel training; security; lighting requirements; and the 
7 
maintenance of a management plan. 
8 
There is the cost of maintaining leak detection and cathodic protection systems, 
9 
inspection of underground piping; 5-year tank life inspections: reports to various 
10 
agencies; tank maintenance; repairs to delivery equipment; maintenance of linings 
11 
and corrosive protection systems; and annual fees for certificate registrations.  
12 
Adequate staffing is required to supervise deliveries, and to verify fuel oil quality 
13 
and sulfur content. 
14 
Security must be provided for delivery areas, and SCT maintains insurance 
15 
covering all aspects of fuel deliveries and potential spills, and contracts for 
16 
emergency response to fuel spills. 
17 
There can be additional costs for emergency deliveries, if necessary. 
18 
Finally, SCT must train staff to meet all these obligations and requirements. These 
19 
costs are incurred regardless of how much oil is burned.

7 
A. We have found the negotiating process difficult.  SCT’s most recent

13 
uncertainty concerning its energy budget.
