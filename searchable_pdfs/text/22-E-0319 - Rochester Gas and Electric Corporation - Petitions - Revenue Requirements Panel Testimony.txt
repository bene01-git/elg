8 
Electric, NYSEG Gas, RG&E Electric and RG&E Gas).  The rate changes are presented 
9 
in the table below and reflect the rate request levels for these one-year rate filings.  The 
10 
Companies are requesting Delivery revenues to be based on a 10.20% return on equity 
11 
(“ROE”) and 50% equity ratio.  The Policy Panel testimony and exhibits provide a 
12 
complete summary of the primary drivers that contribute to the requested increases.  As 
13 
described by the Policy Panel, there are five major categories of costs that drive the rate 
14 
increases.  The first category is the residual rate pressure from the 2019 Rate Case and 
15 
the second category are costs that reflect meeting and maintaining the Companies’ core 
16 
business requirements.  These first two categories of costs represent about 2/3 of the 
17 
average rate increases shown in Table 1 below.  The third category of costs are those 
18 
costs driven by New York State policies.  The fourth category of costs are focused on 
19 
furthering customer benefits, including reliability and resiliency.  The fifth category 
20 
represents primarily the Companies’ proposed ROE and equity ratio which are supportive 
21 
of maintaining cash flow metrics which are critical in minimizing the cost of debt that is
