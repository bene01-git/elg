Q.    
What are Post-Model Adjustments and why are they utilized? 
10 
A. 
Post-Model Adjustments, also referred to as Out of Model Adjustments in the NYSEG 
11 
and RG&E Deliveries and Revenues Panels’ testimony, are made to account for future 
12 
energy efficiency, photovoltaic (PV) solar generation, electric vehicle and heat pump 
13 
impacts on deliveries.  These are collectively referred to as distributed energy resources 
14 
(“DERs”).  These Post-Model adjustments are allocated monthly across the residential, 
15 
commercial, industrial, and municipal rate classes.   
16 
Q.    
Do Staff’s Post-Model adjustments differ from the Companies’ Post-Model Adjustments? 
17 
A. 
Yes.  While both Staff and the Companies’ Post-Model adjustments reduce the level of 
18 
deliveries forecasted, Staff’s adjustments appear to understate the impact of individual 
19 
DERs, which results in an overstated higher net unit forecast.   
20
