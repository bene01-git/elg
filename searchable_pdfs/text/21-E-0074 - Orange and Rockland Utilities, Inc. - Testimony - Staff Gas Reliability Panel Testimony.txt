Design Day Demand – Supply Balance 
2 
Q. 
Define what is meant by a design day and how it 
3 
 
differs from a peak day. 
4 
A. 
A design day is defined as a day of the highest 
5 
natural gas throughput on a natural gas 
6 
distribution system based on the coldest weather 
7 
experienced in that area.  It represents the 
8 
level to which a gas utility builds its system 
9 
and procures capacity and supply assets, 
10 
including energy efficiency and demand response 
11 
assets, to serve its firm load based on historic 
12 
weather conditions, which, in some cases, can go 
13 
back 100 years.  The peak day represents the 
14 
greatest demand that a gas company experiences 
15 
in each heating season.
