Q. Does NYPA provide financial assistance to its customers for 
11 
projects? 
12 
A. NYPA provides its customers with low cost on-bill financing for project 
13 
costs.  On-bill financing allows the customer to finance the upfront costs of 
14 
a project through a loan that is then paid back over time via a monthly 
15 
charge on the customer’s electric bill. This helps address the high upfront 
16 
costs of clean energy projects, making projects more financially feasible to 
17 
customers. Access to NYPA financing typically allows for a shorter project 
18 
payback period compared to market rates that the customer can access 
19 
independently.  NYPA allows for the payback of the loan to be done on a 
20 
customer’s electric bill from NYPA, which in effect allows the customer to
