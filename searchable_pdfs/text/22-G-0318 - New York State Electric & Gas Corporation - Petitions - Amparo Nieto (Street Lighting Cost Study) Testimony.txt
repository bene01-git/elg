A. Conversion Cost Analysis 
4 
Q.  
How did you estimate the total expected conversion cost to LED lights? 
A. 
The replacement cost was computed in 2022 dollars and includes the cost of the lamp 
5 
head in 2022 dollars, and the individual cost components of installation, such as labor 
6 
rates, other materials, and overhead expenses, which differ for NYSEG and RG&E.  The 
7 
prices were provided as of 2022 and were escalated by a 2% inflation rate to compute 
8 
costs in nominal years for the remaining of the five-year period.  After aggregating the 
9 
existing streetlight data into general light categories, I identified the LED sizing chart 
10 
provided to determine minimum wattage-to-lumen equivalencies.  These were applied to 
11 
each of the non-LED bulb types that are currently owned by the Companies.  
12 
Replacement LED lamp heads for each category were then selected by matching the lamp 
13 
head type and requiring a wattage which would meet or exceed the lumen output of the 
14 
existing streetlight.1F2  Strictly speaking, replacement cost is the cost that the Companies 
15 
would incur to replace an existing light, and any necessary fixtures, at current market 
16 
prices, with a similar light that would provide the same functionality.  However, in many 
17 
cases the most similar LED lights will have a greater lumen output despite its lower 
18 
wattage, while remaining cost effective. 
19 
 
2  
Based on the staggered lumen outputs and available selection of LEDs, about 58% of the LED replacement 
selections for NYSEG and 62% of LED replacement selections for RG&E were noted as oversized, meaning 
that the replacement LED bulb will exceed the lumen-based need by at least 20 watts.

12

13

13

14 
considering other loads that will be connected to the same transformer.  As a result, the 
15 
calculation of annualized LED lights does not include the replacement cost of line 
16 
transformer serving streetlighting loads.  The Companies also indicated that they majority 
17
