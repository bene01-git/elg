17 
 
A. 
Net salvage is any cash received for an asset 
1 
removed from service, less the cost to remove 
2 
it.  Depreciation is a method of recovering 
3 
capital cost, less net salvage, related to plant 
4 
in service over the plant’s expected useful 
5 
life. 
6 
Q. 
Please explain the term “average service life”. 
7 
A. 
The average service life is the time period, in 
8 
years, over which the original cost of plant 
9 
should be depreciated.   
10 
Q. 
Please define “depreciation rate” and 
11 
“depreciation expense”. 
12 
A. 
A depreciation rate is a percentage rate applied 
13 
to the gross plant in service, by account, to 
14 
determine the annual depreciation expense.  The 
15 
annual depreciation expense is the product of 
16 
gross plant-in-service and the applicable 
17 
depreciation rate and allows for the recovery of 
18 
an investment, less net salvage, over the 
19 
expected life of the investment.
