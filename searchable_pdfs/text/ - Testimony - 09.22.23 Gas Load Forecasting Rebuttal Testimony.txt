Why do the Companies believe the SFIP’s alternate forecasting methodology

A. 
There are several reasons why the SFIP’s methodology should not be utilized.  For

10

12

13

14 
well the model fits historical data, but not how well the model will forecast future 
15 
data.  When preparing a forecast, it is crucial to measure predictive accuracy of 
16 
competing models and use the predictive accuracy for selection criteria.   
17 
 
18

20 
part of the historical data that forms the test-set and estimates parameters of the 
21

1 
consists of the historical data that occurred prior to the observations that form the

3
