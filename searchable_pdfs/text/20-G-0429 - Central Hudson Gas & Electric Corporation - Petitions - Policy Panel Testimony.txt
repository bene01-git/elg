driven by the capital program; 2) increasing expenses associated with 
9

in employee levels. 
11

12 
plan.

14 
the need to modernize electric, gas, and IT infrastructure.  Modernization 
15 
of electric transmission and distribution infrastructure is required due to 
16 
the underlying age and condition of the assets and the need to enable the 
17 
Distributed System Platform to better monitor and control the distribution 
18 
system while facilitating increasing levels of DER penetration, which is 
19 
directly tied to the goals of CLCPA.  Modernization of electric 
20
