CASE 20-G-0101 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to 
 
the Rates, Charges, Rules and Regulations of

ORDER APPROVING TARIFF AMENDMENTS ON A PERMANENT BASIS 
 
(Issued and Effective November 22, 2021) 
 
 
BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On May 21, 2021, Corning Natural Gas Corporation 
(Corning or the Company) filed amendments to its gas tariff 
schedule, P.S.C. No. 7 - Gas, as authorized by the Commission.1  
The Company’s proposed tariff amendments, which effectuated the 
rates and provisions for the Rate Year (the twelve-month period 
ending January 31, 2022), became effective on a temporary basis 
on June 1, 2021.  On July 27, 2021, and September 28, 2021, the 
Company filed further tariff revisions to clarify certain terms 
approved in the 2021 Rate Order.  These tariff amendments became

BACKGROUND  
 
 
 The 2021 Rate Order approved a one-year gas rate 
plan, which provided a revenue requirement increase of $505,000 
or 1.77 percent.  In addition, the 2021 Rate Order includes 
performance measures to ensure Corning continues its leak-prone 
pipe replacement at its current level, increases its effort to 
reduce its leak backlog, and fully complies with pipeline safety 
regulations.   
 
 
The 2021 Rate Order authorized Corning to file, on not 
less than five days’ notice, to take effect on a temporary basis 
on June 1, 2021, tariff amendments as necessary to effectuate 
the provisions of the 2021 Rate Order.  The Company was required 
to serve copies of its filing on all active parties to the 
proceeding.  The tariff amendments would not become effective on 
a permanent basis until approved by the Commission.   
 
TARIFF FILING 
 
      On May 21, 2021, the Company filed gas tariff 
amendments to P.S.C. No. 7 - Gas to effectuate the rates and 
provisions pursuant to the Commission’s 2021 Rate Order.  On 
July 27, 2021, the Company filed further revisions to address 
the Department of Public Service staff’s request that Corning 
correct an error in the dead band for Loss and Unaccounted for 
Gas on Leaf No. 73 and to clarify that the minimum charge listed 
on Leaf No. 155 applies to each customer.  On September 28, 
2021, the Company filed additional tariff revisions to reflect

terms approved in the 2021 Rate Order.  
 
LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §66, the Commission has the legal 
authority to review proposed tariff leaves, as well as modify, 
reject, or approve such filed tariffs.  As such, the Commission 
has the legal authority to review the Company’s filing and 
approve and make effective tariff amendments as prescribed in 
this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
 The Company’s proposed May 21, 2021, gas tariff 
amendments, as supplemented by the further revisions filed on 
July 27, 2021, and September 28, 2021, properly reflect the 
directives of the 2021 Rate Order.  The filing includes rates, 
charges, revenue target updates, rules, and regulations 
necessary to implement the 2021 Rate Order.  No party has 
objected to these amendments.  The Commission, therefore,
