Q. 
In your opinion, would such steps, if adopted, be sufficient to eliminate the need for a 
9 
prudence proceeding, as DPS’s Office of Investigation and Enforcement (“OIE”) 
10 
recommended in its December, 2022 report in Case 22-M-0645 (“OIE Report”)5?  
11 
A. 
No. I remain steadfast in my belief that a prudence review should occur. The measures 
12 
proposed by the Staff Consumer Services Panel would not be sufficient as a substitute for 
13 
such a review because they would be applied prospectively; they would do nothing to 
14 
address the potential harm that may have been caused by imprudent actions on the part of 
15 
the Company in the implementation of its new CIS system. Such harm can only be 
16 
determined through the sort of prudency proceeding recommended in OIE Report. 
17 
Specifically, I remain concerned about expenses and capital investments that Central 
18 
Hudson may have incurred, or may incur, imprudently in the development of CIS system, 
19 
but that may not have been sufficiently identified or excluded from consideration in Central 
20 
Hudson’s proposals; and the extent to which these problems may have contributed to the 
21 
Company’s deteriorating credit metrics. For all these reasons, I continue to believe that a 
22 
prudence review should be conducted.

Bill Impacts of Staff Proposals For EAP Heating Gas Participants 
14 
Q. 
Please summarize the Company’s and Staff’s revenue requirements in these cases. 
15 
A.  
Central Hudson proposes to increase its electric base delivery revenue by $139.479 million 
16 
during the Rate Year (pg. 17, line 21) and its gas base delivery revenue by $41.478 million 
17 
(pg. 18, line 18). Staff’s recommended revenue requirement increases for electricity and 
18 
gas are, respectively: $74.952 million, or 16.98% (pg. 24, line 1) and $25.153, or 18.55% 
19 
(pg. 24, line 13). (Staff Rate Panel).
