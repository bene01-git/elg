TRAINING PROGRAM ENHANCEMENTS
General Training Needs
We anticipate that the phased development of the Training Academy will enable significantly more training for employees in terms of quality of delivery and
number of course offerings. The human capital resources, physical classrooms, live villages, and equipment will provide the foundation to implement new 
initiatives, best practices and/or enhancements for the following: For purposes of this exhibit, we have broken out the trainings that are expected in RY1 
and those to be initiated post RY1. 
RY1
Increased procedure documentation and maintenance to support Company compliance/regulatory requirements (e.g. OQ, OSHA, Safety Initiatives)
Implementation of industry standardized curriculum and creation of annual refresher training (e.g. O&M and new equipment).
Live gas scenario replicating field conditions for OQ, gas mechanics, commercial reps, meter testers, supervisor on call, service workers, and externa
emergency response agencies.
Live electric scenario replicating field conditions for line, commercial, meter testers, loop crew, electricians, wires down, and supervisor on call
Post RY1
Develop Work Methods documents for current tasks as well as new tools and equipment prior to being placed in service.
Enhanced opportunities to partner with emergency services, municipalities, trades, contractors, education (including PETCH), neighboring utilities (e.g
electrical hazard safety training).

Enhance Expert Line Training from a one day annual training (8 hours) to a five day annual training (40 hours).  The increased training will cover the
following:
Distribution Automation- there will be a fully functional DA scheme installed at the Academy that will allow the linemen to learn hands on
Distribution Generation- There will be a fully functional solar array that will allow the linemen to learn hands on
Equipment training- the expert school will include training for new equipment that is placed in service
Existing Work Methods such as EPZ grounding, cable pulling, winching and Lock Out /Tag Out procedures will be reviewed
