Q. 
Why does the Panel recommend using a 10-year 
20 
weather normalization instead of the Companies’

proposed 30-year weather normalization?

A. 
Compared to a 30-year average weather 
23 
normalization, a 10-year average weather 
24 


--- Page 16 ---

normalization more accurately reflects weather 
1 
trends and is more likely to accurately predict

the weather in the forecasting period.  A 30-

year average incorporates older data that

becomes less relevant when changing climate

17 
KEDLI during the Rate Year.
