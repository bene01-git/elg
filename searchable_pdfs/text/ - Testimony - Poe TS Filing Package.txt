Q. 
Please describe the changes to the Companies’ gas sales forecasts. 
1 
A. 
My corrections and updates testimony and exhibits provided forecasts of the Companies’ 
2 
gas sales for the Rate Year and Data Years using data through February 2019.  My second 
3 
supplemental testimony and exhibits updated the Companies’ sales forecast to reflect the 
4 
operational plan adopted by the Companies in response to the Settlement between the 
5 
Companies and DPS Staff in Case 19-G-0678, which provides for new customer 
6 
connections through September 1, 2021.   
7 
 
8 
 
The EE Order approved certain energy efficiency targets in an effort to reduce energy 
9 
consumption and combat climate change.  The EE Order directs utilities in New York State, 
10 
including the Companies, to invest in energy efficiency and clean heat technologies from 
11 
2020 through 2025.  Once implemented, these technologies are projected to provide 
12 
reductions in energy usage, baseload, and peak demand in the Companies’ service 
13 
territories.  As implementation of the energy efficiency and clean heat technologies will 
14 
occur in the Rate Year and Data Years, the Companies are updating the forecast to 
15 
incorporate the energy reductions these technologies are projected to provide.  Further 
16 
discussion on how the technologies from the EE Order will be implemented is provided in 
17 
the supplemental testimony of the Future of Heat Panel. 
18 
 
19 
For KEDNY, the updates to the gas sales forecast to reflect the incremental demand 
20 
response and energy efficiency result in a decrease in the Rate Year from 1,689,187,510 
21 
therms to 1,687,554,812 therms compared to KEDNY’s second supplemental sales 
22 
forecast.  For KEDLI, these updates result in a decrease in the Rate Year from 
23
