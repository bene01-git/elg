Dear Secretary Phillips: 
Pursuant to the terms of the Joint Proposal (“JP”) adopted by the State of New York Public 
Service Commission the (“Commission”) in its Order Adopting Joint Proposal (the “JP” Order), 
issued October 12, 2023 in the above-captioned proceedings, New York State Electric & Gas 
Corporation (“NYSEG”) and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation (“RG&E” and, 
collectively, the “Companies”) hereby withdraw their requests for waiver of negative revenue 
adjustments (“NRAs) associated with various missed customer service targets.   
By letter dated January 28, 2022 in Cases 19-E-0378 et al., the Companies petitioned for waiver 
of NRAs resulting from the Companies not meeting the targets for Percent of Estimated Bills in 
2021.  On February 7, 2023, the Companies filed a petition requesting a waiver of the 2022 
NRAs incurred as a result of the Companies not meeting the expected levels of performance for 
Percent of Estimated Bills, Calls Answered in 30 Seconds, PSC Complaint Rate, and Customer 
Satisfaction Survey.  Both these petitions remain pending before the Commission.

Pursuant to the terms of the Commission-approved JP, Section XVII.A and Appendix P, the 
Companies agreed that,  
 
upon issuance of a final Commission Order approving the terms of this Joint Proposal, the 
Companies agree to withdraw the above-identified pending petitions for 2021 and 2022 customer 
service performance. The deferred regulatory liabilities for 2021 NRAs ($1.65 million) and for 
2022 NRAs ($16.92 million) will be used for rate moderation during the term of this agreement. 
The Signatory Parties agree that these customer service NRAs, which have been deferred by the 
Companies, will be used in RY1, RY2, and RY3 for the benefit of customers as shown in 
Appendix AA (the “NRA – Customer Service Quality Metrics – New Amortization” line items).  
 
Accordingly, the Companies hereby withdraw the above-identified petitions.  The Companies 
will use the associated NRAs for rate moderation pursuant to the terms of the JP.
