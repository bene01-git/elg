Q. 
Can you describe the process used for identifying and prioritizing the 
9 
technology investments within the Five-Year Capital Plan?  
10 
A. 
The Central Hudson Technology group collaborates and closely partners 
11 
with the 12 major areas responsible for Central Hudson operations to 
12 
create the prioritized portfolio of technology projects in the Five-Year 
13 
Capital Plan.  In 2022, the Technology group implemented an objective 
14 
prioritization framework to support the prioritization and selection of all 
15 
capital technology investments.  This framework, created in partnership 
16 
with the Company’s Technology Steering Committee (“TSC”) and Capital 
17 
Asset Review and Evaluation Committee (“CARE”), consists of six 
18

attributes derived from qualifying questions that are used to evaluate and 
1 
classify project requests.  These questions receive equal weighting when 
2 
determining the priority of a request and are as follows: 
3 
 Does the project satisfy or fulfill a regulatory requirement? 
4 
 Does the project result in cost savings, cost avoidance, and/or 
5 
revenue for Central Hudson or its stakeholders? 
6 
 Does the project enhance Central Hudson’s customer 
7 
experience? 
8 
 Does the project improve or enhance safety and wellness for 
9 
Central Hudson employees, contractors, or the public? 
10 
 Does the project reduce risk, debt, or vulnerabilities (i.e., legal, 
11 
cybersecurity, technology, infrastructure)? 
12 
 Is the project included in the current rate case authorization? 
13 
Projects are evaluated on a scale of zero to six based on the number of 
14 
attributes the investment satisfies, creating a priority score.  Each project 
15 
is then evaluated for relative urgency and classified as an ‘immediate’, 
16 
‘moderate’, or ‘low’ urgency initiative.  The priority and urgency ratings 
17 
combined with Central Hudson’s classifications of ‘maintaining standards’, 
18 
‘system enhancements’, and ‘non-discretionary’ support the global ranking 
19 
and sequencing of projects within the portfolio.  Project ranking is 
20 
leveraged to determine which projects receive capital funding.
