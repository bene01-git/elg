The City is supportive of utilizing renewable natural gas in hard to electrify end

Q. 
PLEASE EXPLAIN THIS DISTINCTION. 
4 
A. 
The decay of organic matter produces a combination of methane and carbon dioxide.  
5 
Methane produced from uncontrolled decay is emitted directly into the atmosphere, where 
6 
it is a far more potent precursor to GHG emissions than carbon dioxide.  Methane produced 
7 
from controlled decay, such as in a wastewater resource recovery facility, is often flared to 
8 
the atmosphere to convert it into carbon dioxide and lower its GHG emissions potential.  If 
9 
that methane is instead captured and re-used, the net effect on the environment is either 
10 
zero or positive.  That is, the captured methane used to create electricity has a zero net 
11 
effect because the methane would otherwise have been flared and produce the same amount 
12 
of carbon dioxide.  To the extent methane that otherwise would have been directly emitted 
13 
is captured and used to create energy, the net effect is positive because the global warming 
14 
potential of the resultant carbon dioxide is 27 to 83 times lower than methane. 
15
