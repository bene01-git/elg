II. 
CUSTOMER SERVICE PERFORMANCE MECHANISM  
1 
Q. 
What customer service performance metrics are included in the Companies’ 
2 
CSPM? 
3 
A.   
Currently, both NYSEG’s and RG&E’s CSPM include the following customer 
4 
service performance metrics: (1) PSC Complaint Rate; (2) Contact Satisfaction; 
5 
(3) Percent of Calls Answered in 30 Seconds; and (4) Estimated Bills. In their 
6 
Rate Plans, the Companies are assigned a customer service quality performance 
7 
target for each metric, and if a Company fails to meet the performance target(s), 
8 
then it will incur an NRA. (See Exhibit__(CSP-5) of the Customer Service 
9 
Panel’s initial testimony for a list of the Companies’ current targets and NRAs for 
10 
each metric). I further note that while the Companies’ performance on each metric 
11 
is measured monthly, the target threshold(s) are applied to the average of each 
12 
Company’s twelve-monthly scores.     
13 
 
14 
Q. 
Please describe the customer service performance metrics. 
15 
A. 
The PSC Complaint Rate metric measures the number of complaints per 100,000 
16 
customers “charged” to the Company on a monthly basis, but the metric’s target is 
17 
based on the average of twelve-monthly scores. Under the Companies’ current 
18 
rate plans, a complaint is considered “charged” when a customer, after first 
19 
contacting the Company to express dissatisfaction with a Company action or 
20

practice, contacts the Commission to express their dissatisfaction with the 
1 
Company’s resolution of their complaint.  
2 
 
The Contact Satisfaction metric measures the satisfaction ratings resulting from a 
3 
random survey of residential customers who have contacted the Company.  
4 
 
The Percent of Calls Answered in 30 Seconds is exactly that and measures the 
5 
percentage of total calls that are answered within 30 seconds by a Company 
6 
representative.  
7 
 
The Estimated Bill metric measures the percentage of occurrences when the 
8 
Company has to render an estimated bill based on an estimated meter read 
9 
because it was unable to obtain an actual meter reading at the time of the 
10 
scheduled meter read.      
11

Q. 
What is the function of customer service performance metrics?  
13 
A. 
Customer service performance metrics provide utilities with incentives to provide 
14 
acceptable levels of customer service. Quality service is important to customers 
15 
and serves the public interest. Due to the monopolistic nature of utility service, 
16 
customers cannot select another delivery service provider if they are not satisfied 
17 
with the quality of service they receive from NYSEG and RG&E. Meaningful 
18 
measures associated with appropriate financial risk ensure that utility companies 
19 
provide adequate service to their customers. Structured correctly, customer
