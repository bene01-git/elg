A.

6

9

15 
to be given consideration are wear and tear,

demand and requirements of public authorities.

plant, means the loss in service value not 
23 
restored by current maintenance, incurred in 
24 
connection with the consumption or prospective 
25 
retirement of gas plant in the course of service 
26 
from causes which are known to be in current
