Q.

Currently, Central Hudson has the following customer service performance 
4 
indicators: (1) PSC Complaint Rate; (2) Residential Customer Satisfaction 
5

6

18 
based on the average of twelve-monthly scores. Under the Company’s current rate 
19

20 
the Company to express dissatisfaction with an action, practice, or conduct of the

Q. 
What is the function of customer service performance measures?  
13 
A. 
Customer service performance metrics provide utilities with incentives to provide 
14 
acceptable levels of customer service. Quality service is important to customers 
15 
and serves the public interest. Due to the monopolistic nature of utility service, 
16 
customers cannot select another delivery service provider if they are not satisfied 
17 
with the quality of service they receive from Central Hudson. Meaningful 
18 
measures associated with appropriate financial risk ensure that utility companies 
19 
provide adequate service to their customers. Structured correctly, customer

Commission order in 2016; that order appeared to be focused on electric utilities

regulatory construct, stating that:

Q. 
What is Community Distributed Generation, or CDG? 
5 
A.  
CDG enables customers for whom rooftop solar was not a viable option to 
6 
directly participate in and enjoy the benefits of renewable energy programs. As 
7 
part of the CDG program, CDG Sponsors can develop an eligible generation 
8 
project—usually a solar photovoltaic (PV) system connected to a utility 
9 
distribution network—and enroll customers served by that utility as members. 
10 
Members then receive credits on their utility bills for the generation the CDG 
11 
project injects into the utility system. In return, members pay the CDG Sponsor a 
12 
monthly subscription fee, which may be fixed or variable, but that is generally 
13 
designed to be some amount less than the value of the credits the customer 
14 
receives.6
