14 
A. 
My testimony examines data reliability issues and affordability and customer service 
15 
matters in several areas including but not limited to evidence that: 
16 
1. Uncertainties arising from unresolved aspects of the Commission’s ongoing 
17 
investigation into problems involving the September 1, 2021, deployment by Central 
18 
Hudson of a new CIS make it difficult to place any reliance on the data included in the 
19 
Company’s filings in these proceedings. Key aspects underlying this uncertainty relate 
20 
to expenses and capital investments that Central Hudson may have incurred, or may 
21 
incur, imprudently in the development of its CIS system, but that may not have been 
22 
sufficiently identified or excluded from consideration in Central Hudson’s proposals. 
23 
It also involves the failure of the Company in its filings to acknowledge that some 
24 
portion of its arrears accumulation after August 2021, may relate to problems with the 
25 
September 1, 2021, CIS launch; and the extent to which these problems may have 
26 
contributed to the Company’s deteriorating credit metrics.

1 
residential customers would be 31.9%. For residential gas, the increase would be 
2 
29.2%. Gas customers with monthly usage at or above 200 cubic feet (“Ccf”) would 
3

4

5 
3. Gas energy affordability program (“EAP”) participants, especially heating customers, 
6 
would be exposed to detrimental increases in their energy burdens as a result of the 
7 
Company’s requested rate increases. Central Hudson proposes to lower gas bill 
8

9

10

13 
progressed from about $800 prior to the COVID-19 pandemic and $600 in the 
14 
aftermath of Phase 1 arrears relief, to more than $1,100 for the year ended September 
15 
2023. 
16

17

18

19

20 
the application Phase 2 arrears relief. During the twelve months ended September 2023, 
21

22 
an average of about $77 million. These amounts are approximately 222% and 1,442%

higher than the number of customers in arrears and their amounts owed in the twelve 
24 
months leading up to the Pandemic.
