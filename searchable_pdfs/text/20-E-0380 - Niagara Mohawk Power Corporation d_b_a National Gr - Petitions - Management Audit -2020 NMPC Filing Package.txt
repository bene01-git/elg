III. 
Management Audit Update 
10 
Q. 
Please discuss the status of the Management Audit. 
11 
A. 
On May 17, 2018, the Commission issued an Order Instituting Proceeding 
12 
and Authorizing Issuance of a Request for Proposals (“Audit Order”) for an 
13 
independent consultant to conduct an audit of National Grid’s New York 
14 
electric and gas businesses in accordance with Section 66(19) of the Public 
15 
Service Law (“PSL”).  In September 2018, the Commission selected SCG 
16 
to conduct the audit under the supervision of Staff.  The Management Audit 
17 
formally commenced in November 2018 with a full-day presentation by 
18 
National Grid senior management on its electric and gas operations to SCG 
19 
and Staff.   
20 
 
21
