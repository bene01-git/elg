I. 
Introduction 
1 
Q. 
Please identify the members of the Gas Supply Panel. 
2 
A. 
The Gas Supply Panel (“Panel”) consists of Elizabeth D. Arangio, MaryBeth 
3 
M. Carroll, Peter C. Metzdorff, and Anthony P. Taddeo.  This is the same Panel 
4 
that submitted direct testimony as part of Niagara Mohawk’s July 31, 2020 
5 
filing.  The terms defined in the Panel’s direct testimony have the same 
6 
meanings here. 
7 
 
8 
Q. 
What is the purpose of the Panel’s corrections and updates testimony? 
9 
A. 
The purpose of the Panel’s testimony is to provide updates to certain exhibits 
10 
based on an updated gas load forecast provided by Company Witness Theodore 
11 
Poe, Jr., as well as to reflect other minor corrections and updates as discussed 
12 
below.   
13 
 
14 
Q. 
Has the Panel prepared any exhibits to reflect its corrections and updates? 
15 
A. 
Yes.  The following exhibits were prepared by the Panel or under its direction 
16 
and supervision: 
17 
• Exhibit __ (GSP-4CU): Projected Monthly Gas Stored Underground 
18 
Volumes and Dollars for Rate Year and Data Years by Storage Facility; 
19 
• Exhibit __ (GSP-6CU): Forecast of Variable Gas Expense for the Rate 
20

2 
 
3 
Q. 
Please describe the impact of the updated gas load forecast. 
4 
A. 
Each of the three exhibits listed above reflect the updated gas load forecast, 
5 
which is provided in the corrections and updates testimony of Company 
6 
Witness Poe.  Overall, the impact of the updated gas load forecast was minimal.  
7 
The total forecast normal firm customer requirements have increased slightly.  
8 
The forecast normal requirements of firm sales customers have decreased, 
9 
while the forecast normal requirements of firm transportation customers have 
10 
increased.  The revised forecast had minor impacts to the volumes and costs 
11 
that were reported in each of the exhibits referenced above.  The design day 
12 
forecast for firm customers increased slightly but does not have a material 
13 
impact on the Panel’s exhibits.   
14 
 
15 
Q. 
Please describe any other corrections and updates to the Panel’s exhibits. 
16 
A. 
In addition to reflecting the updated gas load forecast, Exhibit __ (GSP-6CU) 
17 
and Exhibit __ (GSP-7CU) reflect changes to forecast fixed costs because of 
18 
recent pipeline tariff rate changes.  Further, Exhibit __ (GSP-6CU) and Exhibit 
19 
__ (GSP-7CU) incorporate an update to the US Dollar/Canadian Dollar 
20 
exchange rate.  Finally, Exhibit __ (GSP-7CU) reflects a correction to 
21

anticipated CNG supply fixed costs, as the Company inadvertently excluded a 
1 
portion of CNG fixed costs in its July 31 filing. 
2 
 
3 
In addition to the correction and updates to the forecast fixed costs described 
4 
above, the Company identified an error in Exhibit __ (GSP-4) in which the 
5 
volumes for 2022/23 did not reference the appropriate corresponding months in 
6 
the spreadsheet used to generate the exhibit.  This also led to errors in Exhibit 
7 
__ (GSP-7), as the two exhibits are related.  The updated Exhibits __ (GSP-
8 
4CU) and (GSP-7CU) correct these errors in the original exhibits.

Does this conclude your testimony?
