INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On August 27, 2020, Central Hudson Gas & Electric 
Corporation (Central Hudson or the Company) filed amendments to 
its tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 15 – Electricity and P.S.C. No. 
12 - Gas, to increase the annual electric and gas delivery rates 
for the rate year ending June 30, 2022.  Central Hudson 
requested an increase in annual electric delivery revenues of 
approximately $32.8 million (8.4% increase in base delivery

DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
On July 16, 2021, the Company filed a letter 
discussing their consent to extend the maximum suspension period 
of these proceedings through and including November 20, 2021.  
The Company requested the extension to provide additional time 
to facilitate the proceedings’ settlement process.  The request 
is subject to a make whole provision that would keep 
Central Hudson and their customers in the same economic position 
as they would have been absent the extension.  According to 
Central Hudson’s letter, under the make whole provision, the 
Company would recover or refund any revenue under-collections or 
overcollections, respectively, resulting from this extension.  
Central Hudson would calculate any revenue adjustments as the 
difference between the sales revenues the Company would have 
billed at new rates during the extension of the suspension 
period and the same level of sales revenues at current rates.   
The revenue adjustments would include all applicable surcharges 
and carrying charges and would be subject to reconciliation in 
accordance with all applicable adjustment mechanisms. 
To facilitate the orderly conduct of these 
proceedings, the effective date of the tariff leaves filed by 
the Company is further extended through and including 
November 20, 2021, unless otherwise ordered by the Commission.  
 
The Commission orders: 
1.   The operation of the matters suspended by Order 
issued July 16, 2021, in these proceedings, are further 
suspended and the use of the rates, charges, rules and 
regulations stated in said Order be further extended through and 
including November 20, 2021, unless otherwise ordered by the 
Commission. 
2.   Central Hudson Gas & Electric Corporation is
