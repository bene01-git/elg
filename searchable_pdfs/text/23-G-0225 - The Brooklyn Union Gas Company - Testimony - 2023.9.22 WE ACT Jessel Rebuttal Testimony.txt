Q. 
What is the investment mandate? 
6 
A. 
The investment mandate, codified at Section 75-0117 of the New York Environmental 
7 
Conservation Law, requires that at least 35% of the benefits of agency investments in 
8 
clean energy and energy efficiency flow to DACs. The investment mandate also 
9 
establishes a goal that DACs receive at least 40% of those benefits.

DACs.

13

14 
expenditures within DACs to the proportion of the Companies’ service territory that is 
15 
within DACs.4 The rationale for this analysis is that “the proportion of expenditures 
16 
occurring within [DACs] should roughly match the proportion of the Companies’ service 
17 
territories that are considered [DACs].”5
