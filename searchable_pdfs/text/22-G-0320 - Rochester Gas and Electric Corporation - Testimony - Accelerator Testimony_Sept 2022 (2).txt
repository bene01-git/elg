3. SUMMARY OF CONCLUSIONS  
5 
Q. 
Please summarize your conclusions and recommendations. 
6 
A. 
Although the Companies have made some climate-related progress,  
7 
e.g., RG&E’s heat pump programs, and have proposed some valuable 
8 
initiatives in this filing, e.g., electric heat make ready program, overall, 
9 
the Companies have not moved much away from“business as usual” in 
10 
an era when the State is calling for rapid, equitable decarbonization. 
11 
As such, the Companies should stop investing in, and start downsizing, 
12 
the gas system, increase investments to advance equitable access to 
13 
clean heating and cooling technologies, and establish and track metrics 
14 
that can accurately assess the Companies’ performance on climate and 
15 
equity related targets included in the State’s Climate Leadership and 
16 
Community Protection Act.

Q.  
Have global temperatures warmed at all? If so, are there any  
3

5

6

7

8

9

10 
predicts, with high confidence, that climate change will have negative 
11 
effects on human health, including, but not limited to, heat related 
12 
morbidity and mortality and increases in vector-borne illnesses such as 
13 
malaria, and dengue fever.3  
14

15 
broken, including one snowfall record, five extreme rain records, and
