BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On November 29, 2021, Central Hudson Gas & Electric 
Corporation (Central Hudson or the Company) filed tariff 
amendments to its electric and gas tariff schedules, P.S.C. No. 
15 - Electricity and P.S.C. No. 12 – Gas, respectively.  The 
amendments effectuate the rates and charges for Rate Year 
(RY) 1, the 12-month period ending June 30, 2022, in

BACKGROUND 
 
 
The 2021 Rate Order adopted the terms of the Joint 
Proposal (JP) establishing a three-year rate plan for electric 
and gas service.  The approved rate plan established a decrease 
in the RY 1 electric base delivery revenue, followed by 
increases in RY 2 and RY 3, and gas base delivery revenue 
increases in each of the three rate years.  The Signatory 
Parties agreed to spread out the electric base delivery revenue 
increases evenly across the three rate years.2  This treatment of 
electric base delivery revenues when combined with other agreed 
upon bill credits, the net electric base delivery revenues 
reflect a $1.1 million decrease in RY 1 (0.2 percent decrease on 
total revenues) and increases of $8.0 million (1.2 percent 
increase) and $8.7 million (1.2 percent increase) in RY 2 and RY 
3, respectively.  After applying bill credits to gas, the base 
delivery revenues reflect increases of $3.9 million (1.9 percent 
increase), $3.9 million (1.8 percent increase) and $4.0 million 
(1.8 percent increase) in RY 1, RY 2, and RY 3, respectively.

Implementation of the 2021 Rate Order required 
numerous amendments to Central Hudson’s electric and gas tariffs 
and statements.  The 2021 Rate Order directed Central Hudson to 
file such electric and gas tariff changes to effectuate the 
provisions of the 2021 Rate Order for RY 1, on not less than two

temporary basis.  The amendments would not become effective on a 
permanent basis until approved by the Commission.  In addition, 
the 2021 Rate Order provided parties the opportunity to submit 
comments on the proposed amendments within 14 days of Central 
Hudson’s filing.  A comment was received from the Town of 
Newburgh (Town) on March 18, 2022, after the 14-day comment 
period, seeking reconsideration of the rate increases.  The 
comment received is further described and discussed below.   
 
TARIFF FILINGS 
 
On November 29, 2021, Central Hudson filed tariff 
amendments to amend its electric and gas tariff schedules, 
P.S.C. No. 15 and P.S.C. No. 12, respectively.  The Appendix to 
this Order sets forth a list of the amendments and statements 
filed by the Company.  As prescribed by the Commission’s 2021 
Rate Order, the electric tariff amendments effectuate a $1.1 
million decrease in electric delivery service revenues for RY 1, 
and the gas tariff amendments effectuate a $3.9 million increase 
in gas delivery service revenues.  The 2021 Rate Order waived 
the requirement for newspaper publication pursuant to Public 
Service Law §66(12)(b) for RY 1, but required publication not 
later than six weeks following the effective date of the tariff 
amendments.  Central Hudson complied with the 2021 Rate Order’s 
newspaper publication directive.

COMMENT  
 
On March 14, 2022, the Board of the Town of Newburgh, 
in its capacity as governing body of the Town, passed a 
resolution opposing the announced electricity and natural gas 
supply price increases for energy delivered by Central Hudson 
without reconsidering the delivery rates approved in the 2021 
Rate Order.  This resolution was received on March 18, 2022.  
The resolution states that Central Hudson has announced:  
increases in supply prices for both electricity and natural gas 
based on market price increases stemming from regional and 
worldwide factors, including weather; that delivery rate 
increases were justified partially because of the restrictions 
on bill collections in place during the COVID-19 pandemic, and 
such restrictions will soon expire; that customers are 
experiencing increases in supply and delivery rates during a 
time when inflation is also placing a financial burden on 
customers; and that Central Hudson’s parent company, Fortis, 
Inc., increased its dividend amount per share on December 1, 
2021, over the prior dividend paid.   
 
LEGAL AUTHORITY 
 
 
Pursuant to PSL §§5, 65(1), and 66(1) and (12), the 
Commission has the legal authority to review proposed tariff 
leaves, as well as to modify, reject or approve such filed 
tariffs.  As such, the Commission has the legal authority to 
review the Company’s filings and approve and make effective 
tariff amendments, as prescribed in this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
Authorizing the tariff amendments effectuating RY 1 on 
a permanent basis is a ministerial action that confirms the 
tariff amendments comply with the previously issued 2021 Rate

the rates approved by the Commission in the 2021 Rate Order.
