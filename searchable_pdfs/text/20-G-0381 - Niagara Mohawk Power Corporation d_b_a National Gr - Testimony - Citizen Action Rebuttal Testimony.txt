INTRODUCTION AND OVERVIEW

Q. 
Are you the same Bob Cohen, who submitted direct testimony in this proceeding on 
6

7 
A.

8 
 Q. 
What is the purpose of this rebuttal testimony?  
9 
 A.  
The purpose of this rebuttal testimony is to explain why the recommendation in the direct 
10 
testimony of the Staff Gas Reliability Panel (“SGRP”) of the Department of Public 
11 
Service (“DPS”) for a Non Pipeline Alternative (“NPA”) process for the Albany Loop 
12 
Closure Project1 should be rejected. Adoption of this recommendation would not be 
13 
consistent with the achievement of the greenhouse gas (“GHG”) reduction mandates of 
14 
the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (“CLCPA”). Instead, as we 
15 
recommended in our direct testimony, ratepayer funding for Albany Loop should be 
16 
rejected by the Public Service Commission (“Commission”).

Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“National Grid” or the

“Company”) responded to IR DPS-754 that it had not considered any specific NPAs for

Albany Loop. The Company asserted that no single NPA could provide the same 
3

6 
Q. 
Do you agree with the SGRP proposal to institute a NPA process? If not, why not? 
7 
A. 
No. For two reasons, Citizen Action opposes the DPS staff recommendation to establish a 
8 
NPA process as an alternative to the Albany Loop proposal made by the Company. First, 
9 
the SGRP NPA proposal, like the Company proposal, is not aimed at ensuring, nor would 
10 
it ensure, that the Company does its fair share to meet the GHG reduction mandates of 
11 
the CLCPA. Second, there are far superior alternatives than NPAs to address the heating 
12 
and cooling needs of National Grid customers, including Environmentally Beneficial 
13 
Electrification, even assuming that the Company has made the case that the Project is 
14 
needed from a gas capacity or a reliability perspective, which Citizen Action strongly 
15 
disputes.3

Q. 
Are there alternatives to National Grid’s plan that meet any additional anticipated 
7 
heating and cooling needs of customers in the Company’s service territory?  
8 
A. 
The PACE/AGREE Direct Testimony explains in detail that the “most effective way to 
9 
meeting the state’s aggressive 40% and 85%  GHG emissions mandates is through 
10 
aggressive application of … Environmentally Beneficial Electrification” (see

15 
customers” (Id. at 4). As our direct testimony explained, the Albany Loop Project did not 
16 
meet this criteria for further gas investments. Therefore, ratepayer funding for the Project 
17 
should not be permitted.
