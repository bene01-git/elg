   PULP’s Letter in Support of AGREE’s Motion for Post-Hearing Briefs and Sur-replies 
 
Dear Administrative Law Judges Costello and Leary: 
 
The Public Utility Law Project of New York, Inc. (“PULP”) submits this letter in support of 
AGREE and Sane Energy’s joint motion seeking leave to file post-hearing briefs (and 
alternatively sur-reply statements in opposition to the Joint Proposal). The requested briefs will 
address issues related to the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (“CLCPA”), the 
Department of Public Service Staff’s legal position on the CLCPA set forth in its Reply 
Statement in Support of the Joint Proposal, and the testimony elicited on cross-examination at 
the June 21, 2021, hearing. PULP believes that additional replies will result in a more robust and 
comprehensive record in these proceedings. 
 
If you have any questions or concerns, please contact me directly.
