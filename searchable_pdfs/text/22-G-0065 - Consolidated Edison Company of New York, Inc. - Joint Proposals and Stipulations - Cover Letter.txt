Re:  
Case 22-E-0064 and 22-G-0065 – Consolidated Edison Company of New York, 
Inc. - Electric and Gas Rates – Corrected Appendix 21 to Joint Proposal. 
 
Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
On behalf of the Signatory Parties, enclosed please find attached for filing in the above-
captioned proceedings a corrected Appendix 21 to the Joint Proposal, which is included in the 
file “JP Appendices- May 10 Corrected Combined” which also contains all the appendices to the 
Joint Proposal.  No changes were made to any of the other appendices. 
The edits are solely to Appendix 21, page 1, Section b, Exclusions, removing 
“abnormally high energy commodity supply costs, abnormal economic conditions” so to 
conform the CSPM exclusions to reflect those provided for in the currently effective joint 
proposal in Cases 19-E-0065 and 19-G-0066 (Appendix 18, p. 1).  The removal of this language 
from the Joint Proposal would not prevent the Company from seeking the same or similar relief 
from the CSPM by petition to the Commission.  Please contact me if you have any questions or 
concerns on this matter.
