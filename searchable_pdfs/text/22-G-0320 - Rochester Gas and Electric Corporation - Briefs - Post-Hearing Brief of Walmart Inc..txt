I. 
INTRODUCTION 
Walmart Inc. ("Walmart") hereby submits this Post-Hearing Brief ("Brief") in further 
support of the Joint Proposal.1  On July 17 and 18, 2023, an evidentiary hearing was held for the 
purposes of evaluating the Joint Proposal through the presentation of evidence and cross-
examination of witnesses.  Witnesses from the New York State Electric & Gas Corporation 
("NYSEG") and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation ("RG&E") (collectively, "Companies") 
and New York State Department of Public Service ("Staff") testified in support of the Joint

Proposal and were subject to cross-examination by multiple intervening parties.  Walmart appeared 
at the hearing as a signatory to the Joint Proposal.   
Walmart believes that the testimony provided at the evidentiary hearing was consistent 
with Walmart's support of the Joint Proposal as detailed in its Initial Statement in Support of the 
Joint Proposal ("Statement in Support") filed on June 27, 2023.  In its Statement of Support, 
Walmart did not address and did not take a position on each substantive issue raised by the 
Companies or other parties in the course of these proceedings or resolved by the Joint Proposal 
and the settlement among the various parties.  As indicated in its Statement of Support, Walmart 
continues to believe, that the Joint Proposal as a complete settlement of all issues is in the best 
interest of the Companies, its customers, and the New York public.         
II. 
ARGUMENT 
 
As noted, Walmart did not address every substantive issue presented by the Companies or 
other parties in the course of these cases.  Walmart presented evidence and arguments in the form 
of the Direct Testimony of Alex J. Kronauer, addressing distinct and discreet issues:  (1) the 
Companies' proposed return on equity ("ROE");2 (2) the Companies' proposal to include a stay-
out premium;3 (3) cost of service and revenue allocation;4 (4) the revenue requirement and rate 
design for NYSEG's rate Service Classification No. 7 Secondary, Large General Service with 
Time-of-Use Metering ("SC 7-1");5 (5) the revenue requirement and rate design for RG&E's rate 
Service Classification No. 8 – Large General Service – Time-of-Use Rates ("SC 8");6 (6) the

First, some parties have attempted to indicate that the ROE of 9.20 percent provided by the 
Joint Proposal is unreasonable due to its deviation from litigated positions.  See, e.g., July 17, 2023 
Evidentiary Hearing Transcript ("Tr."), p. 170, line 2, to p. 171, line 12; Initial Statement in 
Opposition of AARP New York to Joint Proposal, p. 11; Reply of the Public Utility Law Project 
to the Statements in Support and Limited Support to the Joint Proposal, p. 4.  Such arguments are 
unfounded.  The very nature of a settlement is a deviation from litigated positions based on 
compromise.  The Joint Proposal's ROE of 9.20 is distinct from the Companies', Staff's, and other 
parties' litigated positions because it is the result of settlement negotiations.  The 9.20 percent ROE 
embodied in the Joint Proposal reflects a significant compromise between the signatory parties and 
provides the Companies with an opportunity to earn a fair return while still protecting customers' 
expectations of safe and reliable service at just and reasonable rates.           
 
Second, and from a broader perspective, Walmart supports the Joint Proposal in full, 
despite addressing discrete issues of importance to Walmart during these proceedings, because a 
complete settlement saves the Commission and the parties, and therefore the New York public, the 
time and expense of fully litigating entire portions of the rate case.  As it is, these cases were

III. 
CONCLUSION 
 
For these reasons, Walmart believes that the Joint Proposal represents a fair, just, and 
reasonable resolution of the issues in these cases.  The Joint Proposal also reflects many hours of 
negotiation between traditionally adverse parties representing numerous public interests within the 
state.  Walmart therefore believes that the Joint Proposal is in the public interest and respectfully 
requests that the Commission adopt it without modification.
