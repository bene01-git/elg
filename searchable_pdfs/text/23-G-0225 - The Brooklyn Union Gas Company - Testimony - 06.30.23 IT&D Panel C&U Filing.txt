Q. 
Please describe the correction to adjust the amortization period for the 
2 
Backoffice Refresh project.  
3 
A. 
Large-scale software projects, such as the proposed Backoffice Refresh, typically 
4 
have a longer amortization period that aligns with their ability to remain functional 
5 
and supported longer than smaller scale IT&D projects.  In the April 28, 2023 filing, 
6 
the Companies inadvertently applied an 84-month amortization period instead of 
7 
the typical 120-month amortization period for projects of this scale.  IT&D Exhibit 
8 
___ (ITDP-4CU) reflects the correct amortization period of 120 months for the 
9 
Backoffice Refresh project.

15 
total forecast expense of $46 million over the Rate and Data Years, as provided in 
16 
Exhibit ___ (ITDP-6CU) and Exhibit ___ (ITDP-7CU).

E. 
IT&D RTB and Operating Expense 
4 
Q. 
Please describe corrections that address RTB and operating expense costs 
5 
inadvertently omitted from the initial rate filing for IT&D projects.   
6 
A. 
RTB costs are for operating and maintaining an IT&D project after it is placed in 
7 
service.  RTB costs include those associated with software licensing and 
8 
maintenance agreements, hardware maintenance agreements, and application 
9 
support contracts, as well as consultant/contractor costs, and internal labor. 
10 
Operating expense costs include operation and maintenance (“O&M”) needs before 
11 
a project is placed in service.  
12 
 
13 
 
In developing their forecast for the April 28, 2023 filings, the Companies 
14 
inadvertently omitted RTB and operating expense costs for certain IT&D projects. 
15 
The updated RTB and operating expense costs for those projects are provided in 
16 
Exhibit___ 
(ITDP-6CU) 
and 
summarized 
in 
Exhibit___ 
(ITDP-7CU).  
17 
Additionally, the RTB and operating expense costs for IT&D projects that were 
18 
inadvertently omitted from the Companies’ rate filing, described earlier in this 
19 
testimony, are provided in Exhibit ___ (ITDP-6CU) and summarized in Exhibit 
20 
(ITDP-7CU).
