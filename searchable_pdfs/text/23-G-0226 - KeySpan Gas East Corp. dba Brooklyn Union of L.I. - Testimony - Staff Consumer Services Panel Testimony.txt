its customers.  Currently, these programs are in

13

service, utilities do not have a market-based 
15 
incentive to provide acceptable levels of

customer service as customers cannot select

another utility based on the quality of service 
18 
provided; however, ensuring that utilities 
19 
provide quality service is extremely important 
20 
to customers and is in the public interest, so 
21 
CSPIs are needed to establish an incentive for 
22 
each company to provide satisfactory levels of

customer service performance.
