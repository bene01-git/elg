Q. 
Please describe the MR STS. 
1 
A. 
Consistent with the Make-Ready Program Order, the MR STS consist of two 
2 
component metrics, one measuring performance of the Level 2 portion of the Make-
3 
Ready Program (“Level 2 EAM metric”), and the other measuring performance of 
4 
the DC Fast Charger portion (“DC Fast Charger EAM metric”).  The Level 2 EAM 
5 
metric compares the Company’s actual Level 2 Make-Ready Program performance 
6 
to the forecast baseline costs established in the Make-Ready Program Order on a 
7 
per-plug basis.  The DC Fast Charger EAM metric compares the Company’s actual 
8 
DC Fast Charger Make-Ready performance to the forecast baseline costs on a per-
9 
kilowatt (kW) of charging installed basis.  The performance incentive under this 
10 
EAM will be calculated on two occasions:  once as part of the midpoint review; and 
11 
again at the end of the program in 2025. 
12
