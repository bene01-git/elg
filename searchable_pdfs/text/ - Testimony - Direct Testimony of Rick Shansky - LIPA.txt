Q. 
WHY SHOULD MBO BE ADDRESSED IN THIS PROCEEDING? 
6 
A. 
MBO associated with interruptible service is a significant portion of the total 
7 
charges paid by the Authority for that service.  In the 12 months ending May 
8 
2019, the MBO was over $7 million.  In theory, MBO is meant to recover 
9 
fixed costs associated with low volume use of KEDLI’s system.  Yet, KEDLI 
10 
has not demonstrated that it incurs any fixed costs to support interruptible 
11 
service, inasmuch as KEDLI’s system design excludes service to interruptible 
12 
generation loads.7  This rate case is the best forum for assessing the fixed cost 
13 
of interruptible service, if any, and determining the proper replacement for 
14 
MBO.
