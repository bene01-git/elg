Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
Pursuant to the Ruling on Evidentiary Hearing Date and Process issued by Administrative 
Law Judges (ALJs) Ashley Moreno and James Costello on December 26, 2023, Department of 
Public Service Staff (Staff) filed corrected testimonies and exhibits in the above-referenced 
proceedings on January 16, 2024, including, inter alia, corrected initial testimony (First 
Corrected Testimony) and corrected exhibits (Corrected Exhibits) of the Staff Rates Panel.  
Subsequently, it came to Staff’s attention that the updated figures in the Corrected Exhibits were

ALJs and all parties at the Procedural Conference held on January 22, 2024.  In accordance with 
the directives of ALJs Moreno and Costello at that Procedural Conference, Staff respectfully 
files the attached clean and redlined versions of the updated Initial Testimony of the Staff Rates 
Panel (Second Corrected Testimony), which now fully reflects the updated figures in the 
Corrected Exhibits.  The Second Corrected Testimony reflects the following additional changes 
from the First Corrected Testimony: 
 
1. All instances of “Exhibit__(SRP” were replaced with “Exhibit__(Updated SRP”. 
2. Page 7 lines 7-10 (statement that post model adjustments were not included) was replaced 
with the statement that they were included and can be found in Exhibit__(Updated SRP-
2).
