CASE 21-G-0394 – Proceeding on Motion of the Commission as to

(Issued and Effective December 21, 2023) 
 
 
BY THE COMMISSION: 
INTRODUCTION 
 
 
On May 18, 2023, Corning Natural Gas Corporation 
(Corning or the Company) filed tariff amendments to its gas 
tariff schedule, P.S.C. No. 7 - Gas, as required by the 
Commission’s 2022 Rate Order.1  The Company’s proposed tariff 
amendments effectuate the rates and provisions for Rate Year 
(RY) 2, the twelve-month period ending June 30, 2024.  The 
amendments became effective on a temporary basis on July 1, 
2023.

BACKGROUND  
 
 
The 2022 Rate Order adopted the terms of the Joint 
Proposal and established a three-year rate plan for gas service 
commencing July 1, 2022, and concluding June 30, 2025.  For RY 
2, the 2022 Rate Order authorized a levelized total revenue 
increase of $1,798,620, or 5.2 percent.  With the removal of 
revenues driven by customer contracts, the levelized base 
delivery revenue increase is $1,743,823 or a 10.19 percent 
increase in base delivery revenues in RY 2.  Under the 2022 Rate 
Order, the tariff delivery rates were to be calculated from the 
unlevelized revenue requirement and a surcharge credit/debit 
mechanism would be used to achieve the levelized amounts for RY 
2. 
The 2022 Rate Order directed Corning to file, on not 
less than 30 days’ notice, to take effect on a temporary basis 
on July 1, 2023, tariff amendments for RY 2 as necessary to 
effectuate the provisions of the 2022 Rate Order.  The 
amendments set forth in the compliance filing would not become 
effective on a permanent basis until approved by the Commission.

TARIFF FILING 
 
 
On May 18, 2023, Corning filed tariff amendments to 
P.S.C. No. 7 - Gas to effectuate the rates and provisions for RY 
2 pursuant to the Commission’s 2022 Rate Order.  The amendments 
filed on May 18, 2023, became effective on a temporary basis, on 
July 1, 2023. 
 
 
The Company complied with the requirements of 
newspaper publication pursuant to Public Service Law (PSL) 
§66(12)(b) and 16 NYCRR §720-8.1.  The Appendix to this Order 
sets forth a list of the tariff amendments filed by the Company 
and the dates of newspaper publication. 
 
LEGAL AUTHORITY 
Pursuant to PSL §66 the Commission has the legal 
authority to review the proposed tariff leaves, as well as 
modify, reject or approve such filed tariffs to ensure that the 
rates and services governed by those tariffs are just and 
reasonable and otherwise consistent with law.  As such, the 
Commission has the legal authority to review the Company’s 
filings and approve and make effective tariff amendments as 
prescribed in this Order. 
 
DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION 
 
 
The proposed tariff amendments to schedule P.S.C. 
No. 7 – Gas properly reflect the directives of the 2022 Rate 
Order and include the rates, charges, rules and regulations 
necessary to effectuate the rates and provisions of RY 2.    
Furthermore, no comments regarding the Company’s proposed tariff 
amendments have been received.  By this Order, the Commission 
authorizes the amendments to the tariff leaves listed in the 
Appendix to become effective on a permanent basis as of the date 
of issuance of this Order.

To avoid customer confusion and to clarify the 
Company’s rates and charges, Corning’s tariff needs to provide 
the units at which the Company’s volumetric rates are being 
charged to its customers.  Our review of the Company’s tariff 
shows that it does not consistently indicate whether the 
volumetric gas rate is per a hundred cubic feet (ccf) or per a 
thousand cubic feet (mcf).  For example, tariff leaves 153, 155, 
and 162 identify the numerical value of the volumetric rates 
approved in the 2022 Rate Order for residential, Bath Electric 
Gas & Water Systems, and firm transportation customers, 
respectively, without indicating the volume unit of gas being 
charged.  Since Corning’s tariff contains volumetric rates per 
ccf and per mcf, the tariff must be clear.  Therefore, Corning 
shall file, within 30 days of the issuance of this Order, tariff 
revisions effective January 15, 2024 that identify for all 
service classifications the unit of gas being charged for each 
volumetric rate listed if such information is currently not 
identified on the respective tariff leaf.  The Commission waives 
the requirement for newspaper publication for these tariff 
revisions as the changes are housekeeping in nature.   
 
The Commission orders: 
1. The tariff amendments filed by Corning Natural Gas 
Corporation, and listed in the Appendix to this Order, are 
authorized to become effective on a permanent basis upon the 
date of issuance of this Order. 
2. Corning Natural Gas Corporation shall file with the 
Secretary to the Commission within 30 days of issuance of this 
Order tariff revisions as directed in this Order, to become 
effective January 15, 2024, on a permanent basis.  Corning 
Natural Gas Corporation shall consult with Department of Public 
Service staff at least five days prior to making this filing to
