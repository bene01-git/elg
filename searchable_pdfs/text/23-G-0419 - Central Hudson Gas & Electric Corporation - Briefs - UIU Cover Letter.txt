Dear Secretary Phillips: 
On March 1, 2024, the Utility Intervention Unit (“UIU”) of the New York State Department of 
State’s Division of Consumer Protection filed its Initial Brief in the above-referenced proceedings. 
After the filing of the Initial Brief, UIU discovered that, unlike other Evidentiary Hearing Transcripts 
filed in the above-referenced proceedings, two versions of the Evidentiary Hearing Transcript for 
Friday, January 26, 2024 (Day 3) were uploaded to the Document and Matter Management System 
(DMM).  
A version entitled “Evidentiary Hearing Transcript - Day 3 - Friday, January 26, 2024 - Empire 
State Plaza - 19th floor, Albany NY” was uploaded to the DMM on February 13, 2024 (“First, Incorrect 
Version”), while another version entitled “Evidentiary Hearing transcript - Friday, January 26, 2024 - 
Day 3 - Albany, NY” was uploaded to the DMM on February 20, 2024 (“Second, Correct Version”).  
The discovery prompted UIU to review its filed Initial Brief, to make sure that the Second, 
Correct Version of the Day 3 transcript was relied upon and referenced by UIU in its filed Initial Brief. 
Upon review, UIU found that it inadvertently relied on and referenced the First, Incorrect Version of 
the Day 3 transcript. Specifically, the page numbers in the two versions are different, and UIU relied 
on the page numbers in the First, Incorrect Version as references in its Initial Brief. The affected pages 
and what the correct ones should be are: 
1. Page 21 of the filed UIU’s Initial Brief: (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1564-1565) should have been (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1592-
1593) 
2. Page 21 of the filed UIU’s Initial Brief: (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1564-1565) should have been (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1592) 
3. Page 21 of the filed UIU’s Initial Brief: (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1602) should have been (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1630) 
4. Page 21 of the filed UIU’s Initial Brief: (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1585; 1804) should have been (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1613; 
1832) 
5. Page 21 of the filed UIU’s Initial Brief: (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1585) should have been (Ev. Hr. Tr. 1613)
