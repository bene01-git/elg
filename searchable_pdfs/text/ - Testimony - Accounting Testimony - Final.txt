New Infrastructure Investment 
2 
 
3 
Q. 
Please discuss the impact of new infrastructure investment on the Company’s rate 
4 
base. 
5 
A. 
The Company has a statutory obligation to maintain safe and reliable electric and 
6 
gas systems in a changing climate.  As discussed by the Company’s Electric 
7 
Infrastructure and Operations Panel (“EIOP”), Gas Infrastructure and Operations 
8 
Panel (“GIOP”), Clean Energy Transition Panel, and other Company witnesses, 
9 
the projected level of spending reflects the investments determined to be 
10 
necessary to install and replace infrastructure and manage risk, meet current 
11 
customer needs, plan for future customer needs, and enable the transition to a 
12 
clean energy system.  The Company makes capital spending decisions following 
13 
its extensive and rigorous analysis that is guided by our long- and short-term 
14 
planning processes and takes into account State and local policy objectives and 
15 
potential climate change impacts.  As the witnesses explain, the Company’s 
16 
strategy is to invest in infrastructure enhancements only when less expensive 
17 
alternative solutions are not available to sustain existing reliability levels, meet 
18 
future resiliency needs, provide for localized delivery capacity needs, provide for 
19 
employee and public safety, and enable the clean energy transition.  And for gas, 
20 
the Company’s capital investment strategy is focused on making the system safer. 
21
