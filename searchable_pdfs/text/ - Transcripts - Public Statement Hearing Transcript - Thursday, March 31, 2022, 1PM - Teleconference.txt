A.L.J. COSTELLO:  I call cases 22-E-
0064 and 22-G-0065, Proceeding on Motion of the 
Commission as to the Rates, Charges, Rules, and 
Regulations of Consolidated Edison Company of New 
York, Inc. for Electric and Gas Service.   
We are here today for a public 
statement hearing that was noticed on February 28th, 
2022.  Consolidated Edison Company of New York, which 
I will refer to from now on as Con Edison, filed 
amendments to its electric and gas tariff schedules 
on January 28th, 2022, proposing to increase its 
annual electric and gas delivery revenues effective 
January 1st, 2023. 
Con Edison proposes to increase its 
electric delivery revenues by approximately $1.2 
billion, a 17.6% increase in base delivery revenues 
or an 11.2% increase in total revenues.  It also 
seeks to increase its natural gas delivery revenues 
by approximately $500 million, which would be a 28.1% 
increase in base delivery revenues or an 18.2% 
increase in total revenues.
