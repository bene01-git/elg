V. 
IMPACTS FROM THE CORPORATE ALTERNATIVE MINIMUM TAX 
Q.   Did the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022 affect the Company’s 
1 
income tax liability?     
2 
A.  Yes.  On August 16, 2022, the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022 
3 
was signed into law and included a new 15% CAMT for corporations 
4 
that report over $1 billion in profits (i.e., based on book 
5 
income).  The IRS has not yet issued final regulations 
6 
implementing this law. 
7 
Q.  How does a corporation determine if it is subject to the CAMT? 
8 
A. 
Setting aside the special rules for foreign-owned corporations, 
9 
the new regulations provide that a corporation is an 
10 
“applicable corporation” for a taxable year if its average 
11 
annual Adjusted Financial Statement Income (“AFSI”) for the 
12 
three-taxable-year period ending with the year prior to the 
13 
taxable year exceeds $1 billion or if the corporation met such 
14 
threshold for any prior taxable year. 
15 
Q. 
How does a corporation calculate its CAMT liability? 
16 
A. 
The new CAMT requires corporations to compute two separate 
17 
calculations for Federal income tax purposes and pay the 
18 
greater of the new minimum tax or its regular tax liability.  
19 
An affected corporation must make several adjustments to its 
20 
net income (e.g., disregarding Federal income taxes, 
21 
including accelerated tax depreciation, adjusting deductions 
22

25-E-XXXX & 25-G-XXXX

DIRECT TESTIMONY OF INCOME TAX PANEL 
 
14 
related to covered benefit plans) when determining its AFSI 
1 
and applying the new 15% minimum tax rate.  If a corporation 
2 
is an “applicable corporation” and its CAMT liability is 
3 
higher than its regular tax liability, the corporation will 
4 
pay the higher CAMT liability.    
5 
 
6 
Q. 
What happens to the difference between the higher CAMT 
7 
liability and the regular tax liability? 
8 
A. 
The Act allows the corporation to carryover a CAMT credit for 
9 
the difference between the CAMT liability and the regular tax 
10 
liability indefinitely.  This CAMT credit carryover can be 
11 
applied to a corporation’s regular tax liability when it 
12 
becomes a regular tax liability taxpayer in the future. 
13 
Q. 
When is this new tax effective? 
14 
A. 
The CAMT regulations applies to tax years beginning after 
15 
December 31, 2022. 
16
