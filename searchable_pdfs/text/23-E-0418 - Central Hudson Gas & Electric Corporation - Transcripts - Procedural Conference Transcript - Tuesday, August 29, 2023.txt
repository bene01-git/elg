not need to be an attorney.  But all persons

before the courts of the State of New York. 
If you have or are requesting party 
status, you are considered to have agreed to conform 
to those standards.  If you fail to adhere to those 
standards, it’s grounds for exclusion from this and 
any later proceedings. 
We have the expectation that all 
parties will treat all other parties, the A.L.J.s and 
the Commission with respect and that they conduct 
themselves with the appropriate decorum for 
participating in a formal legal proceeding. 
By requesting party status, you are 
required to become familiar with and adhere to the 
Commission's rules of procedure and to read and 
follow the rulings and instructions of the A.L.J.s  
The Commission's rules of procedure can be found at 
16 New York Codes Rules and Regulations, parts 1 
through 6.

case by engaging in discovery, filing testimony and

Parties are required to present their 
initial and rebuttal cases by means of prepared 
written testimony and exhibits.  Because this is an 
evidentiary proceeding, testimony should be provided 
by persons with expertise in the various subject 
matters.
