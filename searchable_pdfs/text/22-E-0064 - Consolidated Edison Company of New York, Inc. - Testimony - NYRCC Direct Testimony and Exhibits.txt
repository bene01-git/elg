Q. 
How does ConEd handle billing errors? 
4 
A. 
When there is an error on the customer’s supply charge, ConEd will cancel and rebill the 
5 
customer.  However, this process typically happens in two steps, with long delays 
6

9 
and/or did not charge sales tax.

Equal Access to Natural Gas Storage Capacity at the City Gate 
15 
Q. 
How is the access to storage at the City Gate different for ESCOs and ConEd? 
16 
A. 
ESCOs do not have the same level of access to storage at the City Gate.  For example, 
17 
ESCOs cannot access natural gas held in storage on “Cold Days,” whereas ConEd can 
18 
access natural gas held in storage at the City Gate. The resulting cost to serve natural gas 
19 
customers on Cold Days is significantly higher to ESCOs. When the temperature is below

a certain threshold, ESCOs are prohibited to use capacity the ESCO has stored at the City

3 
City Gate, whereas ConEd is permitted to inject capacity at the City Gate.

14
