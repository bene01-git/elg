To Ms. Phillips and all concerned parties:
It is with the strongest vehemence that I oppose National Grid 
NY's Joint proposal.  
For one, I am extremely concerned about, not only the 
continuance, but what appears to be the expansion of natural gas 
infrastructure by The Company in the areas to be served.   I 
recognize that the National Grid subsidiaries represented in this 
case are solely in the business of gas service and thus they wish 
to maintain their existence as such, however, considering the dire 
global circumstances of climate change, which is proven to be 
extremely exacerbated by methane (natural gas's primary 
component), the companies need to be looking to change their 
means of serving their clients via other modes of heat generation 
as well as non-gas alternatives regarding appliances such as 
cooking stoves and water heaters.  
To be chartered in NYS as a corporation, the corporation must 
show that they provide a public service to NYS people.  This 
public service must be their primary concern, not the proﬁts of 
their share-holders and board members.  When there is a conﬂict 
between a service that actually can cause harm to the public 
(which natural gas is increasingly shown to do in ways that not 
only have grievous individual health impacts but  harm to the 


--- Page 2 ---
planet as a whole), and an alternative one where the people are 
served with much less harm, the latter, the alternative service, the 
non-gas alternative service, would seem to be mandated. 
My sympathies are not non-existent for the workers in the gas 
companies and their concern for the survival of their very jobs, 
however, I must be very clear in asking that far greater 
consideration must be given to those of us who are concerned 
about the survival of all life on this planet if we do not act 
immediately to do everything within our power to curtail climate 
chaos, including stopping all natural gas production and usage as 
quickly as possible, especially as methane is the most egregious 
of green house gases. 
Thank you for your consideration. 
Sincerely, 
Mary T. Finneran, representing herself
