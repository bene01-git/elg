4

6 
Q-2.  Provide a summary of your professional background and experience. 
7 
A-2.  I have over 25 +years of experience working in the field of Human Services.  My 
8 
career spans a variety of positions and experiences servicing communities in need.  I have 
9 
worked with seniors, deaf adults with mental health illness, homeless, veterans, re-entry 
10

11

12

13 
heating issues.

15

16 
providing financial assistance for fuel and other services that community members struggle with 
17 
providing for themselves. The ability to prevent individuals and families from freezing is of the

19 
community members.  The HEAP program allows us to provide help and hope to those in need.

II. Purpose of Testimony and Recommendation 
1 
Q-3.  What is the purpose of your testimony?  
2 
A-3.  To oppose the Central Hudson rate increase, and to share the importance of 
3 
energy affordability for HEAP recipients in Ulster County. 
4 
 
5 
Q-4.  Can you describe the types of community members and residents you work 
6 
with through the HEAP program, and what are their specific needs and concerns? 
7 
A-4.  In Ulster County we work with all types of community members in need.  This 
8 
includes our most vulnerable seniors, children, families and low-income members.  
9 
 
10 
Q-5.  Have you observed any billing problems or affordability issues among the 
11 
low-income residents you assist in relation to their energy bills with Central Hudson? 
12 
A-5.  On several occasions we have had complaints regarding estimated billing and rate 
13 
increases. Our clients are struggling to meet these increases. We have individuals who have filed 
14 
formal complaints with Central Hudson regarding billing and the inconsistency of rates with no 
15 
resolution of the issues that were presented. In addition, Central Hudson is not currently giving 
16 
out Final Termination notices that can be used to apply for emergency temporary assistance from 
17 
the Department of Social Services.

Q-10.  With NYS setting targets for the purpose of increasing the number of 
1 
households to switch from off of gas and oil heat, to electric heat (heat pumps), and to 
2 
increase electrical vehicle purchases and usage, do you believe based on your knowledge, 
3 
experience, or observation that your clients will be inclined to transition to electric cars and 
4 
heat, with Central Hudson as their utility provider? 
5 
A-10.  In my experience and opinion clients will not be inclined to purchase an electric 
6 
vehicle or change their heating source because of their previous experiences.    
7 
 
8 
Q-11.  Do your clients have trust in Central Hudson? Based on your knowledge, 
9 
observation, or experience, what would it take for their trust to be reinstated in the 
10 
company that delivers their electric and gas? 
11 
A-11.  Overall, I think community members are struggling with trusting the processes 
12 
currently. Clients began to lose trust when rate increases and estimated billing went into effect.  
13 
It would take an honest effort from Central Hudson to correct the issues that are currently 
14 
affecting clients, such as a pause in rate increase. 
15 
 
16 
Q-12.  In your opinion, do you believe the proposed rate increases are appropriate 
17 
at this time? If not, please explain. 
18 
A-12.  We do not agree with or support a rate increase.  From our experience, 
19 
community members will suffer greatly, causing them to choose between heating and providing 
20 
the basic necessities for their families.
