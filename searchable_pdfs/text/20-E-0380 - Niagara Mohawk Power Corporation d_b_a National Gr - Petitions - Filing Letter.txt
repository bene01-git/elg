Dear Secretary Phillips: 
 
 
Niagara Mohawk Power Corporation d/b/a National Grid (“Niagara Mohawk” or the 
“Company”) submits its petition for a limited waiver of the Transmission Revenue Adjustment 
(“TRA”) provisions included in Rule 43.5.1 of the P.S.C. 220 – Electric Service Tariff to 
increase the monthly cap on the amount that can be collected from or credited to customers. The 
petition seeks to temporarily increase the cap through the end of the Company’s current rate plan 
to refund customers and reduce the current TRA deferral balance more expeditiously. Niagara 
Mohawk respectfully submits that the limited waiver is in the public interest and should be 
approved by the Commission. 
 
The Company requests a waiver of the newspaper publication requirement under Public 
Service Law § 66(12) and 16 NYCRR § 720-8.1. In accordance with 16 NYCRR § 3.5(i), 
proposed notice of this filing suitable for use under the State Administrative Procedure Act is 
also enclosed. 
 
 
Please contact me if you have any questions regarding this matter.
