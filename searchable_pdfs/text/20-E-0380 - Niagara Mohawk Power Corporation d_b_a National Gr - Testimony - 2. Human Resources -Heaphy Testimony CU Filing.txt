Q. 
Please describe the basis for the change in employee headcount since 
1 
the case was filed. 
2 
A. 
In my direct testimony (at pages 7 and 8), I indicated that National Grid 
3 
expected to fill 389 incremental positions before the end of the Rate Year 
4 
and forecast the need for 490 incremental positions by the end of Data 
5 
Year 2.  Exhibit ___ (MPH-1CU) sets forth an updated list of the positions 
6 
that National Grid forecasts to be filled.  National Grid now forecasts that 
7 
392 and 488 incremental positions will be filled before the end of the Rate 
8 
Year and Data Year 2, respectively.  The filling of these positions, as 
9 
updated, is expected to result in an additional 362 full-time equivalents 
10 
(“FTEs”) for Niagara Mohawk before the end of the Rate Year and 458 
11 
FTEs for Niagara Mohawk by the end of Data Year 2.  These updates are 
12 
explained in the corrections and updates testimony of the following 
13 
witness panels:  Electric Infrastructure and Operations, Electric Vehicles, 
14 
Future of Heat, Gas Infrastructure and Operations, and Revenue 
15 
Requirements.
