Q. 
Please provide an overview of the typical 
4 
management and operations audit process. 
5 
A. 
The Commission first initiates a proceeding that 
6 
provides for the issuance of a Request for 
7 
Proposals (RFP).  The RFP details the scope of 
8 
work and expected schedule for a particular 
9 
audit.  The Department reviews proposals 
10 
received from consulting firms.  The Commission 
11 
then selects a consulting firm to conduct the 
12 
audit.  The chosen consulting firm, the 
13 
Department, and the utility or utilities subject 
14 
to the audit then enter into a multi-party 
15 
agreement which governs the conduct of the 
16 
audit.  The consulting firm develops a work 
17 
plan, interviews company personnel, performs 
18 
their analysis, and submits an audit report to 
19 
the Department.  The Commission then releases 
20 
the report to the public. 
21

6 
 
A. 
When the Commission issues a management and 
1 
operations audit report, utilities are required 
2 
by PSL §66(19)(b) to file with the Commission an 
3 
implementation plan for audit recommendations.  
4 
The Commission may require the utility to modify 
5 
its implementation plan.  Upon the Commission’s 
6 
approval or approval with modification, the 
7 
implementation plan becomes enforceable.  Staff 
8 
monitors the utility’s implementation of the 
9 
audit recommendations pursuant to the 
10 
Commission-approved implementation plan.  
11 
Utilities are required to submit regular 
12 
implementation updates to the Commission, as 
13 
well as evidence that demonstrates 
14 
implementation to Staff for review and approval.  
15 
Accordingly, audit recommendations generally 
16 
fall into one of three categories of completion 
17 
status: In Progress, Pending Review (reported as 
18 
complete by the utility but under review by 
19 
Staff), and Completed.  Utilities are required 
20 
to comply with the implementation plan until 
21 
Staff determines that all recommendations have 
22 
been implemented.
