Q. 
Please describe the changes to the Companies’ gas sales forecasts. 
11 
A. 
My corrections and updates testimony and exhibits provided forecasts of 
12 
the Companies’ gas sales for the Rate Year and Data Years using data 
13 
through February 2019 and assumed the availability of capacity supplied by 
14 
the NESE project by the winter of 2020/2021, which would have enabled 
15 
new customer connections during the Rate Year and Data Years.  While the 
16 
NESE project is currently projected to be unavailable during the Rate Year 
17 
and Data Years, the Commission recently approved a settlement agreement 
18 
(“Settlement”) between the Companies and DPS Staff in Case 19-G-0678 
19 
wherein the Companies’ agreed to lift their gas service moratorium for the 
20 
next two winters (through September 1, 2021).  To enable new customer 
21 


--- Page 5 ---
Page 4 of 5 
 
connections, the Companies have developed an operational plan that 
1 
provides for the expanded use of incremental city gate peaking supplies and 
2 
portable compressed natural gas (“CNG”) injections to maintain system 
3 
pressure during peak conditions, as well as various demand-side and energy 
4 
efficiency measures to reduce peak demand.   The Settlement also provides 
5 
a framework for identifying a long-term solution to address the current 
6 
supply constraints. 
7 
 
8 
 
Because the Companies will continue to add new customers consistent with 
9 
the growth assumptions underlying my corrections and updates forecast, no 
10 
updates are required to the forecast of customers to reflect the lifting of the 
11 
moratorium.  My forecast assumes continued sales growth in the Rate Year 
12 
and Data Years.  However, I have updated the Companies’ sales forecasts 
13 
to reflect the anticipated impact of the incremental energy efficiency and 
14 
demand response programs per the Settlement.   
15 
 
16 
For KEDNY, the updates to the gas sales forecast to reflect the incremental 
17 
demand response and energy efficiency result in a decrease in the Rate Year 
18 
from 1,689,417,139 therms to 1,689,187,510 therms compared to the 
19 
corrections and updates sales forecast.  For KEDLI, these updates result in 
20 
a decrease in the Rate Year from 1,059,164,436 therms to 1,058,941,852
