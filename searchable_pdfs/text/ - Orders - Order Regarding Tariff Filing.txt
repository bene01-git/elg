SUMMARY 
 
On June 28, 2013, pursuant to Commission Order issued 
June 13, 2013,1 the Brooklyn Union Gas Company d/b/a National 
Grid NY (KEDNY or the Company) filed tariff amendments to its 
Schedule for Gas Service, P.S.C. No. 12.2  The tariff amendments 
became effective on a temporary basis on July 1, 2013.  The 
tariff amendments revise the customer charges and rates in 
compliance with the June Order which adopted the terms of a

Joint Proposal extending the Company’s current rate plan, with

DISCUSSION 
 
As set forth in the June Order, there is no change in 
base gas delivery rates other than revenue neutral changes 
previously authorized by the Commission. The modifications of 
the specific provisions in the tariff included: (1) a new rate 
schedule under Service Classification (SC) No. 18 – Non-Core 
Transportation Service to allow for a negotiated Temperature 
Controlled Transportation option; (2) a revision to the SC No. 
6M – Temperature Controlled Sales Service – Multi-Family annual 
cap calculation; (3) a revision to the Lost and Unaccounted for 
Gas (LAUF) section that become effective September 1, 2013; (4) 
a new System Performance Adjustment mechanism to take effect 
January 1, 2015 to provide a surcharge or credit to firm sales 
and transportation customers based on the annual LAUF 
reconciliation; (5) updates to the Low Income Program delivery 
rates for SC Nos. 1AR (Residential Non-Heating) and 1BR 
(Residential-Heating); (6) the establishment of a one-time 
credit to customers served under SC 1AR or 1BR of their 
reconnection fee if their service was disconnected for non-
payment; and (7) update of the Return Requirement percentage 
used in the calculation of the Merchant Function Charge.  
 
Staff has reviewed all the modified tariff provisions 
that have been in effect on a temporary basis and found them to 
be in compliance with the June Order.  
 
 
CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATION 
 
Staff has reviewed the Company’s filing and has 
concluded that the proposed amendments comply with the 
Commission’s June 13, 2013 Order in this proceeding.  Therefore,
