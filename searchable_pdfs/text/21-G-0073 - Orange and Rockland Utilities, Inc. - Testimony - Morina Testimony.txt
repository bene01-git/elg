Q. 
Please provide an overview of the typical 
16 
management and operations audit process. 
17 
A. 
The Commission first initiates a proceeding to 
18 
conduct the audit and provides for the issuance 
19 
of a Request for Proposals (RFP).  The RFP 
20 
details the scope of work and expected schedule 
21 
for a particular audit.  The Department reviews 
22 
proposals received from consulting firms.  The 
23 
Commission then selects a consulting firm to

conduct the audit.  The chosen consulting firm, 
1 
the Department, and the utility or utilities 
2 
subject to the audit then enter into a multi-
3 
party agreement which governs the conduct of the 
4 
audit.  The consulting firm develops a work 
5

analysis, and submits an audit report to the 
7 
Department.  The Commission then releases the 
8 
report to the public.

Q.  What are a utility’s responsibilities regarding 
10 
the outcome of a management and operations audit 
11 
report? 
12 
A.  When the Commission issues a management and 
13 
operations audit report, utilities are required 
14 
under Public Service Law (PSL) §66(19)(b) to 
15 
file with the Commission an implementation plan 
16 
for audit recommendations.  The Commission may 
17 
require the utility to modify its implementation 
18 
plan.  Upon the Commission’s approval or 
19 
approval with modification, the implementation 
20 
plan becomes enforceable.  Staff monitors the 
21 
utility’s implementation of the audit 
22 
recommendations pursuant to the Commission-
23 
approved implementation plan.

Utilities are required to submit regular 
1 
implementation updates to the Commission, as 
2 
well as evidence that demonstrates 
3 
implementation to Staff for review and approval.

Accordingly, audit recommendations generally 
5 
fall into one of three categories of completion
