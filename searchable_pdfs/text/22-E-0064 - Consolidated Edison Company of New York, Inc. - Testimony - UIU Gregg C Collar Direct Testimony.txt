Q. 
Please describe the customer service performance metrics. 
1 
A. 
The PSC Complaint Rate metric measures the number of complaints per 
2 
100,000 customers “charged” to the Company on a monthly basis, but the 
3 
metric’s target is based on the average of twelve-monthly scores. Under 
4 
the Company’s current rate plans, a complaint is considered “charged” 
5 
when a customer, after first contacting the Company to express 
6 
dissatisfaction with a Company action or practice, contacts the 
7 
Commission to express their dissatisfaction with the Company’s resolution 
8 
of their complaint.  
9 
 
 
The customer satisfaction survey metrics measure the monthly 
10 
satisfaction ratings resulting from a random telephone survey of residential 
11 
customers who have recently contacted the Company either by calling the 
12 
Company’s call center and speaking to a customer service representative 
13 
or visiting one of the Company’s walk-in centers.  
14 
 
 
The Call Answer Rate measures the percentage of total calls that 
15 
are answered within 30 seconds by a Company representative.  
16 
 
 
The 
Outage 
Notification 
metric 
measures 
the 
Company’s 
17 
performance for communicating with customers, the public, and other 
18 
external interests during defined electric service outage events.

A. 
Customer service performance metrics provide utilities with incentives to 
1 
provide acceptable levels of customer service. Quality service is important 
2 
to customers and serves the public interest. Due to the monopolistic 
3 
nature of utility service, customers cannot select another delivery service 
4 
provider if they are not satisfied with the quality of service they receive 
5 
from Con Edison. Meaningful measures associated with appropriate 
6 
financial risk ensure that utility companies provide adequate service to 
7 
their customers. Structured correctly, customer service performance 
8 
measures provide utilities with incentives that should result in the high 
9 
level of service to which customers are entitled.  
10

Q. 
How do you typically arrive at your proposed service target levels for a 
11

12 
A. 
I review a company’s annual service quality performance data for the last 
13 
five calendar years. In this case, I referenced data that was provided by 
14 
the Company for 2017 to 2021, in response to UIU IR No. 67, and the 
15 
Performance Indicator Reports filed by the Company each month under 
16 
Case 15-M-0566. I calculate the five-year average and then apply two 
17 
standard deviations. I choose two standard deviations because the result 
18 
is a more reliably accurate reflection of the average. Application of two 
19 
standard deviations in this context is a well-established practice in this 
20 
industry. I note that Department of Public Service Staff (Staff) applied this
